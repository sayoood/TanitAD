    ZOD Drives

    The ZOD Drives dataset consists of 29 multi-minute sequences, with full sensor coverage. For an in-depth coverage of the sensor configuration, please visit the Sensor setup section on the Home page.

    Annotation

    Currently, the ZOD Drives dataset does not include any annotations. Its primary purpose is to support localization and mapping tasks.
