## The method, and the two rules that decide everything

**Each side's edits are measured against THAT SIDE'S OWN BASE** — the backup against `d221843`,
today's worktree against `37645fc` — and applied onto the tip only where they touch disjoint
regions. A plain two-way diff against the tip cannot tell "the tip removed this" from "the
backup added this"; the base can. Blocks that two sides both append at the end are ordered
by date. Any other overlap is refused, never guessed. Worktree writes are compare-and-swap.
