## 3. Task 1: the drivable class, its tensor, its frame (file:line)

- **Head.** refcv7's only map head is the 10 cm `map_hires` branch. A6 removed the 0.5 m map head. The 0.25 m `map_hires_bev` tensor is an encoder feature map with no classes.
  - Scoring tree `C:/Users/Admin/ev7nav` (0c44408, the tree the official scoring imported): `map_head_hires.py:610` (class), `:702` (logits return), `refc_v3.py:1895` (ride-out), `refc.py:4884` (`out["perception"]`).
  - Lander tip 7efc49f2: `map_head_hires.py:779` / `:871`, `refc_v3.py:2149`, `refc.py:5151`.
  - The forward block is byte-identical between the two trees.
- **Tensor.** `out["perception"]["map_hires_logits"]` has shape `[1, 8, 1000, 600]`. The classes are `nocls, drivable, lane, crosswalk, arrow, edge, hatched, sidewalk`, so **drivable = channel 1**.
  - The probability is the head's calibrated posterior `softmax(z − ln w)[1]`, where `w` are the run's frozen sqrt-median-frequency class weights. The head is trained with a class-weighted CE, so its raw softmax is ∝ w·P.
  - This checkpoint's decision rule is `prior_corrected`.
- **Extent and frame.**
  - Extent: x ∈ [0, 100) m ahead, y ∈ [−30, 30) m, 0.1 m cells. Row i ↔ x = (i+0.5)·0.1; column j ↔ y = −30 + (j+0.5)·0.1 (`bev_raster.py:115-120`, `semantic_map_gt_fine.py:219-226`).
  - Frame: the rig frame, +x forward, +y left, origin on the rear axle (`rig6.py:12-18`). That is NavSim's ego frame in x/y.
  - Transform: the identity, which is the same identity the bridge applies to the plan (`knots_to_navsim`). K-FRAME confirms it.
- **Export.** `code/d6_p4_export.py` makes observation-only hooks on the D6 bridge copy; the forward is untouched. The GPU work ran under `with_gpu_lock.py` (job `refcv8-d6-p4`; lock acquired 02:21, 5,912 rows in 3,041 s, released).
  - Stored per scene: the exact bitmask p ≥ 0.5, uint8 p, the raw-softmax bitmask (a diagnostic only), the prior-corrected class codes, and lift-valid.
  - Storage: zlib, 1.19 GB.

## 4. What the PASS does and does not license (Rule Zero: the bar cleared, so the next lever is named)

1. **The PASS is real but thin.** The CI's lower bound is +0.0002. It is one checkpoint and one inference seed. The interval answers *"another draw of logs?"*, not *"another inference seed?"*. The seed floor 0.0065 is cleared 4.1×. It is not a refcv8 number.
2. **How much of it is "the map knows"?** POST-HOC (`raw/p4_post_hoc_G1_vs_DER.json`, not registered):
   - Paired against G1-DER, G1 is +0.0534 [+0.0229, +0.0861] EPDMS and −12.7 pp [−14.4, −11.0] DAC0. The map's content matters.
   - But on S1, a deranged mask drops DAC0 almost as much as the own mask (−29.8 vs −30.3 pp). There the gain is mostly "move off a failing pick", which P1' already predicted: 89 % of S1 fans hold a clean candidate.
   - The map's specific contribution shows up as **not damaging clean scenes** (rest: +3.8 pp DAC0 vs +20.5 pp deranged) and on S2.
3. **The measured bottleneck is map precision at the plan level, not recall.** POST-HOC (`raw/p4_diag_rejections.json`):
   - G1 rejects **39.2 % (1,705 / 4,350) of DAC-clean deployed picks**. **99.1 % of the failing check points of those picks lie on GT-drivable area** (metric cache).
   - Per point the head is good: drivable recall 0.95 at < 10 m (K-FRAME by distance band). But a plan has 40 check points, so a 5 % per-point false-negative rate compounds into a plan-level false alarm.
   - Rejected points concentrate at < 5 m (rear corners at t = 0.5 s; 1,463) and 10–20 m (1,865).
   - Collateral costs: EP −0.065, two-frame EC −0.093 / −0.083 (the pick now changes between consecutive frames), and the rest stratum's DAC0 +3.8 pp.
4. **The next levers, ranked by measured size.** None is run here; each needs its own pre-registration.
   - **(a) A trained DAC-aware selector critic.** This is the committed PASS consequence. G-ORC shows that a perfect drivable signal is worth +0.114, and G1 captures 23 % of that.
   - **(b) A precision-side gate fix.** Options: a plan-level tolerance (k of 40 points), a near-range exclusion where the camera cannot see, or a margin on p.
   - (b) attacks the measured 39 % false-alarm rate on clean picks, which is the arm's largest cost term. Any such variant is POST-HOC until registered. Seeds are needed before any claim at this effect size.
