## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ The finding that matters most

**Two of Waymo's ten lessons are strong independent confirmation of TanitAD's two most contested
bets** — closed-loop over open-loop (L5) and a fast/slow hierarchy (L7) — **and one contains a
concession that undercuts its own headline in our favour** (L3: *"teacher-student models to optimize
onboard compute"* — they train large and **deploy small**).

⛔ **And the methodological verdict on the document: ZERO controlled ablations across all ten
lessons.** *"200+ million fully autonomous miles"* is **exposure**, not an experiment. There is no
counterfactual Waymo — no camera-only, no modular, no map-free Waymo — so the article cannot
separate *"this is what we built"* from *"this is what is necessary"*, which is the entire question.
**Their evidence class is `PUBLISHED-BLOG`; not one number in it could enter our registry.**

⭐ **This is where the programme's comparative advantage actually is: they cannot run counterfactuals
and we can.** That is now strategic guideline **S-5**.
