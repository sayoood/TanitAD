## 2. THE LADDER — what was built, and why the rungs are parameter budgets

Rungs are specified as **parameter budgets** and the width is solved by
`tanitad.eval.sitclf.width_for_param_budget`, so changing the PCA rank cannot silently move a rung.
Comparing PCA-16 and PCA-64 at the same *width* would compare different capacities and call the
difference a representation effect.

| tag | rung | params/head | what it is |
|---|---|---:|---|
| LIN | `ridge_pca16_w8` | **129** | closed-form ridge, PCA-16 × 8-frame window + intercept — **exactly the banked `ridge_img` recipe** |
| LIN | `ridge_pca64_w8` | 513 | same, PCA-64 |
| LIN | `ridge_pca256_w8` | 2,049 | same, PCA-256 — param-matched to the next row |
| LIN | `ridge_raw2048_w1` | 2,049 | **raw 2048-d readout, NO PCA and NO temporal window** — the arm the retracted "2,049" claim described |
| TF | `tf_pca16_d8 … d296` | 2,068 / 7,332 / 27,460 / 106,116 / **417,028** / 971,772 / **2,207,572** | `CausalSitHead` = the deployed `sc_train.SitHead`; **d=128 IS the deployed configuration and its exact parameter count** |
| REP | `tf_pca64_d128` | 423,172 | the deployed budget on PCA-64 — separates "more head" from "more input" |
| OPT | `tf_pca16_d128_ep24`, `tf_pca16_d296_ep24` | 417,028 / 2,207,572 | **3× the epoch budget at the same capacity** — the control that makes B4's question answerable |

**Protocol.** 2 outer folds over whole clip clusters; inside each outer TRAIN half, 20 % of clusters
held out to select the epoch (transformer) or λ (ridge) — the banked `head_img` was selected at
epoch 1 of 8, so a fixed-epoch ladder would not compare like with like. PCA fitted on that fold's FIT
rows only. Every rung scored on **identical rows**.

⇒ **The answer is SITUATION-DEPENDENT, and that is the finding.** The linear floor is separated
BETTER on `intersection` and separated WORSE on `lane_change`. The situation P8 generalised from
cannot separate either way.

### 4.2 Is it an OPTIMISATION shortcut or a REPRESENTATION limit? — the control B4 exists for

| control | `intersection` ⭐ |
|---|---|
| deployed capacity, 8 epochs | 1.326 |
| deployed capacity, **24 epochs** | 1.298 — `tf_pca16_d128_ep24` − deployed = **−0.0279 [−0.1634, +0.0994]**, not separated |
| largest capacity, 8 epochs | 1.327 |
| largest capacity, **24 epochs** | 1.369 — `tf_pca16_d296_ep24` − deployed = **+0.0430 [−0.1432, +0.2095]**, not separated |

⇒ ⛔ **NOT an optimisation shortcut.** 3× the training budget moves nothing at either capacity, and
neither arm comes near the 129-parameter floor. The transformer's disadvantage on `intersection`
survives the one manipulation that would have explained it away.

### 4.3 Head or features? — the representation axis at matched capacity

`tf_pca64_d128` (423,172 params) − `tf_pca16_d128` (417,028 params) = **+0.1598 [+0.0173, +0.3041]
SEPARATED** on `intersection`, **+0.6835 [+0.0291, +1.6273]** on roundabout (unpowered).
⇒ **Input rank IS a real lever for the transformer** — but PCA-64 at 423 k parameters still reaches
only **1.486** against the PCA-16 ridge's **1.677** at **129**. Widening the input does not close the
gap to the linear floor.

Within the ridge family on `intersection` the ordering is **PCA-16 (1.677) > PCA-256 (1.538) ≈
raw-2048 (1.516) > PCA-64 (1.401)** — the program's existing rank-16 choice comes out on top, and the
un-PCA'd raw readout does **not** beat it.

## 8. WHAT I RECOMMEND

1. ⭐ **Deploy the 129-parameter ridge for `intersection`.** It is separated better than the
   417,028-parameter head on AP-lift, on precision, on recall and on anticipation lead, in 5 of 7
   regime strata, with the same alarm budget. It is a closed-form solve — no optimiser, no epoch
   selection, ~4 s to fit.
2. ⛔ **Do not generalise it.** On `lane_change` the same substitution is **separated WORSE**. The
   right shape is **one arm per situation**, not one head for three.
3. ⛔ **Stop buying capacity.** Nothing above ~10³ parameters helps on any situation, and the
   optimisation control rules out "it was undertrained". A 2.2 M-parameter head is **1.327** where a
   129-parameter one is **1.677**.
4. ⚠️ **`roundabout` must not decide anything** at 39 clusters — including the P8 claim that started
   this. If roundabout matters, the fix is more roundabout **clips**, not a bigger head.
5. ⭐ **The next real lever is the INPUT, not the head.** `tf_pca64_d128` beats `tf_pca16_d128`
   (separated) — the transformer is input-starved, not capacity-starved. B5 (a video-pretrained
   frozen encoder) is the experiment that follows from this, not another head sweep.
6. **Promote a situation-classifier trainer into `stack/`.** The primitives are now promoted
   (`ridge_scores`, `head_param_count`, `width_for_param_budget`, `precision_recall_at_budget`); the
   trainer still lives only in hub `incoming/`.

## 9. ⚠️ WHAT THIS RUN CANNOT SAY

* **Absolute APs here are NOT comparable to the banked P8 table.** Different episodes
  (`physicalai-train-14231cd29c74` 400 + `physicalai-val-bb543bdf7836` 100, vs the parity 2376) and
  **3.7× less training data** — 39,793 fit rows per fold here against 147,022 banked train windows.
  Absolute lifts are correspondingly lower. **Every claim above is a within-substrate paired
  contrast on identical rows**, which is what a capacity comparison requires and all it licenses.
* **The corpus is junction-richer** than the banked held-out set: 0.542 vs 0.186 intersection events
  per clip, 0.094 vs 0.021 roundabout. That is why `intersection` is well powered here and why its
  base rate is 0.099 rather than 0.030.
* The encoder trained on some of these clips (`encoder_seen` is true for the train-cache half). Both
  arms of every contrast share the frozen encoder, so the contrast is unaffected; the **absolute**
  level is not a generalisation estimate.
* Two outer folds only. A rung's score comes from a fit that never saw its clip, but the ladder is
  not repeated over seeds — the intervals are cluster-bootstrap intervals, not seed intervals.
