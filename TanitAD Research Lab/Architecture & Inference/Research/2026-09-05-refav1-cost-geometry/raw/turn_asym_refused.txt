# TURN-ASYMMETRY DEMAND PROBE - REFUSED BY ITS OWN CONTROLS, TWICE. NOT A RESULT.
# Banked so the turn-asymmetry stream inherits the two traps rather than re-finding them.

## The question it tried to answer
W_KAPPA penalises k^2, which is SIGN-SYMMETRIC, yet MEASURED suppression under a
decoded turn is -98.0% left vs -27.9% right (3.5x in suppressed fraction). A
sign-symmetric cost cannot do that alone. Cheapest candidate: the corpus's left turns
DEMAND more curvature, so a quadratic penalty pulls them proportionally further.

## Refusal 1 - the low-speed curvature singularity
kappa = yawrate / speed blows up as speed -> 0. Run over all 40 windows the GT control
read max|k|GT = 1315.36 against KAPPA_MAX 0.200, with a left-bin p90 of 394.74.
The medians looked entirely plausible (left 0.12572, right 0.08415, ratio 1.494) and
would have been quotable had the control not been printed beside them.
=> Any curvature statistic on this corpus needs the v0 >= 2 m/s cut feas_audit.py
   already applies, PLUS a per-step speed floor in the denominator.

## Refusal 2 - a different labeller is a different experiment
Version 1 binned turns by the sign of the net GT yaw and got left 8 / right 10,
against the record's own per-class n_true of 11 / 8. Version 2 switched to the dump's
lat_label and got lane_keep 2 / turn_left 0 / turn_right 0.
REASON, measured:
  lat_label value counts over 40 windows: {-100: 32, 7: 2, 6: 2, 0: 2, 5: 1, 4: 1}
  -> -100 on 32 of 40 windows (a MASKED/ignore sentinel), and the non-masked
     values (0,4,5,6,7) are a DIFFERENT vocabulary from the 3-class
     lane_keep/turn_left/turn_right the four-family table reports.
=> lat_label is NOT the tactical true class. The class the record reports is derived
   inside refav1_arm.py from the GT TRAJECTORY and is not stored per window in the
   dump, so binning GT curvature by it requires reading that labeller from source.
