## §1 — ARM 1 (RUNS FIRST): `combined_seed1`

**Flags** — `combined`'s exact command line with **one** token changed,
`--plan-seed 0` -> `--plan-seed 1`. Everything else identical; audited in §4.

**Why it is not bookkeeping.** `combined`'s 0.0000 has been **reported to the PI as
the programme's first zero-violation arm**. refav1's planner is iCEM, which **samples
at inference**, so `CLAUDE.md`'s third-variance rule binds: the same checkpoint
evaluated twice does not give the same answer, and *no arm except `ccos_argmax` has a
seed replicate*. **MEASURED on the one existing pair, `kamm_over_rate` DOES move with
the seed: 0.2963 -> 0.2593, a floor of 0.03704.** A rate that moves by 0.037 under a
seed change is a rate whose *zero* must be re-measured before it is claimed.

### Committed criterion — binary, written before the arm exists

| outcome | reading | what I do |
|---|---|---|
| **HOLDS** | `kamm_over_rate` = **exactly 0.0000** (v0>=2, n=27) | the zero is a property of the CONFIGURATION, not of seed 0. The claim stands, now on two seeds. |
| ⛔ **FAILS** | **any** value > 0.0000 | the zero is **SEED-DEPENDENT**. The claim reported to the PI is **wrong**, is corrected in the same turn in `RESULT.md`, `GOALS_AND_CLAIMS.md` and `RETRACTION_LOG.md`, and the correct statement becomes *"zero on 1 of 2 inference seeds, rate <= X"*. |

⚠️ **No latitude.** 0.0000 vs 0.0370 is not a small delta on this metric — it is the
difference between *"entirely inside the friction circle"* and *"not"*. The claim as
published is qualitative, so the test is qualitative.

**Controls that must hold or the reading is void:**
1. `g` (ground truth) reads `kamm_over` **0.0000** on the same n = 27. *(A real
   vehicle's recorded motion is feasible; if `g` is non-zero the dt/origin handling is
   wrong and nothing in the block is about the planner.)*
2. `ha`, `ha0`, `ha0_ext`, `ol` ADE **bit-identical** to `combined`'s — they do not
   depend on the plan seed, so a difference means the two arms are not on one window grid.
3. At least one arm in the same table reads **non-zero** (`ha0_ext` 0.1852 is present
   in every audit).

**Secondary, not a gate:** the pair yields the **second** seed-floor table, measured
on the `combined` configuration rather than on `ccos`. Banked as
`raw/seed_floor_ext_combined.txt`. Every floor quoted about a cap+ladder arm from here
on comes from that file, not from the `ccos` one.
