## 0. Headline — what the audit found, in the order it matters for v7f

**The Delta-JEPA leak shape — an inverse decoder reading concatenated endpoints `[z_t, z_{t+1}]` — does not occur in the P2 probe set.** The only such decoder in the codebase (`InverseDynamicsHead`, `stack/tanitad/models/inverse_dynamics.py:16-24`) is not wired into the v7 model (`…/2026-08-30-t1-floor-action-sensitivity/RESULT.md` F4.2), and no P2 elimination cites it. **No P2 branch was eliminated on that leak.** But the audit found the other leak classes, and two instrument defects, that change quoted numbers:

| # | finding | class | consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | ⛔ **"Drift" is arithmetic.** With the *future endpoint* of Δz replaced by a random other frame of the same clip (start kept), the E-DEC-59 rig reads r **0.677** (`postrain30k`) / **0.673** (`rdw8p30k`) against the true drift **0.670 / 0.674** — the control reproduces **102 % / 100 %** of "drift" (R3, `raw/drift.json`). Every drift number in the programme (E-DEC-40/45/59/60/61/64/66, MM-E4/E6, gate P3, the 0.199-vs-0.669 freeze story) measures **mean reversion of a change score on its own start point**, not a transition property. | **(ii)** | **P3's metric is RE-OPENED.** The ego-marginal *null* (P2) survives — it is a difference on top of the same baseline. The readiness report's proposed decision G.1 (*"accept P3 as met by `postrain30k_freeze`, drift 0.3905"*) rests on this metric and should not be adopted until the instrument carries this control. |
