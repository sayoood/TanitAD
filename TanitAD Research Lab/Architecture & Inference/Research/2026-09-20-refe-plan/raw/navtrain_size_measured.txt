⛔ THE CONCLUSION, AND IT IS A PI DECISION, NOT A TECHNICAL ONE
  Pulling navtrain to the dev box is FEASIBLE but costs 2-3 DAYS of a link that is already
  carrying the nuPlan val split (itself ~40 h at the same measured rate), and the two would
  share the same bottleneck. On a pod it is ~1 hour.
  ⇒ navtrain should be pulled ON THE POD. This is the same rule the programme already
    recorded after measuring the 1.2 MB/s uplink: move HF-resident sources and build where
    the compute is. It also reframes the pod request -- the pod is needed for the DATA, not
    only for the training.

⚠️ WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
  * It does not say we can skip navtrain_history. REFe consumes the current frame, but the
    feature builder's history window (history_steps 5) may need history frames -- that is
    UNVERIFIED here and must be settled from the loader before anyone pulls only 304.5 GB.
  * It does not price EXTRACTION. These are .tgz; the extracted footprint is larger and is
    not measurable from the metadata API.
  * CAM_F0-only does NOT reduce the DOWNLOAD. The shards are gzipped tarballs of all
    cameras, so the bytes crossing the link are the same; only the retained disk shrinks.
