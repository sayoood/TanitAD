## 1. Question and hypothesis

HYPOTHESIS under test (`D-REFE-PICKRULE-NC-1` correction, R53): the EP-led composite accepts a correctly estimated extra safety risk for an EP gain that its EP row PREDICTS but does not DELIVER. Two readings of the same event, "the composite picks a different proposal than P2 in a set":
- **EP-OVERPREDICTED:** at the composite's pick the EP row claims more EP (against P2's pick) than the label shows. The EP term of the composite's argmax is then inflated, and the safety cost is paid for a gain that is not there.
- **EP-WITHIN-NOISE / EP-UNDERPREDICTED:** the claimed and the realised EP gain agree (or the row under-claims). The PDMS loss is then not an EP over-claim, and the committed next arm audits the rule's weights.

## 3. Scales: what is "predicted" and what is "observed" (stated now, per the brief)

- The planner applies `p = score.sigmoid()` (planner.py:551) before the aggregate, so **the EP row's prediction is `sigma = sigmoid(z_EP)`, in [0, 1], on the SIGMOID scale.** It is computed in float64 (`pickrule_lib.sigmoid64`) from the composite's EP column (and, for NC, DAC, TTC, from P2's columns, which the composite leaves unchanged).
- **The label is `labels6[..., 2]` (EP), in [0, 1], on the same probability scale.** NC, DAC, TTC labels are read as probabilities of the safe outcome (soft, 0 / 0.5 / 1 for NC), as in `posthoc_diag.py` of NC-FASTCAL.
- A raw logit is never compared with a label. Whether `sigma` is ON THE LABEL'S SCALE (a claim about the row, not about its output range) is decided by the scale gate of section 5, because the c\* row is trained mostly by a ranking loss (disclosure 3). **No mapping is fitted** (no temperature, no Platt, no isotonic) in any case.

## 4. Quantities (each per seed s in {0, 1}; point + percentile CI from the paired log-cluster bootstrap of section 2)

Notation. For set i: `a_i = pk_p[i]` (P2's pick), `b_i = pk_e[i]` (the composite's pick), `sigma_i[j] = sigmoid64(z_EP^composite[i, j])`, `y_i[j]` the EP label (float64). Sets where `a_i = b_i` contribute exactly 0 to every gain below; the means run over ALL N = 15,808 sets. `n_changed = #{i : a_i != b_i}`.

**(G) EP gain, predicted vs realised.**
- `ghat_i = sigma_i[b_i] - sigma_i[a_i]` (predicted gain, the composite's own EP row at both picks); `g_i = y_i[b_i] - y_i[a_i]` (realised gain).
- **`Delta = mean(ghat) - mean(g)`**, the CI taken on `mean(ghat_i - g_i)` (paired). Also reported: `mean(ghat)`, `mean(g)` each with a CI; the same three over the changed sets only (descriptive, no authority).
- Descriptive companion, no authority: the same predicted gain read from P2's OWN EP row (`z6[..., 2]`) at the same two picks (what P2's row would have claimed).

**(G-mass) the rank-free mass form.**
- `M_e = sum_i y_i[b_i] / sum_i sigma_i[b_i]`, `M_p = sum_i y_i[a_i] / sum_i sigma_i[a_i]` (observed / predicted EP mass at the composite's picks and at P2's picks; ratios of per-log sums).
- **`D_M = M_p - M_e`**, paired over the same resampled logs (`pickrule_lib.Stats.ratio_diff`). **[impl]:** the brief defines the mass form as "observed / predicted EP mass at the picks" and the decision form below needs one statistic; `D_M` is the difference of the two ratios. `D_M > 0` reads: the composite's picks are LESS reliably predicted than P2's picks, i.e. the row over-credits the composite relative to P2. A global rescaling `sigma -> k sigma` (k > 0) divides both ratios by k and cannot change the sign of `D_M` nor of any of its bootstrap draws, which is why this form needs no mapping.

**(D) Exact attribution of dPDMS (composite - P2) into the PDMS terms.**
- PDMS of a proposal = `NC * DAC * (w_ep EP + w_ttc TTC + w_c C) / W`, `w = V1_W = (5, 5, 2)`, `W = 12` (DDC at weight 0, no TLC). **Source of the weights (cited in the code):** `REFePlanner.V1_W = (5.0, 5.0, 2.0)` at `refe/planner.py:520` (comment there: "navsim @ 3e8291b `pdm_scorer.py:38-42`"), applied as in `aggregate` lines 558-561, imported from the pinned `pickrule_lib.V1_W`, and equal bit for bit to `epr1.pdms_np` on every proposal (control C-PDMS). The attribution is of this landed LABEL-LOOKUP PDMS.
- **Counterfactual substitution in a FIXED order: NC, DAC, EP, TTC, C.** Start from the five terms of P2's pick A. Replace the NC multiplier by the composite pick B's, then the DAC multiplier, then the EP term, then TTC, then C. The contribution of term t is `PDMS(state_t) - PDMS(state_{t-1})`, in float64 (labels cast to float64 first), per set. The five contributions telescope to `PDMS(B) - PDMS(A)` per set. Each contribution is reported x100 as `mean over all sets`, with a CI. The EP, TTC and C contributions are therefore valued at the composite's NC and DAC multipliers (order dependence is the point of the sensitivity below).
- **Sensitivity (descriptive, no authority): the Shapley attribution over all 120 orders** (coalition value `v(S)` = PDMS with the terms in S taken from B and the others from A). Efficiency (sum = dPDMS) is checked.
- **Share of the loss:** `share_t = sum_i contrib_t / sum_i dPDMS_i` (ratio of per-log sums; the denominator is negative, so a custom signed ratio CI is used, NaN draws counted).
- **Dominant term (ATTRIBUTION, not pass / fail), per seed:** if the point dPDMS >= 0 the seed reads `NO-LOSS` and names no term. Otherwise the dominant term is the term with the largest point share, i.e. the most negative contribution, ties to the fixed term order NC, DAC, EP, TTC, C; its `share` CI and the bootstrap frequency with which it is the largest share are reported. The arm names the dominant term iff both seeds name the same one; otherwise it reports `SEED-DEPENDENT` and names both.

**(R) Reliability at picks (MM ruling 4; descriptive, no authority).** For each head h in {NC, DAC, EP, TTC}, at the composite's picks and at P2's picks, from the composite's rows, observed / predicted mass as ratios of per-log sums with CIs:
- the **deficit (risk) form** `sum(1 - y) / sum(1 - sigma)` (the form R53's 0.895 uses for NC; primary for NC, DAC, TTC) and the **positive form** `sum(y) / sum(sigma)` (primary for EP); both forms are reported for all four heads;
- the paired difference (composite's picks minus P2's picks) of the primary form with its CI;
- for EP, also P2's own row at P2's picks (what P2 believed).

## 5. Scale gate (decides WHICH form carries the decision; fixed now)

Is the composite's EP `sigma` on the label's scale for the thing a pick-pair gain measures, a DIFFERENCE of two proposals in the SAME set? The statistic is the **within-set calibration slope on the SAFE proposals** (`safe = NC >= 1 and DAC >= 1`, the population the c\* row's anchor is defined on; `epr1.safe_np`): over every set, subtract the set's mean of `sigma` and of `y` taken over its safe proposals, then
`slope = sum(sigma_c * y_c) / sum(sigma_c ** 2)` (a ratio of per-log sums, sets with fewer than 2 safe proposals contribute 0), CI from the same draws. A global offset cancels in a gain, so the level (mass ratio over all proposals) is NOT part of the gate.
- **Per seed, ON-SCALE iff the slope CI lies inside [0.9, 1.1]** (`lo >= 0.9 and hi <= 1.1`; a NaN bound is OFF). **[impl]:** the +-0.10 tolerance is a round number fixed now, not tuned.
- **The row is ON-SCALE iff BOTH seeds are ON-SCALE.** If the seeds disagree the row is OFF-SCALE.

## 6. Decision (fixed now)

- **Deciding form:** ON-SCALE -> the statistic is `Delta`; OFF-SCALE -> the statistic is `D_M`. No mapping is fitted in either case. The other form is reported beside it with the same rule and NO authority; if the two forms' outcomes differ the result is labelled `FORM-SENSITIVE` and disclosed, not resolved.
- **Per seed:** statistic lower CI > 0 -> `OVER`; statistic upper CI < 0 -> `UNDER`; otherwise `WITHIN` (a NaN bound is `WITHIN`).
- **EP-OVERPREDICTED** iff BOTH seeds read OVER. **EP-UNDERPREDICTED** iff BOTH seeds read UNDER. **Otherwise EP-WITHIN-NOISE** (seeds that disagree are WITHIN).
- **Final label:** `VOID` if a gating control of section 7 fails or the C-NULL fires on 2 or more of its 5 replicates; otherwise the outcome above.
- **(D) names the dominant term** of the PDMS loss (section 4); it is ATTRIBUTION and carries no pass / fail.

**Committed next arm (by outcome, fixed now):**

- **C-REACH (planted):** (a) planting `-d` in the EP LABEL at the composite's pick of every changed set moves `Delta` by exactly `+d * n_changed / N` and `Delta` over the changed sets by exactly `+d`, and leaves the predicted gain unchanged; (b) planting `+s` in the predicted `sigma` at the composite's pick of every changed set moves `Delta` by exactly `+s * n_changed / N`, and leaves the realised gain unchanged. Both are exercised on a SYNTHETIC planted bank (where `0 < n_changed < N` holds by construction) and, as an identity of the arithmetic, on the real arrays through the same function. - **C-POWER (planted):** on the real arrays, for each seed and for EACH form separately, planting `+0.10` in `sigma` at the composite's picks of the changed sets must read `OVER`, planting `-0.10` must read `UNDER`, and planting `0` must reproduce the unplanted class. - **C-SCALE (planted):** the scale gate returns ON for a planted `y = sigma` and `y = 1.05 sigma`, and OFF for `y = 0.5 sigma` and `y = 1.2 sigma`; a global offset added to `sigma` does not change the slope; a RED mutant (level instead of slope; `>=` replaced by `>`; `and` by `or`; bounds changed) must change the gate on its boundary case.

## 9. Limits stated in advance

- **P2 IN-SAMPLE biases the realised gain DOWN and so biases `Delta` and `D_M` toward OVER.** P2's picks are optimistic on these sets (P2 was trained on them) while the composite is out of fold, so `g = y[b] - y[a]` is smaller than it would be on unseen scenes. An OVER call is therefore weaker than it reads; a WITHIN or UNDER call is robust to this bias. The brief fixes the design; it is not softened here.
- **A selection effect also produces OVER.** An argmax over 64 proposals of a noisy but unbiased row has `ghat > g` at its picks (regression to the mean) with no slot prior and no bias at all. OVER therefore does not separate "slot prior" from "selection noise"; the committed next arm (scene-specific EP, pessimistic-EP rule) is what separates them.
- The EP row is about 98 % slot prior (`D-REFE-HEADROW-PROBE-1`), so the predicted gain between two picks is largely a function of the two proposal indices; this arm does not decompose it.
- SELECTION tier, SEEN scenes, label-lookup PDMS, not a closed-loop or navtest number. Tier: T0-class selection read. No capability claim.
- One P2, one S0 bank, one f = 10 % corpus, two EP-row training seeds, 117 logs: the interval answers "another draw of LOGS" only (`H-ESTIM-SEED-1`). The two seeds are a floor on the EP row's training variance, not on P2's.
- The landed PDMS formula has DDC at weight 0 and no TLC; it reproduces the tabulated PDMS (`epr1.pdms_np`), but whether the planner rule's term weights match the formula the labels are scored with is exactly the committed next arm for a non-OVER outcome, so this arm attributes the LANDED formula and does not audit it.
- The scale gate's +-0.10 tolerance, the C-POWER shift 0.10 and `D_M` as the mass-form statistic are my implementation choices (marked [impl]); the Master Mind may overrule them by amendment BEFORE the number they govern.
- The NC = 0.5 labels (8,343 proposals) are read as soft probabilities in (R) and as a multiplier of 0.5 in (D); in the scale gate they are NOT in the `safe` population (`safe` needs `NC >= 1`).
