### 8.1 What the gate does now

*(Round 3's text, kept as written; its file:line numbers are tree `C:/so/t6`'s. `safeobj_gate(dirs)` (`refe/rls1_checks.py:620`) reads `<dir>/safeobj_S.json` as before: a missing, unparsable, non-object or non-`"PASS"` file is refused exactly as before and every arm must pass (the line `if s != "PASS":` is kept verbatim: mutation CH10 of `test_rl_safeobj.py` anchors on it). | barrier | what is checked | refusal text (the substring the tests assert) |
|---|---|---|
| (a) identity | `rec["tool"] == safeobj_read.TOOL` (`"safeobj_read/1"`) | `tool is <x>, not the reader's 'safeobj_read/1'` |
| (b) stamp | `rec["record_sha256"] == safeobj_read.record_digest(rec)` | `record_sha256 does not recompute (the record was edited, or not written by the reader)` |

### 8.12 What the gate still trusts (stated, not hidden), and the decisions

A record now passes only if it equals what the reader writes for the directory as it is at the moment of the check. What that still rests on:

1. **The reader's and the gate's own source.** Someone who edits `eval/safeobj_read.py` or `rls1_checks.py` on the pod changes what "genuine" means. The ship manifest (`launch_gate.py ship-manifest`, hashed over the package tree) and `git` are the defences there, not this check.
2. **The producers' files in the arm directory** (`kill_replay.json`, `rlval_vs_A0.json`, the score dumps, `kill_checks.jsonl`, `KILLED.json`). The reader reads them as they stand; a producer file edited BEFORE the reader ran is read as it is. The reader's own cross-checks are the only defence there: the monitor's rows against the replay, the u1000 numbers recomputed from `rlval_vs_A0.json` against the replay's flags, and the score dumps' sha256 against the ones `rl_val_eval` recorded (so a control read from C0's dumps cannot pass as "own A"). A forger who edits ALL of them consistently, and then runs the reader, gets a genuine PASS: that is garbage in, and the programme rule that forbids hand-written gate inputs covers the producers' files too.
3. **Time of check.** The gate runs once, at R1o's launch, before any directory is created; nothing re-checks during R1o. R1o's own directory is not an input of the gate.
4. **A file that disappears is refused** (`is missing`), **one that appears is refused** (`stale`), **one that changes is refused** (`changed since the read`); what is NOT refused is a change to a file the reader does not read (a log, `egress.jsonl`, `monitor.log`, `sft.log`).
