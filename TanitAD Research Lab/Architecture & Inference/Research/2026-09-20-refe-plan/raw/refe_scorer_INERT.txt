  the ScenarioData produced by DriveRLNuPlanFeatureBuilder has EMPTY

  => a builder-produced ScenarioData is NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT. The engine-side derivation

THE REMAINING GAP, located exactly:
  * `driverl_runtime_map_features.py:470` DOES emit `lanes_centers_groups` (and the ids and
    next-group arrays) -- the lane-graph structure is computed.
  * `feature_builder.py:226`, where ScenarioData is constructed, NEVER PASSES THEM IN. grep for
    `lanes_centers_groups` in feature_builder.py returns nothing.
  => the lane-graph arrays exist upstream and are DROPPED on the way into ScenarioData. The
     calculators that need lane structure (off_road, center_line) therefore see an empty graph and
     fall through to no-event defaults for every candidate.
  * `occupancy_grid` is legitimately empty: the released config sets enable_occupancy_grid: false.
    It is NOT the blocker.
