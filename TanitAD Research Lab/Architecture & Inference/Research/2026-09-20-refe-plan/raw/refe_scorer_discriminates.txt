WHAT ACTUALLY FIXED IT -- AND THE NAMED FIX I HAD QUEUED WAS NOT AMONG THEM
  1. READ EVERY LEAF OF THE NESTED DICT.  Each calculator writes {"reward":..., "info":..., ...}.
     `reward` is the WEIGHTED, SATURATED training signal and is flat across candidates by design;
     `info` is the raw event/offset channel and is where the discrimination lives. My readout
     collapsed each dict to `reward` and threw `info` away. "0 of 9 signals differ" was therefore a
     statement about MY READOUT, not about their calculators. This is the THIRD time in this file
     that reading the wrong level of a nested dict produced a confident wrong conclusion.
  2. SCORE STEP BY STEP, NOT AT THE ENDPOINT.  `extract_recent_agent_polygons` reads ONLY
     timesteps -1 and -2, and OffRoad is a per-step CROSSING detector. A proposal that crosses a
     curb at step 6 and ends 8 m beyond it scores CLEAN at its endpoint. MEASURED: the identical
     over-curb path reads OffRoad.info 0.0000 endpoint-only and 1.0000 under the rollout.
  3. USE A CONTROL THAT ACTUALLY COMMITS THE VIOLATION.  The old arm pushed the teacher's path
     25 m sideways and expected off-road. MEASURED in this scene, +y moves the ego AWAY from the
     nearest curb -- corner distance 10.66 m at 0 m, 19.06 m at +14 m, minimum 0.87 m at +40 m, and
     it NEVER touches. OffRoad staying silent was CORRECT. I was one step from filing "their
     off-road detector is broken" on the strength of a control that never violated anything.
     The replacement is an ANALYTIC target: aim at the nearest curb measured from the map.
  4. PIN DOMAIN RANDOMIZATION.  The builder's `domain_randomization` defaults to None and the
     ScenarioData then DRAWS its reward weights: -1.0415 instead of the configured -1.0, giving
     -1.5194 in one process and -1.7086 in the next for identical input. Within a process it is
     stable 5/5, so one run looks perfectly deterministic. Their released yaml already carries the
     deterministic values; we were not passing them. Now exactly -1.0 across processes.

RETRACTED: "populate lanes_centers_groups / _ids / _next_groups and re-run" was my named next fix.
  It is implemented (`score_proposals.enrich_lane_graph`, and the arrays ARE constructible offline
  in one call -- 1,019 nonzero groups, 4,095 next-group links, 96 centre segments), which also
  retires the older claim that obtaining them "is genuine reconstruction work".
  ⛔ AND IT CHANGES NOTHING. The two arms below are byte-identical on all 28 signals. The lane
  graph is NOT load-bearing for these six calculators. It is kept because the fields are genuinely
  absent from a builder-produced ScenarioData, not because it fixed anything.
  ⭐ The lesson is the one this file keeps teaching: I diagnosed from structure ("an array is
  empty, that must be why") instead of from a control. The control was cheap and settled it.
