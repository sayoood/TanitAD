SCENARIODATA CAN BE REBUILT OFFLINE FROM A SIMULATION LOG -- AND IT IS EGO-CENTRIC. 2026-09-20.

WHY THIS MATTERS: REFe's scorer needs per-proposal PDM targets, the calculators need a ScenarioData,
and I assumed that meant a live simulation. It does not.

MEASURED: `DriveRLNuPlanFeatureBuilder.build(current_input, initialization)` reads only
`current_input.history` (its .ego_states and .observations). A SimulationLog sample carries
ego_state, observation, traffic_light_status and trajectory, so a plain namespace over a window of
banked samples is enough:

    hist = SimpleNamespace(ego_states=[s.ego_state for s in window],
                           observations=[s.observation for s in window])
    ci   = SimpleNamespace(history=hist, traffic_light_data=window[-1].traffic_light_status)
    init = SimpleNamespace(map_api=..., route_roadblock_ids=..., mission_goal=...)
    sd   = DriveRLNuPlanFeatureBuilder().build(ci, init)

RESULT on a real banked log:
    agent_positions_all   (1, 128, 5, 2)     128 agents, 5 history steps
    lanes_centers_points  (1, 1024, 2, 4)
    route_points_array    (1, 100, 2)

!! THE FRAME, AND IT WOULD HAVE POISONED EVERY TARGET SILENTLY:
    ego (agent 0) last position : [0.0, 0.0]
    true world pose at that step: [664433.28, 3998238.98]
   => ScenarioData is EGO-CENTRIC. Injecting a world-coordinate proposal would place the candidate
      ~660 km from the scene, and the calculators would return confident, meaningless numbers --
      not an error. Off-road would read "off road", collision "no collision", and the target bank
      would look entirely plausible.
   => build_targets.py already stores trajectories in the ego frame, so the bank and the scorer
      agree WITHOUT conversion. That is luck confirmed by measurement, not design, and the frame is
      now stated in inject_proposal's docstring rather than assumed.

CONSEQUENCE: the scorer's targets can be PRECOMPUTED offline into the bank from rollouts we already
have, rather than requiring a live simulation per proposal. That is a materially cheaper design than
the one in POD_HANDOFF gap #1.
