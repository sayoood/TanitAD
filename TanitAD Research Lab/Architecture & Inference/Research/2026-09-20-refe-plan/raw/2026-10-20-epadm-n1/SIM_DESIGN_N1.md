## 1. The statistic, exactly
Notation: a token t lies in log l(t); P2's pick slot is `pk_P(t) = rule_pick(P2 rows of t)`; search seed s (s = 0, 1, 2 for the three candidate banks of the adapter) has pick `pk_s(t) = rule_pick(candidate-s rows of t)`; for head h in (NC, DAC, TTC) the label at the candidate's pick is `y_h(t, s)` and the row's sigma there is `sigma_h(t, s)`.
* **Changed (per token AND per seed):** `ch_s(t) = [ pk_s(t) != pk_P(t) ]`, a slot-index difference under the frozen pick rule: the gate's own `pair_terms(...)["changed"]` (`epadm_n1.changed_mask`). "Tokens x search seeds where the search output differs from P2's pick" is read as: pair (t, s) is changed iff `ch_s(t) = 1`. The three seeds move different picks, so each seed has its OWN changed set.
* **N1 for (seed s, head h):** `N1(s, h) = sum_t ch_s(t) (1 - y_h(t, s)) / sum_t ch_s(t) (1 - sigma_h(t, s))`: the frozen A2 ratio (`epreliab_lib.reliability_stats`, composite picks, deficit form), summed over the changed pairs only. The interval is the registered paired log-cluster percentile bootstrap (`Stats.ratio` = `fastneg_lib.ratio_ci`, B = 10,000, seed 20260927, the geometry's logs as clusters). `epadm_n1.n1_reliability` is this statistic through the gate's own `Stats` object; the simulation's per-log shortcut `n1_ci_logvec` is tested equal to it to 0.0 (PC3).
* **The test:** per (s, h), OK iff the LOWER CI is `<= 1.05` (`a2_head_ok_r1(ci, 1.05)`, closed edge, a NaN bound is not OK). 1.05 is the registered margin (`apply_a2_margin.py`; ratified by DQ6). Margins 1.00 and 1.10 are REPORTED beside it, never read for a bar.
* **N1 is the same statistic, restricted:** on a bank where every pick is changed it equals the frozen A2 to 0.0 (tested on a synthetic adapted bank); on a partial bank it differs.

**Not simulated:** a pooled ratio over all (t, s) changed pairs of the three seeds. It would replace the ratified per-bank "any seed" quantifier (DQ6) by a different statistic.

## 2. UNDEFINED is not a pass, and the interaction with the "any seed" rule
* `n1_reliability` RAISES `N1Undefined` when seed s has **zero changed picks**, or when the predicted unsafe mass at its changed picks is **0** for some head. Nothing is returned, so nothing can be read as a pass. In the simulation the same refusal applies to the logs a replicate actually draws (`n1_ci_logvec`).
* **Verdict over the seeds** (`n1_verdict`): `FAIL-N1` iff ANY defined seed has a head with lower CI > margin (the gate's "any seed" quantifier, unchanged); otherwise `UNDEFINED-N1` iff ANY seed is undefined (or there is no seed); otherwise `ADMIT-N1`. A definite refusal outranks an undefined seed in the LABEL; both are NON-admissions. A seed is undefined if any head of it is.
* **Tallies (fixed now):** a **false refusal** is any replicate of the calibrated condition whose verdict is not `ADMIT-N1` (FAIL or UNDEFINED). **Power** is the share of replicates of the planted condition in which ANY DEFINED seed has the planted head's lower CI > 1.05 (an undefined seed never counts as a detection). Both choices are the conservative direction. The number of `UNDEFINED-N1` verdicts is reported per condition (expected 0: a changed share of about 26 % over thousands of tokens).
* **No observed unsafe mass at the changed picks (head h, seed s):** the real gate would see a ratio 0 (predicted mass > 0, observed 0), lower CI 0 <= 1.05, so the head passes. The replicate base's calibration scalar `k = sum OBS / sum PRED` would be 0 and wipe the prediction (0/0); so for such a replicate-seed-head the prediction is left UNSCALED (`k0` flag, **primary "real-sigma" convention**). **Second, "fail-closed" convention:** such a replicate-seed-head counts as a refusal. Both are computed from the same replicates; **the bars are read on the primary; a bar met on it and missed on the second is flagged R-NAN.**

## 4. Bases, generators, calibration, plants
Geometries: **GS41** (the 41 frame-complete S0 logs, 2,782 tokens) and **GS117** (all 117 logs, 8,922 tokens): `geometry_s0.json` unchanged. Unit = one TOKEN; the cluster of the bootstrap is the LOG (41 / 117 clusters). `GS43` is not simulated.
* **Base C (PRIMARY: the candidate is a real search output).** Carve B's adapted bank (`epadm_search/work/carveB_bank2`: 400 tokens, 24 logs, 3 search seeds, real P2HEAD outputs and labels) read through the gate's loaders. Replicate base: every S0 log l draws ONE source carve-B log (probability proportional to its set count), then n_l sets WITH REPLACEMENT from it, the same draw for all 3 seeds (`base_c_n1`, the S0BANK `base_c` with the new salt; its clone integrity is checked, PC7). Each token carries its seed's `changed` flag, so the per-log sums split into changed and unchanged classes (`OBSc`, `PREDc`, `OBSu`, `PREDu`, `CH`).
* **Base X (CROSS-CHECK: the real S0 label distribution, a synthetic candidate).** The R1 simulator's G25 construction (candidate = the EP-led rows on a Bernoulli(0.32) subset of the 15,808 stored sets, about 25 % changed picks, 2 seeds) **recomputed from the banked `arrays.npz`** to obtain the changed split, restricted to the geometry's logs. **PC9:** the recomputed all-pick vectors must equal R2-CONFIRM's `stats.npz` bit for bit, the changed + unchanged sums must equal the all-pick sums, and a RED arm (another subset seed) must NOT reproduce them; otherwise Base X refuses to load. Base X is precision-optimistic (sets, not tokens: about 1.33x in standard error) and has 2 seeds; its "any seed" quantifier is weaker than the 3-seed rule.
* Neither base is S0's own-fan search bank, which does not exist. Their disagreement is reported (R-SEED), not averaged. Both bases must meet a bar for it to be MET (section 7).
* **Generator A (PRIMARY, as in R1, C1 and S0BANK).** `idx = default_rng([N1_OUTER_SALT, rep + offset]).integers(0, N, N)` redraws the geometry's N logs with replacement, shared by all seeds and heads; then the REAL estimator on the drawn logs with `boot_draws(N, 10000, 20260927)`.
* **Calibration of the null AT THE CHANGED PICKS.** In every replicate base, for each seed and head, `k = sum OBSc / sum PREDc` scales `PREDc`, so the observed / predicted ratio of the CHANGED class is exactly 1 before the log redraw (PC1b). This is the class-level analogue of R1's calibrated-by-construction base (R1 scales one scalar for all picks); it is NOT the all-picks scalar, under which the changed class is off 1 (tested). A1 is calibrated exactly as in S0BANK/R1 (`R1.build_condition`).
* **Plant: x rho at the changed picks.** The planted head's `PREDc` is divided by rho (1.25 or 1.5): the observed unsafe mass at the changed picks is rho times the predicted. This is the committed "x1.25" (observed / predicted = 1.25) applied on the picks N1 reads. Because N1 reads only the changed class, and that class is calibrated on its own, the N1 population is the same whether the planted under-prediction is spread over all picks (the S0BANK plant) or localised at the changed picks.
* **Generator P (CROSS-CHECK, Base C only; the non-degenerate replacement of S0BANK's degenerate generator T).** T fixed the logs and calibrated each replicate base on the very sample it then read, so the point estimate was forced to 1. P calibrates ONCE at the POOL level (`k = sum OBSc / sum PREDc` over all 400 carve-B sets, per seed and head), then draws the tokens of a replicate (logs fixed: `idx = arange(N)`); the sample ratio varies around the calibrated population. It carries N1 only (no A1). It is REPORTED; the bars are read on A; a status that differs on P is flagged R-GEN.
* **Reference, not a bar: A2 on ALL picks under the LOCALISED plant ("A2L").** Both classes calibrated to 1, only the changed class planted at rho = 1.25 (DAC), the all-picks ratio read with the frozen A2 estimator. It shows how much of the all-picks statistic's power the dilution by unchanged picks removes, which is the premise of N1. The S0BANK sim planted x1.25 on ALL picks (the generous case for the all-picks A2); its DAC power (31.6 % / 60.4 % on Base X, 7.6 % / 13.8 % on Base C at 41 / 117 logs) is quoted from `s0amend/work/sim/sim_results.json` as INHERITED.

## 7. Status of each bar and the flags (a mechanical rule; the consequence of a status is NOT decided here)
Per geometry and per bar: **MET** = met on Base C (generator A) AND on Base X; **MISSED** = missed on Base C; **BASE-DEPENDENT** = met on C and missed on X. A **cell** (base, geometry) meets every bar iff all three bars hold in it; the geometry is **ALL-MET** iff all three bars are MET. Any other combination is reported with its miss, never tuned and never re-run with another margin, bar, definition or generator.
Flags (reporting only; they change nothing): **R-NAN** (a bar's status differs between the two conventions); **R-GEN** (generator P differs from A by more than 3 percentage points on a rate, or a bar status differs); **R-SEED** (Base C and Base X rates differ by more than 5 percentage points); **R-DUP** (mean distinct logs under generator A); **R-EVENTS / R-DOSE / R-POOLRATIO** (per base and seed: changed picks and share, observed and predicted unsafe mass at the changed and at the unchanged picks per head, the distinct changed picks with unsafe mass, the pooled changed- and unchanged-class point ratios of the banked bases; descriptive, point estimates only, nothing is decided on them); **R-DILUTION** (N1 DAC power against the A2L reference and the inherited uniform-plant all-picks power, same cell).

## 9. What these rates are NOT
* They answer "another draw of tokens and logs from a calibrated population" only, not another training run of P2 (one seed, `H-ESTIM-SEED-1`) and not another inference run beyond the 3 search seeds the gate quantifies over.
* A2-N1's power is limited by the NUMBER OF UNSAFE EVENTS among the changed picks (a ratio of sums with E events has a relative standard error near 1/sqrt(E)); the banks hold few (printed by R-EVENTS), duplicated by resampling on Base C. A candidate that is calibrated on average at its changed picks but wrong in a log-concentrated way is not simulated; the planted shortfall is spread over all changed picks (the easy case for detection).
* The calibrated null at the changed picks removes any winner's-curse effect from the null by construction; whether the real search under-predicts risk at its changed picks is a property of the real bank, UNKNOWN until it exists (R-POOLRATIO reports the carve-B and S0 point ratios, with no interval, as description only).
* P2 is in-sample on S0 and carve B is SEEN-SCENE. Tier: SELECTION, label lookup, T0-class; nothing here is a navtest or driving claim. STRATEGIC PAUSED.
