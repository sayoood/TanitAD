WINNER-TAKES-ALL AT SMALL BATCH: a training pathology the dev box cannot avoid. 2026-09-20.

CERTAIN (arithmetic, not inference):
  WTA routes gradient to exactly ONE proposal per SAMPLE, so the number of distinct proposals
  updated in a step is at most the BATCH SIZE. With M=64 proposals:
      batch   2  ->  <= 2 of 64 proposals touched per step
      batch   8  ->  <= 8 of 64
      batch 256  ->  the regime their training actually used
  MEASURED winner diversity:
      synthetic, batch 8 : 1/8, 2/8, 3/8, 4/8, 5/8 across logged steps (proposals specialising)
      real,      batch 2 : 1/2 on EVERY logged step -- both samples pick the SAME proposal
  => at batch 2 the head is not learning 64 modes, it is learning roughly one.

CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST READING (same session): I described the real-image run as having
"plateaued at ~0.15". It had not -- it continued to 0.0811 by step 150. The trace is NOISY
(0.17, 0.11, 0.19, 0.15, 0.08), which is what batch 2 produces, not a plateau. The starvation
hypothesis below therefore has LESS motivating evidence than when I wrote it, and the arithmetic
half is the only part that stands on its own.

HYPOTHESIS, explicitly NOT established:
  that this starvation is what makes the real-image run plateau at ~0.15 instead of reaching the
  ~0.039 the earlier run did.

!! THE COMPARISON IS CONFOUNDED AND MUST NOT BE READ AS "REAL IMAGES ARE HARDER".
   Three variables changed between those two runs at once:
       run                images      backbone   batch   final traj L1
       refe_overfit       synthetic   ViT-S      8       0.0388
       realimages         REAL        ViT-L      2       ~0.15 (plateau)
   That is the programme's own ten-levers-on-two-axes error. Any of the three could carry it.

THE CHEAP DISCRIMINATING EXPERIMENT (queued):
  ViT-S + REAL images + batch 8, against ViT-S + synthetic + batch 8. That holds backbone and batch
  fixed and moves ONLY the images, which is the variable the confound is actually about. ViT-S fits
  at batch 8 on this card; ViT-L does not.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE POD -- this upgrades the case from "faster" to "necessary":
  the dev box CANNOT train this head properly at any batch it can hold, because WTA specialisation
  needs batch >> 1 and ViT-L at 512x960 peaks at 5.36 GB for batch 2 on an 8 GB card.
  On a 48 GB A40 the first job should measure the largest batch that fits and report it, because
  batch size here is not a throughput knob -- it is what decides whether the 64 proposals exist.
