THE SCORER BANK WAS KEYED BY (log_name, step) AND ONE DRIVE LOG HOLDS SEVERAL SCENARIOS.
MEASURED 2026-09-20. Caught by ARITHMETIC in a report I had just built, not by reading code.

THE DEFECT
  ScorerBank keyed its per-frame candidates by (log_name, step). log_name is the DRIVE LOG.
  One drive log contains SEVERAL scenarios with different tokens, and their step indices
  overlap. So a frame's candidate set was silently POOLED with a different scenario's
  candidates at the same step index, and the scorer was supervised on trajectories belonging
  to another scene.

MEASURED SCALE
  rows                        2,052
  distinct (log, step)          154   max rows per key 27   <- 3x the 9-candidate set
  distinct (log, token, step)   228   max rows per key  9   <- correct
  COLLIDING (log, step) keys     50 of 154
  tokens per log: one log carries 3, another 2, the rest 1

WHAT MADE IT VISIBLE -- and it was not the code
  the per-candidate assignment report printed
      teacher   n 837   mean assign_d  5.73 m
      stopped   n 605   mean assign_d 15.35 m
  against a stated coverage of 605 SAMPLES. n = 837 for a candidate that appears ONCE per
  sample is impossible. The second tell was physical: the winner-takes-all distance to the
  teacher trajectory had fallen to 0.57 while the nearest proposal to the teacher CANDIDATE
  sat at 5.73 m -- the same trajectory, two irreconcilable distances.

WHY THE ORDERING STILL LOOKED RIGHT, WHICH IS THE DANGEROUS PART
  teacher 5.73 < lat+-2 5.91/6.01 < lat+-4 6.41/6.55 < lon 8.27/10.80 < stopped 15.35 <
  over-curb 17.21 -- a clean monotone ordering, exactly what the instrument was built to show.
  A contaminated bank produced a CORRECT-LOOKING qualitative result. Only the count refuted it.

THE FIX
  key by (log_name, token, step). 224 of 231 scorer keys still join the trajectory bank
  (the 7 lost are frames the trajectory bank had already dropped for a missing camera).

THE TRANSFERABLE RULE
  A COMPOSITE KEY MUST BE PROVEN UNIQUE ON THE DATA, NOT ASSUMED FROM ITS NAME. 'log' reads
  like a scenario and is not one. Print the max rows per key next to the distinct-key count
  whenever a bank is joined; those two numbers together are the whole check, and they cost
  nothing. Same family as the earlier failures in this package: the qualitative answer looked
  right while a count did not add up.
