## Why an addendum
The prereg says BASE "must reproduce the banked deployed-model navtest PDMS ... (exact, or the banked read's own tolerance)". Two facts it did not know:
1. **The banked read ran on the dev box RTX 4060; this read's trunk pass runs on the A40.** Bit-identity holds only on one GPU at matched batching (`rl_fan_dump.py` header: "dev box vs the A40's banked sets differ by max abs 0.25", bf16 trainer path; the planner path used here is fp32 and has no measured cross-GPU floor). So "exact" cannot be promised across the two cards, and "the banked read's own tolerance" does not exist for a cross-card comparison.
2. **The banked read is not a table lookup.** No navtest 64-proposal PDM table exists for the adopted config (PLAN.md section 1), so BASE is re-derived from a fresh fan and compared with the banked per-token table.

## Amendment note 2 (same day, still before any point number): the IC3 / IC4(b) / IC6 bars were a bar on HARDWARE NOISE, so they are re-stated
Evidence gathered on 2026-10-17 BEFORE any navtest point existed, on data that is not a point number:
* A CPU run of this exact forward path on two C3 tokens (`work/smoke`) differs from the C3 table's RTX 4060 logits by up to 3.1e-3 (max |dlogit|); it picks the same proposals as C3 (2/2) and its executed poses lie 0.4-0.8 mm from the banked u400 seam. IC2 read exactly 0.0 and the in-process repeat (IC5 forward arm) was bit-identical on that CPU run.
* The pick is a near-tie sensitive function. Adding i.i.d. Gaussian noise to the 1,058 C3 tokens' 64 x 7 logits and re-picking with the trainer's rule flips: **0.19 % of picks at sd 3e-4, 1.1 % at 1e-3, 3.3 % at 3e-3** (20 draws each). Every simulated flip lost <= 0.0020 in `sel_score` against the original pick (99th percentile 0.0014 at sd 3e-3).
So a flat 99.5 % agreement bar would test the difference between two GPU cards, not the instrument; a flat 0.10 on the BASE mean and a bit-equality demand on the C3 lookup have the same defect. The re-stated bars keep a gross-error detector (97 %), demand that every difference be explained as a near-tie (<= 0.01, five times the largest simulated flip), and replace the mean bar by a paired interval that uses the rows' own estimator. IC1, IC2, IC4(a), IC5 and the regression arms are unchanged: they do not depend on the card.
