## B. Choices the frozen text does not fix (estimators and geometry); none changes a threshold

| id | topic | reading used |
|---|---|---|
| AMB-2 | human path, window end | Path = the ego **rear-axle** polyline at 10 Hz over `[t0-2 s, t0+15 s]`, **straight-extended** 60 m behind and 200 m beyond its ends (so a lead beyond the last human pose is still found). The window may cross a nuPlan scene boundary (9,953 of 11,610 windows do; the DB timeline is continuous). A token whose 17 s window leaves the log is **excluded: 536 of 12,146** (`window_outside_log`). |
| AMB-3 | lead | `lead` = nearest tracked object of category **`vehicle`** (bicycles/pedestrians are not leads) whose box overlaps the corridor (half-width 1.5 m about the human path, tested on the 4 corners' lateral interval) and whose front-most corner is ahead of the ego front bumper (rear axle + 4.049 m). `gap = max(0, rear-most corner s - ego front s)`. **Oncoming and stationary vehicles count** (the text says "nearest logged vehicle"; 44 % of leads are stationary, `navtest_behaviour_mine`). |
| AMB-4 | lead speed | `v_lead` (thresholds 3 m/s, 0.5 m/s, 2 m/s, the braking drop) = **scalar speed** of the track. `v_rel` and `TTC_t` use the **along-path component** of the track velocity at the lead's projection (an oncoming car must not read as slower than the ego). |
| AMB-5 | track kinematics | Track velocity = central difference of box-centre positions over +-0.5 s (actual timestamps); `a_lead` = central difference of that speed over +-0.5 s. Not the DB `vx, vy` (they are noisier: median |error| 0.1-0.4 m/s against the positions, MEASURED on one log). Ticks without both ends have no `a_lead` (a steady-lead tick needs one). |
| AMB-6 | curvature | `kappa = d(heading)/d(arc length)` over +-0.5 s on the human ego track; **every tick** of the window must satisfy `abs(kappa) <= 0.02` (max reported). |
| AMB-11 | "human safe" | `min TTC >= 1.0 s` from the lead definition above (nearest lead only), AND **zero** positive-area overlap between the ego box (5.176 x 2.297 m, centre 1.461 m ahead of the rear axle) and ANY tracked object of ANY category (zero tolerance). The overlap test alone removes 41 tokens, the TTC test alone 1,563 (the TTC floor is the dominant one). It removes 0 of the 22 cruise tokens, 25/1,015 steady lead, 227/1,053 braking, 38/577 stop-go, 288/1,041 merge. |
| AMB-16 | "one token per log" | The first passing token of each log in hash order (not the "best" one). |
| AMB-17 | population | The 12,146 tokens of `navtest.yaml` (sha `61284edf...`) located in their DB by `lidar_pc.token`; checked against `navtest_behaviour_mine_20261107/features.parquet`: same token set, **0** log mismatches, **0** t0-timestamp mismatches, ego speed within 5e-5 m/s. |

## C. Sanity cross-check against the pre-screen table (not the frozen rule)
`navtest_behaviour_mine_20261107` uses a fixed ego-frame strip (|y| < 1.8 m, 0 < x < 60 m, oncoming excluded) and a 4 s horizon. At t0 the two tables agree on lead presence for 79.0 % of tokens (ours-only 1,984, mine-only 449 - ours follows the curved path, has no range cap and keeps oncoming vehicles). Where both see a lead, the bumper gap agrees within 1 m for 80.0 % (median |difference| 0.10 m, p90 9.2 m).
