## 4. The admission decision rule (item 4), verbatim from the frozen gate and the R1 ruling
* **A1-r1** (per candidate bank = per search seed): FAIL-A1 iff D_M's **lower CI > 0 OR its point estimate > +0.05**. ADMIT-A1 iff `lo <= 0.0 AND point <= 0.05` (a NaN bound fails closed). D_M = M_p - M_e, the EP mass ratios at P2's and at the candidate's picks; "a per-changed-pick calibration test: a candidate that changes few picks is held to a proportionally smaller absolute D_M" (`MM_RULING_EPADM_R1.md` s1).
* **A2** (per seed, for each of NC, DAC, TTC): FAIL-A2 iff the **lower CI of observed / predicted unsafe mass > 1.05** (the registered margin; the frozen module's constant is 1.00, so the margin is applied by `apply_a2_margin.py` sha256 `b9c2c80d8453fbc6299e97310f43974a677109243dc636f89840e323dab9690b`, which calls the module's own `a2_head_ok_r1(ci, bar=1.05)`); a ratio below 1 is conservative and never refused.
* **"On ANY seed"**: the candidate passes a rule only if EVERY one of the 3 search-seed banks passes it (`any_seed_admits`). `admission_label`: **ADMIT** iff a gating control did not fail, C-NULL is not void (A1 firing on >= 2 of 5 shuffled-pick replicates voids), and every candidate bank passes BOTH A1 and A2; else VOID / REFUSE-A1 / REFUSE-A2 / REFUSE-A1+A2.
* Estimator and its question: the paired log-cluster bootstrap, B = 10,000, seed 20260927, over the bank's logs (41 or 117): "would another draw of LOGS say this?" It does NOT answer another training run (one seed) or another inference run beyond the 3 search seeds.
* The gate's `NEXT_ARM`, verbatim: ADMIT -> "the candidate's shared navtest look ... A1 / A2 are necessary conditions, not a gain" (here: the C2 amendment); REFUSE-A1 -> "NO navtest look: the candidate's EP gain is not real on the selection tier"; REFUSE-A2 -> "NO navtest look: the safety rows are mis-calibrated at the candidate's picks. Register a calibration arm for THAT candidate"; VOID -> "no verdict from this read; the failing control is reported and the instrument repaired first". For the search the reading of a REFUSE is that the search EXPLOITS the scorer's errors at its outputs (a Goodhart reading, HYPOTHESIS); the levers are those named under O3 / TRADE of `PREREG_SEARCH_P2_CONFIRM.md` section 6.
* **NC is not tested when it has no events.** If the candidate's picks carry no observed NC-unsafe event the ratio is 0 (the row's sigma is below 1) and the head passes by construction; the registration states that NC calibration is NOT tested then, and its power at x1.25 is 20-25 % in any case (R2-CONFIRM T2 / T5).
