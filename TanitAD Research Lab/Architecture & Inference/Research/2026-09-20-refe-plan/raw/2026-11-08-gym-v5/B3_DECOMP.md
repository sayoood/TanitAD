# B3 decomposition after M-GYM-5 (2026-11-08)
Instrument: `b3_decomp_v5.py` (CPU, stored cycle records `raw/run_main`, `raw/ladder_20_160`, no simulation) -> `raw/b3_decomp_v5.json`. Evidence MEASURED. Adapted from `pilot_v4/b3_decomp/attrib.py`; the floor counterfactual is exact for scripted rungs as in v4 (cycles with q_free == 0.20 are rescaled).
Question: which term keeps crawl's return high on each of the 10 failing eligible states, and could a lower floor fix them.

## Two failure kinds
* **Kind A, crawl > 0.10 x best (7 states: 20, 46, 73, 108, 118, 119, 134; 118 also has f0.7 at 0.853).** crawl/best: 0.411, 0.118, 0.113, 0.194, 0.154, 0.157, 0.177. In every one the crawl's PROGRESS alone is 0.16-0.35 of the best rung's (prog/best 0.343, 0.183, 0.167, 0.187, 0.164, 0.162, 0.353): a launch from rest limits the fast rungs' progress inside the 8.5 s while a 1.5 m/s crawl still covers about 2 m of 10-11 m. The soft factors take little. q_free is the dominant loss only on 73 (0.58 units) and 134 (1.98 units), and on 118/119 it is 0.10/0.05; q_yield/q_gap dominate on 46 and 134; on **108 no term acts at all** (loss empty, q_free never applicable, cause UNVERIFIED) and on **20 the crawl is at the floor on 0 of 17 cycles** (limit 4.47, crawl 0.34 of the limit, only q_comf 0.018).
* **Kind B, a slow rung >= 0.85 x best (31, 121, 125; also 118 f0.7).** f0.7/best 1.051, 1.019, 1.079 (on 31 also f0.5 0.959). Here the fast rungs are the ones being priced: f0.9/f1.0 lose 0.9-2.3 units to q_gap and q_yield (125: q_gap 4.1 and 4.7 units) because traffic is present, so a faster scripted rung is not better. The crawl passes on these three (0.074, 0.056, 0.016).

## Does a lower floor fix them? No.
`floor_cf` in `raw/b3_decomp_v5.json`: with FLOOR 0.15, 0.10, 0.05 and even 0.0, `would_be_known` is False for all 10 states. Kind A is a progress share (a floor scales only the q_free factor, and q_free is inactive or not binding on 20, 46, 108, 118, 119); Kind B is a penalty on the fast rungs (q_gap/q_yield) that no q_free floor touches.

## Consequence
The B3 bar (every slow hard-free rung < 0.85 best AND crawl <= 0.10 best) assumes that on a free road the fast rungs earn most of the progress and are not penalised. On 8.5 s launch-from-rest windows with traffic around, that assumption fails for reasons outside `q_free`. Candidates, each its own pre-registration, none run: (1) a pace term on the progress side (credit distance relative to a nominal-speed policy); (2) re-examine the q_gap / q_yield triggers that price the 0.9x/1.0x rungs (Kind B); (3) restrict the B3 population to windows where the ego starts at speed on a free road (a sampler change, not a reward change); (4) redefine the crawl rung relative to the limit (already named in PREREG_REWARD_1 section 4). The Master Mind picks.
