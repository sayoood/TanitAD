REFe ViT-L on the dev box: what the 4060 can actually rehearse. MEASURED 2026-09-20.

CARD: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060, 8.00 GB total (torch.cuda.get_device_properties).

PEAK ALLOCATION, fwd+bwd at 512x960, one front camera:
  batch 1   3.26 GB   fits
  batch 2   5.36 GB   fits
  batch 4   9.37 GB   EXCEEDS THE CARD -- and yet it did NOT raise OutOfMemoryError.

!! WHY "batch 4 OK" IS A FALSE PASS. On Windows WDDM the driver spills to host RAM instead of
   failing, so an over-sized batch SUCCEEDS and simply crawls. A run that completes is therefore
   NOT evidence that it fits. The admissible test is the arithmetic: 9.37 GB > 8.00 GB cannot be
   on-device. Same family as every other TanitAD probe-reports-success trap.
   => dev-box rehearsal ceiling is BATCH 2. The A40 is unaffected.

CONTENTION, and a correction to my own first reading.
  Observed 7,700 MiB of 8,188 MiB in use, and nvidia-smi's compute-app list was dominated by
  DESKTOP processes (Explorer, Chrome, Edge, VS Code, Obsidian, NVIDIA Overlay, even Ribbons.scr).
  I initially read that as "the desktop is eating the card". THAT WAS WRONG.
  Stopping my OWN two jobs (PID 57332 the overfit regression arm, PID 58300 the memory probe)
  dropped usage to 1,018 MiB.
  => desktop overhead is ~1 GB; my two jobs held ~6.7 GB.
  => appearing in nvidia-smi's process list is NOT the same as holding memory. The list is long
     because every compositing app registers; the BYTES were mine.

SIDE EFFECT WORTH KEEPING: under that contention the regression arm ran at 4.07 s/step against
0.93 s/step for the real arm, a 4.4x slowdown from sharing the card with my own probe. When timing
anything on this box, check what else of MINE is resident first.
