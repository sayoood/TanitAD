## The two card-section-7 launch blockers (built after the first hand-back; CPU-tested on synthetic data, NOT run on the real model or data)
- **G19 head disjoint from ego / command** (`refe/vdac_probes.py: disjoint_probe`, called in the trainer's `--preflight` iff `--aux-drv-exclude-logs` is named, on the REAL fwd): autograd of the head output w.r.t. the ego and command tensors must be None or exactly 0.0; a control in the same call (the model's own trajectory output) must read finite and non-zero on both, so a zero from a broken probe cannot pass. Deliberate regression: `REFE_AUX_MUTATE_EGO=1` routes `model.ego_enc(ego, cmd_embed(command))` into the head's context. The chain runs it as `MODE=mutate_ego` on the real model and requires the preflight to FAIL with G19 reading "NOT disjoint". Fail-closed: `REFUSED: stage=preflight_<arm>`.
- **G20 label alignment**: on up to 256 real training frames (rank-0 rows with a target path, label stored for the frame and for its successor), pooled fraction of P6's rank-0 target points inside the label >= 0.95 (card / SPEC_P7; the store QC read 99.9989 %), >= 64 frames; two mutants scored in the same call must each read BELOW the bar: the label flipped in y, and the lookup shifted by one frame (the next `step` of the log). A mutant that also passes refuses the check. Fail-closed, same stage.
- Both gates are numbered G19 / G20 (`gate_checks.SCORER_GATES`, required iff the exclusion lever is named, so no older profile changes), and `launch_gate.consume_scorer_preflight` RE-JUDGES them from their raw fields (`vdac_probes.judge_g19 / judge_g20`), never from their `ok`. The chain re-judges them again from `preflight.jsonl` and prints `ZZVDAC_G19_G20 <arm> ...`.
- Tests: `test_vdac_gate.py` V11 (tiny model with the real 2.4 M head: clean = None grads, regression RED, zero-grad admitted, tiny non-zero refused, dead control refused; G20 synthetic corridor: pass, <64 frames, flipped store, symmetric road, same-label shift, low bar all refused; `align_frames` on a fake bank; launch-gate wiring; the chain's G19/G20 python on 7 synthetic preflight logs).
- **RISK (G20, on the pod)**: if on real frames the y-flip or the one-frame shift still reads >= 0.95 (a straight road with the ego near its centre line), the gate refuses by design ("this check cannot see that misalignment"); that is a blocker to bring back to the MM, not a bug to patch around. G-EVAL stays S2's, untouched.
- **Cost**: one more preflight per arm (the ego mutant, model build + preflight, ESTIMATED ~0.1 L40-h): S1a ~4.8 -> ~5.0, S1b ~3.7 -> ~3.8 L40-h (the ~4.8 figure above is the pre-blocker one).
