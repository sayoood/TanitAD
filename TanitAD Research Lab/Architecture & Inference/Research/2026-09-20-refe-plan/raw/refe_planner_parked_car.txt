THE PLANNER WOULD HAVE SILENTLY SCORED A PARKED CAR. FIXED AND PINNED 2026-09-20.

THE DEFECT (found by the paper-conformance review, confirmed from source):
  REFePlanner declared requires_scenario = False, so it never received the only object that
  carries a log_name. PlannerInitialization holds only [route_roadblock_ids, mission_goal,
  map_api]; mission_goal is a StateSE2 of [x, y, heading]. Neither has a log_name, and
  _log_hint was a class attribute that was NEVER ASSIGNED. The camera lookup could therefore
  never resolve and compute_planner_trajectory returned _hold(ego) on EVERY step.

WHY A HOLD IS THE WORST FAILURE MODE HERE, and why the fix is two changes not one:
  a crash is visible; a hold is a LEGAL TRAJECTORY. The benchmark scores it, the number reads
  as a weak policy rather than a broken one, and nothing in the log says the planner never saw
  an image. ⇒ resolving the name is only half the fix. REFUSING after N consecutive holds is
  what converts the silent failure into a loud one.

THE FIX
  requires_scenario  False -> True, so the runner hands over the scenario
  initialize()       reads scenario.log_name and RECORDS where the hint came from
  __init__           accepts scenario= (requires_scenario passes it to the constructor)
  _image_for         the two dead lookups are removed -- they always fell through, and
                     leaving them in kept implying the name arrives from the initialization
  hold path          raises after max_consec_holds (20) with a message that says this is NOT
                     a driving failure
