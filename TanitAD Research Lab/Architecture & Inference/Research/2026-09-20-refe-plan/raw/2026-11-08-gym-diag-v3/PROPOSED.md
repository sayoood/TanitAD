# PROPOSED fixes - derived from main states {4, 8, 10, 19} and stall states {7, 10, 11, 15}
All of these come from the USED main and stall sets. **They cannot be validated on those sets. A fresh seeded set of 20 states (new seed, navtest excluded, both samplers) is REQUIRED before any claim that a fix works.** PREREG thresholds, MAX_MAP_SLACK_M (1.0 m) and `pilot_v2/` code are unchanged by this document.

1. Oracle definition (from stall 7 only). Rank arms lexicographically (fewer hard events first, then return) or add a terminal penalty larger than the best horizon return. Re-words a PREREG regression arm, so it needs PI sign-off; the PREREG pass text ("oracle has no hard event") stays.
2. ttc predicate (from stall 10, 7, 11, 15). Make the ego advance braking-aware (variants 3 and 6 m/s^2 are in `gym_env_cf.py`) and/or require the object inside the ego lane corridor. Measured (`cf_stall{7,10,11,15}.json`, 24 arms each): braking-awareness alone does NOT fix stall 10 (24/24 ttc at cycle 6 with 3 or 6 m/s^2) and only trims stall 7 from 18 to 16 hard arms; the corridor requirement is the effective part (predicate off: stall 10 oracle clean at 1.070 but 8/24 real collisions; stall 11 and 15, 18 arm-episodes of pure false alarms, 0 collisions). So item 2 should be a lateral/corridor condition with braking-awareness secondary.
3. Map pre-screen (from main 10). At state build, place the footprint on the route centreline for the next 40 m; flag `map_unreliable` (or exclude) when the strict overshoot exceeds a tolerance. Main 10 reads 0.254 m. Do not widen the slack cap.
4. All-tie cells (from main 19). When all 24 returns tie at exactly 0.0 in cycle 0, mark the cell label-free instead of scoring it.
5. q_stall excuses (from the `q2_*` probe, 12 states, 653 excused cycles, `q2_agg.out`): precision lead 343/427 = 80.3 %, conflict 265/306 = 86.6 %, red 460/460 = 100 %; only 10 of 653 (1.5 %) excused cycles have no valid cause (all in stop-policy scenes). The excuse is fit for purpose; tightening is low priority. Candidate tightening: lead needs the strict ego-width corridor and a non-receding object; conflict needs an object ahead that enters the strict corridor; red needs an on-route connector.
6. Regression suite: add a constant-speed ladder (0.3-1.0 x limit plus 1.5 m/s crawl; measured: on pilot 5 and stall 9, 0.7x pays 8.63 / 9.00 vs 13.36 / 12.10 at 1.0x, crawl 0.27-0.37; `q3_*.json`), report q_stall fire count at crawl (0 by rule; a crawl is priced only by progress x q_free) and count q_stall activation on the stop policy, where it fires in 67 of 169 (main) and 37 of 220 (stall) precondition cycles, so the term is not reported as 0 fired. If a free-road crawl must be priced harder than progress x q_free does, that needs a new term or a higher q_free floor; not proposed, since the ladder shows no slow target competes.
