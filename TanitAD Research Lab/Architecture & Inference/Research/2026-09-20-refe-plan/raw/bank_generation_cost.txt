WHAT A REAL REFe TARGET BANK COSTS TO GENERATE. MEASURED 2026-09-20.

The rollouts, not the training, are the expensive half -- and this was not in the first handoff.

  MEASURED teacher rate, dev box, sequential worker: 114 s/scenario (15m12s for 8 scenarios).

  num_scenarios_per_type x 14 types      scenarios   x2 ranks   SEQUENTIAL hours
     10                                      140         280          8.9
     20  (the yaml's default)                280         560         17.7
     50                                      700       1,400         44.3
    100                                    1,400       2,800         88.7

!! THIS IS SIMULATION, NOT TRAINING. It is mostly CPU, so a pod's A40 does NOT make it ~3x faster
   the way it does for REFe's forward/backward. Do not assume the pod absorbs this cost.

THE LEVER THAT DOES APPLY: their runner exposes DRIVERL_EVAL_WORKER_MODE=ray_local, which
parallelises simulations across cores. With OMP_NUM_THREADS set (the documented TanitAD trap --
torch spawns ~113 threads per process and concurrent arms otherwise sit at 0-6% GPU looking hung),
a 4-8x speedup is plausible, taking the 20-per-type bank from ~17.7 h to roughly 2-4 h.
  => MEASURE that speedup before relying on it.

!! AND THE PARALLELISM CEILING IS RAM, NOT CORES -- MEASURED 2026-09-20 16:3x:
      logical processors : 24
      total RAM          : 31.8 GB
      FREE RAM           : 6.7 GB   (79 % used; top holders are ANOTHER AGENT's python at 2.42 GB,
                                     Obsidian 1.8 GB, and three more pythons)
   A nuPlan simulation worker costs roughly 1-2 GB, so free RAM admits about THREE workers, not the
   eight the core count suggests. The programme has already paid for this mistake once: a job was
   read as 'PCIe-bound' at 41 % GPU when the HOST was thrashing, and the rule banked from it is
   PRICE AGAINST FREE RAM, NOT TOTAL.
   => realistic speedup here is ~3x, so the 20-per-type bank is ~6 h rather than ~2-4 h.
   => and that budget moves when other agents' jobs start or stop. Re-check free RAM immediately
      before launching, not once.
