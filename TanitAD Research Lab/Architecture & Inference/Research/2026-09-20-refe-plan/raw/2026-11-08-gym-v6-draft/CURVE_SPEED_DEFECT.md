## Part 1. The hypothesis is CONFIRMED, and the defect is wider than suspected
**Cause (MEASURED).** `curve_speed` (`gym_env.py:433-441` in v5) takes `max(|dheading| / max(ds, 1e-3))` over consecutive centreline points. The centreline built from the nuPlan map contains **zero-length segments**: in all 158 states, **3,375 of 440,816 segments have ds exactly 0**, and in all 3,375 the two points have *identical x, y* (`raw/explore_minseg.out`; `raw/cl_fast`). They are segment joints where one lane or connector baseline ends and the next begins: the joint point is emitted twice, with a small heading mismatch between the two copies, at irregular spacing (state 108: joints at s = 43.1, 58.5, 60.9, 79.2, 104.4, 137.1 m, i.e. lane lengths, not a sampling grid; all other spacing is 0.25 m). The heading mismatch has median 0.00023 rad, 90th percentile 0.0051 rad, maximum 0.0356 rad (2.0 degrees). Dividing it by the 1e-3 floor gives k = dh / 1e-3: **18.8 % of joints exceed the 2 rad/m that makes vstar < 1 m/s** (k > 2). It is not a unit error, not a lane-connector curve, not sampling spacing.
**State 108, the points** (`curve_speed_defect.json` `part1_probe_rows`, idx 108): index 236 and 237, both at x = 588008.0374, y = 4475354.8133, s = 58.531 m, heading 2.463164 then 2.448915. dh = 0.014249 rad, ds_raw = 0.0, ds_used = 1e-3, **k = 14.25 rad/m**, `curve_speed` = sqrt(2 / 14.25) = **0.375 m/s** on a road whose heading-based curvature over a 3 m arc is 0.135 rad/m (vstar 3.85, then capped by the 11.18 m/s limit once the window leaves the joint region). State 20: indices 169 and 170, s = 41.932 m, dh = 0.00431 rad, k = 4.31, vstar 0.68 m/s (3 m-arc curvature 0.063 rad/m).
**Position signature agrees (MEASURED).** State 108 starts at s0 = 39.04 m; the 14 rad/m joint is at s = 58.53, i.e. 19.5 m ahead of launch. v5's 30 m window `[s_end, s_end + 30]` holds that joint until the ego passes it: q_free inapplicable up to ~19 m of path, applicable beyond. `REWARD_2_COUNTERFACTUALS.md` read exactly this (18-19 m, applicable from 20.7 m) from stored records.
**Prediction check (MEASURED).** Rebuilding the 158 centrelines through the gym's own calls (`build_scenario`, `make_planner`, planner `_centerline`, cl_pts/cl_s as `RewardEvaluator.__init__`), reconstructing each cycle's path position from stored `progress_legacy` (APPROX: exact unless progress was clipped at 1.5), and applying the v5 formula: `vstar >= 1.0` equals the stored `active.q_free` on **5,805 of 5,805** no-lead, no-conflict, no-hard cycles. Zero disagreements, in either direction.
**Probe set** (`raw/probe_b.json`, `raw/probe_c.json`, table `part1_probe_rows`): 108, 20, plus 14 more of the 34 (5, 16, 17, 44, 47, 49, 58, 66, 69, 77, 83, 100, 105, 115) and 10 controls (7, 8, 11, 26, 27, 37, 54, 56, 59, 62). Max pointwise k anywhere in 90 m ahead of launch (rad/m) v5 vs heading over a 3 m arc: affected states 2.29 to 28.66 (v5) against 0.04 to 0.34 (arc); controls 0.25 to 9.74 against 0.004 to 0.144. All 26 have zero-length segments (4 to 29 each); in every worst window ds_raw = 0 except control 26 (0.0197, k 0.25, harmless). Note controls 8, 27, 56 are in the 34-list's complement but turned out affected on other rungs (below), so the "34" was a floor.
**Wider than suspected (MEASURED).** Over all 158 states: **45 states** have a no-lead/no-conflict cycle with vstar < 1.0 under v5 (34 was the crawl-rung-only count); 1,380 of 5,805 free cycles. Worse, **153 of 158 states** have cycles whose 30 m window holds a short (< 0.05 m) segment; in 7,295 of 8,346 such cycles the v5 vstar is off by more than 15 % (median min-vstar of the 45 affected states 0.52 m/s). A spurious vstar of 1 to 5 m/s does not skip q_free, it makes the term applicable but trivially satisfied (`vbar < 0.9 vstar` never holds), which removes the penalty. This second effect is why states 73, 118 and 119 change verdict below although Q1b's "make q_free applicable" model touched none of them.
**Cross-check by an independent estimator (MEASURED).** Heading change over a 3 m arc on the same centrelines: at most 0.34 rad/m anywhere (radius 2.9 m, a tight junction turn), consistent with the fix below.

## Part 2. The fix (new copy, switch off by default)
`pilot_v6/scorer_gym/` = copy of `pilot_v5/scorer_gym/` (no `__pycache__`). Changes, all else byte-identical (`git diff --no-index` vs v5):
* `gym_env.py`: new module function `curve_speed_dedup(cl_pts, cl_s, s0, ahead, a_lat, min_seg_m=0.05)` drops centreline points closer than `min_seg_m` of path to the last kept point, then does v5's computation unchanged (same window, max of point-wise steps, sqrt(a_lat/k), 99.0 returns). `RewardEvaluator(curve_min_seg_m=None)` and `GymState(curve_min_seg_m=None)` carry the switch; `curve_speed` branches on it. **Default None = the v5 code path.**
* `run_v5.py`: optional `--curve-arc` (min segment in m; default None; recorded in `run_meta.json`) passed to `GymState`. (The flag name is a misnomer kept from the first draft; its value is `min_seg_m`.)
* `reward1.py`: `STATES_V5` points at the pinned list in `pilot_v5` (not copied). No logic change.
**Why this and why 0.05 (MEASURED, `raw/explore_minseg.out`).** The cause is a divide by a floored zero, so the smallest change removes the zero. The heading step across a joint is kept (it is the real kink) and divided by the real distance to the neighbouring point. `min_seg_m` is insensitive over 0.01 to 0.1: max k after the fix 0.342 rad/m for 0.01 and 0.05 (0.342 also for 0.1), none above 0.5 in 9,483 one-metre-spaced windows (v5: 22.7 % above 2, max 35.6). Segments between 1e-3 and 0.05 m number 911; 0.05 was picked from the spacing histogram (0.25 m grid, shorter pieces at lane ends), not from verdicts. Free cycles with vstar < 1.0 under the fix: **0 of 5,805** at 0.01, 0.05, 0.1 and 0.2.
