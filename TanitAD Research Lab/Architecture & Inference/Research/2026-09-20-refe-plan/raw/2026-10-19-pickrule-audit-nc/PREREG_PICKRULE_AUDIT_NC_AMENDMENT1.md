## A1.1 Correction of one sentence in the parent's section 9 (forecast; not a bar)

The parent says VETO-RECOVERS "can only hold if COMPOSITION does, because a veto at tau <= 0.5 cannot remove a pick whose NC sigmoid is >= 0.5". The reason is true for tau <= 0.5 only. The grid also holds tau = 0.7 and 0.9, and a veto there CAN remove a pick whose NC sigmoid lies in [0.5, tau). So VETO-RECOVERS can hold under ROW-MISS or MIXED for tau in {0.7, 0.9}. The forecast's numbers (0.55 / 0.30 / 0.15 for ROW-MISS / MIXED / COMPOSITION) stand; the VETO-RECOVERS clause is withdrawn. This changes no rule: the next arm is chosen by section 7's table from Q1's class, and VETO-RECOVERS only splits the COMPOSITION row.

## A1.2 Implementation notes (they pin down what the parent leaves implicit; no design change)

1. **Dtype of the NC sigmoid in Q1, Q2 and the veto:** `1 / (1 + exp(-z))` in float64 of the stored float32 logit, compared with `< 0.5` (Q1, Q2) and `>= tau` (eligibility). A logit of exactly 0 has sigmoid exactly 0.5: it is NOT "< 0.5" (not flagged) and it IS eligible at tau = 0.5.
2. **C-NULL permutation:** one permutation of the 64 proposals per set, `np.argsort(default_rng(20261019 + 7919 * rep).random((N, 64)), axis=1)`, the same for every (source, seed); it is applied to the NC label column (permuting the other five columns jointly cannot change Q1, which reads only the NC label). Rows, picks and sigmoids are untouched.
3. **What a FIRING null means, stated now so it is not invented later:** the null's Q1 is the share of EP-led picks with NC sigmoid < 0.5 among the sets that a RANDOM label assignment makes an "A" set. It fires only if EP-led picks have a low NC sigmoid in general, independent of the labels. Then the real Q1 > 0.5 would reflect the rule letting low-NC proposals through everywhere, not a row that flagged the actually unsafe ones; the registered consequence is VOID (parent section 8), and the result then reports the real Q1 as UNREADABLE as a flag statistic.
4. **C-ID2 tolerance:** absolute 1e-9 on `new_pdms_x100`, `p2_pdms_x100`, `unsafe_rate_new`, `unsafe_rate_p2`; EXACT equality on `oof_picks_changed`. "Unsafe" in C-ID2 alone is `epr1`'s (not (NC >= 1 and DAC >= 1)), because that is what the banked numbers mean; Q1 to Q3 use the parent's section 5 definitions.
5. **Fallback accounting:** per tau, `n_fallback` = sets with no eligible proposal (pick = unvetoed pick); `n_changed_vs_unvetoed` counts sets whose vetoed pick differs from the unvetoed pick of the SAME rows; `n_changed_vs_p2` counts sets whose pick differs from P2's unvetoed pick.
6. **Inputs not in the repo tree:** the banked final rows and `epr1_result.json` are read from byte-exact exports of the tip (`git show`, `ref/epr1/`), and their sha256 are recorded in the prepare report and checked against `final_rows[*].sha256` of the EP-R1 result.
7. **Power of the controls is shown on synthetic planted data** in `test_pickrule.py`: a planted COMPOSITION case (unsafe-labelled EP-led picks carry NC sigmoid 0.45, safe-labelled 0.95), a planted ROW-MISS case (0.85), a planted MIXED case, and a planted STRUCTURAL case (every EP-led pick 0.45 whatever its label) in which the null fires. The test-suite also holds a RED mutation of every decision function of section 7 and 8.
