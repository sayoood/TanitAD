## 5. Limits, stated

* Synthetic backend is a CONTROL (forbidden in the launch profile, `--label-backend`). The trainer-level proofs run on it because the NAVSIM stack is not importable in the trainer venv; the PDM identity is proved separately with the real stack.
* The `--fast` suite uses ViT-S, 1 camera, `--weights none`, 6 tuples: it proves wiring and gradients, not learning. No claim about driving follows from any of it.
* `REFE_LAB_MUTATE` (`stale`, `no_labels`) are deliberate-regression switches inside the labeller, in the style of `REFE_GROW_MUTATE_FRESH_SNAPSHOT`. They are test hooks; nothing sets them in a launch.
* The labeller's failure policy is a choice, not the paper's: a missing parts file or a worker error masks that sample (trajectory loss only) and is counted; > 2 % failures after 200 sets raises; a micro-batch not back in `--label-timeout-s` (900) raises.
* `cov_norm` for the online score loss is the constant 64 (every proposal of every sample), so a guard-skipped sample contributes less gradient rather than being up-weighted. Differs from the on-policy path's measured coverage; the value is a choice.
