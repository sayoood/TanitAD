## 4. Per arm (order `C0 R0 C1 R1 R2`; arm names, seeds and argv are the gate's: `launch_gate.py` `RLS1_ARM_SEEDS`)

0. **The update budget (TL-1):** `rls1_checks.py updates-check --args "$ARGS" --n-sets 16000` must print `ZZRLS1_UPDATES_OK`: the trainer ends at `min(ceil(n / 8 * epochs / 8), --max-updates)` and never complains, so `--epochs 2` on 16,000 scenes would silently stop at 500 updates (and the reads below name `update1000`). The argv carries **`--epochs 4`** (16,000 / 64 x 4 = 1,000 updates exactly; each scene is seen 4 times instead of 1.33). `--epochs` is not pinned by the gate (`HANDOFF_RL_S1.md`), every arm shares it, and the PI's attention is asked for it (hand-back).
1. `scorer_finetune.py $ARGS --preflight` (G1-G11 incl. the EPO gate G11); `ZZSFT_PREFLIGHT_PASS`.
2. `launch_gate.py run --profile refe_rls1 --arm-name <ARM> --gate-preflight-json <arm>/preflight.jsonl` (profile `refe_rls1b` for R0b / R1b, and for C1 when `RLS1_ARMS` names an RL-S1b arm) mints `lg/<ARM>/PASS_*.json`; `token_ok` = `launch_gate.py verify` re-measures argv, tree and data (never a grep). It runs after the minting, before the stage is marked, and again immediately before the trainer starts. **No token, no trainer.**
3. Train (first `gpu_idle_wait`: three idle GPU polls while this chain holds `chain.lock`; own process group, its pgid verified equal to its pid; `--eval-every 250`, `--dump-scores <arm>/scores`): `hf_egress.py watch` beside it (pushes every settled `scorer_states`, halts the run after two failed pushes plus the next checkpoint) to the per-arm repo `Sayood/tanitad-refe-rls1-<arm lowercase>`, and the kill monitor `rls1_checks.py monitor`, SUPERVISED by the chain (`supervise_arm`, section 6b): it must print `ZZRLS1_MONITOR_READY` and stay alive while the trainer runs, otherwise the chain stops the trainer by its process-group id and the arm is `K-MON`. ## 6b. The queue, the fail-closed monitor and RL-S1b (2026-10-14)

Full evidence, numbers and the P6 finding: `raw/2026-10-14-rls1-monitor-fix/REPORT.md`. Everything here is MEASURED on the dev box (`test_rls1_queue.py`, `test_rls1_chain.py`); nothing has run on a pod.
