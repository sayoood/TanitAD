# IMPL_ADDENDUM_LADDER_READ (disclosure only; `IMPL_CHOICES_LADDER_READ.md` sha256 `bdbf488a...` is unchanged)
Added after the IMPL hash and before any deciding arm read: control 2b (`lr_lib.check_cells_against_states`): in every arm the Copy-Last row of every (level, k, context) cell is compared bit for bit with the encoded state at the anchor frame, and the Oracle with the encoded state k steps later, both read independently from the flat per-level states. A Copy-Last reference shifted by one step, or an Oracle one step early, makes it raise (`test_cells_against_states_check_goes_red_on_a_shifted_copy`). The M2b control read MRR differences of exactly 0.0 against IMAG1B (tolerance 1e-3). The two smoke reads (C0 s0, R35 s0, 19 reference draws) were run before this addendum and decide nothing.
