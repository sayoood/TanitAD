## Reading choices made here (the card is silent; stated in `obd_read.py`'s docstring and in `RESULT_OBD.json`)
(o1) point values and floors are unrounded mean differences (the OTBAND read compared the estimator's rounded `delta`); (o2) a cell with no floor is never a gain or a cost, and a question with no floor at all is NOT-YET; (o3) the detach reference follows the parity state (holds: DAG-OACC; fails: OBD-NULL of the same seed; unknown: not read); (o4) DK-VIOL, the code-drift row, CTL/BAND against DAG-OACC and the DAG-ORC pair floor (labelled seed plus trainer version) are beside rows; (o5) the controls that make the read INVALID: window identity per load, DAgger bank prefix (197a68bb / 36c7aea9), bank manifest vs done-file checkpoint sha256, built detach and band weights read back from `selfcheck.json`, identity control. A missing floor arm is NOT-YET, not INVALID. The chain's own G-SUITE / token / purity gate is NOT re-run here (printed as such).
