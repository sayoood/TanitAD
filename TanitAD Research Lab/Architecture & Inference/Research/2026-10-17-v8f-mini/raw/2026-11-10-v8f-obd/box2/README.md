## Failure behaviour
* Ship, pilot, the 12 h rule, a wait that times out, a post-check failure, a trainer crash, a pull mismatch, low disk, a transport or package problem, an unreadable parity input: **the chain stops** (`STOP.txt`, a named exit code), no later arm starts.
* A **gate / pin / token refusal of one full run** (stage rc 11) writes `ARM_REFUSED_<tag>.txt` and the chain **goes on with the next run** (independent arms, no GPU used); it then ends with `STOP.txt` (rc 4), never `DONE.txt`. A refusal inside the pilot (which gates BAND_s0, CTL_s0 and NULL_s0) stops the chain.
* Exit codes: 3 12 h rule; 4 gate/pin refused; 5 job or transfer failed; 6 disk low (box 2 D: below 5 GB; a constant, nothing measured); 7 pulled copy differs; 8 package, commit or transport; 9 ship refused; 10 pilot unreadable; 11 box-2 timeout or unreadable probe; 12 box busy, GPU not idle, or EGOWM not ended; 13 blocked tag; 14 NULL parity input unreadable; 16 transport not fail-closed.
* Sentinels, each in its own file (never in `chain.log`): `STOP.txt` (`ZZ OBD-STOP`), `DONE.txt` (`ZZ OBD-DONE`, lists the run order incl. a conditional arm), `ARM_REFUSED_<tag>.txt` (`ZZ OBD-ARM-REFUSED`).
