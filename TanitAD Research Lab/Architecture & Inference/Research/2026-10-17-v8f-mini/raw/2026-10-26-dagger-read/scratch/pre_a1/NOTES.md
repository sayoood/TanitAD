## Choices where the PREREG is silent (all in code, all tested)
1. **Precedence** REFUSED > BLOCKED > HARMS > the head count (s.7 closing bullets). A failed bank control stops the read before COVERAGE (so a REFUSED bank never yields a BLOCKED). BLOCKED needs no arm file at all and does not read any that exist.
2. **RF is read through the same estimator as the row** (`f1_pair_row`, matched cells, delta rounded to 4 dp) so bar and delta are on one footing; the unrounded gap rides beside as `RF_unrounded`.
3. **K-B3 "bank clips subset of TRAIN" is implemented as EQUALITY**: the bank's `clip_ids` must hash (the trainer's own formula, `v8f_mini_train.py:1074`, pinned by a test against the tip source) to the trainer's recorded `train_sha256` AND to the bank header's `window_list_sha256`. s.1 says the bank covers "every window of M2b's own TRAIN list", so equality is the design; a partial bank would need an amendment.
4. **K-B0, K-B1, K-B4, K-B5 are RELAYED, not re-run** (they need the rollout machinery): each must record PASS plus its evidence (n_checked > 0 and n_mismatch 0; n_replayed >= floor(0.5 % of windows) = 1532 and n_mismatch 0; all six matcher cases True; both K-B5 errors <= 1e-4 m). Reported `verified_by_reader: false`. Re-derived by the reader: K-B2 (generator sha vs the CLI M2b sha), K-B3 (ids and stable sids vs eval-141, plus the reader's own deliberate regression: a bank with one eval clip appended must be refused, else the check is "blind"), the accounting (2 x windows own states; kept + cuts = before; eligible <= kept), the rollout configuration, the TRAIN size 4,512 / 306,566, `hmax` 16, and the matching-constants sha256 **prefix `438366d5`** (only the prefix is in the PREREG).
5. `--dagger-seed`: the PREREG does not give its value; required named once as an integer, value unconstrained.
6. DAG-0 / DAG data manifests are not compared (see "removed" above); they are reported.
7. Token HMAC is NOT verified (key on the launch host); commit equality across runs not required (as ORC).
