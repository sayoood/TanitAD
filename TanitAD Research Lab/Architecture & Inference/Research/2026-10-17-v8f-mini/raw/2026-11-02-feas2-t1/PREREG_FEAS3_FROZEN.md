## Two pre-registered disclosures, reported beside the outcome (they change no outcome word)
The margin of lonfloor (2 m/s) equals the 2 m/s of the tick-level crawl flag of `t1hd_read.py:422` (`v0 < v_gt - 2.0`, used by the S-POS read). The M1 row of the 19 is a different object (`rr_lib.m1_window`: per window, among GT-fast pairs, GT pair speed >= 5.2 m/s, the share whose own pair speed is < 5.2 m/s). Both are therefore reported:
* **(i) M1 at margins.** Per window, among the GT-fast pairs, the share whose OWN pair speed is < GT pair speed - m, for m = 1, 2, 3 m/s, beside the row's own 5.2 m/s definition; for X, Y and P10 (P10 = the fresh Y run's own P10 floor run); X - P10, X - Y, Y - P10 with the paired bootstrap. **THRESHOLD-LOCAL** (committed reading): an arm whose M1 X - Y is separated-better on the 5.2 row or the 2 m/s row but not separated-better on the 1 m/s or the 3 m/s row is written as threshold-local in the headline.
* **(ii) Hard-braking check.** The windows in the top 5 % of GT peak deceleration (peak of -(d vG / dt) over the GT pair speeds; fixed by GT alone, one window set for every arm: 120 windows from 19 episodes, threshold 2.372 m/s^2 on the 2,399 windows): along-track MAE and S-POS@30/50/80, X - Y on those windows. **BRAKING-BLOCKED** (committed reading): an arm that reads MOVED or DOMINANT but is separated-worse on any of these four rows on that set is written as braking-blocked in the headline. 19 episodes give wide intervals: a null here is not evidence of safety, and is written so.
