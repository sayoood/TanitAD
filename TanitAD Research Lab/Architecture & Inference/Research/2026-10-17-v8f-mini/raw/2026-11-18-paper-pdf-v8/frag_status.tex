\paragraph{Established (MEASURED, each with its tier).} At \tier{T1-kin}: the vision-fed distance rule is limited first by the gap read; a margin on that read calibrated on training clips brings it to the label-fed rule on unsafe steps in both head arm types and both seeds, and so does a margin that grows with the read; the head's closing-speed output is not needed; the plan can be made to keep the distance by a term on its own plan, but only by slowing the model everywhere.
