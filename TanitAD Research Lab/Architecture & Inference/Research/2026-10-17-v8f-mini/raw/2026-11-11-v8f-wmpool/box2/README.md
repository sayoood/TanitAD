## Failure behaviour
A gate, pin or token refusal of one full run (stage rc 11) writes `ARM_REFUSED_<tag>.txt` and the chain goes on with the next independent run; the chain then ends with STOP, never DONE. Because the pilot gates all three argvs first, a refusal of a built lever normally stops the chain at the pilot (rc 4) before any run. Every other failure (ship, cells, pilot, 72 h rule, training, post check, disk, pull, transport, timeout, box busy) stops the chain. Exit codes: 3 72 h rule, 4 gate / pin / token / cells check refused, 5 job or transfer failed, 6 disk low, 7 pull sha mismatch, 8 package, commit, transport or test hook, 9 ship refused, 10 pilot unreadable, 11 box-2 timeout or unreadable probe, 12 box busy / GPU not idle / predecessor not ended, 13 blocked tag.
