## 1. Question, and why this arm is not ORC or DAG-ORC

ORC, DAG2 and DAG-ORC read HARMS with slowed histories and UNTOUCHED targets. Eval-141 data (the SREP / Y runs) NEVER shapes training: the SREP Y runs are not read by the trainer, the bank holds no eval clip (checked at load by K-B3 and by `assert_disjoint`).
INHERITED (not re-verified here): that bank2's generating checkpoint is the DAG checkpoint (DAG2's launch passed K-B2). **The table (derived deterministically by the trainer at startup, `RecoverDevTable`):** for every bank record with `reason == ok`, finite, at own row j: the own state minus the LOGGED state of the same clip at the SAME clock row j, 5 numbers:
`dv = v_own - v_log` (m/s, kin3[0]), `da = a_own - a_log` (m/s^2, kin3[1]), `dom = om_own - om_log` (rad/s, om = wrap(psi_j - psi_{j-1}) / 0.2 from the pose rows, own from the record, logged from the clip store, so a definition offset cancels), `dy` (m, signed lateral offset of the own position in the logged frame at j, left positive), `dpsi = wrap(psi_own - psi_log)` (rad). Records whose logged row j or j-1 is not a real kinematic row are dropped and COUNTED. The bank's own cuts (lateral > 3.0 m, heading > 30 deg off the route, end edge) bound the law and are disclosed, not undone. The along-track offset is NOT a component (the tuple is the five numbers the PI named).
**Draw:** per selected sample ONE record, uniform over the table, independent of the sample's clip and time (a MARGINAL draw, not speed-conditioned; coverage of the 3-8 m/s band where the excess lives is UNVERIFIED and is REPORTED by the trainer as the table's share of records with logged v in [3, 8]).
**A-priori law** (`--recover-dev-law apriori`, NOT used by any of the 6 registered runs; it is the committed NOT-MOVED next arm, section 8): dv ~ U[-3.0, 0.0] m/s (v >= 0 after the clamp); da ~ U[-1.0, +0.5] m/s^2; dy ~ N(0, 0.5 m); dpsi ~ N(0, 3 deg); yaw-rate offset ~ N(0, 2 deg/s). Fixed now, disclosed now.

## 4. The perturbation and the target
**Eligibility:** NOW is a real kinematic row (`t >= 1`, `frame_ok[-1]`, finite `kin3`). No speed cap (the law is empirical; a cap would be a free parameter).
**Applied to a selected sample** (probability `--recover-p`, ONE record, v >= 0 clamp after the offset): the kinematic channels (v, a, omega) on EVERY real history frame are offset by (dv, da, dom) (the ORC convention: a constant offset over the window), v' = max(v + dv, 0). The tensors rebuilt through the programme's one spelling (kappa by `_pose_kappa0`, features by `kin_features`, base by `kdx_base`) are exactly ORC's `EGO_PERTURB_REBUILT` = `p10f, base, kin3, p10_t, pose, pose_t, pose_s, nav` PLUS `pose_now` (the anchor moves by the lateral and heading offset). Pose history: each row moves by the speed-induced displacement (-dv x age x 0.2 s along the deviated heading) and, with a linear ramp that is 1 at NOW and 0 at age 6 steps (= `max_age_s`), by the lateral / heading offset. `nav[:, 3]` (the route progress) is rebuilt by the same distance change on the frames sharing NOW's token and validity, as ORC does. `lab_s.rc` is re-expressed by the frame change `v8f_dagger.py:182 rc_frame_change` (the one label the DAgger swap touches, here for the moved anchor); `act` (the world-model transition record), `feats`, `frame_ok`, `limit*`, `a_t`, `a_s` are kept. The images stay as logged.
**Target** (`--recover-target rejoin`, the registered setting): the logged future `gt` [30, 2] (NOW frame, knots t+1..t+30) re-expressed in the perturbed frame, `g'_i = Rot(-dpsi) (g_i - (0, dy))`; for the first K = `--recover-rejoin-steps` = 10 knots (i = 0..9) blended by smoothstep `w = 3x^2 - 2x^3`, `x = (i+1)/K`, from the STRAIGHT continuation of the deviated heading `(L_i, 0)` (L_i = the logged cumulative arc length to knot i) onto `g'_i`: `target_i = (1 - w) (L_i, 0) + w g'_i`; knots i >= 10 are `g'_i` (the logged speed profile applies). `--recover-target logged` (pure frame change, no blend) is the attribution switch. `s_h`, `lead_gap_h`, `gt_ok`, every `lab*` entry except `lab_s.rc` are the human's and are the same objects.
**Exclusivity / defaults:** `--recover-p` with `--ego-perturb-p > 0` or `--dagger-p > 0` is refused. The lever's off state is p = 0: the SAME batch back, no draw, no copy (bit-identical to a run without the class). Requires `--kin-mode yaw`.

## 7. Frame-freeze control (T1-kin validity; frozen with this card, zero new training)
The risk (v2 section 8.1): in T1-kin the camera features are LOGGED and advance at the human's pace, so a model trained to recover the logged speed from a deviated history can read the human's speed from frame motion, a signal real driving does not give. **Tool:** the `--scene hold_now` switch of the T1 tool, built by another agent in `dagland/framefreeze/` (referenced, not built here): every row of `it["feats"]` replaced by the current frame's row, repeated, so no frame motion is available; `--scene logged` / flag absent is bit-identical to the existing tool.
Arms per seed s = 0, 1 (8 rollouts, about 22 min at 16 threads): `FZ-R_s`, `FZ-C0_s` (the section 2 checkpoints), `FZ-Y_s` (DAG-OACC_s itself, the freeze's own effect on the init), `FZ-HLVL_s` (DAG-OACC_s with `--histswap cv_logged --scene hold_now`, the known-answer positive control).
Controls (any failure REFUSES the read): KFZ1 every batch's `feats` rows equal the last row (max abs deviation exactly 0.0); KFZ2 the apply line deleted turns a test RED and the flag absent is bit-identical; KFZ3 FZ-Y's plan differs from Y's by > 1e-3 m at ticks >= 1; KFZ4 FZ-HLVL keeps its closure (M1 X - Y separated-better at both seeds, share closed >= 0.5).
**Read.** G_L = M1 share of R - C0 under logged frames (section 6); G_F = the same under freeze (FZ-R - FZ-C0), each against its own frame mode's (C0 - P10) excess. **ROBUST:** G_F separated-better at both seeds AND mean share closed >= 0.5. **ARTEFACT:** the logged-frame read passes P and under freeze G_F is separated-better at fewer than 2 seeds OR mean share closed < 0.25. **PARTIAL:** anything else.
**Reading rule (committed): a recovery arm whose M1 gain disappears under frame-freeze is a T1-kin artefact, not a driving capability.** Disclosed limits (v2 8.5): the current frame still depicts the human's position and may carry blur; four identical frames are off the training distribution (the freeze's own effect is measured by FZ-Y / FZ-C0); T2 re-perception is not provisioned, so ROBUST is necessary, not sufficient.
