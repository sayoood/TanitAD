# A6 note: how known answer 3 ("delta 0.5 is partial") is read, and why

* **The card's words (section 2).** "Its bind rate lies strictly between those of delta = 0 and delta = 10^9, and the bound s' - s <= delta * t + 1e-6 m holds on every knot of every bound tick." The card is frozen (sha256 fbf5de02...) and was not edited.
* **What the first KA run measured (`raw/_old_KA_LF6_first_run_rollout_level_K3.json`).** Read at the level of the ROLLOUT (the share of ticks with `lf_bind`, 64 windows, 192 ticks), delta 0.5 binds MORE often than delta 10^9:
  heads bare 0.4844 vs 0.4271, heads composite 0.4844 vs 0.4271, logged bare 0.4271 vs 0.4219; delta 0 binds 0.0000. The tool is not the cause: K1 and K2 (delta 0 = floor off, delta 10^9 = the LF5 tool) were bit for bit, the bound held on every knot
  (max s' - s - delta t = 2e-16 m), and an independent fine-grid recomputation of s' from the dumped plan_s agreed to 3e-8 m.
* **Why.** A rollout drives its own states. A smaller lift leaves the car slower, so F meets more crawl ticks, where it binds; the bind rate of a rollout is therefore not ordered by delta. At FIXED inputs the order is a property of the operator
  (the bound set grows with delta), and the unit tests pin it (`test_bind_count_is_monotone_in_delta_and_partial_in_between`).
* **The reading used (a deviation from the card's literal wording, for the Master Mind to rule on).** `code/ka_lf6.py` computes the three bind rates on the SAME ticks (the inputs F read in the delta 0.5 smoke's own rollout: the plan after S rebuilt from the dumped
  `plan_raw` and `P` by the tool's own `smooth_plan` [bit for bit equal to the dumped `plan_s` on the composite smoke], `v0`, the dumped `lf_vhat`, `lf_pi`, `lf_gap`): delta 0 and delta 0.5 by the tool under test, delta 10^9 by `level_floor.apply_floor_batch` (A5's F,
  independent code). "Strictly between" is required of that. The rollout-level rates are recorded beside, not gating. The mutation (delta term deleted) must break the same-input leg; it does (its rate equals A5's).
* **Margin, disclosed.** On the 64-window smoke the same-input rates are close: delta 0.5 leaves 1 to 3 of 192 ticks unbound that delta 10^9 binds. Strictly between holds, narrowly; on the full rollouts the instrument `n_bound_delta1e9_not_bound_by_delta0.5` is not recomputed (it needs A5's unbounded rollout on the same states).
* **Wording, after the run (Master Mind, 2026-11-06 08:17).** Two key names in `raw/KA_LF6.json` and `code/ka_lf6.py` were renamed to `rollout_level_bind_rates` and `rollout_level_bind_rate_under_mutation`, and the first-run file to `raw/_old_KA_LF6_first_run_rollout_level_K3.json`, under the programme's wording rule. No value changed. The ruling on this reading is `a6_prereg/A6_RULING_1.md`.
