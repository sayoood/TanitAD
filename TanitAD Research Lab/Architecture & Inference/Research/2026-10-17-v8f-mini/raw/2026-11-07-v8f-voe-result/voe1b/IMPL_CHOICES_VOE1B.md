## 1. ACTION-SWAP-M (the card's donor rule, made exact)
* Per window, over the swapped transitions 4..11 (the same eight as ACTION-SWAP): `mabs_lon`, `mabs_yaw` = the mean of |a| and of |yaw rate| (raw cache channels, as VOE1's ACTION-SWAP); `lon`, `yaw` = the SIGNED means.
* Candidate donors of window i = windows j of ANOTHER episode with BOTH `|mabs_lon[j] - mabs_lon[i]| <= 0.10 * mabs_lon[i]` and `|mabs_yaw[j] - mabs_yaw[i]| <= 0.10 * mabs_yaw[i]` (tolerance relative to the window's own value), AND an opposite sign on at least one channel: lon channel opposite iff `|lon[i]| >= 0.5` and `|lon[j]| >= 0.5` and `sign(lon[j]) = -sign(lon[i])`; yaw channel opposite iff `|yaw[i]| >= 0.05` and `|yaw[j]| >= 0.05` and `sign(yaw[j]) = -sign(yaw[i])`.
* **Reading disclosed:** the card says the sign is tested "where that mean is at least 0.5 m/s^2 or 0.05 rad/s in size". The committed reading requires BOTH means (window and donor) to reach the threshold: the stricter one, so every donor qualifying under it also qualifies under the window-only reading. n under the looser reading is given below as a disclosure only.
* The donor is one uniform draw among the candidates, windows in ascending order, one `numpy.random.default_rng(0)` stream (a separate generator instance from ACTION-SWAP's; both seeded 0). Frames are the real frames; transitions 4..11 take the donor window's actions 4..11 (index map identical to ACTION-SWAP). Windows with no candidate are dropped from this row.
* **Qualifying n (labels and ego only, `counts_voe1b.json`, computed before any surprise): 530 windows in 104 episodes** of the 1,976 / 141. Candidates per qualifying window: min 1, median 2, max 7. Donor / window ratio of mean |lon| 5th / 50th / 95th percentile 0.911 / 1.001 / 1.089, of mean |yaw| 0.909 / 0.997 / 1.087. Sign opposite on the lon channel for 385 and on the yaw channel for 182 of the 530. The 300-window UNDERPOWERED bar is cleared (530 >= 300). Looser readings (disclosure only, not run): window-only sign test 545 windows; magnitude match alone 1,714 windows.
* ACTION-SWAP (VOE1's, donors differing by at least 1.5 m/s^2 or 0.15 rad/s) is kept and reported beside; it feeds no trigger.

## 2. The untrained reference
* **16 seeds per trained chassis, 32 models.** For each trained chassis c in {0, 1} (the `adapter.*` and `pos` of M2b s_c) and each `torch.manual_seed` in 100..115: a fresh `WMAux(cfg)` with PyTorch default init of every layer; **every `wm.*` parameter is re-initialised (133 tensors, 2,007,874 parameters, none equal to the trained one; measured per model in `bank_voe1b.report.json`)**. Tags `UT{c}_{seed}`. The reference set of M2b s_c is the 16 accuracies of `UT{c}_100..115`.
* **Reading disclosed:** the card says "16 untrained world models ... Each keeps the trained M2b chassis". Because the two M2b seeds have different chassis, each model seed is compared with the 16 untrained models on ITS OWN chassis (like-for-like tokens). The `wm.*` weights of `UT0_k` and `UT1_k` are bit-identical (same seed, same config; the hashes are equal); only the tokens differ. The 16 accuracies "for that manipulation and level" are therefore 16 per model seed.
* Untrained members get the accuracy cell (two-part rule) only for the manipulations, plus the real-vs-real cell and the event AP (no interval): rules (a) and (c) need nothing else. The null-accuracy and paired-difference cells are computed for the two trained tags only.

## 3. Labels (the card's rule, per manipulation x level x model seed)
* (a) the accuracy is two-part separated above 0.5. (b) [TELEPORT, REVERSE, SPLICE only] the paired model-minus-null difference is two-part separated above 0. (c) the accuracy is **strictly above the maximum** of the 16 untrained accuracies of that chassis, manipulation and level (point values).
* BLIND = (a) fails. NULL-ONLY = (a) holds and (b) fails. INIT-LEVEL = (a) and (b) hold [for FREEZE, ACTION-SWAP, ACTION-SWAP-M (b) does not apply: (a) holds] and (c) fails. NOTICES = (a), (b) [where it applies] and (c) hold. The four are exclusive and exhaustive.
* **Label of a (manipulation, level) over both model seeds:** NOTICES iff both seeds NOTICES (the card). Equal labels stay. Otherwise VOE1's reading extended: both seeds above 0.5 (NOTICES / INIT-LEVEL / NULL-ONLY) and at least one NULL-ONLY -> NULL-ONLY; one BLIND and one INIT-LEVEL -> `BLIND/INIT-LEVEL` (neither seed notices; the "BLIND or INIT-LEVEL" triggers count it); anything else DISCORDANT.
* **Triggers**, mechanically on the over-both-seeds labels, exactly as the card words them: ACTION-SWAP-M BLIND or INIT-LEVEL at every level (labels in {BLIND, INIT-LEVEL, BLIND/INIT-LEVEL} at levels 1, 2 and 3); FREEZE the same; TELEPORT and SPLICE both NULL-ONLY at every level; NOTICES on FREEZE or ACTION-SWAP-M at any level. The consequences are the card's, printed verbatim. A trigger on a row with n < 300 prints "not evaluable (UNDERPOWERED)".

## 4. Controls, as wired (the run is INVALID if one fails; the report checks them FIRST and prints no label table if one fails)
1. **Real-vs-real, calibrated.** Copy A = the window, copy B = the window 0.2 s later (VOE1). The 6 M2b cells (2 seeds x 3 levels) are scored with the two-part rule; **real count** = the cells separated on either side. **Reference count** = for each of the R = 199 reference draws the same count, each draw scored as if real by the same rule (the draw's own bootstrap interval from its per-episode sums AND its point against the cell's 97.5th / 2.5th percentiles of the 199 draws; each cell's draws come from its own stream, so the six counts are independent). Pass iff real count <= `numpy.percentile(reference counts, 95)` (linear interpolation, like every percentile in this instrument; the `inverted_cdf` value is printed beside). The 96 untrained real-vs-real cells (32 models x 3 levels) are reported beside (number separated); they gate nothing.
2. **Untrained leave-one-out.** Within each chassis family of 16, each member in turn is "the model" and the other 15 its reference: NOTICES iff rule (a) holds at that member (its own two-part cell) AND its accuracy is strictly above the maximum of the other 15. Cells: (chassis 2 x member 16) x (FREEZE, ACTION-SWAP, ACTION-SWAP-M) x level 3 = **288**. Pass iff **at most 10 %** of the 288 come out NOTICES (pooled; the per-chassis and per-manipulation shares are printed beside). Under exchangeability at most one of the 16 can pass (c), so the expected share is at most 1/16 = 6.25 %.
3. **Calibration** (VOE1's wiring): 20 permutation draws (seeds 1001..1020) scored as if real against each cell's own reference; the share of rows separated (either side for RVR, above for the others) per family at most 5 %. Families: ACC (every accuracy cell: M2b and the 32 untrained x 6 manipulations x 3 levels = 612 cells), NULL-ACC and DIFF (the two M2b tags x 6 x 3 = 36 cells each), RVR (34 tags x 3 levels), AP-CHANCE and AP-NULL (the two M2b tags x 3 events x 3 levels). The families are mine, as in VOE1.
4. **Synthetic** `test_voe1b_synth.py` (pytest, log `PYTEST_VOE1B.log`, hashes `PYTEST_VOE1B.hashes`): VOE1's 13 tests kept (the planted toy gives NOTICES on FREEZE and TELEPORT by rules (a), (b); the action-blind toy BLIND on ACTION-SWAP; the index maps, donors, AP, window permutations, level labels and the causal chunk form of the real stack pinned), plus: the (c) rule and the four labels on hand-made rows; the planted toy still gives **NOTICES** once rule (c) is applied against 16 untrained toys; **the added test: a toy family whose members are biased in random directions gives INIT-LEVEL, never NOTICES, for a "trained" member drawn from the same family** (every member in turn is the "trained" one against the other 16 of 17 exchangeable members; at most the single highest-accuracy member can be NOTICES, and at least one member with (a) holding is INIT-LEVEL); the ACTION-SWAP-M donor rule on hand-made windows; control 1's reference count and control 2's leave-one-out share on synthetic rows.
