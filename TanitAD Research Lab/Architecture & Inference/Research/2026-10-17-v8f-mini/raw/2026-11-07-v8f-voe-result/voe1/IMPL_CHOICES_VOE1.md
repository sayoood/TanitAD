## 1. Time, windows, onset, levels (the card does not fix them)
* One cache step = 0.2 s. A window is the 16 cache frames of `latents.pt` (window frame k = clip frame g0 + k, g0 = t - 15, t = the window's NOW step); the 1,976 windows with every frame real (`ok` all true), 141 clips, 14 or 15 windows per clip, spaced 4 steps (0.8 s) apart. Neighbouring windows overlap by 12 frames; the episode-cluster bootstrap is the answer to that.
* **t0 = window frame 4** (clip frame g0 + 4): four frames of context, the onset frame kept, then the 1.6 s = 8 steps after it are window frames 5 to 12. Each manipulated copy and its unmodified copy use window frames 0 to 12 (13 frames, 12 transitions). Every quantity is causal (the predictors attend to the past only; `encode` pools earlier actions), so a 13-frame copy gives the same scored steps as a 16-frame one. The event traces use the full 16-frame unmodified windows.
* **Levels** (WMAux strides 2 and 2): level 1 state j is window frame j (0.2 s per step), level 2 state j is the lift of frame 2j (0.4 s per step), level 3 state j is the lift of frame 4j (0.8 s per step). Because t0 = 4 is a multiple of 4, the onset falls on a state of every level. **Scored steps = states whose frame lies in (t0, t0 + 8]:** level 1 frames 5 to 12 (8 steps), level 2 frames 6, 8, 10, 12 (4 steps), level 3 frames 8, 12 (2 steps). Level 1 has 12 steps in a 13-frame copy, level 2 has 6, level 3 has 3.
* **Surprise** at a step j of level l: ||F_l(z_{j-c..j-1}, e_{j-c..j-1})[-1] - z_j||^2 summed over the 128 latent dims, the one-step prediction of the TRAINED level predictor `stack.predictors[l-1]`, teacher-forced on the given (manipulated) encoded states and the action embeddings `HJEPAStack.encode` builds from the given actions (level 1: `act_embed` of the scaled raw action; levels 2 and 3: the macro-action encoders). Context c = min(3, j) states (3 is the predictors' trained context; the first position of a chunk attends to itself only, exactly the `_pred_loss` form). Level-2 and 3 states are `encode`'s lifts of the (manipulated) level-1 sequence, so a manipulation reaches them through the encoders. The action of transition j -> j+1 is the logged action at frame j (`WMAux.forward`: `scale_actions(act)[:, :-1]`).
* **Copy-Last null** at the same step and level: ||z_{j-1} - z_j||^2 between consecutive encoded latents OF THAT LEVEL.
* **Normalisation:** each series is divided by its own median over the unmodified (13-frame) windows' scored steps, per tag and level. The pairwise accuracy and AP are rank statistics and do not depend on it; it only scales traces and figures.
* **Window score** = the maximum over the level's scored steps of the normalised surprise. **Pairwise accuracy** = mean over pairs of x, x = 1 if the manipulated copy's score is above its unmodified copy's, 0.5 on an exact tie, 0 otherwise (the card does not say how ties count; exact ties occur only where the two copies are bit-identical, e.g. the Copy-Last null under ACTION-SWAP, and counting them 0.5 keeps that null at exactly 0.5).
* **Frame bank:** the card's "cached per-frame tokens" enter the world model only through the per-frame level-1 latent `z1 = wm.proj(mean_over_tokens(adapter(tokens) + pos))`. A manipulation replaces frames, hence `z1` rows; the bank holds `z1` for every frame of the 141 eval clips (14,237 frames) per tag. No image is edited or rendered.

## 2. The manipulations as index maps (`voe1_core.idx_manip`, pinned by `test_index_maps`)
Frame map of a copy = the bank frame shown at window frames 0..12; action map = the logged action used for transitions 0..11. The unmodified copy is the identity map. Actions stay as logged in every manipulation except ACTION-SWAP.
| manipulation | frames 5..12 of the copy show | actions |
|---|---|---|
| TELEPORT | window frames 13..20 (1.6 s later in the same clip; clip frames up to g0 + 20, present in every clip: smallest margin to the clip end 27 steps) | logged |
| FREEZE | frame 4 for frames 5..8 (0.8 s), then the video resumes from the held frame: frames 9..12 show window frames 5..8 (a pause; no jump at the resume, so the Copy-Last surprise of the copy is 0 on 4 steps and then an unmodified subset) | logged |
| REVERSE | window frames 12, 11, ..., 5 (the 1.6 s after t0 reversed in time; new[j] = old[17 - j]) | logged |
| SPLICE | the donor window's frames 5..12 (same window positions), donor from another episode | logged |
| ACTION-SWAP | real frames | transitions 4..11 take the donor window's actions 4..11 |
* **SPLICE donor:** `tanitad.models.hjepa.donor_indices(n, episode_ids, seed=0)` over the 1,976 windows (a seeded derangement of episodes, same within-episode rank); `voe1_core.donor_derangement` re-states its torch.Generator draws and the run asserts equality with the tanitad function.
* **ACTION-SWAP donor:** for window i the candidates are windows of OTHER episodes whose mean raw longitudinal action over transitions 4..11 differs from i's by at least 1.5 m/s^2 OR whose mean yaw rate differs by at least 0.15 rad/s; the donor is one uniform draw among them, windows in ascending order, one `numpy.random.default_rng(0)` stream. Raw action channels = the cache `a` and the yaw rate `ch1` (the logged action sequence the model is fed, units as in the cache).
* **FREEZE condition:** the logged speed (native pose track, raw frame 2g, identical to the cache `v`: max abs difference 0.0) must exceed 3 m/s at every frame 4..8 (the held frame and the frozen stretch).
* **REVERSE** has the same onset jump as TELEPORT (a jump of 8 steps instead of 9) followed by reversed motion; it is read as the card words it. **Alternative reading not run:** reversal about t0 (the video retraces the 1.6 s before t0). Disclosed, not run.

## 4. Models and the random-init control
* **Deciding:** M2b s0 and s1 (`v8f-mini-hacc/M2b_s{0,1}_hacc.pt`), zero training. Their `wm.*` is bit-equal to the sfresh `ckpt_last.pt` (WMACT `weights_check.json`).
* **Control 2 (random-init world model), the gating one: tags RI_s0 / RI_s1.** `torch.manual_seed(s)` then a fresh `V8FMini.WMAux(cfg)` (the same config as M2b, PyTorch default init of every layer): **every parameter under `wm.*` is re-initialised: 133 tensors, 2,007,874 parameters** = `wm.proj.0` (LayerNorm), `wm.proj.1` (Linear 256 -> 128), `wm.stack.act_embed`, `wm.stack.encoders` (the level-2 and 3 lifts), `wm.stack.macro_encoders`, `wm.stack.predictors` (all three levels), `wm.stack.idm_heads`. **Kept trained (the "chassis"):** `adapter.*` and `pos` of M2b s_k (seed s uses the s-th chassis). The readers and every other head of V8FMini are not used by VOE1. The z1 of the control is therefore a random projection of the trained adapter tokens. wm state sha256: s0 `322dfe91...`, s1 `58eb98bb...` (`bank_voe1.report.json`; the run rebuilds both and asserts the hashes).
* **Beside, NOT gating: tags RIs_s0 / RIs_s1.** The same fresh stack (same seed, so bit-equal stack to RI_s) with the TRAINED `wm.proj` copied in: 1,974,466 parameters re-initialised (every `wm.stack.*` tensor), `wm.proj.*` (4 tensors, 33,408 parameters) trained. Its latents equal the M2b ones, so it separates "what the trained predictor adds" from "what the trained latent geometry gives for free". The card's wording ("encoder side is the trained M2b chassis") admits both readings; the first is gating, the second is reported beside and any NOTICES on FREEZE or ACTION-SWAP there is disclosed in the result.
* **Reproduction check (MEASURED, `bank_voe1.report.json`):** the bank rows gathered at the windows of `latents.pt` vs its stored `Z1`: max abs difference **3.81e-06** (M2b s0), **2.86e-06** (M2b s1) for values up to 4.9 (float32 summation order: per-clip batches here, 64-window batches there); the raw actions **0.0** exactly. The run asserts both stay below 1e-4 / 0.

## 5. The two-part rule, cells, families (all as `voe1_core.two_part` / `ap_cell`)
* A cell = (tag, manipulation, level) for each of three series: the model's accuracy (claim 0.5), the Copy-Last null's accuracy (claim 0.5) and their paired difference per pair, x_model - x_null (claim 0). Statistic = mean over windows (a ratio of per-episode sums). **Interval:** episode-cluster bootstrap, B = 2,000, seed 0, the same rng calls as `wmact_lib.boot_weights` (draws depend only on the number of episodes; the run asserts equality). **Permutation reference:** R = 199 draws, seeds 1..199: each episode independently with probability 1/2 has the two copies' labels swapped (accuracy x -> 1 - x; difference d -> -d) in all its pairs; the stream of draw s of a cell is `default_rng([s, sha256(cell key)[:8]])`, so cells are independent. **Separated above the claim** iff the interval's lower end is above the claim AND the point is above the 97.5th percentile of the cell's reference; below symmetrically with the 2.5th percentile.
* **Labels per (manipulation, level, seed):** BLIND = the accuracy is not separated above 0.5. For TELEPORT, REVERSE, SPLICE: NOTICES = separated above 0.5 AND the paired difference (model minus null) separated above 0; NULL-ONLY = above 0.5 but the difference is not. For FREEZE and ACTION-SWAP: NOTICES = separated above 0.5 (the null enters not at all); the null's accuracy is reported (the card: it reads at most 0.5 by construction).
* **Label of a (manipulation, level) over both seeds:** NOTICES iff both seeds NOTICES; BLIND iff both BLIND; NULL-ONLY iff both seeds are above 0.5 and at least one is NULL-ONLY; anything else (the seeds disagree on above 0.5) is **DISCORDANT** and is neither BLIND nor NOTICES. "At every level" in the triggers means the over-both-seeds label at all three levels.
* **Control 3 families** (rows of the run scored as if real by the 20 draws, seeds 1001..1020, against each cell's own reference; the share of rows separated above, or either side for the two-sided family): ACC (model accuracy vs 0.5, all 6 tags x 5 manipulations x 3 levels = 90 cells), NULL-ACC (same for the null), DIFF (the paired difference), RVR (control 1's cells, either side), AP-CHANCE and AP-NULL (the event cells of the two deciding tags). Bar: at most 5 % per family; any family above 5 % makes the run INVALID. The card names no families; these are mine, fixed here.

## 6. Event alignment (IA-6d)
* **Event onsets, from the bank's labels** (ego and cuboids allowed): the series are the logged action channels per clip frame. **brake:** the cache acceleration `a` < -2 m/s^2, the first such frame after at least 5 frames (1 s) without one. **turn:** |yaw rate| > 0.1 rad/s likewise. **lead:** a lead change with the gap closing, on the trainer's lead labels (`lead_label_arrays` on `lead_eval139.npz`, near lead <= 40 m, gap = 40 m x y): a near lead appears after 2 frames without one, or the gap jumps by more than 5 m between consecutive frames (the lead file carries no track id; a different lead), AND the gap closes over the next 0.4 s at more than 0.5 m/s (a near lead 2 frames later), refractory 5 frames. Frame 0 of a clip is never an onset. Onsets in the 141 eval clips: **brake 47 (39 clips), turn 100 (62 clips), lead 27 (22 clips)**; the lead and brake events are rare and their intervals will be wide.
* **Points and labels:** the unmodified 16-frame windows' traces at each level: step j of level l, all windows (level 1: 15 steps x 1,976 = 29,640 points; level 2: 7 steps = 13,832; level 3: 3 steps = 5,928). A point is positive iff an onset of that event lies within +-0.4 s (+-2 cache steps) of the step's frame, inside the window's clip. Positive rates (chance): brake 0.0224 / 0.0212 / 0.0199, turn 0.0364 / 0.0368 / 0.0363, lead 0.0107 / 0.0107 / 0.0110 for levels 1 / 2 / 3.
* **AP** = average precision of ranking the pooled points by surprise (all steps, no peak picking; ties none in practice); **chance** = the event rate; the null's AP uses the Copy-Last trace of the same level. Peak-restricted scoring is not run.
* **Statistics:** AP - chance and AP - AP_null, each through the two-part rule: episode-cluster bootstrap B = 2,000 seed 0 (weighted AP: a point of an episode drawn m times carries weight m), reference R = 199 label-permutation draws (the label traces of whole windows move to windows of OTHER episodes, scores fixed; `window_perm`), calibration draws 1001..1020. **ALIGNS** per (event, level) iff both statistics are two-part separated above 0 at BOTH seeds. Reported per event and level; no pooled score over events. Random-init and beside tags: AP, null AP and chance only (no interval).
* **Hierarchy hypothesis (no bar):** AP - chance of level 3 minus that of level 1, per event and seed, with its episode-cluster interval; reported only.

## 7. Controls, as wired
1. **Real-vs-real:** copy A = window (frames 0..12), copy B = the window starting 0.2 s later (the bank shifted by one frame), both scored with the same medians; x = B above A. Gate: none of the 12 cells (M2b s0/s1 and RI_s0/RI_s1, 3 levels) is two-part separated from 0.5 on EITHER side. RIs_s* are reported beside.
2. **Random-init:** RI_s0 and RI_s1 must not be NOTICES on FREEZE and ACTION-SWAP at any level (12 cells).
3. **Calibration:** as in section 5.
4. **Synthetic test** `test_voe1_synth.py` (pytest, log `PYTEST_VOE1.log`): the toy constant-velocity model must give NOTICES on the planted FREEZE and the planted TELEPORT (against the null) and BLIND on ACTION-SWAP when action-blind; beside it, an action-using toy gives NOTICES on ACTION-SWAP, the toy RVR is not separated, the toy calibration share is at most 5 %, and the index maps, AP, donors, label rules and the causal chunk form of the real stack are pinned. The toy uses the same `eval_tag` driver as the run.
* The report script checks controls 1 to 3 from `RESULT_VOE1.json` and control 4 from the pytest log (and the test file's hash) FIRST; if one fails, `RESULT_VOE1.md` prints INVALID and no label table.

## 8. What the report prints, and the triggers (exactly as the card commits them)
Per manipulation x level x seed: accuracy with interval, the Copy-Last null's accuracy beside it, the paired difference, n windows and episodes, the label; the over-both-seeds label; the event table (AP, chance, null AP, ALIGNS); the L3-minus-L1 line; the four triggers evaluated mechanically: ACTION-SWAP BLIND at every level (levers: E7 DIVRS plus a counterfactual-target term; joins IMAG1 IA-3, whose result is not available here, so the join is reported as OPEN); FREEZE BLIND at every level (MRATE; joins G1); TELEPORT and SPLICE both NULL-ONLY at every level (IMAG1 IA-2 before any new arm); NOTICES on FREEZE or ACTION-SWAP at any level (the first positive imagination-is-used evidence on video; per-level table and trace figure for the paper). Tier T0 on every number.
