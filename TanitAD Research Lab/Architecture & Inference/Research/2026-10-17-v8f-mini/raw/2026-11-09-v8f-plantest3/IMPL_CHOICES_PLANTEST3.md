## 2. Where speed comes from on the eval windows
`sfresh_inputs/inputs/native_tracks.pt`, `tracks[clip id]` (rows = raw frames, columns x, y, yaw, v; 2 raw frames per model frame; eval clips are raw-frame indexed). A window of the eval latent file has `t` = clip frame of its LAST (16th) latent, so its model frame k (k = 0..15) is clip frame `g + k` with `g = t - 15`, raw row `2 (g + k)`. **Speed at knot k = `tracks[clip][2 (g + k), 3]`** (the same index PLANTEST reads for v0 at k = 0, extended to every knot). Per-knot speed IS available on the eval windows; nothing else is substituted. A knot whose row is missing (past the clip end, NaN) drops that (window, knot) sample from the speed fit and score; yaw samples are unaffected. Counts of dropped samples are printed in the gate record.
**Yaw rate at knot k = `A_raw[:, k, 1]`**, the measured ego yaw rate the logged action carries at that knot (the same quantity HOLD holds and the LATERAL family scores). The TRAIN latent file has no speed, so no TRAIN window is used by a decoder.

## 3. The decoders (card: "The decoder")
* `D_1`: features = the encoded level-1 latent `Z1[:, k]` (128-d). `D_2`: features = the pointwise level-2 encoding `E_2(Z1[:, k])` of the same latent (the stack's w = 1 encoder; the level-2 state at index i of a lifted trajectory sits at level-1 knot 2i, so the readout is trained at every knot k and applied at even knots). Targets: `(speed, yaw rate) / TUNE std`, the std over all valid TUNE samples (TUNE windows only).
* Ridge, closed form in float64 (the `fit_kin_readout` pattern): centre X and Y, `W = (Xc'Xc + 1e-3 n I)^-1 Xc'Yc`, `b = ym - mu W`, n = number of samples (pooled TUNE window-knots). No gradient training. The cost uses `D(x) - D(g) = (x - g) W` (the intercept cancels).
* Per training seed (s0, s1), fitted on TUNE windows only (control 4). Two fits: the **gate decoder** on the inner-fit episodes, and the **planning decoder** refit on all TUNE episodes (the one every arm uses).
* **Inner split** (gate): the 70 TUNE episodes ranked by `sha256("plantest3-dec-v1|<clip id>")`; the first floor(70/4) = 17 are held out, the other 53 are the inner-fit set. Episode-disjoint, inside TUNE.
* **Gate read** (before any planning): held-out R2 = 1 - SSE / SST (SST about the held-out mean) of speed and of yaw rate, pooled over all held-out (window, knot) samples, on **encoded** latents (the DECODER-WEAK quantity: speed < 0.70 or yaw < 0.50 at either seed flags the whole read; the flag does not stop the run) and, beside, on encoded latents at k = 12 only, on the **rollout at k = 12 under the logged actions** (level-1 rollout from `Z1[:, 0]` under the 12 scaled logged actions, decoded, against the knot-12 targets), and for `D_2` on encoded latents. The gate is a diagnostic; its logged actions do not enter any planner.

## 4. Cost spec and solver hooks
`plan_topdown3` is a copy of `plan_topdown2` with ONE new argument, `cost` (default `None`, which is the unchanged code path and returns bit-identical plans: control 1). A `CostSpec(mode, c, dec)`: `mode` in `latent | end | path`, shrink `c` (None or 1.0 = the stack's own `rollout`, called unchanged, so c = 1 is bit-identical by construction and not by a floating-point coincidence), decoder `dec`.
* **FLAT1 (level 1 is the top, `last_k` 1)**: `end`: `sum_j ((roll[12] - g[12]) W_1)_j^2`; `path`: `sum_{k=1..12} sum_j ((roll[k] - g[k]) W_1)_j^2` (summed over the 12 knots, not averaged; the larger scale is the card's "summed"). `g` = the encoded true-future latent at the knot (G-TRUE), the same quantity of the donor window (G-RAND, the PLANTEST2 donor map).
* **TD3**: levels 2 and 3 unchanged. Level 1: `dd = sum_j ((lift(roll) - sub) W_2)_j^2` per lifted state (6 states, level-1 knots 2, 4, ..., 12), combined with the stack's own subgoal weighting `dd[-1] + 0.5 sum dd[:-1]`. `end` only. The level-3 goal is the unchanged latent goal of PLANTEST2.
* **SHRINK(c)**: the level-1 step `z_{k+1} = z_k + c (pred(z_k, a_k) - z_k)`, context 1, same predictor call as `HJEPAStack.rollout`; for c = 0 the rollout is exactly constant and the cost gradient exactly 0.0. c from `RESULT_WMACT_SHRINK.json`: row `M2b_sX/L1/k1`, field `c_train`: s0 0.49619369970619825, s1 0.525653520686631 (read from the file at run time and asserted equal to these). The same shrunk rollout builds the saved level-1 trajectory of the chosen plan.
* SEED + REG(lambda) of PLANTEST2 unchanged (candidate 0 = HOLD_c, REG on the level-1 cost of every candidate, reference HOLD_c, std = unwidened TRAIN prior std). REG is added to the decoded cost as it was to the latent cost. The decoded cost is on a different scale from the latent cost (standardised units; PATH is a sum over 12 knots); the lambda grid is the card's {0, 0.1, 1}, applied as given.

## 5. Arms, units, order
Arms (FLAT1): DEC-END, DEC-PATH, each plain and SHRINK = 4. TD3: DEC-END, in the plain or SHRINK form that FLAT1 DEC-END chose on TUNE (lower TUNE G-TRUE MAE at its chosen lambda, per training seed and family; tie to plain), at FLAT1's lambda for that form. Batching, generators, early stop, 2 workers, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, `maxtasksperchild=4`: as PLANTEST2 (consecutive batches of 64 inside a subset; generator `seed x 100003 + batch`, identical across goals and lambdas; the early stop is batch-coupled, disclosed).
Run order (a stopped run keeps the priority): (0) gate and KIN-CEILING units (2 seeds); (A) FLAT1 TUNE units: 4 arms x 3 lambdas x 2 seeds = 24, G-TRUE, planner seed 0; `tune_choice.json` written from them; (B1) FLAT1 SCORE, planner seed 0, G-TRUE and G-RAND at the chosen lambda (per family; a family pair with two different lambdas is planned at both); (B2) TD3 SCORE, same, DEC-END; (B3) planner-seed replicates (G-TRUE, planner seed 1): FLAT1 then TD3. Every unit is its own file under `runs/` and is skipped if present.
Lambda choice (TUNE only): per arm, training seed and family the lambda in {0, 0.1, 1} with the lowest mean TUNE MAE (LONGITUDINAL acc MAE, LATERAL yaw MAE) under G-TRUE; ties to the smaller lambda; G-RAND uses the G-TRUE lambda.
**RAM guard** in the runner: before each unit, wait while free physical memory < 6 GB (heartbeat line every 60 s). At most 2 workers; CPU only (`CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1`, `NVIDIA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1`); the dev-box GPU lock is never touched.

## 6. KIN-CEILING (beside the planners, no world model, no planner)
For a window, the 12 actions from the DECODED true-future latents `Z1[:, 0..12]` (planning decoder, the training seed's own): `acc_k = (s^_{k+1} - s^_k) / 0.2`, `yaw_k = w^_k`, k = 0..11, where `s^`, `w^` are the de-standardised decoded speed and yaw rate. The forward difference is the project's own unicycle convention (`v' = v + a dt`, `psi' = psi + w dt`, action k acts from knot k). Scored as the card's MAE against the 12 logged actions, on SCORE, EC-BOOT paired against HOLD. KIN beats HOLD = KIN minus HOLD separated-better at both seeds. Beside: KIN with the donor's decoded true future (KIN-RAND), to show the ceiling is goal-specific.

## 7. Read, statistics (SCORE only)
As PLANTEST2 section 7: paired episode-cluster bootstrap over the 71 SCORE episodes, B 2,000, seed 0 (the `wmact_lib.boot_weights` draws), ratio of per-episode sums, 95 % percentile, separated-better = interval wholly below 0. Labels PLANNABLE / GOAL-BLIND / NO-GAIN (and WORSE-THAN-RAND as in PLANTEST: outside the card's list, triggers nothing), per arm, planner, family, both seeds required else SEEDS-DISAGREE. HOLD-COLLAPSE: mean |plan - HOLD| over SCORE windows and 12 steps in the family's channel, divided by the TRAIN prior std (raw units), below 0.10, on G-TRUE. HIER-ADDS: TD3 DEC-END minus FLAT1 DEC-END (same form and lambda) under G-TRUE, separated-better at both seeds. Beside, no bar: speed, heading, ADE at 2.4 s (unicycle code of PLANTEST), distance from HOLD, chosen lambda and form, decoded-cost monotonicity (negative Spearman of the decoded distance to the END goal against time along the chosen plan, Tab. 10 statistic; the latent-distance monotonicity of PLANTEST2 printed next to it), the planner-seed replicate, the gate record. TACTICAL: not computed (n = 0). STRATEGIC: PAUSED (PI item 25). The interval answers "another draw of episodes"; training-seed variance is read from the two seeds and inference variance from the replicate.

## 8. Controls (a failure makes the read INVALID; each has a deliberate-regression arm that must fail)
1. **Reduction.** 16 windows (`IDX = arange(0, 1976, 124)[:16]`, M2b_s0), G-TRUE, TD3 and FLAT1, lambda 0 and 0.1: `plan_all3(cost=None)` and `plan_all3(CostSpec(latent, c 1))` equal `plan_all2` bit for bit on actions and every cost / iteration / monotonicity array. Mutation: the DEC-END cost on the same windows must differ.
2. **Planted dynamics.** PLANTEST's planted linear world plus a planted linear decoder (32 -> 2) aligned with the world's action input matrix, `W = B1 (B1'B1)^-1` (so `W'B1 = I`). A random 32 -> 2 decoder recovered the DEC-PATH actions only to MAE 0.0831 in the same solver budget (identical at decoder scales 1 to 30: a conditioning limit of recovering 24 actions through `W'A^m B1` chains, not a scale effect), above the bar of 0.05; this was found in a synthetic dev run before any eval window was read, and the aligned decoder is the planted decoder of the control. FLAT1 DEC-PATH: action MAE <= 0.05 and <= 0.2 x the prior-mean baseline (24 numbers identified by 24 decoded numbers). DEC-END (FLAT1 and TD3) identifies only a 2-d functional of the 24 action numbers, so its recovery is read on the decoded end state: error of the recovered plan's decoded end state against the planted one <= 0.05 and <= 0.2 x the same error of the prior-mean plan (decoded standard units). HOLD seed = first planted action held; lambda 0 and 0.01. Mutation: predictors ignore the action: every arm must fail its bar.
3. **Decoder.** A synthetic known linear map (noise 0.01) is recovered: held-out R2 >= 0.99 and max relative weight error <= 0.05; shuffled targets give held-out R2 <= 0.02. Mutation: the recovery bar applied to the shuffled-trained fit must fail.
4. **Split.** Decoder fit and lambda choice read no SCORE window: the episode sets are disjoint (asserted on real ids); the decoder fitted on a ctx whose SCORE rows are NaN latents, garbage actions and garbage speeds is bit-identical to the one fitted on the real ctx, and so is the lambda-choice table; the indices handed to the fitter and the planner are asserted disjoint from SCORE. Mutation: a fit over all windows goes red (NaN reaches the weights).
5. **Shrink identity.** c = 1 gives the stack's `rollout` array bit for bit and a plan identical to the plain arm (16 windows); c = 0 gives a rollout identical to the start latent at all 12 steps and a decoded-cost gradient with respect to the actions of exactly 0.0, while c = 0.5 and c = 1 give a gradient with max abs > 0 (the probe can read non-zero). Mutation: a shrink that ignores c fails the c = 0 check; c = 0.999 differs from plain (the identity check can fail).
6. **Goal plumbing and bit-repeat on the new cost.** For DEC-END and DEC-PATH (FLAT1) and DEC-END (TD3): G-TRUE / G-RAND from `plan_all3` equal the direct `run_planner3` calls bit for bit and differ from each other; a repeat is bit-identical; another planner seed differs; only the first logged action (a0) reaches a planner. Mutations as PLANTEST2 control 5 (a pipeline ignoring the goal name goes red; an all-logged-actions seed differs and sits nearer the log).

## 9. Disclosures (decide nothing)
* The decoder has two outputs; DEC-END is therefore a 2-number target and cannot select 12 actions on its own. HOLD_c seed and REG carry the rest, as in PLANTEST2; the card's outcome table is applied as written.
* DEC-PATH's goal is the true future path (an oracle path), the card's ceiling read; G-RAND is the donor's path.
* A window whose later knots lack a speed row (clip end) loses those samples in the speed fit only. The planning, the metrics (acceleration, yaw rate) and KIN-CEILING do not use measured SCORE speed; v0 only enters the beside unicycle metrics, as in PLANTEST2.
* The shrink factor is the TRAIN-fitted `c_train` of level 1 at k = 1, per training seed.
