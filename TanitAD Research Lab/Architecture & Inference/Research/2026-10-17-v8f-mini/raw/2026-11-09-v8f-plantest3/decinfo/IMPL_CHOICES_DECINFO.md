## 3. Readers
* **Ridge** (every rung): `plantest3_lib.ridge_fit`, lambda 1e-3 times the sample count, intercept unpenalised, features NOT standardised (the gate's form, needed for control 4); grids: the flattened PCA scores (1,280 per frame; 5,120 for four frames).
* **MLP** (vector rungs R0 to R2, one and four frames): `diag_mlp.train` imported unchanged (2 hidden layers of 256, GELU, dropout 0.2, AdamW 1e-3, wd 1e-2, batch 512, at most 120 epochs, patience 12, inputs standardised), seed 0, early stopping on the 20 % of fit episodes with `sha256("plantest3-mlp-es|<id>") < 51`; trained on the other fit episodes; held-out episodes read once.
* **Conv** (grid rungs): input (frames x 32, 4, 10), three 3x3 convolutions (padding 1) of 32 channels with GELU, flatten (1,280), dropout 0.2, linear to 1. AdamW lr 1e-3, wd 1e-2, batch 256, at most 80 epochs, patience 10, per-channel input standardisation and target standardisation from the training part, early stopping on the same 20 % fit-episode part as the MLP. Three initialisation seeds 0, 1, 2; the reported conv R2 is the mean of the three held-out R2 (each also stored).
* One model per target (speed, yaw rate), separately.
* The deciding quantity of a rung is the maximum over its readers of the reported R2 (optimistic; stated in the report). Four-frame and one-frame readers of a rung are both readers of that rung.

## 4. Controls (runtime and pytest, `test_decinfo_controls.py`)
1. **Shuffled targets**: the fit windows' target tracks (16 values per window) are permuted among fit windows so that no window gets a track from its own episode (asserted); held-out targets stay true. Every rung, reader, m and target has one shuffled run (conv: seed 0 only). INVALID if any reads above 0.05. Mutation in the test: no permutation reads at the true level and fails the bar.
2. **Planted signal**: target = (X w) / std(X w) + 0.3 N(0,1), w ~ N(0,1) fixed (seed 0), X = that rung's input as the ridge sees it (fit and held-out); ridge held-out R2 must be at least 0.80 (noise-free ceiling 0.917); zeroed features (fit and held-out) must read at most 0.02. Run per vector rung and per grid rung and m on the real inputs, and in the test on synthetic inputs of the same shapes.
3. **Split**: `split_guard(fit_eids, held_eids, es_eids, score_eids)` asserts the sets are pairwise disjoint and that fit and held-out windows are TUNE windows; PCA and standardisation statistics are computed from fit rows only; a test fits on real-shaped data with garbage in the held-out rows and requires bit-identical fitted weights and PCA. Mutation: a held-out episode inside the fit set makes the guard raise.
4. **Reproduction**: R0 ridge at one frame on M2b s0 and s1 against `plantest3/runs/pre_M2b_s*.json` `enc_L1.r2_speed` / `r2_yaw`, tolerance 0.01 (run on the stored latents `wmact/latents.pt` in the test, and again on the model-derived z1 in the run). Mutation: target shifted by one frame leaves the tolerance.
5. **Chain identity**: on 16 TUNE windows (every 6th by tune order from the fit side), `torch.equal(wm.z1(adapt), wm.proj(adapt.mean(2)))` for M2b s0 and s1; R2's pooled token is a separate call result. Mutation: pooling in float64 then casting breaks equality.
