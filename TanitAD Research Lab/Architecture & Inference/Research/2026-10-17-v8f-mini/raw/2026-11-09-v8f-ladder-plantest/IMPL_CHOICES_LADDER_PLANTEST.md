## 2. Two rollout forms
* **c1 (the cards' form):** the planner conditions level 1 on the current latent only (`context 1`, `st.rollout`). The deciding rows of both cards.
* **c3 (beside, labelled, no bar):** level 1 conditioned on 3 states: the REAL encoded latents at t-2 and t-1 (frames g-2, g-1) and the logged measured actions taken at them (past data), then NOW and the plan. Implemented by `lp_lib.rollout_h` (a rollout whose first context states are real history); with no history it equals `st.rollout` bit for bit (control). Windows without both past frames (g < 2: 141 of 1,976) are not scored under c3; c3 rows state their n and the c1 rows of the same windows are the paired comparison. Upper levels of TD3 keep context 1 in both forms (history is a level-1 notion here). The ladder's k-step loss grows its context from the rollout's own states; this real-history form is the PI-lead's definition (brief), and the own-state form is not run.

## 3. TD3 on a (3, 5) model is not computable; it is reported, not changed
TD3 as the card defines it needs level steps of 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 s (cumulative strides 1, 2, 4 frames; `lp_lib.td3_defined` reads them from the BUILT model). R35 and R35H have cumulative strides (1, 3, 15): the level-3 step is 3.0 s, so the 2.4 s goal is not on level 3's grid and the 3-step top horizon would be 9.0 s. Those rows read `NOT COMPUTABLE` with this reason and n = 1,976. C0 (2, 2) has the card's structure and runs TD3. FLAT1 (level 1, 12 steps, goal = the level-1 latent at 2.4 s) exists on every arm. HIER-ADDS is therefore computable on C0 only. No TD2 variant is substituted.

2. **Latent route**: M2b_s0 whole-clip latents against `latents.pt` Z1: max abs difference <= 1e-4 (reported; where it exceeds it, the first knots, the K-history replication, are named).
3. **Reduction**: `plan_all3(c1, prior, lam 0)` equals `plantest_lib.plan_all` bit for bit, and `plan_all3(c1, hold, lam 0.3)` equals `plantest2_lib.plan_all2` bit for bit, 16 windows (difference 0.0).
4. **History is real**: the first predicted state under c3 equals a direct predictor call on [z(t-2), z(t-1), z(t)] with the logged actions: max abs difference <= 1e-6. Regression: the same with the repeated latent [z(t), z(t), z(t)] must differ by > 1e-3 (the check goes RED).
5. **Reachability** (real batch of 8 windows per arm): cost gradient with respect to the plan, mean abs per channel > 1e-6 in BOTH action channels, at c1 and c3; the planted-world zero control (actions disconnected from the predictor) reads exactly 0.0 at c1 and c3.
6. **Planted dynamics** through `plan_topdown3`: planted-recovery MAE <= 0.05 and <= 0.2 x baseline at c1 and c3 (FLAT1 and TD3); the disconnected arm must fail it. **Limit**: lambda 1e6 plan equals HOLD within 1e-4 prior-std units. **Goal plumbing / bit-repeat / no-leak** as the cards (16 windows).
