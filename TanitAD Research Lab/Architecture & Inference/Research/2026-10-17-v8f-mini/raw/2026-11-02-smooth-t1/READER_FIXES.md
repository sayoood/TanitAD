Scope rule held throughout: **no outcome rule, row, bar, operator or estimator was touched.** The unchanged functions are `better`, `worse`, `read_a`, `read_xy`, `arm_outcome` (the card's DOMINANT / HARMS / MOVED / NO EFFECT rule), the 19 rows and their pools (`gapdecomp.decompose`, landed), `D1_POINT_M = -0.2415`, `ADE_GUARD_M = 0.0604`, the CRAWL set, B = 2,000, seed 0. The PREREG card (sha256 4030d22f...) was not edited.

## Why the first run failed (`logs/read.out`; the per-run extract log of that failure was overwritten by the successful re-extraction, the line below is quoted from `read.out`)
`extract K1OFF_DAGOACC_s0 failed rc=2: REFUSED: windows / floor differ from CTRL's (same_windows=True, same_floor=False)`. The extractor (`code/extract_smooth.py`, the landed `extract_arrays.py` plus two path arguments) required the re-rolled P10 floor `fl` to be **bit-equal** to the banked one. It is not: the banked rollouts ran at torch's default 16 threads, the SMOOTH-T1 rollouts at 1 thread (the lab's CPU cap), and float32 reduction order differs by thread count. Measured on the `--smooth off` rerun against the banked dump (`raw/K1_DIAG.json`): `fl` max abs deviation 3.76e-3 m (mean 2.1e-6), `cl` 6.32e-3 m (mean 1.8e-5), tick-0 plan `ol` 1.9e-5 m. The same noise breaks every other exact comparison to the banked run, which is the source of fixes 1-4.

Chronology (honest): fixes 1 to 3 were made after the K1 deviation was known and before any smoothed row table had been computed; the first complete read (outcome words printed) happened after fix 3. Fixes 4 and 5 were made after those words had been printed (`logs/read3.out`) and touch neither rows nor the outcome rule; `logs/read4.out` (after fixes 4 and 5) and `logs/read5.out` (the final run, after fix 6; it produced `raw/SMOOTH_T1.json`) print the identical outcome lines (checked by diff).

## What the first measurement of the amendment showed (recorded the moment it was computed, `raw_a1/NONDET_BOUND.json`, written by `a1_nondet_bound.py` before any X row)

Y' and Y'' (the same `--smooth off` configuration, DAG-OACC s0, 16 threads, run twice) are **bit-identical in the dump and in the ticks**: N_dump = N_floor = N_tick0 = N_ticks = N_row = 0, so every tolerance of the amendment stays at the card's flat 1e-4. The rig is deterministic run to run at a fixed thread count; whatever separates the banked Y from a fresh `--smooth off` run is therefore systematic, not run-to-run noise. The amendment's ladder handles this case (QUOTABLE ON Y' when only the banked-reference comparisons exceed the tolerance).
