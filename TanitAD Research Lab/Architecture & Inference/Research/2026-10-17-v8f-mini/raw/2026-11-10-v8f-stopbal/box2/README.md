Each: disk read, GPU idle, argv pin, DAgger bank re-hash, gate, token, training under the token, post check, bank (each pull sha256-checked against box 2's own `certutil` hash, 3 attempts), cleanup.

## Dropped against the OBD checker (and why)
* `otband_probe` (detach-only / band probe, `purity.by_term`, `code_edge_max_dknot`), `w_op` / `w_tac_traj`, band G-LIVE terms, `ACC_PROBE_REQUIRED` and the `acc`-in-purity needle: the DAG-OACC recipe does not carry the detach or the band loss, and the code of record has no such probe (`grep` of the export: `--w-op` 0, `oband` 0). The arms never detach, so the argv must not carry `--detach-upper` (asserted) and an `otband_probe` in a selfcheck is a refusal.
* The acceleration probe itself is KEPT: DAG-OACC's recipe has `--w-acc 49.6969`, and `acc_probe` exists in the trainer.
* The conditional arm (OBD-NULL_s1 appended when parity fails) and its exit 14: the card's NULL_s1 is a planned run, and parity is never a stop.
* The needles `--w-op`, `--w-tac-traj`, `--detach-upper`, `def bad_oband`, `def oband_probe`, `keys = (*keys, "acc")` and the data needle `def attach_stok` are replaced by the STOPBAL ones (both new flags, `goal_pos_weight_stop`, `lon_class_weight_stop`, `LON_STOP_I`, the two loss lines, `def acc_probe`, `def dagger_probe`, `LON9`).

## Token, post check, bank, failure behaviour, exit codes, isolation, fail-closed transport
As OBD (README of `theory/obd/box2/`), with the names changed: the per-run token is written only by `stopbal_check.py gate` on box 2 after the assertions pass, binds tag, commit, argv sha256, the six data-file md5s, the DAgger bank sha256, the shipped tree manifest sha256, the trainer sha256 and the selfcheck sha256, and the run step refuses to train without a matching token. A gate / pin / token refusal of one full run (stage rc 11) writes `ARM_REFUSED_<tag>.txt` and the chain goes on with the next independent run, ending with `STOP.txt` (rc 4), never `DONE.txt`; every other failure stops the chain. Exit codes: 3 12 h rule; 4 gate/pin refused; 5 job or transfer failed; 6 disk low (box 2 D: below 5 GB, a constant); 7 pulled copy differs; 8 package, commit or transport; 9 ship refused; 10 pilot unreadable; 11 box-2 timeout or unreadable probe; 12 box busy, GPU not idle, or OBD not ended; 13 blocked tag; 16 transport not fail-closed. Sentinels, each in its own file: `STOP.txt` (`ZZ STOPBAL-STOP`), `DONE.txt` (`ZZ STOPBAL-DONE`), `ARM_REFUSED_<tag>.txt` (`ZZ STOPBAL-ARM-REFUSED`). Cleanup (own gate dirs, by literal path, logged, only after the dev box proved the bank; never a checkpoint) follows the PI's auto-delete rule.
