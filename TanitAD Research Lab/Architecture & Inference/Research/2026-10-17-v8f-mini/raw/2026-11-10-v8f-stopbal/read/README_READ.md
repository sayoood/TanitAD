## Reading choices, committed here before any STOPBAL number exists (the card's rules are quoted, never changed)
* **(r1)** Stop-point windows = the STOPDEC set: `strat_tlstop` == STOP_POINT AND has-label AND at least one present speed cell (117 at the banked dump, re-asserted per arm; the sets must be identical across arms). All windows = every eval window with a finite value in the cell. P cell = tactical (knots 11-30) ADE on the stop-point windows; NR cell = tactical ADE on all windows.
* **(r2)** Delta = arm minus BAL-NULL of the same seed (unrounded point difference; interval from the paired bootstrap). Floor = |mean(BAL-NULL_rep) - mean(BAL-NULL_s0)| over the same finite windows of the same window set, read at seed 0, applied to both seeds. Gain = separated AND better side AND |delta| > floor; cost = separated AND worse side AND |delta| > floor. A cell with no floor is never a gain or a cost. Lower is better everywhere except the TAC accuracies. Every cell (both window sets, both bands, all four families) is read and every cost and gain is named in `named_costs` / `named_gains`.
* **(r3)** M: hit rate = share of stop-point windows on which the lever's decision fires (BAL-G `p(STOP_POINT) >= 0.5`, goal index 6; BAL-L lon argmax == STOP, LON9 index 2); false-positive rate = share of the other has-and-present windows (2,234 at the banked dump). M = hit >= 0.30 AND fpr <= 0.05. Each with an episode-cluster interval, beside.
* **(r4)** The card's predicates are evaluated literally; where several hold the headline takes this precedence, and all that hold are listed: BAL-REGRESSES (NR is a cost at either seed) > BAL-ADOPT (P, NR, M at both) > BAL-GAIN-OTHER-PATH (P and NR at both, M fails at both) > BAL-DECISION-ONLY (M and NR at both, P fails at both) > BAL-FP-COSTS (hit >= 0.30 at both, fpr > 0.05 at either) > BAL-RECALL-SHORT (hit < 0.30 at either). No predicate holds => SEEDS-DISAGREE (e.g. P at one seed only with M passing at both). The committed trigger text of the headline is printed with it; the card's two-lever ADOPT rule is printed as `both_levers`.
* **(r5)** RECALL-SHORT branch of the trigger: recall up = hit(arm) - hit(NULL) > |hit(REP) - hit(NULL_s0)| at both seeds; unchanged = within that floor at both; AUROC within the floor = |AUROC(arm) - AUROC(NULL)| <= |AUROC(REP) - AUROC(NULL_s0)| at both. (a) up and AUROC within the floor: second dose. (b) unchanged: read-back / gradient first, then the perception lever to the PI. Otherwise "neither", named for the Master Mind.
* **(r6)** Controls: 1 (lever read-back and G-DVB from `selfcheck.json`), 2 (`live.terms.goal` / `lon` finite, no dead `h_goal*` / `h_lon*` parameter, `groups_grad.T` > 0), 3 (STOPDEC-controls block per arm exactly 0.0 / in range, window counts 2,399 / 2,365 / 117, planted AUROC 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.0, decisions recomputed from the probabilities equal the npz's), 4 (BAL-NULL_s0 and BAL-NULL_rep present). A control that fails OR cannot be read makes the outcome INVALID (fail closed). Control 5 (the chain's G-SUITE and the Master Mind's pre-launch suite step) is NOT checked here and is printed as such. Without control 4 no gain or cost is read at all.
* STRATEGIC: PAUSED (PI item 25), always printed, never a pass, never omitted. Bands: operative knots 1-10, tactical 11-30 (the full-track band of OTBAND is not read). DK-VIOL is not read; distance keeping is the absolute error against the human's own minimum headway and time gap (OTBAND r2).
