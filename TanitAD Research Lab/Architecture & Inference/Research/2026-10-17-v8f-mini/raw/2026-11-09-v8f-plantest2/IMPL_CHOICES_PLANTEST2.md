## 1. The solver code (SEED and REG)
`HJEPAStack.plan_topdown` lives in the read-only export and is not edited. `plantest2_lib.plan_topdown2` is a line-for-line copy with two hooks, `seeds` and `reg`; with both `None` it draws the generator in the same order and returns the same plans (control 1 asserts bit equality against PLANTEST's own `plan_all`). Default of every new argument leaves PLANTEST's behaviour unchanged (`seed_mode="prior"`, `lam=0`).

## 2. SEED
* HOLD = `A_raw[:, 0]` (a0, yaw rate0) held 12 steps, converted to the planner's level-1 scaled units by `wm.scale_actions` (the inverse of `plantest_real.to_raw`). **HOLD_c** = HOLD clipped to the level-1 prior box (prior mean +- 2 std): the plan can never leave the box, so HOLD_c is the closest feasible HOLD. HOLD_c is the SEED centre and the REG reference. The metric reference HOLD stays the unclipped raw HOLD of PLANTEST. The fraction of windows where HOLD_c differs from HOLD is reported.
* Level 1: candidate 0 = HOLD_c; candidates 1..15 = HOLD_c + (prior_std_1 x N(0,1)) where `prior_std_1` is the widened std (1.5 x the TRAIN std), then clipped to the box. The normal draw has the same shape and order as PLANTEST's, so candidate noise is paired across goals AND across lambdas.
* Upper levels (TD3): the macro encoding of the HOLD_c sequence, `st.encode(z_seq, HOLD_c)` with `z_seq` = 13 copies of the start latent (the macro-action path reads the actions only; the states enter only to give shapes). Level 2 seed = `enc.act[1]` (6 macro-actions, the level-2 horizon), level 3 seed = `enc.act[2]` (3 macro-actions). Candidate 0 = this seed, others seed + widened std x N(0,1), clipped to that level's box. Upper levels carry no REG.
* The logged actions enter the planner only through a0 (the first action of the window), by construction of the card's HOLD. Control 5 asserts that changing actions 1..14 leaves the plans bit-identical and changing a0 changes them.

## 3. REG(lambda)
Cost term added to the level-1 cost of every candidate (TD3's level-1 subgoal cost, FLAT1's goal cost): `lambda x mean over the 12 steps and the 2 channels of ((a - HOLD_c) / s)^2`, `a` in scaled units, `s` = the fitted TRAIN std of the level-1 prior BEFORE widening (the stack's `prior_std_1 / 1.5`; raw units: about 0.86 m/s^2 and 0.095 rad/s). Grid {0, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0}; lambda 0 adds nothing (SEED alone). The best-iterate tracking and the early stop therefore see goal cost plus REG, as an optimiser would. The reported goal-cost diagnostics (`cost1`, `init1`) include REG when lambda > 0 (disclosed); monotonicity uses the pure goal distance.

## 4. The split
`split_episodes`: the 141 distinct clip ids, key = sha256 of the string `plantest2-v1|<clip id>`, ranked ascending (ties by id); the first floor(141/2) = 70 are TUNE, the remaining 71 SCORE. Independent of window order. Windows follow their episode. Asserted: disjoint episode sets, nothing dropped.

## 5. Lambda selection (TUNE only)
Per planner (TD3, FLAT1), training seed (s0, s1) and family (LONGITUDINAL: `acc_mae`, LATERAL: `yaw_mae`): the lambda of the grid with the lowest mean MAE over the TUNE windows under G-TRUE, planner seed 0; an exact tie goes to the smaller lambda. 2 x 2 x 2 = 8 choices, stored in `tune_choice.json` before any SCORE window is planned. G-RAND on SCORE uses the lambda chosen for G-TRUE of the same (planner, seed, family). A family whose two lambdas differ is planned at both.

## 6. Batching, execution
Windows of a subset (TUNE, SCORE) in ascending index order, consecutive batches of 64, generator `seed x 100003 + batch index` (`P.make_gen`), identical for every goal kind and every lambda. The early stop is a batch-mean rule, so a window's iteration count depends on its batch; plans of one subset differ from PLANTEST's full-set plans for that reason (disclosed). CPU, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1`, 2 worker processes with `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, `maxtasksperchild=4`. Stage A: 24 TUNE jobs (2 seeds x {TD3, FLAT1} x 6 lambdas, G-TRUE). Stage B (after `tune_choice.json`): per (seed, planner, chosen lambda) SCORE jobs G-TRUE and G-RAND at planner seed 0, plus G-TRUE at planner seed 1 (the replicate). Each job is saved on its own (`runs/`), resumable.

## 7. Statistics and the read (SCORE only)
Paired episode-cluster bootstrap over the 71 SCORE episodes, B 2,000, seed 0, the rng calls of `wmact_lib.boot_weights` (EC-BOOT), statistic = ratio of per-episode sums of the per-window difference, 95 % percentile interval; separated-better = interval wholly below 0 (MAE). Labels as the card; WORSE-THAN-RAND added as in PLANTEST (G-TRUE minus G-RAND separated worse; triggers nothing). Both seeds must agree, else SEEDS-DISAGREE. HIER-ADDS: TD3 minus FLAT1 under G-TRUE, each planner at its own chosen lambda for that family, separated-better at both seeds. Estimator caveat as PLANTEST: the interval answers "another draw of episodes", is blind to training-seed and planner-seed variance; the planner-seed replicate is printed beside.
**HOLD-COLLAPSE, exact computation.** For a (planner, training seed, family, lambda) row: `d = mean over SCORE windows and the 12 steps of |plan_raw - HOLD_raw|` in the family's channel (acceleration, or yaw rate), divided by the TRAIN prior std of that channel in raw units (section 3). The flag is set when `d < 0.10`. Evaluated on the G-TRUE plan (the deciding arm; G-RAND's d is printed beside). A flagged row keeps its label with the suffix `+HOLD-COLLAPSE` and does not count as planning. Window-level equality with HOLD is not required; this is a mean.
Beside, no bar: speed, heading and ADE at 2.4 s (PLANTEST's unicycle code), d per family, chosen lambda, the TUNE MAE grid, cost monotonicity (Tab. 10 statistic), the planner-seed replicate, fraction of HOLD_c clipped. TACTICAL: not computed (n = 0, no decision output). STRATEGIC: PAUSED (PI item 25).

## 8. Controls (a failure makes the read INVALID; each has a deliberate-regression arm that must fail)
1. **Reduction.** 16 windows (`IDX = arange(0, 1976, 124)[:16]`, M2b_s0), G-TRUE, TD3 and FLAT1: `plan_all2(seed_mode="prior", lam=0)` equals `P.plan_all` bit for bit on actions and on every cost / iteration / monotonicity array. Mutation: `seed_mode="hold"`, lam 0 must differ.
2. **Limit.** Same 16 windows, lambda = 1,000,000, G-TRUE, TD3 and FLAT1: `max |plan_scaled - HOLD_c| / s <= 1e-4` over all windows, steps and channels (candidate 0 enters at HOLD_c with zero REG and every other iterate pays about 1e6 x d^2, so the best iterate is the seed; the tolerance covers float32 only). Mutation: REG switched off in the cost (`reg_term` returns zeros) must exceed the tolerance.
3. **Planted dynamics.** PLANTEST control 1's world, tolerance and baseline, now through `run_planner2`: TD3 and FLAT1, `seed_mode="hold"` with HOLD = the first planted action held, at lambda 0 and at lambda 0.01: MAE <= 0.05 and <= 0.2 x the prior-mean baseline (64 planted windows). Dev reading (synthetic only, before this file): TD3 0.0050 / 0.0050, FLAT1 0.0096 / 0.0103 (lambda 0 / 0.01), prior-mean baseline 0.3647, HOLD baseline 0.4548; lambda 1.0: TD3 0.0254, FLAT1 0.1004 (REG pulls toward HOLD by design; not in the control). Mutation: predictors ignore the action: every arm must read MAE > 0.05.
4. **Split.** TUNE and SCORE share no episode (asserted on the real ids); the split is order independent (shuffled ids give the same sets); the tuner is run on a ctx whose SCORE windows are replaced by NaN latents and garbage logged actions and returns bit-identical MAE and lambda, and the set of window indices it passed to the planner is disjoint from SCORE. Mutation: a tuner that reads all windows goes red (the NaN reaches its MAE).
5. **Goal plumbing, bit-repeat, leak.** PLANTEST controls 2 and 3 on `plan_all2` (G-TRUE / G-RAND equal the direct `run_planner2` calls bit for bit and differ from each other; same seed repeats bit-identically; mutations as PLANTEST: a pipeline that ignores the goal name goes red, a different seed differs), and the a0-only statement of section 2 with its mutation (a planner seeded from all 12 logged actions differs and sits closer to them).

## 9. Disclosures (decide nothing)
* HOLD_c clipping and the REG standard deviation (unwidened TRAIN std) are this file's reading of the card's "units of the prior std" and "plausible action".
* The seed gives candidate 0 the HOLD action, so G-TRUE at lambda 0 can only stay at or leave HOLD by the optimiser's gradient steps; the best-iterate rule keeps the lowest goal cost, which is a property of the planner, not of the metric.
* G-RAND donors come from `donor_indices` over all 1,976 windows (a derangement over the 141 episodes); a SCORE window's donor may sit in a TUNE episode. Only the donor's goal latent is used. G-TRUE tuning uses no donor.
* The early stop is batch-coupled (section 6). FLAT (last-2 cost) is not run; FLAT1 is the flat arm.
