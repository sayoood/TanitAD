## 1. Geometry, read from the BUILT model (`hwm_plan.Geometry.from_stack`)
Counts are level-1 steps (0.2 s) unless named. `s2`, `s3` = `st.cfg[1].stride`, `st.cfg[2].stride`, cross-checked against the macro encoders' stride and `WMAux.built_strides()` (a mismatch raises; control h). Window 1 at every level (the planner's `initial_latents` needs it).
* `K1 = ceil(n_op / s2)`: level-2 states that level 1 matches; level 1 plans `h1 = K1 * s2` steps (at least 10).
* `K2 = ceil(ceil(n_traj / s2) / s3)`: level-3 states that level 2 matches; level 2 plans `h2 = K2 * s3` macro steps (covers at least 30 level-1 steps).
* `T3 = goal_steps` (default K2): level-3 steps of the top plan; the goal frame is `T3 * s2 * s3` steps ahead. A longer `goal_steps` lets level 3 plan beyond 6 s while level 2 follows its first K2 states.
| model | s2, s3 | step L1 / L2 / L3 (s) | K1, K2, T3 | h1 | h2 | goal frame | MEASURED by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M2b | 2, 2 | 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 | 5, 8, 8 | 10 | 16 (6.4 s) | 32 (6.4 s) | `test_h`, built M2b s0 |
| ladder (3,5) | 3, 5 | 0.2 / 0.6 / 3.0 | 4, 2, 2 | 12 | 10 (6 s) | 30 (6 s) | `test_h`, built (3,5) stack, random init |
* `Geometry.plantest(st)` is PLANTEST's geometry on (2,2): T3 3, K = (3, 6), 12 knots, all from level 1. It exists for the reduction control only.
* n_traj 30 (6 s), n_op 10 (2 s): R-TRAJ, R-OP. On M2b the level-2 plan covers 6.4 s; the trajectory takes the first 30 knots.

## 2. Solver (`SolverCfg`, all parameters)
Defaults equal PLANTEST's values (`plantest_lib`): S 16, at most 90 AdamW iterations, patience 10, rel_tol 1e-4, lr 0.1 / 0.3 / 0.3, sub-goal weight 0.5, candidates clipped to prior mean +- 2 std, initial Gaussians 1.5x wider, cost = squared distance of the last 2 states (level 1) or the last state (upper levels) to the goal / sub-goals. The solver function is `plantest2_lib.plan_topdown2` (imported, not copied): PLANTEST2's copy of `HJEPAStack.plan_topdown` with the SEED and REG hooks.
* `seed_mode`: `hold` (candidate 0 = HOLD at level 1; upper levels seeded from the macro encoding of the HOLD sequence) or `prior` (PLANTEST's initialisation). `lam`: REG(lambda), a cost on level-1 actions, lambda x mean squared deviation from HOLD in prior-std units; needs `hold`. **Placeholders `hold`, `lam 0` until PLANTEST2's result chooses; the Master Mind sets them at freeze.**
* Planner seed: `torch.Generator` seed `seed * 100003 + batch index`, identical for every goal and arm (paired initial candidates), batches of 64 consecutive windows (the early stop is a batch-mean rule). `batch0` lets a worker plan a slice of the batches and reproduce the whole-run plans.
* HOLD = the first logged action a0 (measured acceleration and yaw rate at NOW, the measured ego state) held, in scaled units, clipped to the level-1 box. **This is the only logged action any planner reads** (control f).
* FLAT: the same function with goal level 1, horizon = the goal frame (30 or 32 level-1 steps), no sub-goals, the same S, iterations, learning rate and prior. `last_k` is a parameter (None = production last-2 cost; PLANTEST noted that this cost is not identifying on a stiff planted system, so FLAT with `last_k 1` is available and recommended beside).

## 3. Trajectory: L1 for knots 1-10, L2 decoded for 11-30
`HJEPAStack` has no learned decoder from macro-actions to primitive actions. The harness inverts the macro encoder: `decode_macro` minimises the squared distance between `macro_enc(act_embed(p))` and the planned macro-action (relative to its norm), over primitive actions p, by AdamW from the HOLD sequence, 300 iterations, lr 0.1, clipped to the level-1 box, optional pull to HOLD (`decode_lam`, default 0.0; 0.01 raised the primitive error on a random-init model). It is deterministic and uses no sampling. Level-2 steps `n_op // s2` to `ceil(n_traj / s2) - 1` are decoded and the primitives from knot 11 on are taken (M2b: steps 5-14; ladder: steps 3-9, whose first primitive lies inside the level-1 band and is dropped).
* The relative residual is saved per window (`decode_resid`) and read as a health flag (DECODE-WEAK, draft card). On the planted world it is below 1.4e-7; on a random-init (3,5) model the inversion reaches a residual of about 0.05, so on a trained model the residual is a number to read, not assume. **UNVERIFIED on a trained ladder model.**
* Disclosed: the level-2 tail is conditioned on level 2's own rolled state, not on level 1's end state (SPEC R-TRAJ asks for a continuity term in training). The draft card commits a re-plan of level 2 from level 1's end state as the lever if BAND-SPLIT appears.
* Kinematic model (`hwm_plan.integrate`): unicycle, position by the speed at the step start (as PLANTEST), acceleration clipped to +-0.7 g, yaw rate clipped so that v |w| <= 0.7 g (v floored at 1 m/s in the clip), speed in [0, 40] m/s. The friction coefficient 0.7 is `v8f_mini.project_speed`'s default; the 40 m/s cap and the 1 m/s floor are this file's choices (ESTIMATED). The logged actions are integrated by the same model, so bounds act identically on both sides; the fraction of steps where a bound acted is returned.

## 4. Goal, and what it is computed from (admissibility)
* **HWM-ORACLE / FLAT-ORACLE:** the level-1 latent of the true future frame at the goal frame, encoded to the goal level by the pointwise encoders (`initial_latents`). Computed from the true future: T0 only.
* **HWM-PLAN / FLAT-PLAN:** `PredictedGoal(fn, ...)`: `fn(GoalInputs) -> (B, D)`, where `GoalInputs` holds only the level-1 latents up to NOW, the navigation command and the measured ego history. Nothing else is passed, so the goal path cannot receive a future frame or any situation-classifier output (class posterior, argmax, embedding); the source scan of `test_i` (`v8f_mini.scan_source_for_forbidden`) finds none of the classifier identifiers in `hwm_plan.py`, `hwm_eval.py`, `hwm_data.py`, and the scan is shown to fail on a planted identifier. Inference is vision-only plus the measured ego history (a0 as the HOLD seed, yaw rate not steering angle). A goal head, once trained, must state what its own input is computed from; if a shared trunk feeds both the goal and the situation head, that is a back door to justify, per the 2026-08-03 ruling.
* **Stub:** `stub_goal_fn` returns the current latent (G-STAY). It exists to exercise the plumbing. A number read with the stub is not a HWM-PLAN result, and `RESULT_HWMPLAN_<tag>.json` says so in its meta.
* Nothing is trained here. The goal head's training is a separate card.

## 6. Evaluation (T1-kin) and statistics
* The plan (30 knots, raw acceleration and yaw rate) is integrated by the kinematic model and compared with the logged actions integrated by the same model, from the same v0 (`native_tracks.pt` column 3, as PLANTEST). Bands: 0-2 s = steps 0-9, 2-6 s = steps 10-29, full 6 s = steps 0-29.
* Families, separately, never pooled: **LONGITUDINAL** acceleration MAE and speed error at the band end; **LATERAL** yaw-rate MAE, heading error at the band end, curvature error (steps with logged speed >= 1 m/s, windows without such steps are dropped from that metric and counted), cross-track offset of the plan end from the logged-action trajectory end; ADE beside. **TACTICAL:** not computed, n = 0, the planner emits no manoeuvre decision and no label is wired. LONGITUDINAL headway, time gap and TTC: not computed, n = 0, no lead-agent state is wired. **STRATEGIC: PAUSED (PI item 25).** Missing instruments are work items for the Master Mind (headway needs the lead-gap labels of the eval join; tactical needs a manoeuvre readout).
* Estimator: paired episode-cluster bootstrap, `taniteval.ci.paired_episode_cluster_bootstrap` (B 2,000, seed 0) and `episode_cluster_bootstrap` for single arms. Lower is better; separated-better = the interval lies wholly below 0. Contrasts: HWM-ORACLE and FLAT-ORACLE against HOLD, HWM-ORACLE against FLAT-ORACLE, HWM-PLAN and FLAT-PLAN against HOLD, HWM-PLAN against FLAT-PLAN, HWM-PLAN against PRIOR.
* Inference-seed replicate: every sampling arm is planned at seeds 0 and 1; seed 1 minus seed 0 is reported per family, band and metric. **A contrast smaller than the replicate difference is not an effect.** The bootstrap answers "would another draw of episodes say this?"; it does not answer the training-seed question, which is why both training seeds are required by the draft card (`H-ESTIM-SEED-1`).

**Headway, time gap, TTC (`hwm_eval.lead_band`, `hwm_data.lead_for`).**

**TACTICAL (`hwm_eval.tac_band`, `hwm_eval.tactical_blocks`).**
* **This is a plan-derived decision, not a vocabulary output.** HWM-PLAN emits no manoeuvre token. The planned trajectory and the logged-action trajectory are classified by ONE rule, so the confusion reads what the plan implies (columns) against what was executed (rows).
* The rule is the programme own: `taniteval.four_families.maneuver_kinematics` (path tangent and chord speeds) into `tanitad.refs.refc_tactical.factor_from_kinematics`, lateral {lane_keep, turn_left, turn_right} and longitudinal {brake_stop, steady, accelerate}, collapsed to the 5-way by `COLLAPSE_TABLE`. No manoeuvre gate is reimplemented. Per band the path is knots lo+1..hi re-expressed in the frame of the pose at knot lo, so the 2-6 s band is classified on its own 4 s of path from the 2 s pose (the re-cut to the tactical band of `horizon_review/SPEC_HWM.md`). The thresholds (turn |dyaw| > 0.15 rad, |dv| > 1 m/s, stop v < 0.3 m/s) are those of the 2 s labeller and are applied to each band unchanged, not rescaled; for the 4 s and 6 s bands this makes the classes more sensitive, a property of the rule and not tuned to anything.
* The rule reads path geometry only. Its inputs are the integrated positions of the plan and of the logged actions. The goal that conditioned a plan (a true-future goal for the oracle arms, T0) is not an input of the rule, and nothing the situation classifier produces enters it (`test_k_tactical_rule_is_geometry_only_and_one_rule_for_both_sides`, plus the existing source scan of test i over `hwm_eval.py`). The oracle arms plans stay T0, so their tactical rows are diagnostics, never driving numbers.
* Reported per row and band (`report["tactical"]`): confusion (logged rows, planned columns), per-class recall, precision and n, never-predicted classes, Cohen kappa and agreement, for lateral, longitudinal and the collapsed 5-way. Lateral and longitudinal come first because the 5-way mixes them. Per-window disagreement (`tac_man_dis`, `tac_lat_dis`, `tac_lon_dis`, 0 or 1) feeds the same paired bootstrap, lower is better.
* Still n = 0: `TACTICAL.goal_setting` (the planner exposes no goal or anchor selection).
* Class balance carried from `four_families`: lane_keep and steady dominate, so read kappa and per-class recall, not agreement alone.

**Controls (`test_hwm_plan.py`, group k, each with its regression arm):** (a) a plan equal to the logged trajectory gives zero headway, time-gap and TTC error and a diagonal confusion in every band; a different plan leaves the zero and the diagonal. (b) a plan that brakes in front of a lead raises headway, time gap and minimum TTC (signed differences positive) and reads as brake_stop; an accelerating plan reads the opposite sign. (c) the logged headway equals the planted lead gap exactly (g0 - 4.0 m), and shuffling the lead rows across windows breaks that equality and changes the error. Also: the lead join reads raw frame 2 x NOW and marks a missing row NO_ROW (a shifted key goes red); a window with no lead in the corridor is NaN with a reason, not zero; test g asserts that without a lead block the headway metrics are NaN (n = 0), never zero.

**Shadows, not edits.** `plantest2/plantest2_lib.py` and the ladder tree are untouched. `hwm_plan.shadow_solver(st, plan_ctx, levels, init_fix, cost_wrap)` sets instance attributes on the stack for the length of one plan call and restores the previous ones (also on an exception; a test asserts `rollout` and `_optimize` are gone from `st.__dict__` afterwards): `st.rollout` is wrapped to pass `context = plan_ctx[level - 1]`; `st._optimize` is wrapped so that the i-th call is the level `levels[i]` (the library visits the levels top down: `[3, 2, 1]` hier, `[1]` flat), with a hook that may replace the initial candidates and a hook that may wrap the cost. With `plan_ctx` all 1 and no hook nothing is installed, so the default call is the library's own call.

**G1, `plan_ctx`.** The library's solver calls `st.rollout(level, z0, a)` with context 1; the ladder trains the k-step term with `--wm-ctx 3 3 3`. Context c feeds the predictor the last c states of the free run (`HJEPAStack.rollout`, cap = the predictor's own context, a larger value raises). The read at 1 and at 3 is the asked comparison; the paper-equal arm is a later retrain with `--wm-ctx 1 1 1`, not built here. NOW + frame all lie in the clip and are real (the harness keep rule at F = frame + 1); `run_hwm windows --t3-counts 2 4` prints them and stores them in `windows.npz` meta (`t3_goal_frame_windows`). **G6, vocabulary seeds.** `Vocabulary.default(n)`: the nine (lat, lon) classes of `tanitad.refs.refc_tactical` (lateral lane_keep, turn_left, turn_right; longitudinal brake_stop, steady, accelerate), each a constant raw (acceleration, yaw rate) template: -2.5 / 0.0 / +1.5 m/s^2 and 0 / +0.12 / -0.12 rad/s (**amplitudes are this file's choice, ESTIMATED**). `vocab_macro_seeds` encodes each template (scaled and clipped to the level-1 box, as HOLD) through `st.encode` into the level-2 macro-action units (the macro path reads the actions only, as `hold_seeds_g`; a test shows one reference latent serves all windows). In `shadow_solver(init_fix)` the level-2 call has its initial candidates 1 .. m replaced by these encodings clipped to the level-2 box, m = min(9, S - 1); candidate 0 stays the HOLD seed of level 2, the rest keep their noise. Which classes: if `vocab_scores` (n, 9) is given (per-window T posteriors) the top m by score, else all nine in fixed order. **No per-window T posterior keyed to these windows (clip id, NOW cache step) was found**: the dumps under `TanitAD-artifacts/refcv5cmp/dumps` and `out/*_dump` are per-model open-loop dumps on the reactive models' own evaluation windows and were not joined (UNVERIFIED beyond that look), so the default seeds all nine classes, and the interface for a posterior exists (`--goal-aux` key `vocab_scores`). Flat mode ignores the vocabulary (no level 2). Controls on a planted vocabulary world (nine template classes, the planted actions of a window are its class template plus noise): the best initial level-2 candidate starts under 0.2 times the cost of the HOLD-seeded planner; seeds replaced by HOLD (zero templates) lose that; another vocabulary changes the short-solver plan; with S = 4 the top classes of a posterior beat the fixed order.

**G7, constraints.** `Constraints(v0, v_cap, alat_cap, brake_cap, weight, readout)` per window. `constraint_penalty(level, a, ...)` integrates the candidate through the harness kinematics (differentiable, speed in [0, 40]) and returns, per candidate, the mean over steps of three squared relative exceedances: speed after the step against `v_cap`, lateral acceleration `v |w|` against `alat_cap`, deceleration against `brake_cap`; exactly 0.0 inside every cap. Level 1: the candidate's actions are the primitives (raw = scaled * (2, ch1_scale)). Level 2: the macro-action is decoded by `KinReadout`, a ridge-regression readout fitted on the TRAIN windows (`fit_kin_readout`: macro-action -> mean raw (acceleration, yaw rate) over its c2 steps) because the macro encoder has no learned decoder and the iterative inversion of section 3 cannot sit inside a differentiable cost; its R^2 per channel is stored (`readout.r2`) and read as a flag (READOUT-WEAK below 0.8). The planner adds `weight * penalty` to the cost of levels 2 and 1 (hier) or level 1 (flat). Weight 0.0 with the hooks installed equals no constraints bit for bit (test). **The caps are placeholders**: the harness has no output of T (no vocabulary head is read), so `run_hwm` sets speed cap = v0 + `--constr-v-margin` (5 m/s), lateral 3 m/s^2, braking 4 m/s^2 (all ESTIMATED; the Master Mind sets them, or replaces them by T's constraints, at freeze). The penalty scale against the latent cost is not calibrated (UNVERIFIED): the weight is a sweep parameter. Controls: exact 0.0 inside caps, positive outside, non-zero gradient, both levels reached (spy), a planted goal that asks for a speed above the cap gives a final speed under the free plan's, the penalty cut leaves the plan equal to the free plan.

**G4, G5, the goal head (`goal_head.py`).** Predicts the level-3 goal latent at the level-3 steps j = 1 .. n_steps (default 6, 3 s each, up to 18 s) as `base + delta`: base = the current latent encoded to level 3 by the frozen pointwise encoders (the stub goal), delta = a 2-layer MLP (width 512) of: the last latent, its change over the history, the flattened ego history of (acceleration, yaw rate), the navigation command (5-d), plus a projection of the strategic token added to the first hidden layer (`GoalInputs.ctx`, new field; `ctx_dim` 0 builds the off arm). Target: the encoded true-future level-3 latent at frame NOW + j * s2 * s3, masked where the frame is not in the clip or not real (a masked step contributes exactly 0.0 to the loss and the gradient); loss = mean over valid steps of the per-step mean squared error. The world model (the stack) is frozen and never written; adapter and `wm.proj` are not part of it (the latents come in). `PredictedGoal(fn, ..., level=3)` takes the head's prediction at step T3 as the top goal as it is (a level-3 latent has no level-1 pre-image, so the flat arm refuses it); `run_hwm --goal-head <ckpt>` wires it. Disclosures: (1) the harness window holds only the NOW frame, so the head is trained and used with a history of length 1 (the architecture takes any length); (2) the harness windows carry no navigation command and no strategic token: `--goal-aux` (npz with `eid`, `now`, optional `nav`, `stok`, `vocab_scores`, joined by (eid, now), a window without a row is refused) is the supply, and **stream C's `stok` dump and a per-window nav source are not wired yet**; until then the head runs with nav 0 and no token, and that is not the arm of record; (3) the `build-data` subcommand reads the ClipStore of `wmact_lib` (the eval cache on this machine); its `--split train --store-root` path for box 2 follows `wmact/collect_latents_train.py` and is UNVERIFIED on box 2.
* **Admissibility.** The head's inputs are the five fields of `GoalInputs`. The token enters detached (`DETACH_CTX`, read back by the selfcheck); the source of `goal_head.py` is scanned for the situation-classifier identifiers with the others. If a shared trunk produced the token and the situation head, that is the token's producer's statement to make (stream C): the head's loss puts exactly 0.0 gradient on it, so it cannot steer the producer.
* **Selfcheck (RECOVER-NEXT style, before step 1; `train` refuses to start on a red check).** It reads back what was built (steps, target level 3, context path, detach flag, head parameter count, world-model trainable count 0, source scan), then probes: a non-zero gradient on every head parameter the batch reaches, an exact-0.0 control (all steps masked: loss and every head gradient exactly 0.0), no gradient on the world model, no gradient on the token, the world model's sha256 unchanged by the probe. After the run the world model's sha256 (state dict, parameters and buffers) is asserted bit-equal to its value before. The readback is stored in the head checkpoint.
* **Controls (`goal_head.controls`).** Loss of the head, the stub goal, a shuffled goal (predictions of other windows), a context swap (tokens of other windows) and the head without its token. On the synthetic world where nav and token carry the target: the head is under 0.3 times the stub; shuffled, swapped and token-off are over 2 times the head.
