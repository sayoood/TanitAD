## 1. What it is

`dag_t1_chain.sh` (+ `dag_t1_chain.cmd`, CRLF, for a detached WMI start) is one chain, zero training, zero GPU. For each run in the PREREG's order (DAG s0, DAG-0 s0, DAG s1, DAG-0 s1):

1. waits for box 2's pull `<ART>/M2b_s<S>_<dag|dag0>/` to be COMPLETE by content (`dagt1_pullcheck.py run`);
2. two T1-kin rollouts, plain, CPU, with the registered flags (`--limit causal` decisive, `--limit logged` for K4): the orct1 command, tags `DAG_s0_m10_causal_heads` etc.;
3. the fresh-age T0 eval of the checkpoint on CPU (`v8f_mini_eval.py run --ages fresh`, K4's comparator); box 2 runs only the standard eval (decision O1, enforced: section 3);
4. two lanes (causal; logged + fresh eval) = at most 2 processes. After all four runs: the bank gate (both pulled `bank_s<S>/` directories COMPLETE: 11 files each, bank.json == the gate's declared `--dagger-bank-sha256`; `--expect-shards 4`; a WRONG bank STOPs), `dagt1_manifest.py manifest`, `m2bsha`, then `dag_read.py`. Markers `ZZDAGT1-DONE <n>/8 ..., reader rc <rc>` or `ZZDAGT1-STOP <why>`.

ORC-0 is not re-run. The manifest points its `ORC0` arm at the banked `E:/Projects/TanitAD-artifacts/v8f-orecover-t1/` (rollouts, fresh evals) and `v8f-orecover/` (pull); the chain checks at start that they are present and bound to the banked checkpoints (`dagt1_manifest.py orc0`, MEASURED OK on the real files: `dry_run_real_config.txt`).

Resume = re-run the same command (finished rollouts skipped by a done record naming this checkpoint's sha256 and the tool digest; an earlier incomplete attempt is MOVED to `<OUT>/_incomplete_*`). Pins in `<OUT>/PINS.txt` (tree digest, key files, reader, t1hd/t1k, PREREG, release, T0 inputs) are re-asserted before and after every rollout.
