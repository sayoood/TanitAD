## Blinding: what was NOT run, and why

The pull check stream-hashes every file a run's `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists, `eval_M2b-dag2*_s*.{json,npz}` included, and the old fixture builder copied the real eval JSON. Under the
"do not open, read or hash a DAG2 eval file" constraint the self-test now:
* writes a synthetic stand-in eval in every fixture (the copies of the real eval JSON that the previous run left under `_st/` were overwritten; two 2-byte `{}` fixtures remain);
* does NOT run `P REAL pulled M2b_s0_dag2` and `P REAL pulled M2b_s0_dag20` (default; `--real-pulls` runs them) and does NOT run the `CH real` dry-run scenario;
* pins the chain's default ART, tool tree and PREREG pin by literal text instead (`CH static defaults`), which is what kills C03 now that `CH real` is off.
`dry_run_real_config.txt` is therefore PRE-AMENDMENT evidence (old pin, old path) and was not regenerated. Before the real launch run
`python dag2t1_selftest.py --real-pulls` and `bash dag2_t1_chain.sh --dry-run` once, from a session that is no longer under that constraint.
