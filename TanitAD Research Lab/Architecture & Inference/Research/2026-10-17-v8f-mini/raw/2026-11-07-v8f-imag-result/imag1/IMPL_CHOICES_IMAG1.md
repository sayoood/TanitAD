## 3. Probes and scores
* **Ridge**: lambda 10 on the standardised latent (standardised on the labelled fit frames), LATSEM2's. **Logistic**: C 0.1, lbfgs, max_iter 5,000 (binary and 7-class). Non-convergence is counted, never tuned.
* **MLP** (`imag1_probes.MLPBank`): 128 -> 64 (ReLU) -> out, one network per (target, probe seed in 0, 1, 2), all trained at once in a batched module. Inputs standardised on the fit fold's frames. Continuous: standardised y, MSE; binary: BCE on the logit; 7-class: cross-entropy. Adam lr 3e-3, minibatch 256, **at most 15 epochs**, early stop on an inner split of the fit fold (the fit-fold episodes with `sha256(episode + "|inner") % 5 == 0`, about 20 %; patience 3 epochs; the best weights per network are kept; no refit). The three probe seeds are ensembled: continuous = the mean of the three values; binary = mean probability >= 0.5; 7-class = argmax of the mean softmax. The fit-fold rows with a NaN label do not enter that network's loss.
* **Scores** (higher = better, so every claim is "separated above 0"): continuous **1 - NMSE**, NMSE = sum (pred - y)^2 / sum (y - mean of y on the scoring fold)^2 (scoring-fold mean, pooled by per-episode sums; the card's "Var(y) on the scoring fold"); classes **balanced accuracy minus the fit-fold majority baseline's** (LATSEM2B's `bal_row`). NMSE rows are printed as NMSE = 1 - score. A paired difference is a difference of scores of the same windows.
* IA-1 (capture) scores bank frames of the scoring fold. IA-2 scores windows whose label at the future frame is finite; the denominator mean is that of those windows in the scoring fold.

## 4. Separation: the two-part rule (card, LATSEM2B)
* **Single-latent probe rows** (ENCODED, Oracle readability, MRR): the bootstrap 95 % interval excludes 0 on the claimed side AND the point is beyond the 97.5th percentile of a **reference of R = 199 draws** s = 1..199 (cell by cell). A draw: per model tag, `rng = default_rng(s)` consumed in the order level 1, 2, 3; targets in the order of section 2; fold 0, 1; each call is LATSEM2's `perm_labels` (fit-fold episodes with a finite label put in a random order, each takes the previous episode's label sequence cycled to its length; no episode keeps its own). Ridge, logistic and MLP are refitted on the same permuted labels and scored against the true labels (class baseline = the true fit-fold majority). Calibration: s = 1001..1020, scored as real rows against the same cells' reference.
* **Differences between two latents of the same window** (IMAGINES, WORSE-THAN-COPY, IA-3 gains, control 2): the same bootstrap criterion AND the point beyond the 97.5th (2.5th) percentile of **R = 199 paired sign flips of whole episodes' differences** (rng seed 777, shared). For a gain tested against g0 = 1.5 the sign flips act on the residual per-episode sums (A - g0 D) and the threshold is g0 plus the flipped-residual percentile.
* **MRR**: reference = R = 199 draws in which each query is matched to a random other window of its clip (a derangement, rng 10,000 + s) and the rank of THAT window's encoded k = 1 future is scored.
* The two model seeds are two training runs: every "at both seeds" demands the criterion at s0 and at s1 separately.

## 5. Modules
**IA-0.** Per level and seed: **RankMe** (exp of the entropy of the normalised singular values of the N x 128 matrix, uncentred as defined; the centred version beside) of (a) the encoded bank latents, (b) the predicted k = 1 latents, (c) the encoded true k = 1 futures of the same windows. **MRR**: the query is the predicted k = 1 latent of a window; candidates are the encoded k = 1 true futures of all windows of the same clip (squared L2 in the level's latent; ties get the mean rank); score = mean over windows of 1/rank minus the exact chance mean(H_n / n) over the clip sizes n (a clip with one window is skipped). **UNHEALTHY** = not two-part separated above chance at both seeds; the rows of that level are computed and printed but marked NOT READ and excluded from the triggers.
**IA-1.** The probe on the encoded bank latent of the level, cross-fitted, ridge and MLP, per target, level, seed; the **random-init rows** (the same architecture with untrained weights, `torch.manual_seed(5000 + r)`, r = 0, 1, default init, probes refitted on real labels) are **computed on eval141 only**: TRAIN would need all 141 TRAIN clips re-streamed from box 2 (about 5 min and the ssh path), and TRAIN is the optimistic beside set; their absence on TRAIN is stated in the result. ENCODED = IA-1 separated above 0 (below NMSE 1) at both seeds.
**IA-2.** Start state = frame 0 of the window; horizon k = 1, 2, 3 steps of the level = frames k, 2k, 4k (L1 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.6 s; L2 0.4 / 0.8 / 1.2 s; L3 0.8 / 1.6 / 2.4 s); predicted = `HJEPAStack.rollout` (context 1) from the encoded start state under the TRUE logged actions (raw at L1, macro-action embeddings above), **raw, with no L1 shrinkage** (the card names none; WMACT's c_k exist only for k = 1, 5, 10). Copy-Last = the encoded start latent; Oracle = the encoded true future (same frame). Labels per probe kind: UNREADABLE (Oracle not separated above 0 at both seeds) > IMAGINES (pred minus Copy-Last separated above 0 at both seeds) > WORSE-THAN-COPY (separated below 0 at both) > COPIES. **Decision label = the label both probes (ridge, MLP) give; otherwise `PROBE-DEPENDENT(ridge:X|mlp:Y)`**, which satisfies no trigger. Each probe's own label is printed beside. **Kinematic null** (beside, no bar): speed = v(t) + a h with a = (v(t) - v(t - 0.4 s)) / 0.4 from the logged speed (windows with a prior speed), yaw rate held.
**IA-3.** For every window the logged 16-frame action sequence with, "everything else held": longitudinal channel +1.0 or -1.0 m/s^2 on all frames (lon+, lon-), yaw-rate channel +0.25 or -0.25 rad/s on all frames (lat+, lat-); each of the four sequences goes through `scale_actions`, `HJEPAStack.encode` (macro-actions re-encoded) and `rollout`. Imagined effect = mean over windows of decode(pred under +) - decode(pred under -), decoded by the **same cross-fitted speed and yaw-rate probes** as IA-2 on predicted latents (per probe kind and seed). Physical effect (+ minus -): speed lon: 2.0 x h (h = k x 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 s); yaw rate lat: 0.5 rad/s. **Gain** = imagined / physical (lon: speed probe; lat: yaw-rate probe). **Direction**: the Frobenius cosine between the imagined 2 x 2 Jacobian (rows speed, yaw rate; columns lon, lat; entries = decoded effect per unit contrast, 2.0 or 0.5) and the physical [[h, 0], [0, 1]], per level and horizon, point estimate (interval beside); the cosine in gain units (rows divided by their physical diagonals) is printed beside. Labels per row (probe kind, seed, level, k, channel), in this order: WRONG-SIGN (gain separated below 0) > INERT (not separated above 0) > OVERSHOOTS (gain separated above 1.5) > WEAK (point < 0.5) > ANSWERS (point in [0.5, 1.5] and cosine > 0.7) > **UNRESOLVED** (an addition of this file: separated, in range but cosine <= 0.7, or above 1.5 without separation). A level-horizon-channel label needs both seeds to agree (else `SEEDS-DISAGREE`); the decision label needs both probe kinds to agree.
Beside, no bar: **inverse probe** 2 x 2 (ridge lambda 10 from [z_t, z_{t+k}] to the mean logged raw (a, yaw rate) over the horizon; fitted on real pairs (encoded) or predicted pairs, scored on both; cross-fitted by fold; R^2 per channel, bootstrap interval); **IWM action bank** (16 candidate action sequences per window: the logged one and 15 drawn without replacement from the other windows of the deciding set, rng seed 0; each rolled out from the window's start state; MRR of the logged candidate ranked by squared distance to the encoded true future, against the exact chance mean(1/r), r = 1..16, = 0.2113); **turning share** (|mean logged yaw rate over the 16 frames| > 0.1 rad/s, counted over the deciding windows and over the TRAIN windows, which are a 141-clip sample of M2b's training clips, not its full set; the full count needs box 2's cache and is not claimed).
**IA-6.** TSI_l = mean of (1 - score for continuous, 1 - balanced accuracy for classes) over the fast targets minus the same over the slow ones, per probe kind and seed; the difference TSI(L3) - TSI(L1) by bootstrap, and its reference = the same quantity of the 199 permuted draws. **SPLIT** = separated above 0 at both seeds (per probe kind; the decision needs both probe kinds, else NO-SPLIT only if both say no, else PROBE-DEPENDENT). **Cross-level consistency**: decoded delta-speed = decode(pred) - decode(start) with the level's speed probe, for L1 k=2 vs L2 k=1 (0.4 s) and L2 k=2 vs L3 k=1 (0.8 s); y = the coarser level, x = the finer; OLS slope with intercept pooled over windows, episode-cluster bootstrap; CONSISTENT = interval contains 1 and lower bound > 0 at both seeds.

## 6. Controls (a failure makes the read INVALID; numbers stay in the json as uncontrolled)
1. **Reported, not a bar:** for every continuous probe cell, NMSE on its fit-fold frames against its scoring-fold NMSE; the count of cells where the in-sample value is not below the out-of-sample one.
2. **Real-vs-real.** The "predicted" latent is replaced by the encoded latent of the same clip 0.2 s later (frame f_k + 1 of the same window, the same per-frame bank latent another window of the clip would supply). Pass iff, for every (probe kind, seed, level, k) and every target family with at least one readable (Oracle-separated) target, the **family-pooled gap** (equal-weight mean over the family's readable targets of score(Oracle) - score(offset)) is two-part separated above 0 (bootstrap on the pooled mean; reference = the pooled mean of the paired sign flips), AND no pooled family cell has the offset latent beating the Oracle (the pooled gap separated BELOW 0 at both seeds: "labelled IMAGINES against the Oracle" read at the level where it is used). Pooling is this file's reading of "its gap to the Oracle": a target that does not change within 0.2 s (acceleration, yaw rate) cannot separate an offset latent from the Oracle, and a single row would then violate the control by chance; row-level gaps and row-level offset-beats-Oracle cases are printed beside and decide nothing.
3. **Calibration.** The 20 draws scored as real. **Read before any statistic exists, from a synthetic dry run:** a permuted-label probe reads a fixed latent direction consistently, so the paired sign-flip rule (which assumes a difference centred on 0) may declare more than 5 % of the pred-vs-Copy and gain rows separated on permuted probes. The card fixes that rule and this control; both are applied as written, and a failure makes the read INVALID. To let the Master Mind see which row kind fails, the result prints the shares by row kind, and **beside, deciding nothing,** it recomputes the pred-vs-Copy flags, the gain flags and their labels (and the calibration shares) against a LATSEM2B-style cross-draw reference (the point beyond the 97.5th / 2.5th percentile of the 199 permuted-probe points of that very cell), labelled "cross-draw reference". Families: LON, LAT, TAC, SCENE and HEALTH (MRR); rows counted: IA-1 rows, Oracle rows, both sides of every pred-vs-Copy test (IMAGINES and WORSE), both sides of every gain test (lon to LON, lat to LAT), the MRR rows. Pass iff at most 5 % of rows come out two-part separated in each family. The share declared by the bootstrap interval alone is printed beside.
4. **Synthetic planted effects** (`test_imag1_controls.py`, log `TEST_IMAG1.log`): predicted = future + noise gives IMAGINES, predicted = present gives COPIES, a rollout that moves the decoded speed and yaw rate by gain 1.0 gives ANSWERS and by gain 2.0 gives OVERSHOOTS, through the same functions as the real run (references R = 19 in the smoke). It must pass before the real run.

## 7. Triggers (the card's, evaluated on decision labels of HEALTHY levels; single-probe variants beside)
* E3 CAPLADDER: lead_gap UNREADABLE at every k of every level and all three levels healthy.
* E7 DIVRS: the lateral IA-3 label is WEAK, INERT or WRONG-SIGN at every k of every level (all healthy); the turning share is its design input.
* L1 residual arm: L1 healthy and the longitudinal label is OVERSHOOTS at some k.
* NO-SPLIT: MRATE read for TSI and a level-factor sweep carded. UNHEALTHY level: collapse diagnosis of that level, no arm. L2 / L3 IMAGINES on a readable LON target while L1 (same k) COPIES or is WORSE-THAN-COPY: "agrees with WMACT, points at MRATE's L1 spacing, no new arm". Any outcome: E9 REL is carded from the levels and targets that come out IMAGINES or ANSWERS (listed).

## 8. Execution and projected wall time
CPU only (`CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1`), single-thread worker processes, at most 7 (the box is shared). Per draw: 12 banks (28 s) + logistic/ridge/decoding (estimated 20 to 40 s) = about 50 to 70 s. 220 draws per dataset / 7 workers = about 30 min per dataset, plus the real stage; **projected total about 1.1 h for both datasets, below the 3 h ceiling**, so no cost reduction is made (no row, seed or reference is dropped).
