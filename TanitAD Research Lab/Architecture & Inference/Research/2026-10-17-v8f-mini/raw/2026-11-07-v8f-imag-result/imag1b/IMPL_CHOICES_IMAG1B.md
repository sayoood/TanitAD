## 2. Change 1: one reference for every row, refit under permutation (how each row's statistic is recomputed per draw)

The label test of every row kind is the same two parts: the row's bootstrap 95 % interval excludes the null on the claimed side AND its point is beyond the 97.5th (above) or 2.5th (below) percentile (numpy linear interpolation, `nanpercentile`) of that row's own 199 reference points. ## 3. Change 2: control 2, the not-future
* **Not-future latent.** For window i (episode e_i) the not-future is the encoded true-future latent at the same position (level l, horizon k) of another window j drawn uniformly from the windows of a DIFFERENT episode: `rng = np.random.default_rng(0)`, windows visited in order 0..n-1, `j = rng.choice(flatnonzero(qe != qe[i]))`. One draw per dataset, shared by both seeds, all levels, all k and both probe kinds. The label scored is window i's own label at t + k, so the only difference to the Oracle is content. (No fold restriction on j: the card says a different episode.)
* **Pooled cells.** A cell is (probe kind, seed, level, k, family) over LON, LAT, TAC, SCENE. Its target set is the family's targets whose IA-2 label for that probe kind is not UNREADABLE (Oracle two-part separated above 0 at BOTH seeds); a family with no such target has no cell. Statistic = equal-weight mean over the set of (score(Oracle) minus score(not-future)) (= NMSE(not-future) minus NMSE(Oracle) for continuous targets). Separated (Oracle below not-future) when the pooled bootstrap lower bound > 0 AND the pooled point > the 97.5th percentile of the same pooled mean over the 199 refit reference draws (each draw stores the per-target oracle-minus-not-future point). **Control 2 passes iff there is at least one cell and every cell is separated, at both seeds.** A cell that fails is listed.
* **Beside, no bar (decorrelation curve, real stage only):** per (probe kind, seed, level, k, family) the same pooled gap (Oracle minus latent) for same-clip offsets of +0.2, +0.8 and +1.6 s after the future frame (bank latent of frame f_k + 1, + 4, + 8 of the same window's clip, f_k = k x stride; a window whose bank frame does not exist is dropped from that offset, with Oracle rescored on the same windows; n printed), beside the different-episode not-future gap. Labels stay at t + k. Nothing is read from it.

## 5. Change 4: workers, memory, TRAIN
* **Workers: at most 3** (a resource choice, not a statistical one; the card's cap was 4 and the Master Mind lowered it to 3 on 2026-11-07 after VOE1B's out-of-memory crash at 6 workers). * **Memory gate:** the job first runs `gate_mem_imag1b.py`: free physical memory at job start >= 6 GB (Windows `GlobalMemoryStatusEx`) starts it at once; below 6 GB it waits until `../voe1b/VOE1B_RUN.done` exists (polls every 30 s, 8 h ceiling, then exits non-zero and nothing runs). Free memory was 7.11 GB when this was written (VOE1B re-running with 2 workers).
* **Order and banking:** eval141 (deciding) runs first and `RESULT_IMAG1B.json` is written the moment its analysis ends, before TRAIN starts. TRAIN runs beside, decides nothing, and if it cannot run (free memory below 3 GB at its start, a worker error, an out-of-memory error) it is recorded as `NOT RUN` with the reason in the json and the report, and the run's rc stays 0 when eval141 completed (`IMAG1B_RUN.done` line 1 `rc=0`, line 2 `eval141=OK`, line 3 `train=OK` or `train=NOT RUN: <reason>`). rc = 1 only if eval141 fails.
* **Projected wall time (INHERITED, not re-measured: `HANDOFF_IMAG1.md` states about 47 s per draw at 7 workers):** one draw about 45 s; a dataset is 1 real stage (about 2 min with the random-init rows on eval141) + 219 draws = 73 draws per worker at 3 workers = about 55 min; eval141 is therefore banked about 1 h after the job starts and TRAIN about 55 min later, **about 2 h in all**, plus any memory-gate wait. The added decorrelation and not-future scoring is a few decode calls per draw (under 5 %).
* After rc = 0 the job runs the report, then the figures, then deletes its own new scratch (`F:/TanitAD-scratch/imag1b_*`, each logged with its size in `DELETIONS_IMAG1B.log`). IMAG1's caches are left for the Master Mind.
