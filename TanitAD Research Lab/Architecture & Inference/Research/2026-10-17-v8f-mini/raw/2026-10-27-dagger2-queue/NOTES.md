## 2. Reachability trace (CLAUDE.md: "built, tested, and unreachable from its caller")
Every hop below is run, not read. **No trainer or generator change was needed; no HEADLINE fix.**
* **Generator** `v8f_dagger_bank.py gen` accepts DAG's checkpoint as the rollout policy: `load_ckpt` on both real DAG checkpoints, `check_readable_rows`, config / state-dict equality against the banked M2b (`reach_real_ckpt.json`: **26/26 PASS** on the real DAG checkpoints + `argv.json` against the tip code, with controls that must refuse).

* **The REACHED control in the chain**: after each training the chain reads the trainer's record and requires dag2 `dagger_n / dagger_eligible` in 0.1 +- 0.02 and dag20 **exactly 0**.
