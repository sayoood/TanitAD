# Note N1 to card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD99 (CROPREAD): definitions the card left open

Written on 2026-11-21 after the kits were built and smoke-tested on 2 evaluation and 2 train clips, and before the full stage, any token of the full run, any fit and any read exist. The card is unchanged (7945c62c2a5a101a). No control, bar or name is changed by this note; it fixes what the card's text leaves open, as the kits implement it. What the smoke showed is control values only (c1, c7, p1 on 4 clips); no head was fit.

1. Elevation 0 (card section 3). The frame build of the caches centres its rectified frame on the optical axis and applies no mount pitch, so that frame has no horizon row of its own. Elevation 0 is the centre row of the builder's rectified frame, the optical axis; azimuth 0 is its centre column. The windows of arm D are fixed in the image by these angles and are not shifted per clip. MEASURED in the smoke: the axis is pitched 0.07 to 1.7 degrees below the horizontal in the four clips.
2. Window size. A window is the builder's cylindrical frame with the given horizontal size at 256 by 256 pixels: W32 is 32 degrees wide and 31.2 degrees high, W16 is 16 degrees wide and 15.9 degrees high.
3. Arm B, frames with no lead (card section 3): frames on which the join's own lead rule finds no lead (its range goes to 100 m, wider than the 0 to 60 m of a valid lead gap). Those frames carry the tokens of arm D. Arm B centres both windows on the centre of the lead's box.
4. Control p1 (ii), path curvature of the ego: the heading change over the next 30 m of the ego's own track divided by the arc, left positive; azimuth left positive. Known weakness, stated before the full run and left as frozen: at the clause's thresholds a part of the azimuth comes from the position in the lane, whose sign is not tied to the curvature, so the clause can fail with windows that are on the lead. By the card's map this would leave arm B unopened and arm D untouched.
5. Control p1 (iii), bottom of the box: the centre of the bottom face at the end of the box's long axis that is nearest to the camera.
6. Control p1 (i), clock: the boxes are taken at the builder's frame times (the times of the cached frames).
7. Replacement rule (card section 2): a train clip counts as staged when its video, its timestamps, its ego track and its obstacle labels are all fetched.
8. Control c1: the card's two clauses give the control's pass. In addition the queue requires that the window route with the full frame's parameters gives the builder's frames exactly for the c1 clips; that check can stop the queue and opens nothing.
9. Head (card section 5), choices the card does not name: GELU in the MLP; the position vectors and the query start from N(0, 0.02); the warm-up is linear per step over the first epoch, the cosine per step; the epoch kept is the first with the lowest loss on the selection clips; for control c5 the label gaps are permuted within the fit frames and within the selection frames separately.
10. Dump (card section 6): a row is valid when the present reader's dump marks it valid and the head has an output (head H5 has none on frames 0 to 3 of a clip, which the present dump does not mark valid either).
11. Run: the data side writes under `/workspace/v8proof/cropread/enc_out/`; fits, dumps and the read use that folder as their root. Fits start only with 28 GB of memory free on the pod.
