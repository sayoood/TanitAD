
\paragraph{Does more data lift the read of the lead? (row 135, H-V8F-PROOF-135).} Card ADD45 trains the same token probe on the corpus clips alone (4,512 clips) and on the union of the corpus and the 10k clips (14,512 clips), four seeds each, at matched steps, and reads the lead gap on the 429 scored windows of the 48 evaluation episodes (\tier{T0}: a probe on cached tokens says what the tokens allow, not what a model does with them). The first read of record stopped on a control of the training logs for the five probes of the union list. From the logs alone: at matched steps a union probe sees each clip about 1.25 times, its lead loss falls from 0.75 at the first step to about 0.40 within a hundred steps and then stays level, so a clause that asks the last thousand steps to lie below the first thousand compares two stretches of one level. The clause was given a form anchored at the first step (the last thousand steps at most 0.80 of the first step; an untrained probe reads 1.00) in a note written before any outcome was opened, and the note says that this was done after the control details were seen. The re-read passes every control. The gap R2 of the union probe minus that of the corpus probe is $-0.0752$ [$-0.2200$, $+0.0468$], with none of the four seeds above 0, and its gap error is 1.15 times that of the corpus probe: the read is flat with scale. The union probe with twice the steps reaches the corpus probe (gap R2 0.719 against 0.712) and does not pass it. Beside, without a name of the card: the presence read is higher for the union probe at each seed (AUROC 0.899 to 0.910 against 0.860 to 0.883). More clips of this kind therefore do not lift the gap read through pooled tokens; with row 134 the limit of the reader-fed form is placed at what the pooled tokens carry about the lead, the closing speed first. The lever that remains for the gap read needs camera frames or a new encode and goes to the PI as a card with its cost.
