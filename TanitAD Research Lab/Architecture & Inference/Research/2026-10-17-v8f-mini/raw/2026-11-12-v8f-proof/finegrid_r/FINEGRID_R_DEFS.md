# FINEGRID_R_DEFS: what card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD43 leaves open, and how the tools fill it

Written 2026-11-19 on the dev box (synthetic data only). The card is not edited. Tier T0 (a diagnostic on logged frames, never driving performance). LONGITUDINAL (distance keeping) is the family this lever serves and is not measured by this read; LATERAL, TACTICAL: not measured; STRATEGIC: NOT COMPUTED (a token probe reads no strategic quantity; n = 0). Items marked REVIEW are the lead's to confirm before the first real number.

## Tool (`tok_probe_gr.py`, made from `finegrid/tok_probe_g.py` by `make_tok_probe_gr.py`, 12 asserted edits, each old text exactly once)
* **R1 (REVIEW, the main finding). The two processes would NOT see the same windows with the unchanged tool.** The trainer sizes the clip store by the token count (`v8f_mini_train.py:2736`: 174 clips at 40 tokens, 43 at 160 on the pilot's data, longest clip 105 frames; pilot `train_meta.json` says lru 43) and the sampler chunk is `max(4, min(48, lru - 2))` (line 2851): chunk 48 at 4 by 10, 41 at 8 by 20. A different chunk changes the pools the windows are shuffled in, so control r1 would fail by construction. Fix: lever `--sampler-chunk-like GRID` (default empty = the original expression). Process A and the control arm TK16SH_s2 pass `--sampler-chunk-like pool8x20` (chunk 41, computed from the run's own longest clip, not typed in); process B is unchanged from the pilot, so the pilot's F arms and the new F arms share one sampler. Consequence: the new 4 by 10 arms see another window order than the banked TK16 / TK64 of card ADD27 (chunk 48); the banked arms are not pooled and are read only for control g7. Both choices are logged (`sampler_chunk`, `lru_trainer` in `train_meta.json` and the train-log header).
* **R2 Control r1 log.** `r1_batches.json` in each train folder: per batch, in order, a sha256 prefix of the (clip, t) pairs and of the whole item (with the two age draws), for the first min(200, total) batches; `tok_probe_gr.py r1 --a --b --out` compares two folders (exit 0 equal, 2 not). A separate file, not a train-log record, so the train-log format the readers parse is unchanged. Logging only: the batches pass through the wrapper unchanged.
* **R3 p5 for permuted-label arms.** The train log of `tok_probe_g.py` already carries the parts (`lead_sup_bce_mean`, `lead_sup_gap_mean`, `lead_sup_clos_mean`; checked on the real log `finegrid/in_pod/p3`), so the log format is not changed. `check_log` reads an arm with SH in its name on presence + gap (last 1,000 steps below the first 1,000; gap-labelled frames above 0; complete) and reports the closing part beside; every other arm keeps the original form. The reader computes p5 itself from the logs (`p5_form`).
* **R4** Everything else (probe, optimiser, loss line, g2, extract) is the pilot's text. Control g1 compares the changed tool with `tok_probe_g.py` on the default path; it needs torch and is in `run_checks_pod.py` (NOT RUN on the dev box).

## Pod script (`pod_finegrid_r_run.sh`)
* **P1** 32 commands: g2, train A, 16 extracts, train B, 8 extracts, r1, p3 A + extract, p3 B + extract. Extracts take `--splits eval` only. Slot wait (the pilot's: under 3 GPU compute processes and over 28 GB free on two probes 45 s apart, poll 60 s, 10 h) before each of the 30 GPU steps, so about 25 min of probe gaps in all (ESTIMATED).
* **P2** Markers: `FINEGRID_R_STARTED.txt` by noclobber (a second start exits 3; for a new run give a new `OUT`), `FINEGRID_R_DONE.txt` (tool sha256, 26 checkpoint sha256), `FINEGRID_R_STOP.txt`. A train rc of 2 (p5 not met) goes on; the reader applies p5. No rm, no mv, no process-name test.
* **P3 Volume.** Eval states only. From the pilot's file sizes: eval state F16 73 MB, TK16 about 18 MB (a quarter of the tokens), F64 292 MB; head files 0.8 to 2.8 MB. 8 x (18 + 73) + 8 x 73 + 2 controls + checkpoints and logs: about 1.5 GB ESTIMATED (the pilot's five state sets with train-probe states were 7.65 GB).
* **P4 GPU memory, 16 arms in one process (ESTIMATED, not measured).** The arms train one after the other on the shared batch; one 4 by 10 batch of 64 x 16 frames x 40 x 1,024 in float32 is 168 MB, the layer-norm copy and the 128-wide hidden layer add about 0.2 GB, parameters and Adam state per arm are under 3 MB: a few GB in all, well under the 3-job budget. At 8 by 20 four times that (under 8 GB); eight arms are not more than four. Host memory: the tool's own estimate (refused above 8 GB) is unchanged.
* **P5 Time (ESTIMATED).** Pilot: 73 min for five arms in one slot on the 8 by 20 grid; banked 4 by 10 run 25 min for four arms. Process A (16 arms) 40 to 100 min, B (8 arms) 60 to 110 min, p3 2 x 6 min, 26 extracts, probe gaps: 3 to 5 pod-h. The card says 2 to 3 (REVIEW: it may be low).

## Reader (`finegrid_r_read.py`; imports the pilot reader `finegrid_read_n1.py`)
* **D1 Per arm** M1, M2, M4, gap MAE: the pilot reader's functions (`read_arm`, `summarize`), eval windows only (`do_train` false).
* **D2 Two-level bootstrap (REVIEW).** Per seed index and pair a point difference and the vector over the 2,000 episode draws (`boot_diff`; equal to the pilot's `pair_r2`, `pair_mae`, `pair_m1` rows, a test asserts it). Outer: 8 seed indices with replacement per replicate (rng 0). Inner: **one** episode draw per replicate shared by the chosen seeds (the 48 episodes are the same for every seed: a crossed design), the card's order "seeds, then episodes". An independent draw per chosen seed is computed beside (`per_seed`) and names nothing; it gives a narrower episode part.
* **D3 Outcome rule** as the card: READS-GAP-R = both M2 intervals above 0 and at least 6 of 8 seeds above 0 in each and mean gap MAE of F16 at most 0.90 times the lower of the TK16 and TK64 means; WORSE-R = both intervals below 0; else FLAT-R; READS-CLOSING-R = the M1 rule without the MAE clause. REVIEW: the card does not say whether "mean over the seeds" of the MAE is the mean of the per-arm MAE (used) or the mean of paired differences; they are the same number.
* **D4 Controls** all gated: g1, g3, g4, g6 forward (from `controls_devbox_r.json`, which must carry the sha256 of the tool the pod trained with: `tool_sha_matches_the_devbox_controls`); g2; g5 and r2 from the state record of each of the 26 arms (grid, 40 or 160 tokens, tokens seen in training, state width, width, `ln`, step 19,164 or 2,000); g6 for both control arms (closing R2 at most +0.02); g7 (all reader controls on every new arm, the TK reproduction of the banked arms at `--g7-tol 1e-5`); p5 (R3 above); r1 (equal sequences); r3 (run on synthetic outputs derived from the real labels: gain at every seed read as separated, two of eight not, eight identical pairs exactly 0). The p3 pair (TK16SH vs F16SH r1) is not gated: the card names the two main processes.
* **D5 Withholding** If any gated control fails: `names` is null, no paired difference, no two-level read, no beside comparison is computed or written; per-arm reads are written.
* **D6 Beside** std over seeds of M2 per type, gap MAE by gap range, M4 per arm, the pilot's seeds 0 and 1 (`--pilot-f-dir`, pairs G16 and G16w, not pooled), the distance of each deciding comparison from its bar (the pilot's note N1 guard is NOT applied as a rule; REVIEW).
