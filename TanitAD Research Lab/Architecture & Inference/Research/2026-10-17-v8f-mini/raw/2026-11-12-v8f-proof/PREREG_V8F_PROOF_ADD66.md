# Card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD66 (STRATROLL10K): the strategic head's route checkpoints along the model's own rollout, read from the banked dumps; no new rollout, no training

Card of the v8F proof study, written by the coordinator fork after the read of card ADD63 (row 119) and while the runs of card ADD65 are on the pod, before any tool of this card exists and before any strategic number of any dump has been computed (time of the freeze: the line of this card in `TIMES_NOTE.md`; hash in the sidecar file). Before the freeze the lead read from one banked dump only its structure: the field names and shapes, the tick times, the hold pattern of the strategic head and one row of raw checkpoints to see their frame. Tier: the first tick of each window is T0; the later ticks are T1-kin (kinematic rollout on the model's own actions; logged camera frames; the lead does not react); never driving performance.

## 1. Why

Every row of this line since card ADD46 reports STRATEGIC along the rollout as NOT COMPUTED (n = 0, no instrument), by note N1 of card ADD46. The programme's rule is that a family without an instrument is a work item. The pinned tool already writes what an instrument needs: with `--dump-heads` every replan tick of every banked run carries the strategic head's three route checkpoints (x, y, heading at the 30, 50 and 80 m arcs), the pose of the frame the strategic head ticked in, and its age. Measured structure of the banked dumps (model seed 0 of `R0`; the reader asserts it for every run): three ticks per window at steps t0, t0 + 10 and t0 + 20; the strategic head's output held at those ticks was computed at steps t0 (age 0), t0 + 6 (age 4) and t0 + 18 (age 2). So the output read at the first tick is a T0 output (logged history only) and the outputs read at the second and third ticks were computed 1.2 s and 3.6 s into the model's own rollout: on logged camera frames, with the ego history the rollout produced and the route countdown dead-reckoned from its own motion. Windows start every 4 steps inside an episode.

The inference-side candidate of cards ADD63 and ADD65 is stated on three families. Whether the readout or the rule moves the fourth is not known.

## 2. Question

(a) Does the strategic head's route goal keep its T0 quality when it is computed on the model's own rollout? (b) Does the state readout (pinned, causal with all of the own acceleration, readout K) change it? (c) Does the rule-driven rollout change it, in the programme's convention and in the candidate of card ADD65?

## 3. The instrument (study instrument of this fork; nothing of the programme's evaluation convention changes)

Inputs per run: the tick file of the run (`ticks_*_heads.npz`: `s_ckpt`, `s_pose`, `s_ckpt_w0`, `pose_w0`, `age_s`, `j`, `t0`, `tick`, `win`, `ci`) and the logged 10 Hz pose track of each clip as the pinned tool loads it. The reader reads no model and runs on the pod CPU.

* The strategic output of a tick row: the raw checkpoints `s_ckpt` in the frame of `s_pose` (the executed pose at step j minus `age_s`), put into the world frame.
* The anchor: the logged step whose logged position is nearest to the position of `s_pose`, searched within 25 steps of step j minus `age_s`. At the first tick the executed pose is the logged pose, so the anchor is t0 with distance 0.
* The route label, by the rule of the programme's data builder (`v8f_mini_data.py`, route checkpoints `rc`) applied from the anchor: the cumulative arc over the logged positions after the anchor; for each arc A in 30, 50, 80 m the first logged step at which the arc reaches A; the label is that logged position and that logged heading. A row is valid at an arc only if the logged track reaches that arc.
* Cells per tick k in 0, 1, 2 and arc A, never pooled over arcs: `pos` (m), the distance between the checkpoint and the label (the T0 checkpoint position error of the other session's evaluator, carried along the rollout); `lat` (m), the distance from the checkpoint to the logged route (the polyline of the logged positions from the anchor on; valid if the nearest point is not the last logged point); `along` (m), the arc along the logged route from the anchor to the foot of the checkpoint, minus A; `head` (deg), the absolute difference between the checkpoint heading in the world frame and the label heading.
* The T0 reference for an output computed on the rollout. The frames t0 + 6 and t0 + 18 are not window starts, but window starts lie 2 steps before and after each: REF1 of window w is the mean of the first-tick cell of windows w + 1 and w + 2 of the same episode (frames t0 + 4 and t0 + 8), REF2 the mean of the first-tick cell of windows w + 4 and w + 5 (frames t0 + 16 and t0 + 20). A window enters a rollout read only if its cell and both reference cells are valid and the window starts are 4 steps apart; the counts are reported.

Estimator of every difference: paired episode-cluster bootstrap on the same windows (episodes drawn with replacement, B 2,000, fixed seed; both model seeds' windows of a drawn episode stay together), pooled over the two model seeds, with the difference per model seed beside it. A cell reads BETTER when the difference is below 0 at both model seeds and its pooled interval lies below 0, WORSE when above 0 at both and the interval lies above 0, else NOT-SEPARATED. The bootstrap answers another draw of episodes; the two model seeds are the only replicate for training variance; the rollout does not sample.

## 5. Reads and names (fixed here)

Named cells: `pos` at 30, 50 and 80 m. `lat`, `along` and `head` are reported beside for every pair. The family rule over the three named cells of a pair: BETTER if all three read BETTER, WORSE if all three read WORSE, NOT-SEPARATED if none reads BETTER or WORSE, else MIXED.

* ROLL (question a), in `R0`: ROLL1 = `pos` at the second tick minus REF1, ROLL2 = `pos` at the third tick minus REF2, per arc. `STRATROLL10K-STRATEGIC-DEGRADES-ON-ROLLOUT` if ROLL2 reads WORSE at two or three of the arcs; `STRATROLL10K-STRATEGIC-HOLDS-ON-ROLLOUT` if neither ROLL1 nor ROLL2 reads WORSE at any arc; else `STRATROLL10K-STRATEGIC-MIXED-ON-ROLLOUT`. The size is quoted in metres and as a share of the first-tick level, per arc. The same two reads are reported beside for every other run.
* READOUT (question b), at the third tick, same windows and frames: K = `R0G0X` minus `R0`; G1 = `R0C10` minus `R0`. `STRATROLL10K-K-STRATEGIC-BETTER-THAN-PINNED`, `-WORSE-THAN-PINNED`, `-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-PINNED` or `-MIXED` by the family rule; the same four endings for `STRATROLL10K-CAUSAL-STRATEGIC-`. Beside: `R0G0` and `R0Bk10` minus `R0`; all pairs at the second tick.
* RULE (question c), at the third tick: per form, the convention run with the rule minus `R0` (`RLc`, `RQ50`): `STRATROLL10K-<form>-RULE-STRATEGIC-BETTER`, `-WORSE`, `-NOT-SEPARATED` or `-MIXED`. Beside: `RLcF5`, `RQ50F5` minus the tick-only run.
* CANDIDATE, at the third tick, if the runs of card ADD65 are admitted: per form, the whole candidate (`RLcKF5`, `RQ50KF5`) minus the convention run (`RLc`, `RQ50`): `STRATROLL10K-<form>-CANDIDATE-STRATEGIC-BETTER-THAN-CONVENTION`, `-WORSE-THAN-CONVENTION`, `-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-CONVENTION` or `-MIXED`. If they are not admitted these names are not given and the reason is stated.
* Reported without a bar: the level of every cell per run, tick, arc and model seed with its count of valid rows; the distance from the executed pose to its anchor at the second and third tick per run (how far the car is from the logged track when the strategic head ticks).

LONGITUDINAL, LATERAL and TACTICAL are not read by this card (they are the reads of the cards that ran these rollouts). This card reads STRATEGIC only: the route goal of the strategic head. A strategic decision between routes is not scored (the route command is an input of the model at training and at inference, and no window of this evaluation set carries a label of a route choice): n = 0 for that part, stated in every row that uses this instrument.

## 6. Controls (all must pass; else nothing is named)

* s1 (frames, known value): the reader's own transform of `s_ckpt` with `s_pose` into the window-origin frame equals the tool's `s_ckpt_w0` on every row (largest difference at most 1e-3 m and 1e-3 rad).
* s2 (anchor, known value): on every first-tick row the anchor is t0 and its distance is 0 (at most 1e-6 m).
* s3 (label rule, known value): where the route label `rc` of the window frame can be read from the programme's own label code for the evaluation clips, the reader's first-tick label equals it at every valid arc (largest difference at most 1e-3 m and 1e-3 rad) and the validity flags agree. If that label cannot be produced on the pod without a new model pass, the control is reported NOT COMPUTABLE with the reason, and s4 carries the label rule alone; this is stated in the row.
* s4 (synthetic known values, before any dump is read): on a straight logged track the labels lie at the first logged steps beyond 30, 50 and 80 m and a checkpoint placed on the label reads `pos` 0, `lat` 0, `head` 0; a checkpoint moved 1.5 m sideways reads `lat` 1.5 m and `pos` 1.5 m; a checkpoint moved 4 m along the track reads `along` larger by 4 m and `lat` 0; on a circle of known radius the label positions equal the analytic ones to the step of the track; a track that ends before 80 m reads that arc invalid. A planted fault (the label taken one arc too far) must fail the check.
* s5 (first tick does not depend on readout or rule, known value): at each model seed the first-tick cells of every run equal those of `R0` (largest difference 0.0).
* s6 (structure): every run has 2,399 windows and 7,197 tick rows with the tick steps and ages of section 1; 141 episodes.
* s7 (reference bias, known value, measured on first-tick rows of `R0` only): the mean of the first-tick `pos` of windows w and w + 2 minus that of window w + 1 (a wider bracket than the one the reads use), per arc: its pooled interval contains 0, or its absolute value is at most 2 % of the first-tick level at that arc. If s7 fails, ROLL is not named (the other reads pair runs on the same frames and do not use the reference).
* s8 (estimator): a pair with itself reads exactly 0 with interval [0, 0]; a swapped pair reads the negative.
* s9 (inputs): the sha256 of every tick file read is recorded; for the runs of card ADD65, its controls passed.

## 7. What follows (committed before the data)

* All controls pass: from then on the rows of this line report STRATEGIC along the rollout with this instrument, beside the first-tick statement of note N1 of card ADD46, as a study instrument of this fork. The instrument and its numbers go to the other session and the PI with the decision card; adopting it as the programme's STRATEGIC read at T1-kin is their decision.
* `STRATEGIC-HOLDS-ON-ROLLOUT`: the route goal of the strategic head does not depend measurably on the model's own rolled history at this cycle; the dose of card ADD63 is then a matter of the layers below it.
* `STRATEGIC-DEGRADES-ON-ROLLOUT`: the strategic head too responds to the model's own rolled history; reported with its size per readout. It is then a second measured case for the training lever of the other session, and the READOUT read says whether readout K reduces it.
* `K-STRATEGIC-WORSE-THAN-PINNED` or a candidate that reads `WORSE-THAN-CONVENTION`: reported as written; the proposal of readout K in the decision card then carries a cost on the fourth family and says so.
* `K-STRATEGIC-BETTER-THAN-PINNED` or `NOT-SEPARATED`: the statement of card ADD63 (readout K against the pinned readout) is completed on the fourth family with these numbers.
* A control fails: nothing is named; the tool is repaired, logged in `TIMES_NOTE.md`, and the read is repeated; outcome numbers of a failed read stay unread until the repair is written.

## 8. Limits

The camera frames are the logged ones, taken from the logged pose and not from where the rollout has driven the car: the strategic head's inputs differ from T0 only through the ego history and the dead-reckoned route countdown; a tier with re-perception could read differently. The route label is the one logged human path. The label is anchored at the nearest logged position, so a car that has left the logged track sideways is scored against the route point at the same arc; the anchor distance is reported. The T0 reference of ROLL is an interpolation over 0.4 s on each side, checked by s7 on a wider bracket. Two model seeds of one training recipe; 141 episodes are read again with no adjustment for the repeated reads. The named cells and the tick of the named reads were fixed here without any strategic number in view; the readout and rule runs were chosen knowing their results on the other three families.
