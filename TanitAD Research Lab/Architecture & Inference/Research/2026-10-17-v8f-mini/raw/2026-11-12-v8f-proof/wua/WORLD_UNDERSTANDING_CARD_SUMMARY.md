## What the Card says

The flagship's world model carries WHERE the lead and the nearest agents are (position beyond ego motion: agents at L1, the lead's gap at L2 / L3), but not HOW they move (closing, relative acceleration and the next 2 s of the gap are absent at every level, also from 1 s of state history and from the encoded true future). Its imagination copies the scene and rolls the ego: only ego speed beats copy-last, and the imagined lead gap moves the wrong way under the ego's own acceleration. The planner nevertheless reads the lead region of the camera tokens directly (A5), so the lead enters the plan through perception, not through imagination. The plan's expectation (HYPOTHESIS: ego passes, lead and agents at most partial, imagination copies) held for imagination; it was too pessimistic for position: the lead gap is ENCODED above the 0.10 bar at L2 and L3.
