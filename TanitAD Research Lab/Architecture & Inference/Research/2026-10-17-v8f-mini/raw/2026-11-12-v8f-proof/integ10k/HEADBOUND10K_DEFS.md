# HEADBOUND10K_DEFS.md -- what each read, control and name of card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD67 is computed from

Tier of every rollout number: T1-kin (kinematic rollout on the model's own actions; the lead does not react and follows its logged track); never driving performance. Reader: `read_headbound10k.py` (numpy; for STRATEGIC the module `strat_roll_read.py` of card ADD66, unchanged, same folder). No training. The tools have not been run on any data of the card.

## Tools

* Wrapper `v8f_t1_eval_reader_rule_rzgfh.py` = `v8f_t1_eval_reader_rule_rzgf.py` (sha256 prefix 969936595512ef80, asserted) plus `--kappa-max K` (written by `make_headbound10k.py`, asserted text edits; 80 lines added, 4 changed).
* The bound (`bound_heading`): after the chosen readout returned (xy, psi, v) for the 2m frames of the executed steps, frame f in order, from `prev` = psi0 (the heading before the block) and `xy_prev` = knots[0]: d = wrap(psi[f] - prev), lim = K x |xy[f] - xy_prev|; if |d| <= lim the output is psi[f] itself, else prev + sign(d) x lim (wrapped into [-pi, pi)); prev = output, xy_prev = xy[f]. xy and v are not changed. K must be finite and above 0 and needs `--readout` (pinned allowed, so the readout log with the fields of the bound is written); absent, the wrapper equals the parent bit for bit.
* psi0 and the output: in the pinned tool (`v8f_t1_eval.py`) `commit_plan` stores the returned psi as the executed heading (track column 2, line 191) and `pose = track[2j]` gives `float(pose[2])` as psi0 of the next commit (line 189), so the bounded heading is what the next plan is laid in and what the yaw history reads.
* Readout log fields (new): kappa_max, frames_total, frames_bound_acted, max_excess_rad, max_abs_diff_where_not_acted, max_abs_diff_xy, max_abs_diff_v, by_speed_bin (lt2, 2to8, gt8 by the frame speed v).
* Tool check `check_head_bound.py` (control h4, pod CPU, synthetic plans): jitter around a standing car, arc of 10 m at K 0.2 (untouched, bit for bit), the same arc at K 0.05 (every frame bounded, step = K x distance), K 1e9 equals the parent on 203 plans, the log counters, the refusals, planted faults (bound not applied; wrong sign) both caught. `check_rule_every_rzgfh.py` and `check_acc_gain_rzgfh.py` are copies of the checks of card ADD65 with the wrapper name as the only change.
* Queue `pod_headbound10k_queue.sh` (waits for `CAND10K_DONE.txt` or `_STOP.txt`; `WAIT_MAX` 86400 s): takes the sha256 of the files of the two runs of card ADD65 into `small_hb/REUSED_CAND_TAKEN.txt`; the three tool checks (marker `hb6check`); controls `I10R0HbID` (pinned, K 1e9; marker `hb1cmp` against banked `I10R0_s0`) and `I10R0KHbID` (readout K, 1e9; `hb2cmp` against `I10R0G0X_s0`); then at both model seeds `I10R0Hb20`, `I10R0KHb20`, `I10R0KHb10`, `I10RLcKF5Hb20`, `I10RQ50KF5Hb20` (twelve rollouts in all). Slot rule MAXGPU 3 and MINGB 28 as the parent.
* Chain `chain_headbound10k.sh`, pod read `podread_headbound.sh` (the two pairs HC are added only if `READ_CAND10K.json` has controls_all_pass true), waiter `wait_podread_headbound.sh` (results to `read_headbound/pod/`).
* Reused: `I10R0`, `I10R0G0X`, `I10RLc`, `I10RQ50` (22 files hashed on the pod before the queue: `HEADBOUND10K_REUSED.json`, list in `HEADBOUND10K_REUSED_FILES.txt`); `I10RLcKF5`, `I10RQ50KF5` of card ADD65 (hashed by the queue after that queue ended).

## Reads (paired episode-cluster bootstrap, pooled over the two model seeds, per-seed values beside)

* HP = `I10R0Hb20` - `I10R0` (PINNED); HK = `I10R0KHb20` - `I10R0G0X` (K); HC = `I10RLcKF5Hb20` - `I10RLcKF5` (LABEL-CAND) and `I10RQ50KF5Hb20` - `I10RQ50KF5` (READER-CAND). Each: U, C, F and every family cell.
* Beside, names nothing: `I10R0KHb10` - `I10R0KHb20` (dose); `I10R0KHb20` - `I10R0`; `I10RLcKF5Hb20` - `I10RLc`; `I10RQ50KF5Hb20` - `I10RQ50`.
* Family rule per pair: LONGITUDINAL speed_mae_mps and along_mae_m; LATERAL heading_mae_deg and yaw_rate_mae_degps; TACTICAL ade_tac_m; STRATEGIC `pos` at 30, 50, 80 m at the third tick (`pair_read`, ticks=(2,)). BETTER if every named cell reads BETTER, WORSE if every cell reads WORSE, NOT-SEPARATED if none reads either, else MIXED. Never pooled.
* Names per pair P (PINNED, K, LABEL-CAND, READER-CAND): `HEADBOUND10K-<P>-<FAMILY>-BETTER|WORSE|MIXED|NOT-SEPARATED` and `HEADBOUND10K-<P>-U-LOWER|HIGHER|NOT-SEPARATED` (one sign at both model seeds and the pooled interval on that side of 0).
* The consequences fixed in section 6 of the card are listed mechanically per pair under `reported_no_bar.consequences_fixed_by_card_section_6_per_pair` (BETTER on a family, WORSE on none, U not higher: proposal to the PI; all NOT-SEPARATED: note; WORSE or U higher: as written; the dose reading worse: the point where the bound starts to cut turns a car makes). The read itself changes nothing.
* Reported without a bar: per run the share of executed frames where the bound acted, by the speed bins below 2, 2 to 8, above 8 m/s (readout logs); the heading offset from the logged heading at the third tick by the same bins for every run and its partner (as `strat_roll_decomp.py`: executed `s_pose` heading minus the logged heading at step j - age_s, speed v0 of the tick row, both seeds pooled).

## Controls (all must pass; else no name, no level, no difference)

* e1 the pod read reproduces the banked dev-box read of card ADD46 leaf for leaf. h1, h2 `CMP_R0HbID.txt` and `CMP_R0KHbID.txt` read 141 files, 0 differing.
* h3 per run, from its readout log: kappa_max as given, largest excess of a heading step over K x distance at most 1e-9 rad, max_abs_diff_where_not_acted, max_abs_diff_xy and max_abs_diff_v exactly 0.0, speed bins add up, form, 10 steps, readout fields (pinned, or causal_g gain 0 path_exec for K with the speed and acceleration differences at most 1e-6), rule logs as the parent.
* h4 the three tool-check outputs end with their `all hold: True` line and carry the sha256 of the wrapper of the runs. h5 the first 10 executed positions of every run equal those of the banked run of the same rule form and model seed (`I10R0` for the no-rule runs; `I10RLcKF5` / `I10RQ50KF5` for the candidate runs, only if card ADD65 is admitted; else not computable and recorded).
* h6 counts: 141 dump files per run, 2,399 windows, 642 lead windows, tick rows = windows x 3, later-look rows for the rule-every-5 runs. h7 the reused files are the files hashed before the queue (and the ADD65 runs as taken by the queue). h8 reader controls of the headway and family readers, and controls s1 s2 s3 s4 s5 s6 s9 of card ADD66 on the runs read (s5: first-tick cells equal those of `R0` at the same model seed). h9 checkpoint sha256 and one wrapper sha256 everywhere.

## What was NOT exercised

* The wrapper has not been run on the model, the cache or the eval windows; the reader has not been run on any data (import and syntax only); the queue (DRY only), chain and waiter have not been started. With a huge K the bound reproduces the readout only where the distance between two frames is not exactly zero while the readout's heading still changes; a case of that kind would show as a difference in h1 or h2.
