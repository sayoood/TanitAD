## 1. Control p1, second part (constant gap of 100 m, note N1 A2): NOT met as written

MEASURED: 140 of 141 episode files equal `off` bit for bit, 1 differs. Cause: 2 of 3,012 active ticks (one clip, third tick of two windows) on which the model's own plan has a speed of 95 and of 257 m/s at the 2.0 s knot (26 and 66 m/s at 1.0 s). For such a plan 100 m is less than TAU times the speed, and the rule slows it as written. The tool does what the rule says; the constant was chosen for plans of real speeds. The first part of p1 (candidate set {1.0}, no fallback) is met: 141 of 141 files equal.
* **Read form p1b:** the constant gap is 1e9 m (above TAU times any speed); the dump equals `off` bit for bit.
* Beside, a property of the harness that this control exposed: the pinned T1-kin tool executes a plan of any speed. The number of ticks whose plan exceeds 60 m/s at a check horizon is reported per arm with every read of the card.

## 2. Control p4 (actions of the logged path against the logged actions): NOT met as written; one tool change

MEASURED on the full set (2,399 windows, 10 steps): correlation 0.979 on the acceleration channel and 0.105 on the yaw channel (bar 0.99 each); median absolute difference 0.034 m/s2 (bar 0.1, met) and 0.0016 rad/s.
* Acceleration, cause measured: the first step of a sequence reads 0.946, steps 2 to 10 read 0.995, the mean over a window 0.9993. The first action is the difference between the measured speed at the tick and the first speed that the pinned tool rebuilds from the knots; two spellings of the speed meet there. The same seam is in every track the pinned tool executes, so a candidate's actions are the ones the executed world would show.
* Yaw, cause measured: 17 of 2,399 windows in 8 clips, the ego standing or crawling (speed at the tick: median 0.0, maximum 0.93 m/s). The pinned tool rebuilds the heading from the tangents of the knots; where the logged path jitters back and forth the heading flips by pi and the yaw channel reads pi / 0.1 s = 31.4 rad/s (corpus maximum 0.55 rad/s). Without these windows the correlation is 0.9926. A candidate that carries such a value feeds the world model an input far outside anything it was trained on.
* **Tool change (made before any rollout of the tool is read for an unsafe rate):** every candidate action is bounded by the turn a car can make, |yaw rate| <= 0.2 1/m times the track speed at that step (`imag_plan.limit_yaw`, KAPPA_MAX = 0.2 = a turning radius of 5 m); values inside the bound are unchanged bit for bit; the number of bounded steps is logged per tick (`n_yaw_lim`). Tests of the tool after the change: 26 passed, 10 of 10 one-line mutations red with a green control (`imagplan/work/lead/tests_lead3.log`, `mutations_lead.log`). The acceleration channel is not changed.
* **Read form p4b**, on the full set with the changed tool: yaw correlation at least 0.99 over all steps; acceleration correlation at least 0.99 over steps 2 to 10, the first step reported beside; median absolute acceleration difference at most 0.1 m/s2.

## 3. Control p5 (the tool's imagined gap against the banked read): cannot be evaluated as written

MEASURED: the banked windows of `imag_dump.py` have their present frame at 0 mod 4 of the clip, the T1-kin windows start at 3 mod 4: no (clip, frame) is common to both at the first tick (the first match in `PLUMB_LFT_s0.json` paired the banked end frame with the window start and is not a same-frame compare). At the nearest frames, one step of 0.2 s apart, the head's present read of the tool and the banked one differ by a median of 0.64 m (458 windows) and 0.67 m (534 windows).
* **Read form p5b (response reproduced; no value of it had been computed when this note was written):** from the tool's log, over the active ticks and the candidates whose progress at 2.0 s lies 1.0 to 4.0 m behind the plan's (the range of the acceleration offsets of card ADD36): the slope through the origin of (imagined gap of the candidate minus imagined gap of the plan) on (progress of the plan minus progress of the candidate) at 2.0 s. Bars: at least 0.5 for an arm that cards ADD36 and ADD37 read `IMAG-RESPONDS`, below 0.25 for an arm they read `IMAG-DEAF`. It is read on LA (responds, reader slope 0.912 and 0.799) and on LFT (deaf, reader slope -0.406 and -0.497) before the tool is used on a card arm; the slope of every card arm is reported.
* The equality of the tool's rollout with an independent rollout of the same states is a unit test (1e-4 m, tiny model); the tool calls the functions of `imag_dump.py` and does not copy them.
