Evidence class: MEASURED. Designed after the reads of cards ADD104 (`VISSEED10K-HOLDS`) and ADD105 (`VISENS10K-WITHIN-HALF-THE-GAP`). Authority: PI, 2026-11-21, to the question whether to fit the head on all 1,679 train clips if the three new fits hold, verbatim: "Yes, if it holds". Card ADD104 read `VISSEED10K-HOLDS` with all controls passed. Budget of this card, ESTIMATED: 3 to 4 pod-hours on the fork pod in all (staging and encode about 2, three fits about 1, ten rollouts about 1) and about 50 GB of pod disk. ## 1. Question

The head of cards ADD99 to ADD105 is fit on 500 train clips. Fit on every eligible train clip, does it read the gap with less error (T0), does the form with no label at inference still hold with it, and does the larger fit move contact, the unsafe rate or falling behind (T1-kin)?

## 3. T0 read

Measure: the mean absolute gap error on the rows of the evaluation join with a lead in the label and an output of every head compared (the measure of the record of card ADD105). Statistic: the mean of the error of L2, L3, L4 divided by the mean of the error of the 500-clip fits of seeds 2, 3, 4; interval by a cluster bootstrap over the 141 evaluation clips, 2,000 draws, the fits held fixed.

* `CROPSCALE-T0-GAP-ERROR-CUT`: the upper end of the ratio is below 1.0 and each of the three large fits has an error below the smallest error of the five 500-clip fits on record (the spread of those five is the fit noise the bootstrap does not see).
* `CROPSCALE-T0-GAP-ERROR-CUT-WITHIN-FIT-NOISE`: the upper end is below 1.0 and the second clause does not hold.
* `CROPSCALE-T0-MORE-GAP-ERROR`: the lower end is above 1.0.
* else `CROPSCALE-T0-GAP-ERROR-NOT-SEPARATED`.

Printed with no name: the error of every fit, the correlation of each head's gap with the label gap, the kept epoch and selection loss of each fit, the number of fit clips and of clips left out.

## 4. T1-kin arms and names

Arms, all with the form VD5c of card ADD102 (wrapper variant 28bc31f21ecb4ccb unchanged, the run rule of note N1 of card ADD102), both model seeds, ten rollouts: G2, G3, G4 (the gap file of the large fit of that seed), GEc (the mean gap of L2, L3, L4, built as in card ADD105), E3c (the mean gap of the three 500-clip fits of seeds 2, 3, 4: the reference of GEc with the same number of heads). References, banked and recomputed by the reader: RR (0.0954), RLc (0.0000), VD5c (0.0169), F2 (0.0138), F3 (0.0185), F4 (0.0123), E5c (0.0138). Half the gap H is half of the point value of the contact of RR minus that of RLc (0.0477). Paired differences, two-level bootstrap over model seeds and evaluation episodes, 2,000 draws (the rules of cards ADD101 to ADD105).

* Per arm X of G2, G3, G4, GEc, the four names of card ADD104: against RR `CROPSCALE10K-<X>-CUTS-CONTACT`, `-MORE-CONTACT` or `-CONTACT-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-RR`; against RLc `-WITHIN-HALF-THE-GAP`, `-HALF-THE-GAP-NOT-REACHED` or `-HALF-THE-GAP-NOT-DECIDED`; against VD5c `-LESS-CONTACT-THAN-VD5c`, `-MORE-CONTACT-THAN-VD5c` or `-CONTACT-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-VD5c`; falling behind by more than 10 m against RR `-FALLS-BEHIND-MORE`, `-FALLS-BEHIND-LESS` or `-FALLING-BEHIND-NOT-SEPARATED`.
* The form with the large fit, by the count rule of card ADD104 over G2, G3, G4: `CROPSCALE10K-HOLDS` (all three CUTS-CONTACT, at least two WITHIN-HALF-THE-GAP, none NOT-REACHED), `CROPSCALE10K-DOES-NOT-HOLD` (one not CUTS-CONTACT or one NOT-REACHED), else `CROPSCALE10K-NOT-DECIDED`.
* The effect of the larger fit, per measure M of CONTACT, UNSAFE, FALLING-BEHIND, over the three pairs of the same fit seed (G2 minus F2, G3 minus F3, G4 minus F4): `CROPSCALE10K-SCALE-LOWERS-<M>` (at least two pairs with the upper end below 0 and none with the lower end above 0), `CROPSCALE10K-SCALE-RAISES-<M>` (at least two with the lower end above 0 and none with the upper end below 0), else `CROPSCALE10K-SCALE-<M>-NOT-SEPARATED`.
* The mean pair GEc minus E3c, per measure M: `CROPSCALE10K-MEAN-LOWERS-<M>`, `CROPSCALE10K-MEAN-RAISES-<M>` or `CROPSCALE10K-MEAN-<M>-NOT-SEPARATED`.

Stated before the data: the contact rate of the 500-clip fits is 8 to 14 windows of 650, with 0 for the label gap, so there is little room for the larger fit to show in contact, and NOT-SEPARATED is the expected reading there; the measures with room are the T0 error and falling behind (the 500-clip fits fall behind more than the label gap form).

Printed with no name: levels of every arm per model seed and pooled; falling behind against RLc; the share of rule steps with a lead and of them with a non-zero closing input per rollout.

Readings committed in advance. T0 CUT and HOLDS: the large fit is the head to carry; whether a further scale of the data is worth its cost is read from the two points (500 and all eligible clips) and goes to the PI. T0 CUT and no effect at T1-kin: at this level the rule is not limited by the gap error, and the next lever is elsewhere (the price of the slope, the presence output of the reader). T0 not cut: more clips of the same kind do not help this head on this input, and the lever is the input and not the amount of data. DOES-NOT-HOLD: reported as written; the 500-clip results stand as measured, and the large fit is looked at under a new card.

## 5. Controls

All have to pass before any name or any value of this card is opened; logged in `TIMES_NOTE.md` before the names. This card has no map of arms to controls: with one control failed nothing of it is opened, the card is reported as failed with no name, and a changed form is a new card.

* g0 (lists): every clip of the large train list is in `train_eligible.txt` of card ADD99; no clip of it is one of the 141 evaluation clips; it holds the 500 clips of the pilot with their roles; the selection set is the pilot's 50 clips; clips left out are listed with a reason.
* g1 (tokens): every new token file has the shape and type of the pilot's token files and its hash is in the manifest; the token files of the pilot are unchanged against the pilot's manifest; three train clips of the pilot, encoded again by the call of this card into a check folder, equal the pilot's tokens of arm D within 1e-3 in the largest absolute difference.
* g2 (fits): each of the three fit records names its seed (2, 3, 4), head H5 on arm D, the settings of card ADD99, no permuted labels, the large fit set as its fit clips, the pilot's 50 selection clips, no evaluation clip; the three head files differ from one another and from the five on record.
* g3 (gap files): the dumps are on the frames of the evaluation join, each dump's meta names the head file of its fit, each saved gap file equals a second routine on every row; the two mean files equal a second routine's mean within 1e-6 m with an output exactly where all three have one.
* g4 (wrapper): the wrapper file is 28bc31f21ecb4ccb, the pod check record of card ADD102 names it with a pass, and every run text names it.
* g5 (each arm is what the card says): in the rule log of every rollout, on every logged step where the rule had a lead and the gap file has an output, the gap the rule received equals the gap of its file at that clip and frame within 1e-3 m; a step with a lead and no output equals a gap of the control run V0 of card ADD102 within 1e-3 m; the meta names the gap file and the closing switch; the closing input is non-zero on a positive share of the steps with a lead.
* g6: the reader recomputes contact 0.0954 for RR, 0.0000 for RLc, 0.0169 for VD5c, 0.0138 for F2, 0.0185 for F3, 0.0123 for F4 and 0.0138 for E5c from the banked rollouts.
* g7: the tests of the kit pass in the lead's run on the dev box before the rollouts.
* g8: the engine's own controls of the grid read and of the strategic grid pass.
* g9 (the T0 measure): the T0 reader recomputes the mean absolute gap error of the five 500-clip fits as the record of card ADD105 has it (3.960, 3.913, 4.112, 4.079 and 4.056 m on 3,841 rows) within 1e-3 m.
* g10 (deliberate regression): the fit with permuted labels has more gap error than the large fit of the same seed: the lower end of the ratio of its error to that of L2 (cluster bootstrap over the evaluation clips) is above 1.0.

## 6. Four metric families

LONGITUDINAL (distance-keeping): contact, unsafe rate, falling behind, with the engine's pair read. LATERAL, TACTICAL: the engine's pair read against the logged drive. STRATEGIC: read inside the reader under its own engine controls for G2, G3, G4 and GEc against RR, RLc and the 500-clip arm of the same kind (F2, F3, F4, E3c).

## 7. Limits

T1-kin only, no re-perception; the lead moves as logged whatever the ego does; the heads read crops of logged frames; two model seeds of one line; three fit seeds per size of the fit set, so the effect of the larger fit is read against three pairs and against the spread of five 500-clip fits, not against a large sample of fits; the interval of the T0 ratio holds the fits fixed; the selection set stays at 50 clips; the settings of the fit are those tuned on 500 clips and are not tuned again; contact differences rest on few windows.
