# BOXSIZE_FIT_DEFS -- definitions of the kit of card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD124 (BOXSIZE-T0), tier T0

**Encoding.** For the angular width and height of the lead's box in degrees, u = ln(angle) - 1.6. The box loader returns, in the shape and order of the locator's `(has, az, el)`, the first value 16 u_w and the second value -2 + 8 u_h. The locator's targets (`targets_of`) are then u_w and u_h limited to -2..+2, i.e. 0.67 to 36.6 degrees (`LIMITS`); its read-out returns the same encoding. `encode(ang_w, ang_h) -> (a, b)` and `decode(a, b) -> (ang_w, ang_h)` (no clipping) are importable from `boxsize_fit.py`.

**The one changed value.** `boxsize_fit.py` imports `locator_fit.py` unchanged (`--locator`, default `/workspace/v8proof/lookat/kit/locator_fit.py`) and sets the module value `load_box` for the time of the run; it is put back in a `finally` block. `has` = has_lead and finite ctr_az, ctr_el and finite positive ang_w, ang_h. Model, loss, samples, fit settings, prediction, `--identity` and `--permute-targets` are the imported tool's own `main`, called with the remaining argv.

**Records.** After the imported main returns, `BOXSIZE_FIT_RECORD.json` is written beside the fit record (`<O>/fits/LOC_s<K>[_perm]/`); an `--identity` run writes `<O>/BOXSIZE_IDENTITY_RECORD.json`. Keys: card, tier, module_values_changed (`[{"name": "load_box"}]`), module_values_restored, locator_fit_sha256, tool_sha256, argv, seed (null for identity), permute_targets, finished_utc. An existing record is kept (a predict-only call does not rewrite it).

**Output paths (the locator's names are kept).** Fit: `<O>/fits/LOC_s<K>[_perm]/{FIT_RECORD.json, head.pt, BOXSIZE_FIT_RECORD.json}`. Prediction: `<O>/pred/LOC_s<K>[_perm].npz` and `_meta.json`; its columns `az_pred`, `el_pred` hold the ENCODED values (first, second), decoded with `decode`. Identity: `<O>/IDENTITY_K5.json`. `<O>` is `/workspace/v8proof/boxsize/out` (env `BS_OUT`).

**Queue** (`pod_boxsize_queue.sh`, one step at a time): 1 identity run, 2 to 4 fit + prediction for seeds 0, 1, 2, 5 permuted fit seed 0. Before each step: fewer than 3 GPU processes and more than 28 GB free; the wait counts into the stop rule `BS_STOP_S` (5400 s since the start marker). Markers in `/workspace/v8proof/boxsize/`: `BOXSIZE_START.txt` (`<epoch> <UTC time> pid <pid>`), `BOXSIZE_DONE.txt` (last line `ZZ BOXSIZE DONE <UTC time> all steps done`), `BOXSIZE_STOP.txt` (the reason). Queue folder `/workspace/v8proof/boxsize/queue`. `DRY=1` prints and creates nothing; `RESUME=1` continues after a stop; finished fits are skipped.
