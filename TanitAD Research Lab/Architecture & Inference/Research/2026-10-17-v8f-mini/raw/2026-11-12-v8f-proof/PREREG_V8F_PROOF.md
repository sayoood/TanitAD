## H-V8F-PROOF-1 SOUP: does averaging two same-init replicates in weight space realise the ensemble gain?
* Members: `stopbal_BAL-NULL_s0` and `stopbal_BAL-NULL_rep` (same argv except the seed, same init). SOUP2 = 0.5*(theta_a + theta_b) over every floating-point tensor; integer buffers from member a.
* Controls that must read known values: (c1) the interpolation at alpha = 0 and alpha = 1 reproduces each member's dump bit-for-bit; (c2) every tensor that is frozen in training is bit-identical in both members (else STOP: not same-init); (c3) both members are re-dumped in the same environment and thread count as the soup.
* Identity (VC-G1, exact): err(mean of outputs) = mean member squared error - 0.25*||y_a - y_b||^2. So the OUTPUT-ensemble gain is computed from the two member dumps before the soup is evaluated: G_ens. The soup's gain over the member mean is G_soup; kappa = G_soup / G_ens.
* Primary metric: tactical band (2-6 s) ADE and 6 s ADE, T1-kin, paired per window: soup minus the mean of the two members. Four families reported; lateral and tactical must not carry a separated cost.
* Outcomes: SOUP-REALISES (primary delta < 0 with CI excluding 0 on both primaries, kappa >= 0.5); SOUP-PARTIAL (CI excludes 0, kappa < 0.5); SOUP-NULL (not separated); SOUP-BARRIER (soup worse than the member mean, CI excluding 0: the members are not linearly connected).
* Next lever, committed: REALISES or PARTIAL -> three-member soup with NULL_rep2 when it is banked, and tail-averaging inside one run as a training arm (asked with its own card); NULL or BARRIER -> output ensembling (exact gain G_ens by VC-G1, cost 2 forward passes) and the barrier profile alpha in {0.25, 0.5, 0.75}.
