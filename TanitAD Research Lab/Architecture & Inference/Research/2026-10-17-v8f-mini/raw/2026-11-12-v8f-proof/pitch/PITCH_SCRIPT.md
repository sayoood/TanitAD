# TanitAD pitch film: scenes, speaker notes and the source of every figure

Film: `tanitad_pitch_film.html` (a deterministic animation page; `render.py` renders it frame by frame with headless Chrome into an MP4, 1920 x 1080, 30 frames per second, 7 min 48 s, silent, on-screen text only, made to be spoken over). Built 2026-11-15.
Language rule of the film: plain words, no abbreviations, no project-internal names. This file is the bridge back to the evidence: every figure on screen with its artifact, tier and limit. Paths are relative to `raw/2026-11-12-v8f-proof/` unless stated.

Scope that the film states on screen (scene 8) and that every speaker should repeat: all measurements are diagnostics on recorded real drives (tier T0, or T1-kin: kinematic rollout on the model's own actions on logged frames). Nothing in the film is driving performance on a road or in a simulator that reacts. STRATEGIC family: PAUSED (PI item 25); the film shows the route level as part of the design and makes no claim for it.

## What the film deliberately does not claim
* No road driving, no reactive simulation, no safety certification.
* No claim that the model foresees the motion of other vehicles 2.0 s ahead: three arms read flat there so far.
* No claim for the route (strategic) level: PAUSED.
* The shield result uses a privileged stop cue; the camera cue is an open step.
* The advantage of the hierarchy is shown for the adopted training protocol; from scratch it is not established.
