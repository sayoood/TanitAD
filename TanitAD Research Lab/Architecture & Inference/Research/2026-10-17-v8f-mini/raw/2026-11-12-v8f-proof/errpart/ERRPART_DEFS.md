## Choices (the card is silent)
* Mix frame. Valid label gap in the evaluation join (`lead_valid == 1`, finite, `0 <= L <= 60`) and a read (dump `valid`, finite raw gap, inside the join's rows of its clip) of the reader seed of the model seed. g = min(r, Q(r)) with the q50 map of that reader seed; e = g - L. A frame with a valid label gap that is absent from the dump counts as "no reader read".
* Clip part m: mean of e over all mix frames of the join clip, whatever the runs.
* Run. Two mix frames of one clip with join rows r and r + 1 and an absolute change of the label gap of at most 3.0 m (compared in float64 on the stored float32 values, in both routines) are neighbours; a run ends where one of the three conditions fails. A frame that is no mix frame ends a run.
* Window of the slow part: the frame itself and at most 5 mix frames before and 5 after, inside the run, cut at the run ends (at most 11 values; a run of one frame gives s = r, f = 0). The card says "at most 5 frames before and 5 frames after the frame"; the frame itself is taken as part of the window (a centred window). The test `window_other` (3 frames on each side) turns q1 red.
* Joins: M: P = L + m; W: P = L + r; S: P = L + s; F: P = L + f. One join per arm and model seed (eight files); no limit applied; P below 0 and above 60 counted (`n_mixed_below_0`, `n_mixed_above_60`, `min_mixed`, `max_mixed`), no pass criterion. The rule takes a gap as a lead only from 0 to 60 m, so on such a frame the rule sees no lead.
* Control q0 is the e0 of card ADD96 computed again for this build (both reader seeds, same facts and bars).
* Control q1 asks: (1) the saved gap equals, within 1e-4 m, L plus the part computed a second time by `second_parts`, a plain loop clip by clip and frame by frame from the dumps and maps, written separately from the build (which works on whole arrays, with cumulative sums for the windows); (2) the sum identities (P_M - L) + (P_W - L) = g - L and (P_S - L) + (P_F - L) = P_W - L on every mix frame, from the four saved float32 files of one model seed read back (`identities` per model seed in the record); (3) the mean of P_W - L over the mix frames of every clip is 0 within 1e-4 m (`clip_mean_P_minus_L_max_abs` of the W files; recorded for the others, no bar); (4) the number of runs and their median length of the build equal the ones of the second routine; (5) frames with a valid label gap and no reader read carry the label gap and are at most 5 percent, per file; (6) every other field equals the evaluation join (value, dtype, shape) and no frame changed outside the mix frames; (7) map files are `QMAP_LR16_s<s>_q50.json` with a sha256, the dump has a sha256, window and run rule of the record are 5 and 3.0, the eight file hashes are distinct, the eight arm names with the right part and seed, the counts below 0 and above 60 are present, dtype of `lead_gap` kept.
* The saved gap is float32 (the dtype of the join); the build works in float64. The 1e-4 m bar holds for gaps up to about 1,600 m in magnitude (half a float32 step), and for the sum identities (three roundings) up to about 800 m.
* Error statistics of P minus the label gap on the mix frames use the terms of row 154 as in `ERRCUT_DEFS.md`; per join also the number of runs and the median run length.
* Names (card section 3): three per arm (`-CARRIES-CONTACT` / `-NO-CONTACT-SHOWN` on the interval of C(X) - C(RLc); `-FALLS-BEHIND-MORE` / `-FALLS-BEHIND-LESS` / `-FALLING-BEHIND-NOT-SEPARATED` on F(X) - F(RLc); the three-way K100 name on C(X) - C(K100)) and the name of the card from M and W. `read_errpart10k.card_check` derives the card name a second time from the intervals and the names stage refuses on a mismatch.
* The names stage refuses a controls file unless `controls_all_pass` is true, the six controls q0 to q5 are all present and each reads PASS, and a payload exists; it binds the result by the sha256 of the controls file. With one control failed nothing is opened, no map of arms to controls.
* Control q3: round(C(RR), 4) == 0.0954, round(C(RLc), 4) == 0.0000, round(C(K100), 4) == 0.0477. q5: the engine's grid read ALL_PASS and the strategic grid stage A and B; the STRATEGIC pair reads (each arm against RR and RLc) are computed in the controls stage. K100 is in `R.ALL_RUNS` and in the pairs definition, so C, U and F differences against K100 are in the payload; `contact_over_k100` gives C(X) over C(K100) and the interval of the difference over the point value of C(K100).
* Queue: root `$GP` = `${GR_ROOT:-/workspace/v8proof/errpart10k}`; joins `${GR_JOINS:-$GP/joins}`; small folder `$GP/small_ep`. One process per model seed with markers `START_ERRPART10K_s<k>.txt`, `ERRPART10K_DONE_s<k>.txt`, `ERRPART10K_STOP_s<k>.txt`, log `queue_errpart_s<k>.log`; with `GR_SEED=ALL` the done marker is `ERRPART10K_DONE.txt`. Run folders `$W/t1/I10RLcKF5P30Hb20q<ARM>_s<m>`. The admission checks all eight join files whatever the seed scope; the banked K100 runs (`T1_DONE`) are required as in the ADD97 queue.
* Not built: `lead_pytest.out` (the run of the tests that the lead makes on the dev box, control q4); the pod copies of the dump, map and join files.

## Order of use
1. dev box: `python make_part_joins.py --selftest`; `python read_errpart10k.py --selftest`; pytest (command in the head of `test_errpart.py`, the whole file), output to `errpart/lead_pytest.out`; `python make_errpart_queue.py` (only to rebuild the queue).
2. pod: `make_part_joins.py --eval-join /workspace/v8proof/hierrep/pins/join_eval141.npz --dump-dir /workspace/v8proof/closeread/dump/both --qmap-dir /workspace/v8proof/integ10k/qmap_sweep --out-dir /workspace/v8proof/errpart10k/joins` (one run; refuses an existing folder; last line `ZZ PART-JOINS q0 PASS q1 PASS`).
3. pod: copy the kit to `$GP/kit`; `DRY=1 bash pod_errpart10k_queue.sh`; then `GR_SEED=0 bash pod_errpart10k_queue.sh` and `GR_SEED=1 ...` (or ALL).
4. pod read: `bash podread_errpart10k.sh controls`, then `bash podread_errpart10k.sh names`.
