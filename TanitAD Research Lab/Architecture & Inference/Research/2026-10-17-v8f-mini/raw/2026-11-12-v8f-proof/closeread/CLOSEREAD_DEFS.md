## 2. Estimators and names (as implemented)

* Input per frame: (gap read, presence p, closing output); stacked over the last H frames (H = 5, 10, 25), oldest first; target = label closing speed at the last frame.
* Fit set per reader seed = the 432 held-out clips of that reader; score set = the evaluation clips. The validation split of the fit set is by clip (25 % of the clips, fixed generator), used for the ridge weight (arm L, grid `logspace(-6, 1, 15)` times n, mean squared error on the validation clips; the chosen weight is then refitted on all fit rows) and for early stopping (arm N). Arm N: 2 hidden layers of 64 ReLU units, Huber loss (delta 1 m/s), Adam 1e-3, batch 256, at most 120 epochs, patience 10; seeds 0, 1, 2; the arm's estimate is the mean of the three nets (interpretation: the card gives three seeds and one name per arm and history).
* Pooled r = Pearson over the stacked (reader seed, frame) rows. Interval: two-level bootstrap, reader seeds with replacement, then evaluation episodes with replacement (the episode draw is shared by the drawn seeds: same frames), B = 2,000, percentile 2.5 / 97.5. Paired difference = mean |est - y| minus mean |0 - y| on the same frames, same draws.
* `READS-CLOSING` needs: pooled r >= 0.30, lower end of its interval > 0, r >= 0.30 in each reader seed, upper end of the paired difference interval < 0. Else `NO-CLOSING`. `CLOSEREAD-A-CLOSING-IS-READABLE` when at least one of the six cells reads; else `CLOSEREAD-NOT-READABLE-FROM-READER-OUTPUTS`. One passing cell is marked as one of six.
* Beside, naming nothing: the reader's closing output at the frame, and `(gap read at f - gap read at f-H+1) / ((H-1) * 0.2 s)` (the form of row 106 over H frames; interpretation: H frames = H-1 intervals).
* Controls: c1 (rule recomputed from the manifest, exclusion applied, count and list hash against `train_meta.json`, fit clips against score clips and the reader's training list, planted shared id fails); c2 (label two-point change over 5 frames against label closing: r >= 0.70, positive; shifted +-10 frames lower); c3 (p and gap after the map of the run equal the dump, tolerance 1e-3, every row with the window in the clip, and the first-tick rows `k == 0` are counted); c4 (permuted fit labels, both arms, H = 25 only: the card does not name H; the choice keeps the chance of a false failure at about 10 % instead of about 26 % for six cells); c5, c6 synthetic; c7 counts. If any fails: no name, no score value (stdout and files), the controls are in `CLOSEREAD_CONTROLS.json`.
