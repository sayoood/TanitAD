## 0. FIRST: clauses of the card that could fail by construction, and what the code cannot do as written

1. **Does the record of card ADD102 name the dump hashes at all? Yes, from the code** (`visrule/make_vis_gapfile.py`: `measure_file` writes `"dump_sha256": sha_file(dump_path)`, and `build` stores it as `rec["files"]["VGAP_s<k>"]`). It is under `files.VGAP_s0` and `files.VGAP_s1`, NOT under `dumps.*` (those entries carry the `e0` facts and the dump path only). `make_ens_gapfile.py` reads that key. The hash is the sha256 of the whole dump file at the time the gap file of card ADD102 was built. If a dump was rebuilt or replaced since, e0 fails: that is the control working. What I could not look at: which folder the lead gave to `--dump-root` then. The default here is `/workspace/v8proof/cropread/run1/dump` (the folder named in the brief); if the dumps of fit seeds 0 and 1 lie elsewhere, pass `--old-dump-root` (the hash decides, not the path).
2. **e0 needs the record of card ADD104 to exist, with x0 PASS and x1 PASS, before the gap file of this card can be built.** Order on the pod: the three fits and dumps of card ADD104, then its `make_seed_gapfile.py` (record `/workspace/v8proof/visseed10k/gapfiles/VISSEED_GAPFILES_RECORD.json`), then this card's `make_ens_gapfile.py`. If card ADD104 stops at x0 or x1, this card cannot pass e0 by construction. The names of the keys read: `files.VGAPF_s<S>.dump_sha256` (S = 2, 3, 4) and top-level `x0.PASS`, `x1.PASS` (written by `make_seed_gapfile.py`, `build`). Note that the record of ADD104 also holds a hash of the same dump under `dumps.*.dump_meta`; only `files.*` is read.
3. **"The five dumps are on the same rows (clip and frame)"** is coded as: the sorted list of (clip name, frame) of every dump equals that of fit seed 0, every row once, every row inside its join clip (frame 0 to n-1). The extra clauses (once, inside the join) follow the dump format and are met by the dumps of cards ADD102 and ADD104 (their v0 controls). A dump with a row outside the join, or with a row twice, fails here too.
4. **"The saved gap equals a second routine's mean within 1e-6 m"**: second routine = plain Python loops over the five dumps alone (sum of five floats / 5); the saved gap is `numpy` mean of the stacked arrays. They differ by about 1e-14. Not expected to fail. Checked on every row of the join (a deviation of 2e-6 on the first or the last row turns it red; 5e-7 does not).
5. **Output flag** = all five finite. A dumped gap above 60 m is a finite output and is kept as dumped (no map, no clip, no minimum); the head has no output at frames 0 to 3, so those rows have none (not expected to differ from the five dumps).
6. **T0 diagnostic placement.** The card says it goes into the gap-file record under a key that the reader prints only at the names stage. It is `t0_gap_error_diagnostic` in `VISENS_GAPFILES_RECORD.json`; the record is written with it only when e0 passes. **The pull (`pull_visens.sh`) carries that record file, so its values sit in a text file on the lead's disk before the controls are read.** The reader prints them only at the names stage; in the controls file they are inside the payload (under `withheld` when a control fails, as every other value). The lead must not open the key, nor the payload or `withheld` block, before the names stage.
7. **e2, "the gap the rule received equals the gap of the file"**: the reader reads `VGAPE5.npz` as it lies on disk (its sha256 is checked against the record in e0 and against the `vis_gap_file_sha256` of each rule-log meta), not the dumps. The five-dump mean is checked in e0. A lead step with no output equals a V0 gap at the same clip and frame (any of the values logged there) within 1e-3 m, as in the readers of cards ADD102 to ADD104. The positive share of non-zero closing input is read per rollout (both must have one).
8. **e3 is the three values of the card** (RR 0.0954, RLc 0.0000, VD5c 0.0169, four decimals). VD5cx (0.0200 in card ADD104) is printed, not a control here; the banked rollout of VD5cx must still exist (the queue and the pod script require its `T1_DONE`).
9. **e5 inherits the unverified point of card ADD103 (its DEFS 0.6):** whether an engine control inside `ALL_PASS` assumes a zero closing column for RR-form rule logs. The engine reads RR, RLc, VD5c, VD5cx, E5c (and R0) here; the strategic grid reads R0, RR, RLc, VD5c, E5c (VD5cx and the F arms are not in the strategic grid). Could not be run here.
10. **F2, F3, F4 (single-fit arms of card ADD104) enter the engine run list only with `--add104-controls` on a file with card ADD104 and x0 to x6 all PASS.** Then their runs are also part of the engine read, so an engine control failing on those runs fails e5 and with it the whole card (the card says nothing about this). Without the flag or with a failing file, the printed line says they are left out. `podread_visens10k.sh` gives the flag only when `/workspace/v8proof/visseed10k/read/VISSEED10K_CONTROLS.json` exists and the six F run folders have `T1_DONE`; the reader itself reads only the PASS flags of that file (no value).
11. **H** in the names is half of the point value of C(RR) minus C(RLc) (pooled); `h_note` in the names result gives it beside 0.0477 and flags a difference. A pooled RR of 0.0954 and RLc of 0.0000 give exactly 0.0477.
12. **Not done by code (lead):** freezing the hash file, logging e0 to e5 in `TIMES_NOTE.md` before the names, running the pytest on the dev box (e4 reads the text of `lead_pytest.out`: "N passed" and no "failed"/"error"), running the rollouts. With one control failed nothing is opened: the names stage refuses unless e0 to e5 are all present and PASS.
13. **The gap file's `seed` array is -1** (the file is not one fit seed's). The wrapper variant reads only `clip_index`, `frame`, `gap`, `has_out` (`visrule/make_wrapper_v.py:48`); the layout and dtypes equal those of the gap files of card ADD102 (asserted in a test against `make_vis_gapfile.build_arrays`).

## 2. Definitions with sources
* **Gap file** (`make_ens_gapfile.py:build_arrays`): arrays `clip_index`, `frame`, `gap`, `has_out`, `clips`, `seed` (-1), one row per join frame; `gap` = `numpy` mean over the five stacked dump gaps (each laid on the join rows by `frame_table`: `cid` indexes the dump's own `clips`, as in `cropread/fit/FIT_DEFS.md` section 2) where all five are finite, NaN elsewhere; `has_out` = all five finite. Dumps: fit seeds 0, 1 at `<old-dump-root>/D_H5/reader_s<k>_score.npz`, fit seeds 2, 3, 4 at `<new-dump-root>/D_H5/reader_s<S>_score.npz`.
* **Record** (`VISENS_GAPFILES_RECORD.json`): `card`, `eval_join_sha256`, `files.VGAPE5` (`file_sha256`, row counts, `dump_sha256.fit0..fit4`), `inputs` (dump paths, path and sha256 of the two records), `facts` (what e0 read), `e0` (`PASS`, `failures`, `clauses` a to e), and with e0 PASS `t0_gap_error_diagnostic` (n rows with a label lead and an output of all five; mean absolute gap error of the mean gap and of each single fit; label fields `lab_gap`, `lab_present` read from the dump of fit seed 0, number of rows where the other four differ reported).
* **e0** (`e0_check`): clauses a (hashes ADD102), b (hashes ADD104 and its x0, x1), c (same rows), d (saved equals the second routine, 1e-6 m), e (flag exactly when all five have one). The reader repeats the whole of it from disk (`MEG.check_now`: the five dumps, both records, the gap file on disk), and requires the hashes now to be the ones the record read.
* **e1** `ctl_e1`: wrapper file hash, last line of the pod check, hash in `CHECK_WRAPPER_V.hashes`, the two run texts. **e2** `e2_from_files` / `w2_one`. **e3** `ctl_e3`. **e4** `ctl_e4`. **e5** `ctl_e5`.
* **Arm** (`pod_visens10k_queue.sh`, last rollout lines): per model seed k, form RR, reader `lr16/TK16_s<k>`, q50 map, the arguments of the VD5c call of card ADD102 plus the closing switch and the gap file: `VGAP=$GF/VGAPE5.npz VCLOSE=1`, run folders `integ10k/t1/I10RQ50KF5P30Hb20eE5c_s<k>`.
* **Admission** (queue): wrapper 28bc31f21ecb4ccb with the pod check record of card ADD102; `$GP/gapfiles/VISENS_GAPFILES_RECORD.json` with `e0` PASS, the sha256 of `VGAPE5.npz` and of the evaluation join; banked runs RR, RLc (dump folders), V0, VD5c (ADD102) and VD5cx (ADD103) with T1_DONE, both seeds; the pinned tool, overlay tree, checkpoints, grid, join, cache as in the ADD104 queue. Not read: F2 to F4 (card ADD104's rollouts).
* **Names** (`names_of`, `card_name`, `card_check`): four names vs RR, RLc (with H), VD5c and falling behind vs RR; the card name by the order of the card (DOES-NOT-CUT, WITHIN-HALF-THE-GAP, HALF-THE-GAP-NOT-REACHED, else HALF-THE-GAP-NOT-DECIDED), re-derived from the intervals by `card_check`; the names stage refuses on a mismatch. Intervals: the engine's paired differences, two-level bootstrap over model seeds and episodes, 2,000 draws (`--n-boot`; the engine controls of the controls stage use 300 as in cards ADD101 to ADD104).
* **Printed with no name** (payload, printed at the names stage): contact, unsafe rate and falling behind per model seed and pooled (levels, levels per seed, pair intervals); falling behind against RLc; single-fit arms (E5c, VD5c, VD5cx always; F2, F3, F4 only with `--add104-controls` on an all-PASS file, else a note that says they are left out); the T0 diagnostic; shares of rule steps with a lead and of lead steps with a non-zero closing input per rollout.
* **Families:** LONGITUDINAL (C, U, F, the engine's pair read), LATERAL and TACTICAL (engine pair read), STRATEGIC (engine strategic grid with its own controls; E5c against RR, RLc and VD5c).
* **Readings committed in advance:** card section 3, last paragraph; not coded.
