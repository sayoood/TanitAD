## 0. FIRST: clauses of the card that can fail by construction, and what the code cannot do as written

1. **Control s5 (via c3) and every `REACHES` name inherit the ceiling of the tool's slope.** `STEERREAD-S-H5d-REACHES` needs the lower end of r of RG10 at or above 0.9, and r is read against a closing speed that is itself a slope of at most 5 label frames; even the label gap as a read (tool arm RL10, `kinclose4.py:236-268`) does not give r = 1. c3 (white error 0.25 m gets REACHES) can fail on this ceiling and on short runs (sd of the slope error 0.14 m/s over a 10-frame run, 0.39 over 5, 0.88 over 3). The same text is in `cropread/fit/FIT_DEFS.md` section 0 points 1 and 2. **The lead reads `arms.ALL.RL10.r_ci` of `GAPRANGE_RESULT.json` before the heads.** Control s5 is the one control that can fail for a reason outside this kit.
2. **s3 (data side) is a bit-level claim on a GPU encoder**: arm S tokens built with the box directions as steering must give arm B's tokens at cosine 0.9999 on every frame with a lead. A different batch composition or kernel choice between the arm B pass and the arm S pass can move a token by more than 1e-4 in cosine on a few frames; the clause is "every such frame". Not in this kit; named because it is the second control that can fail with a correct route.
3. **s6 can fail only if the permuted S-H5d output correlates positively with the label on the evaluation frames** (error sd = sqrt(var(out) + var(L) - 2 cov) falls below 0.8 sd(L) only for cov above zero); a constant output gives ratio 1.0 and passes. Same as c5 of card ADD99. The direction input does not change this: with permuted labels it carries nothing about the label.
4. **s2 tolerance 1e-3 degrees**: loc_steer.py writes with the device the queue uses (cuda, TF32 switched off in the script) and reproduces the 6 clips by a second pass with another chunk size (7 against 64) from the reloaded `head.pt`; a difference above 1e-3 degrees is not expected (the output range is 16 degrees, float32), but GPU kernels are not bit-identical across batch shapes, so the clause is a real test and not an identity.
5. **The localiser cannot reach a box centre beyond 16 degrees by construction** (azimuth = 16 tanh, elevation = 16 tanh, card section 2). A lead with the box centre outside that range (a lead far off-axis in a bend, a high or low box) saturates the output; its smooth L1 loss never reaches 0. This is no control clause; it bounds how often the box centre can lie inside the W16 window (the 8 and 7.95 degree half sizes) and is part of what the printed share measures.
6. **Card items that the card leaves open, set by me (all recorded here, none changes a control or a name):**
   * **"The evaluation frames with a lead box"** (section 4, printed with no name): I print two sets. `scored` = rows of the present reader's seed 0 score dump marked `valid` (the frames the heads are read on) with a lead box; `all_frames` = every frame of the 141 clips with a lead box. A lead box = box file `has_lead` true and `ctr_az`, `ctr_el` finite. `read_steerread.py:221`.
   * **S-H5d vector**: "appended to the frame's vector" is read as per frame: `[z_f (256), az_f/16, el_f/16]` for the five frames oldest first, flattened to 5 * 258 = 1290 (`crop_fit_s.py:43-60`). The other reading (five z, then the ten angles) has the same size; it is not used.
   * **The localiser's own MLP** reuses the reader of card ADD99 (`crop_fit.make_model("H1").embed`); its MLP is replaced by an identity and a new MLP 256-256-2 with GELU (as the card's head, which names no activation) sits on top; position vectors and query start from N(0, 0.02) as in note N1 (`steer_common.py:137-157`).
   * **Localiser fit**: no selection clips, no sel loss; loss per epoch is the train loss; the last epoch is kept. Batches are shuffled with numpy seed 0, torch seed 0 for all six fits. The 450 fit clips are read in list order of `lists/train_final.txt` (role `fit`), fold = position modulo 5 over the fit clips only.
   * **Frames of the localiser's fit**: frames with box `has_lead`; H1-style single frames (no history).
   * **s2's "6 evaluation clips (first by sha256 hex of the clip id)"**: sorted ascending by `sha256(clip id utf-8).hexdigest()`, the first 6 (`steer_common.py:119`).
   * **The steering sha256 carried by the data side**: the card names no key. `SC.find_steer_shas` (`steer_common.py:208`) accepts a map clip -> hex under any key containing `steer`, a clip -> {key containing `steer`: hex} map, or a list of rows with `clip` and a key containing `steer`; it searches `ctl/S8.json` and `tok/S/MANIFEST.json`. If neither carries such a map, control s2 FAILS (it fails safe; the data side's tool and this finder have to agree on one key name containing "steer").
   * **s1 is in two places**: `ctl/S1.json` (written by `loc_steer.py`) covers the localiser part; the head fits do not exist yet then, so the reader's `control_s1` redoes the localiser audit from the records on disk and adds the head-fit part (fit records read only train clips, none an evaluation clip, train list sha256 equal to the file). `S1.json` alone is not control s1.
   * **Names with no interval** (a cut whose bootstrap gave no finite interval) get the undecided or not-separated form (`...-NOT-DECIDED`, `STEERREAD-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-FIXED`, `...-FROM-BOUND`), never a decisive one.
   * **Per-form names** are given for the two forms of this card (`STEERREAD-S-H5d-<REACHES|BELOW|NOT-DECIDED>`, `STEERREAD-S-H5-...`). The references D-H5 and B-H5 get no per-form name here.
7. **Nothing in the card that the code cannot do as written** other than the open items in point 6. The controls and the name rules are implemented as written; no control or name rule was changed to make a test pass.

## 2. Definitions with sources

* **Localiser** (`steer_common.py:137-157`, `loc_fit.py:31-100`): arm D tokens of one frame [130, 1024] -> `crop_fit.make_model("H1").embed` (layer norm, linear 256, position vectors, one query, one cross-attention layer of 4 heads, token dropout 0.1 in training) -> MLP 256-256-2 -> 16 tanh per angle. Smooth L1, beta 0.5 degrees, mean over the two angles and the samples. AdamW lr 1e-3, weight decay 0.05, batch 256, 20 epochs, `crop_fit.lr_at` (warm-up 1 epoch, cosine to 0, per step), last epoch kept, seed 0. Samples: frames with box `has_lead` of the fit clips of the set (`crop_fit.build_samples`, which also refuses a clip whose token frame count differs from its box frame count).
* **Folds** (`steer_common.py:75-95`): fit clips in list order, position i; `Lk` reads positions with i mod 5 not k (360 clips) and steers positions with i mod 5 = k (90 clips); `LA` reads all 450 and steers the 50 selection and the 141 evaluation clips (191 clips). 450 + 50 + 141 = 641.
* **Refusals** (`loc_steer.py:51-62`, audit `steer_common.py:98-116`): a record that reads an evaluation clip, a record whose `clips_read` differs from the clips its name defines, a clip steered by a localiser whose record lists it, a `head.pt` whose sha256 differs from its record, an existing steering file (unless `--resume`), an existing `S1.json` or `S2.json`. Steering is written whole to a temporary name and renamed once.
* **Steering file**: `steer/<clip>.npy` float32 [n_frames, 2] (azimuth, elevation in degrees), every frame, finite; frame count = the clip's arm D token frames (`SteerStore` refuses another shape, dtype, a non-finite value or a frame count that differs from the tokens).
* **S_H5 / S_H5d** (`crop_fit_s.py`): S_H5 is `crop_fit.make_model("H5")` unchanged on `tok/S`. S_H5d: the same reader, MLP 1290-256-1 on `[z_f, az_f/16, el_f/16]` x 5 frames; gap = 60 sigmoid. Fit exactly as `crop_fit.run_fit` (40 epochs, batch 256, smooth L1 beta 1 m on frames with a valid lead gap and the five frames in the clip, the epoch with the lowest sel loss kept, `PERM_SEED_BASE` permutation within fit frames and within sel frames for control s6). Refuses unless `ctl/S8.json` has `pass` true and `tok/S/MANIFEST.json` exists; the record carries the steering sha256 of every fit and sel clip.
* **Dump** (`crop_dump_s.py`): the dump routine of card ADD99 (`crop_dump.dump_from_present`, `head_outputs` for S_H5 unchanged); S_H5d adds the direction (`head_outputs_s`). Fields and `valid` as there.
* **Controls** (`read_steerread.py`): s1 `:66`, s2 `:115`, s3 and s8 = top-level `pass` of `ctl/S3.json`, `ctl/S8.json`, s4 = `RC.control_c2` (wrapper equals `GAPRANGE_RESULT.json` within 1e-6, tool hashes), s5 `:153` (= `RC.control_c3` plus planted cuts: the present reader's error scaled to 0.4 must read `-REACHED`, to 0.9 `-NOT-REACHED`; B = 2,000), s6 `:168` (dump `S_H5d_perm` seed 0: not `REACHES` by the tool's rule on RG10, error sd at least 0.8 of the label gap sd), s7 = `RC.control_c6` (the pytest output has `N passed` and none of the words failed, error, errors), all of s1..s8 plus `controls_all_pass` before `names` opens anything (`check_controls`, `:206`).
* **Names** (`run_names`, `:284`): card name `STEERREAD-CUT-BY-2-{REACHED|NOT-REACHED|NOT-DECIDED}` on S_H5d, `STEERREAD-TOKENS-ONLY-CUT-BY-2-...` on S_H5, both by `RC.cut_ratio` (sd of the gap error of the form over the present reader's, frames both measure, pooled over the two seeds, paired bootstrap 2,000 draws, seed 99) and `RC.cut_name` (upper end at or below 0.5 REACHED, lower end above 0.5 NOT-REACHED). Per form `STEERREAD-<S-H5d|S-H5>-{REACHES|BELOW|NOT-DECIDED}` (the tool's `r_name` on RG10 over all scored frames). `STEERREAD-BEATS-FIXED` (ratio S-H5d / D-H5, upper end below 1), `STEERREAD-WORSE-THAN-FIXED` (lower end above 1), else `STEERREAD-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-FIXED`; `STEERREAD-BELOW-BOUND` (ratio S-H5d / B-H5, lower end above 1) else `STEERREAD-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-BOUND` (`vs_fixed_name`, `vs_bound_name`, `:38-56`). D-H5 and B-H5 dumps are read from `<run1>/dump/`.
* **Printed with no name** (`print_form`, `localiser_stats`): gap error by range bin, RG10 r per form and per fit seed, the split of row 154, the three parts of card ADD98 on the raw gap beside the present reader's numbers (a different object), sel loss and kept epoch, the cut intervals, the two ratios, and `LOCALISER` lines (median and 90th percentile of the absolute error in azimuth and in elevation, share of frames whose box centre lies inside the W16 window of arm S: absolute azimuth difference at most 8 degrees and elevation difference at most 7.95 degrees).
