## 3. A fourth source: `cand-imagkin` (the imagination for the lead, the known motion for the ego)

The gap at a horizon is the lead's motion minus the ego's motion. The ego's part of a candidate is known exactly (its own progress); only the lead's part needs a forecast. Source `cand-imagkin` uses the imagination for the part that must be learned and the kinematics for the part that is known:

* gap_d(h) = imagined gap under the model's plan at h (one rollout of the tick state under the plan's own actions, the d = 0 candidate of the decel family, with the yaw bound of note N2) + (a_P(h) - a_d(h)) + a_l(k) - a_m(k), with a_P and a_d the progress of the plan and of candidate d at the horizon. Decel family of note N3, the same gate, horizons, safe test, choice of the smallest safe d and fallback as `cand-imag`.
* It differs from `cand-cv` in ONE thing: the level of the gap at the horizon under the plan comes from the imagination (which can anticipate the lead) and not from the constant-velocity extrapolation of the head's present read. The response to a candidate is the kinematic one in both. It differs from `cand-imag` in one thing: the differences between candidates are the known ones and not imagined ones.
* It needs an arm that keeps the level (row H-V8F-PROOF-77: arm RL reads `IMAG-HEAD-ADDS`), not an arm that responds. Arms: the control arm RLFT and arm RLA at both bases (and arm X if it is another arm). Limit, stated in the row: on an arm whose response to the ego's action is reversed, the imagined level under the plan carries that reversed response wherever the plan departs from the logged habit.
* Names (each needs both bases; the definitions, estimator and thresholds of card section 4 with `cand-imagkin` in the place of `cand-imag`): `IMAG-LEVEL-CUTS-UNSAFE` / `IMAG-LEVEL-NO-CUT` (against `off`); `IMAG-LEVEL-BEATS-EXTRAPOLATION` / `-EQUALS-EXTRAPOLATION` / `-WORSE-THAN-EXTRAPOLATION` (against `cand-cv` of the decel family); `IMAG-LEVEL-DOMINATES`; `IMAG-LEVEL-REACHES-ORACLE` (against `cand-label` of the decel family); `IMAG-LEVEL-NO-COST` / `IMAG-LEVEL-COSTS-<family>`. Beside (names nothing): `cand-imagkin` against `cand-imag` on the same checkpoint (what imagined differences add or cost); the same on RLFTb as the floor of two checkpoints.
* Controls of this source (all must pass; else none of its names): p1 first part and p1b (identity: the set {0} with fallback none, and a constant gap of 1e9 m, equal `off` bit for bit); p2 (`cand-label` cuts U); p3 (noise after the last tick changes nothing, bit for bit); p6 (20 m subtracted from the imagined level raises the position error over 6 s, interval excluding 0); p7; p8; and
  * k1 (the response is the known one): on every active tick and candidate of a run, gap_d(h) - gap_0(h) equals a_P(h) - a_d(h) to 1e-9 m (read from the run's log).
  * k2 (the level is the imagination of the plan): at tick 0 the logged gap of d = 0 equals the gap of d = 0 of a `cand-imag` run of the same checkpoint within the difference of a twice-run compare (stated).
  * k3 (a deliberate regression): with the imagined level replaced by a constant of 1e9 m no tick changes the plan (p1b), and with the level shifted by -20 m more ticks choose a d above 0 than without the shift.
