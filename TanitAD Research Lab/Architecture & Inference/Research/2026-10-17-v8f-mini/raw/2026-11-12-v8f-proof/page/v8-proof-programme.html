v8 proof programme: what is proved, what is measured, what comes next

Nine theorems tie the measured state of v8F to a way forward. The algebra behind each is checked by a solver; the premises are experiments. Seventeen were read. One refuted a prediction of this page, and a later read showed part of that refutation was a limit of the probe; one gave a mixed answer and one follow-up failed its bar; the weight average of two replicates realised 0.74 of the ensemble gain; the direct read on un-pooled and higher-resolution tokens found a small gain from un-pooling and none from the full higher-resolution grid; a one-scalar weight edit reproduced a trained lever on its ROC; pictures decoded from the world-model state show the scene gist and no object; and the change of the lead gap over two seconds was not readable from 50 episodes by any linear read, ego motion included, so that read moved to the training clips; there two untrained variants missed their fidelity control and are not quoted, and the first trained world-model state holds where the lead vehicle is, beyond ego motion, and nothing about how the gap changes.

The short version
  • v8F-mini is a three-reader hierarchy on frozen, heavily pooled vision tokens. It beats its kinematic prior by about 1.2 m at 6 s on its own motion, and its levels do listen to each other. It has no planner, no selector and no working predictive world model yet.
  • The camera geometry is fixed and solver-checked: a 0.4 m stop line is below one pixel beyond about 12.5 m, and the 4x10 grid puts all ground beyond 6.3 m in one row. The prediction drawn from it failed its first test. The pooled 4x10 tokens do carry coarse lead range, lead presence, nearest-agent range and agent count beyond ego motion, and the finer 8x20 grid adds nothing measurable under a rank-256 probe. A later read with the full kernel shows the finer grid does carry more lead content (lead present +0.043 AUROC), so a finer interface is a live lever beside the training signal; the probes are also still data-limited at 111 episodes.
  • A hierarchy cannot beat a flat model through squared error alone. The matched flat control, already run by the programme, is not established (-0.35 m at one seed, -0.05 m at the other). Read by window class on the banked rollouts, the hierarchy is ahead where the future is not the common case (-0.48 / -0.16 m) and, without own-state supervision, behind where it is (+0.32 / +0.49 m). The margin did not sit in the selection term as the theory was read to predict: most of it is on windows where both models miss the logged mode. A commit rule gated by the tactical confidence does not rescue it (that confidence tells the two window groups apart with AUROC 0.64): the accuracy of the decision is the lever.
  • Rules can be guaranteed by a shield for every possible network; a loss term cannot do that. What limits the stop rule today is the cue, not the mechanism. Measured this night on real plans (T1-kin, a diagnostic): with a perfect stop cue the verified shield removes every overrun and lowers stop-window error by 3.5 / 3.4 m at the two seeds, at no measured cost; with the cue the model emits it gains nothing. A probe on the pooled tokens does not supply the cue either: vision adds nothing to ego motion for the stop flag and nothing to a constant for the stop distance. The conformal margin holds its 0.9 coverage, at the price of stopping about 7 m short.
  • Imagination-driven improvement is admissible only when the imagined gain exceeds twice the model gap. With world-model skill at zero that is not yet the case. Measured this night: a closed-form stacked predictor beats the learned world model at every horizon (at 2 s, skill -0.15 becomes +0.17), and the multi-level criterion holds at 0.42. Even so it explains at most 17 % of the latent change.

2. Theorems, what each says about v8F, and the premise that must be measured

Each statement is exact. "Checked" names the solver-verified conditions; "Reduction" names the step that is still a paper argument.

T1Multi-level prediction beats composed one-step prediction exactly when the jump model is better than the composed bound.

One-step error <= eps and Lipschitz constant L give e_H <= eps*(L^H - 1)/(L - 1) (= eps*H for L = 1); a level that jumps k steps with error eps_k gives eps_k*(L^H - 1)/(L^k - 1). The bound is attained (a constant bias along an expanding direction), so in the worst case over all models with these constants the multi-level model dominates at every horizon **iff eps_k < eps*(L^k - 1)/(L - 1); for L = 1, iff eps_k < k*eps**. Checked: VC-A1..A5. Reduction: induction on the step count; machine-checked on 2026-11-13 by the Lean 4 kernel for every step count, jump length and horizon (lean/LEAN_RESULT_HIER.md: compose_bound, compose_bound_attained, jump_bound, jump_beats_compose_iff, jump_beats_compose_iff_one).

  • v8F: the 2 s world-model skill is negative while the 0.2 s skill is zero. That is the signature of composition error. MEASURED in the linear class (WMCEIL part d, T0): the direct 2 s error is 0.416 [0.389, 0.449] / 0.433 of ten times the one-step error, and the composed error is above the direct error on every fold. The criterion holds with margin and strengthens with horizon (JUMPCURVE: 0.74, 0.57, 0.52, 0.42 at 0.4, 0.8, 1.0, 2.0 s). The realised penalty of composing a well-fitted linear step is small (composed over direct error 1.07 / 1.06 at 2 s); the learned network is the one whose ten-step rollout fails (-0.15 / -0.17 against +0.17 / +0.16 for a closed-form stack, wm/VERDICT_WMSTACK.md).

T2A decision before the trajectory: exact risk split, and when it cannot help.

For any plan c that is constant on (context, mode) groups: sum w*||y - c||^2 = within-mode scatter + sum w*||mu_mode - c||^2. Checked: VC-B1, VC-B2. Reduction: linearity of finite sums; machine-checked on 2026-11-13 by the Lean 4 kernel for outcomes in any real inner-product space, any weights and any finite set of modes (lean/LEAN_RESULT_HIER.md: risk_split_one, risk_split; the corollary below is mean_is_optimal).

  • Corollary (non-dominance): under squared error and equal information, a selector that reads only the context cannot beat the conditional mean. A hierarchy therefore pays through three channels only: (a) supervised intermediate labels (the typed tactical code adds label bits per clip that a flat regressor never sees), (b) an acceptance region that is not convex, (c) inputs that enter at the upper level (nav).
  • Channel (b), checked as VC-B3 and VC-B4: with two modes at distance D, minority weight p, mode radius r and tolerance tol, if p*D > tol + r the averaged plan misses every outcome of both modes, while a decided plan hits its mode whenever r <= tol. In Lean 4 (lean/LEAN_RESULT_HIER.md) this holds in any normed space (decided_hits_mean_misses) and is widened from the averaged plan to EVERY single plan: with the modes further apart than 2*tol + 2*r, all outcomes that one plan hits lie in one mode (flat_hits_one_mode), so a single plan hits at most as many outcomes as the larger mode holds (flat_hit_count_le); a decided plan hits every outcome whose mode was selected correctly (decided_hit_count_ge); hence, if the selector is right on more outcomes than the larger mode holds, the decided plan hits strictly more outcomes than every single plan (hierarchy_dominates_flat). This is the dominance statement of the programme for the planning hierarchy in its exact, conditional form: the premise is selection accuracy above the share of the larger mode at a mode separation above 2*tol + 2*r, and where the premise fails the theorem is silent. Premise for v8F: OPEN (top-1 57-59 % is measured; the share of the larger mode per context and the mode separation against a tolerance are not yet tabulated on the eval windows).

T6A yardstick for the world model.

Skill over persistence equals the predictable fraction of the change (VC-H1). For a single AR(1) feature with lag correlation rho, its own past gives skill (1 - rho)/2 (VC-H2); a predictor that reads all features and the actions can exceed that value. So a pooled, slowly varying target has little to predict at 0.2 s, and a model that is negative at 2 s is below a closed-form linear predictor, which can never be negative in sample.

T7Imagination is admissible only above twice the model gap.

If the imagined and true returns differ by at most G, an imagined gain above 2G is a real gain (VC-D1, M-D1 refuted), and choosing the candidate with the lower imagined cost costs at most 2G (VC-D2). G follows from T1. PUBLISHED (MBPO, Janner et al. 2019, arXiv 1906.08253, Theorem 4.1, banked): eta[pi] >= eta_model[pi] - [2*gamma*rmax*(eps_m + 2*eps_pi)/(1-gamma)^2 + 4*rmax*eps_pi/(1-gamma)], eps_m the model error and eps_pi the policy shift in total variation. Its Theorem 4.2 bound is largest at rollout length 0; model use is only motivated when the model error is measured under the updated policy and stays small (Theorem 4.3, model term k*eps_m/(1-gamma)).
