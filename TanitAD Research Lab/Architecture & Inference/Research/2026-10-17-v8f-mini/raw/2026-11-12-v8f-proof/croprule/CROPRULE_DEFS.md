Tier T1-kin/hb0.2 (kinematic rollout on the model's own actions), pre-roll of 30 steps (6 s), UE_s0 and UE_s1. The card file and the kit of card ADD97 (`smoothgap/`) were read, not edited. Everything is in `F:/TanitAD-scratch/v8proof/croprule/`. No result file or dump of real data was opened; all runs are on synthetic data.

## 0. FIRST: clauses of the card that could fail by construction, and what the code cannot do as written

1. **q0, "every dump row has its join row; clip order and frame counts agree", is implemented as written and is the clause that can fail by construction.** The present reader's dump has one row per TOKEN frame `T` of a clip (`closeread/closeread_dump.py:164-182`), and the label code there allows `T > n` (the join's frames of the clip: `closeread_dump.py:93`, `if j >= n: continue`; the ADD97 kit therefore restricted its p0 to the label frames and counted only those). If any clip has more token frames than join frames, q0 fails on `n_rows_without_join_row` and `n_frame_count_mismatch_clips`, and with it the whole card (no map of arms to controls). I could not check T against n (no real data opened). The lead can look at the two counts in the record's `dumps.<ARM>_s<k>.e0` before anything else. I did not loosen the clause. Not in the card but kept from the ADD97 p0: the label gap of the dump must equal the join's lead gap within 1e-4 m on every label frame (a misalignment check; `n_label_frames_compared == n_label_frames`).
2. **q1 has no bar on the frames with a valid label gap and no pilot output**: the card says "counted", so they are counted (`n_without_pilot_output`, `fraction_without_pilot_output`) and printed, and the 5 percent bar of card ADD97 is not carried over. H5 has no output at frames 0 to 3 of a clip (`crop_dump.py:29-35`), so the count is not zero.
3. **H.** The card prints H = 0.0477; the names use H = half of the point value of C(RR) minus C(RLc), as the rule of card ADD97 does (q3 fixes both to four decimals, so they differ by at most 5e-5). A name of KB5 or KD5 could differ between the two only for an interval end inside that sliver; the names stage writes `h_note` (the name of both arms under both values, `differs`), no name is changed by it.
3b. **q2** is the check of the wrapper hash against the ADD95 pod check record, as in ADD97 (`ctl_q2`); "the rule-join argument unchanged" is covered by the rollout function being byte equal to the ones of ADD97, ADD96 and the model queue (`test_croprule.py`), not by a separate runtime check. **q4** reads the text of `lead_pytest.out` ("N passed", no "failed"/"error"); the code cannot know who ran it.
4. The card's logging of the controls in `TIMES_NOTE.md` before the names is the lead's step (no code).
5. With one control failed nothing is opened: the names stage refuses unless all six controls q0 to q5 are present and PASS and a payload exists; the controls stage writes the values under `withheld` and no strategic reading when q5 fails.

## 2. Definitions with sources
* **Arms.** KB5 = dump folder `B_H5`, KB1 = `B_H1`, KD5 = `D_H5`, KD1 = `D_H1` (`make_crop_joins.py:26`), under `/workspace/v8proof/cropread/run1/dump/<folder>/reader_s<K>_score.npz`; the join of model seed k reads fit seed k. Joins `<ARM>_s<k>.npz`, eight different files (no pair is shared, unlike E and ES25 of ADD97).
* **Dump fields** `cid, frame, clips, gap, valid, lab_gap, lab_present, clip_index, seed`: `cropread/fit/FIT_DEFS.md` section 2, `cropread/fit/crop_dump.py:54-70`. `gap` is the head's output in metres, `valid` = present valid AND finite gap.
* **Mix frame** (`valid_mask` :48, `pilot_read_mask` :69): label gap valid (`lead_valid == 1`, finite, 0 to 60 m: `distrule/v8f_t1_eval_rule.py:88`, `LabelSource._ok`) AND a pilot output (dump `valid`, finite `gap`, frame inside the join's rows of its clip). On a mix frame the saved `lead_gap` is the dumped gap (float32, the dtype of the join): no q50 map, no minimum, no smoothing, no mean. Every other frame and every other field is the evaluation join's. Values outside 0 to 60 m are kept and counted (`n_mixed_below_0`, `n_mixed_above_60`, `min_mixed`, `max_mixed`).
* **q0** (`q0_check` :311, `e0_stats` :74): for all eight dumps: dump seed equals k; every dump row has its join row (`n_rows_without_join_row == 0`); every clip name is in the join; `clip_index` equals the join row of the clip name on every row; per dump clip the number of rows equals the join's frame count (`n_frame_count_mismatch_clips == 0`); no (clip, frame) key twice; the dump's label gap equals the join's lead gap within 1e-4 m on every label frame and none of those frames is invalid in the join. Counted, no bar: `n_join_clips_not_in_dump`.
* **q1** (`q1_check` :340, `measure_file` :228): per join: the saved gap on every mix frame equals `second_routine` (a plain loop clip by clip and frame by frame from the dump alone) within 1e-4 m (holds for gaps up to 1,638 m in float32); frames with a valid label gap and no pilot output carry the label gap (`n_label_frames_without_output_not_label_gap == 0`) and are counted; every other field equals the evaluation join (value, dtype, shape) and `lead_valid` is equal; no frame outside the mix frames changed; the dump is the folder of the arm and the file of the model seed, with sha256; the record is bound to the evaluation join and to every join file by sha256 (`ctl_q1`).
* **Planted faults** (selftest and tests): other model seed, other head, other kind (bound/fixed), a minimum with the label gap, a causal smoothing, a one-row shift, a scale of 0.9, a replacement outside the mix frames, zero on the no-output frames, another field changed (all turn q1 red); a label gap off by 0.01 m, a wrong clip index, a row beyond the join, missing rows, a duplicate row, a wrong dump seed, an unknown clip name (turn q0 red).
* **q2, q3, q4, q5** as the ADD97 reader: `ctl_q2` (CHECK_WRAPPER_J_DONE.txt last line `ZZ CHECK-WRAPPER-J PASS` and wrapper hash cbf7063d867c9f4e in `CHECK_WRAPPER_J.hashes`); `ctl_q3` (round to 4 decimals: C(RR) 0.0954, C(RLc) 0.0000, C(K100) 0.0477, recomputed from the banked rollouts in the controls stage); `ctl_q4`; `ctl_q5` (grid read ALL_PASS and the strategic grid stage A and B).
* **Names** (`names_of`, card section 3): per arm four: `CROPRULE10K-<ARM>-` + `CUTS-CONTACT | CONTACT-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-RR | MORE-CONTACT` (interval of C(X)-C(RR)); `WITHIN-HALF-THE-GAP` (upper end <= H) `| HALF-THE-GAP-NOT-REACHED` (lower end > H) `| HALF-THE-GAP-NOT-DECIDED` (C(X)-C(RLc)); `LESS-CONTACT-THAN-K100 | MORE-CONTACT-THAN-K100 | CONTACT-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-K100`; `FALLS-BEHIND-MORE | FALLS-BEHIND-LESS | FALLING-BEHIND-NOT-SEPARATED` (F(X)-F(RR), falling behind by more than 10 m). Card names (`card_names`): `CROPRULE10K-BOUND-<half>` from KB5 and `CROPRULE10K-FIXED-<half>` from KD5; `card_check` derives both a second time from the intervals of the payload and the names stage refuses on a mismatch. Estimator: the engine's paired differences, two-level bootstrap over model seeds and evaluation episodes, 2,000 draws (`--n-boot`, default 2000; the engine controls of the controls stage use 300 as in ADD97).
* **Printed with no name** (in the payload): unsafe rate U (levels and pairs), values per model seed (`levels_per_seed`), the size of the pilot's error on the join's frames (`pilot_gap_on_the_join_frames.<join>.error_statistics_pilot_minus_label`), the frames on which the gap leaves 0 to 60 m, the share of the contact gap, differences against K100 (C, U, F).
* **Families:** LONGITUDINAL (C, U, F, the engine's pair read), LATERAL and TACTICAL (engine pair read against the logged drive, `families`), STRATEGIC read inside the reader under the engine's own controls for every arm against RR and RLc (`strategic`); with q5 failed no strategic reading is written.
* **Queue:** root `${GR_ROOT:-/workspace/v8proof/croprule10k}`; check folder `${GR_CHECK:-/workspace/v8proof/gaprule10kb/check_j2}`; joins `$GP/joins`; markers `START_CROPRULE10K_s<k>.txt`, `CROPRULE10K_DONE_s<k>.txt`, `CROPRULE10K_STOP_s<k>.txt`; log `queue_croprule_s<k>.log`; run folders `$W/t1/I10RLcKF5P30Hb20p<ARM>_s<m>`; the admission reads the record (`q0.PASS`, `q1.PASS`, the hash of the evaluation join and of all eight joins); the banked RR, RLc and K100 runs are required.
