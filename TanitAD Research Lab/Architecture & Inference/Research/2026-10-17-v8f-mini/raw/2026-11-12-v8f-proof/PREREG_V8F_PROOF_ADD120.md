## 1. Why this card

Row 182: at the convention the unsafe rate of the label-free candidate above the label form (0.0569 against 0.0320) has two parts, the camera gap value and the reader's presence. Row 183: a margin of 10 % on the gap closes the unsafe rate under readout K at a price, and does not close it at the convention. Row 184: a presence head on existing tokens, joined with the reader by OR, misses 0.0338 of the lead frames against the reader's 0.1558, with 0.3217 extra marks against 0.2244. This card measures what that presence input does in the rollout, alone and together with the margin, and whether the candidate with both levers reaches the label form at the convention.

Stated guess before data: at the convention the presence input lowers the unsafe rate by about 0.005, not separated, and raises falling behind (the extra marks); with the margin of 10 % the unsafe rate comes to about 0.040, above the bound of the label form plus 0.0045. Under readout K the presence input leaves the unsafe rate unmoved (row 181) and raises falling behind.

## 3. Measures and estimator

Per setting on its 642 windows with a lead per model seed: contact C, unsafe U, falling behind by more than 10 m F; levels pooled and per model seed for PO, POA, GEc, A90, PL, RLc, RR. Pairs per setting, paired on the same windows, two-level bootstrap over model seeds and evaluation episodes, 2,000 draws: PO − GEc (the presence lever), PO − PL (distance to label presence at the same gap), POA − A90 (the presence lever on top of the margin), POA − GEc (both levers), POA − RLc (the candidate with both levers against the label form), GEc − RLc (identity with rows 179 and 181).

Four families per setting as in card ADD117: LONGITUDINAL (the measures above and the engine read of each pair), LATERAL and TACTICAL (engine family reads of each pair), STRATEGIC (the strategic grid at T1-kin for each pair, position error at the marks of 30, 50 and 80 m). The interval answers what another draw of episodes and of these two seeds would say, not what another training run or another head fit would say.

## 6. Committed readings

* **BOTH-LEVERS-REACH-LABEL-FORM (either form) at CONV** → the label-free candidate with the joined presence and the margin of 10 % is put to the PI and the other session as the proposed form of the lead rule's inputs, with its price table at both settings; the next steps named there are the aged settings for this form and further model seeds of the 10k line (a PI decision).
* **PRESENCE-LOWERS-UNSAFE at CONV, both levers do not reach** → the presence input is kept as a study-arm option with its measured effect and price; the remaining distance is the gap value, and a more exact gap read is a new encode (the PI's decision, with its cost).
* **PRESENCE-UNSAFE-NOT-SEPARATED or PRESENCE-RAISES-FALLING-BEHIND with no lowered unsafe rate at CONV** → reported as written: the bar at T0 was met and the lever does not carry into the rollout at this number of windows; the presence lever with existing tokens ends here, and what remains is stated as the limit of both inputs.
* K0: by row 181 the presence input is not expected to move the unsafe rate there; whatever it does to falling behind is reported as the price of the extra marks.
* The two settings disagree → reported as written.
