# FINEGRID_DEFS: what the frozen card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD41 leaves open, and how the tool fills it

Written 2026-11-18 on the dev box (CPU, synthetic data only) while building `tok_probe_g.py`, `pod_finegrid_run.sh` and `finegrid_read.py`. The card is not edited. Tier T0 (a diagnostic on logged frames, never driving performance). LONGITUDINAL (distance keeping) is the family this lever serves and is not measured by this read; LATERAL, TACTICAL: not measured; STRATEGIC: NOT COMPUTED (a token probe reads no strategic quantity; n = 0). One line per decision; the ones marked REVIEW are the lead's to confirm before the first real number.

## Tool (`tok_probe_g.py`)
* **T0 Source tree.** `C:/Users/Admin/v8proof_c/leadsup/tree_patch` (the tree the banked tests point at) does not exist on the dev box now. REVIEW: tests use a rebuilt copy `finegrid/tmp/tree_leadsup` = `v8proof_c/target/tree_patch` plus `spatial/leadsup/patched/`; the five pinned files match `spatial/leadsup/PINS.txt` (sha256 equal). The tool code never names a tree: `--tree` is the pod's `/workspace/v8proof/tree_leadsup`.
* **T1 The lever.** `--grid pool4x10|pool8x20`, default `pool4x10`, on `train` and `extract`. In `train` the grid goes into the argv of the trainer's own data builder (the `--grid` of the given argv text is replaced, `GRID-ARGV` line), so `ClipStore` reads the cache key of that grid (`v8f_mini_data.py` ClipStore key = the flag value) and the trainer sizes the clip LRU by `cfg.n_tok` (`v8f_mini_train.py` line 2736). Nothing of the data path is re-implemented.
* **T2 Arm names.** `TK<W>[SH]_s<seed>` belong to pool4x10, `F<W>[SH]_s<seed>` to pool8x20; the other pairing is refused (`check_arm_grid`, at train, at extract, and in `build_probe`). Default arms: TK16_s0 TK16_s1 TK64_s0 TK64_s1 for pool4x10; F16_s0 F16_s1 F64_s0 F64_s1 for pool8x20. The shuffle arm is TK16SH_s0 / F16SH_s0 (W 16, seed 0).
* **T3 Control g5 (declared against built).** (a) the parsed trainer argv must carry the declared grid and `cfg.n_tok` must be 40 or 160 as declared (else REFUSED); (b) every training batch is checked as `[rows, frames, n_tok, 1024]` with frames at least 5 and rows at most the batch size (a sampler chunk can end with a short batch, so fewer rows is allowed, more is not; `TOKCOUNT-REFUSAL` line); (c) the checkpoint records `grid`, `n_tok` (the probe's) and `n_tok_seen` (the shape actually seen); (d) extract refuses a checkpoint whose grid differs from `--grid`, and at pool8x20 a checkpoint whose `n_tok_seen` is not 160; (e) extract refuses a clip whose cache key is missing or whose shape is not `[manifest frames, n_tok, 1024]`.
* **T4 Host memory.** The trainer's LRU is `max(8, 1.5e9 / (frames_max * n_tok * 1024 * 2))` clips, so the clip store stays near 1.5 GB at 160 tokens. The tool adds an ESTIMATE to `train_meta.json` (clip store + 5 batches of 16 frames + 1.5 GB for the process) and refuses above 8.0 GB; at 160 tokens the batch term is four times the 4 by 10 one (test).
* **T5 Extract memory.** The state array is filled in place from the manifest frame counts (one copy in memory, not two: the F64 train state is 121,166 x 10,240 x 2 B = 2.5 GB). A clip whose frame count differs from its manifest entry is refused. Numerics are the original's (test g1 compares the state and lead files of the two tools array by array).
* **T6 Control g2 as a subcommand.** `tok_probe_g.py g2` (CPU): the first 20 clips of the train manifest order (`D.read_manifest` order, the literal reading of "the first 20 of the manifest order", not the sha12 order the extract uses), 10 frames each at `unique(round(linspace(0, T-1, 10)))`, float8 stored values cast to float32; the 8 by 20 tokens are read row-major (token index = row * 20 + column) and averaged 2 by 2 into the 4 by 10 layout (index = row * 10 + column). Correlation = Pearson over all elements pooled; the median and the maximum absolute difference are reported; the column-major reading is printed beside (`alt_colmajor_corr`) and gates nothing. REVIEW: the row-major order is an assumption about the encoder script (not on the dev box); a low correlation with a high `alt_colmajor_corr` would mean the grid is stored column-major, and the card then says "another cached grid".
* **T7 Input normalisation.** `--input-norm ln` is the form of note ADD27_N1 and is passed by the pod script on every train line.
* **T8 Checkpoint compatibility.** A checkpoint of `tok_probe.py` has no `grid` key and is read as pool4x10 (g1 test extracts the banked tool's checkpoint with both tools).

## Pod script (`pod_finegrid_run.sh`)
* **P1 Slot probe.** One probe = fewer than 3 lines of `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps=pid --format=csv,noheader | grep -c .` AND container free memory (`memory.max` minus the `anon` line of `memory.stat`, whole GB) strictly above 28 GB. Two probes 45 s apart must both pass; failed probes repeat every 60 s up to 10 h, then `FINEGRID_STOP.txt`. An unreadable `nvidia-smi` counts as 99 jobs (busy); `memory.max` = `max` falls back to MemAvailable; an unreadable `anon` counts as 0 GB free.
* **P2 g2 runs before the GPU step**, on the CPU (`CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1`), writes `$OUT/G2_GRIDS.json`, and never stops the run (the reader applies the bar).
* **P3 Markers.** Refuses (exit 3, nothing written) over `FINEGRID_DONE.txt`; an old `FINEGRID_STOP.txt` is moved to `FINEGRID_STOP.prev.<time>.txt`. No process-name test anywhere; no `rm`.
* **P4 Dev-box knobs** (`DEVICE`, `G2_ARGS`, `TRAINER_ARGV`, `CG_MAX`, `CG_STAT`, `PROBE_GAP_S`, `POLL_S`, `MAX_WAIT_S`, `STEPS_EXPECT`, ranges) exist only to test the script on CPU; the defaults are the card's and the pod's.
* **P5 Pod volume.** ESTIMATED from the sizes of the banked TK states (same arithmetic, 4 times the tokens): state files F16 0.70 GB per seed, F64 2.78 GB per seed, F16SH 0.70 GB: 7.65 GB. The card says under 8 GB; this leaves little room. The same 7.65 GB land on the dev box when the reader is fed.

## Reader (`finegrid_read.py`)
* **R1 Imports.** `tokprobe_read.read_arm`, `paired_read_controls`, `arm_files` and, through it, `leadsup_read_n1` (fid_one for M1 and M2, auroc_row, pair_m1, control_k1 to k4, check_records, the bootstrap). Nothing of M1, M2, M4 is re-implemented; a test asserts it.
* **R2 Gap MAE.** Mean absolute error of the head's gap output at S0 on the windows of M2 (`valid` of the context: scored with a lead, finite label, finite output, inside an episode; n = 413 for TK16_s0 in the banked read), with an episode-cluster interval on the same draw. The clause of section 5 reads the point MAEs of one seed index.
* **R3 Paired differences.** G16 = F16_sK minus TK16_sK, G16w = F16_sK minus TK64_sK, G64 = F64_sK minus TK64_sK; each on M2 (R2), on the gap MAE (metres, negative = F better; `rel` is the difference over the TK MAE) and on M1; B 2,000, rng 0, the same windows and the same episode draw (`RP.boot_counts` of the context); M1 is the imported `pair_m1`, M2 is the same estimator with the gap target (a test equals it to `pair_m1` for the closing target).
* **R4 Floor.** Largest `|M2(s0) - M2(s1)|` over the four arm types (TK16, TK64, F16, F64); the same for M1. "Each mean is larger than the floor": the mean over the two seeds of G16, and separately of G16w, on M2, strictly above the floor. REVIEW: the reading of "each mean".
* **R5 Names.** READS-GAP: G16 and G16w on M2 above 0 with the lower bound above 0 at both seeds, each mean above the floor, and `MAE(F16_sK) <= 0.9 * min(MAE(TK16_sK), MAE(TK64_sK))` at both seeds (exactly 10 % below counts). WORSE: the same four differences below 0 with the upper bound below 0 (no floor, no MAE clause). Else FLAT; checked in that order. READS-CLOSING: the four M1 differences above 0 with the lower bound above 0 and each M1 mean above the M1 floor, no MAE clause; otherwise the closing line reads "none". G64, M4, the in-sample reads and the gap-range table name nothing.
* **R6 Controls first.** Gated: g1, g3, g4 and the forward part of g6 (dev-box results in `controls_devbox.json`, handed in with `--controls-json`; a missing one counts as not passed), g2 (file, corr at least 0.99, exactly 20 clips and 200 frames), g5 (state records of every F arm and the shuffle arm: grid, 160 tokens, 160 seen, width 160 x W), p3 (closing R2 of F16SH_s0 at most +0.02), p5 per arm, g7 (all reader controls on every F arm: a1 to a5, k1, k2, k3 closing, k3 slot N1, k4, the paired-read controls; for the TK arms the record checks and the reproduction below). `k3_card_form_slot` is printed and gates nothing (note ADD24_N1). The names, the paired differences, the floor and the gap-range table are computed and written only if every gated control passed; otherwise `names` is null and the outcome differences are withheld (per-arm reads are still written).
* **R7 g7 reproduction.** Every numeric leaf of M1, M2 (head) and M4, on the scored windows and on the train probe clips, of each TK arm read here from the banked head outputs against `tokprobe/out/RESULT_TOKPROBE.json`: maximum absolute difference exactly 0.0. The states of the TK arms are read from `moved_from_c/tokstates` (the `tokprobe/in` folder holds only their records and head outputs); `--tk-states-dir` overrides.
* **R8 p5 form.** The reader computes p5 as the card words it (lead term of the last 1,000 steps below the first 1,000; gap-labelled frames above 0 at every logged step, the instantaneous count). The tool's stricter interval-minimum count is printed beside and gates nothing: on the banked TK run it failed in 21 of 384 intervals while the card form passed (`tokprobe/controls_lead.json`).
