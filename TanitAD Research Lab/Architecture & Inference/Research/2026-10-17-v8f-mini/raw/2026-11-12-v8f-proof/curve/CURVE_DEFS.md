* R2: full-kernel ridge strength scale is trace(Kc)/d with d = number of training frames; the card says only "ridge strength by the same inner split".
* R3: E is scaled to the rank-256 score variance of the same fit set at EVERY rank and in the full kernel (RESPROBE convention at 256), so the ego weight does not change with rank.
