## 1. A1 — beyond-ego probes (ENCODED)

**Targets** (per frame t; NaN where undefined; counts reported per target):
1. `lead_present` (binary): join `lead_valid` = 1.
2. `lead_gap` [m]: join `lead_gap` on lead frames.
3. `lead_closing` [m/s]: (gap(t-2) - gap(t)) / 0.4 s where both lead-valid; |value| > 20 set NaN (track switch).
4. `lead_rel_acc` [m/s^2]: (closing(t) - closing(t-5)) / 1.0 s where both defined.
5. `gap_change_2s` [m]: gap(t+10) - gap(t) where both lead-valid (the lead's near future; predictable-from-now target).
6. `ag1_pos`, `ag2_pos`, `ag3_pos` [m]: rig-frame (x, y) of the k-th nearest agent in the camera's field (x > 0.5 m, |atan2(y, x)| <= 60 deg, range <= 50 m), raw frame 2t of the agent join; a 2-D target, R^2 = 1 - SSE(x, y) / SST(x, y).
7. `ag1_vel` [m/s]: apparent rig-frame velocity of agent 1, (p(2t) - p(2t-4)) / 0.4 s by track id (includes ego-induced motion; the EGO control absorbs the ego part).
8. `curv_ahead` [1/m]: wrap(yaw(2t+40) - yaw(2t)) / arc length over the next 4 s of the native track, where arc >= 5 m.
9. `lon7`, `lat_cls` (classes): join `lon_cls`, `lat_cls` where `has` and >= 0.
10. `tl_red_vs_green` (binary, beside only): clips whose v7.2 eval record carries `TRAFFIC_LIGHT_REACT_RED` (1) or `_GREEN` (0), frames within +-1 s of the record's anchor `t0_s`; n is small (8 red, 15 green clips), it decides nothing.
11. `speed` (ego, positive anchor): cache v at t; `accel`, `yaw_rate` beside.

**Probes:** (P-LIN) ridge on [state, EGO] against ridge on [EGO], alpha by inner 3-fold CV on the training folds only, from {0.1, 1, 10, 100, 1000}; logistic for binary and classes. (P-MLP) a small temporal MLP (one hidden layer of 64, early stopping on a 10 % split of the training folds) on [state at t-4..t, EGO] against the same MLP on [EGO]; run for L1, L2, L3 and TOK-MEAN on targets 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (k = 1) and speed. **Split:** 5 folds by clip (fold = int(sha256(clip_id)[:8], 16) mod 5), out-of-fold predictions pooled. **Metric:** selectivity dR^2 = R^2(state + EGO) - R^2(EGO); dAUROC for binary; d balanced accuracy for classes.

**Labels per (representation, target, probe):** ENCODED-BEYOND-EGO if the seed mean of the metric >= the bar AND the 95 % CI lower bound > 0 at each of the 3 seeds; PARTIAL if the lower bound > 0 at all 3 seeds and the mean is below the bar; NOT-ENCODED otherwise. **Bars:** dR^2 >= 0.10 for `lead_gap` and `ag1_pos` (the plan's proposed bar, PI to confirm, decision 3 of the plan); 0.05 for every other target (this card's choice). The P-LIN label is the primary label; a cell that passes only under P-MLP is labelled ENCODED-TEMPORAL-ONLY.

**Controls (A1 is INVALID for a seed if one misses):** c1 shuffled label: the target of each clip replaced by another clip's (a fixed derangement of clips, frames aligned by index, NaN pattern kept), P-LIN on L1 and TOK-GRID: |dR^2| <= 0.02 for `lead_gap`, `ag1_pos`, `speed`. c2 planted variable: y = (w . L1 residualised on EGO over all frames, w a fixed random unit vector) + Gaussian noise scaled for a population R^2 of 0.5: read-back dR^2 >= 0.40. c3 noise state: an i.i.d. N(0, 1) state of the same width in place of L1 and of TOK-GRID: dR^2 <= 0.02 on every continuous target. c4 alignment: R^2(EGO -> speed at t) >= 0.95.

## 2. A2 — ego-matched counterfactual pairs (IMAGINED, cleanest "not only ego" test)

**Pairs:** frames t (clip i) and t' (clip j != i), both lead-valid at t and t+10, with matched ego history: max over the 11 frames t-10..t of |dv| <= 0.5 m/s and |d yaw rate| <= 0.05 rad/s, and |da(t)| <= 0.5 m/s^2. **DIFF pairs:** |gap_change_2s(i) - gap_change_2s(j)| >= 2.0 m; **SAME pairs (control):** <= 0.3 m. At most 5 pairs per clip pair (the first 5 in frame order); frames with t >= 10 and t + 10 <= 100. Sub-read beside: PURE pairs = DIFF pairs whose present gap differs by <= 3 m and present closing by <= 1 m/s.

**Scorers** (each gives a predicted gap_change_2s per member; out-of-fold in clip folds): IMAG-L1 = gap probe (P-LIN on L1 + EGO, fitted on encoded frames) applied to the L1 rollout k = 10 minus the same probe on z(t); IMAG-L2 = the same at L2 with k = 5 (2.0 s); rollouts use context 1 (the planning form) under a COMMON action for both members (the pair mean of the raw (a, yaw rate) at t, held for the horizon), so the ego's logged reaction to the lead cannot enter; ENC-L1 = ridge [L1(t), EGO] -> gap_change_2s (the present state's knowledge of the lead's future); ORACLE-L1 = probe on the encoded true future z(t+10) minus probe on z(t); EGO = ridge [EGO] -> gap_change_2s.

**Metric:** pair-discrimination AUC = share of pairs whose score difference has the sign of the true difference (ties 0.5); CI by a two-way clip bootstrap (a pair enters with weight = product of its two clips' multiplicities), B 2,000. **Labels per scorer:** DISCRIMINATES if the AUC lower bound > 0.5 at all 3 seeds AND the paired AUC(scorer) - AUC(EGO) lower bound > 0 at all 3 seeds; BLIND if the AUC CI contains 0.5 at >= 1 seed; otherwise WEAK. **Controls:** c5 SAME pairs: every scorer's AUC CI contains 0.5; c6 matching works: AUC(EGO) on DIFF pairs <= 0.60 (point), else A2 reads MATCH-FAILED and no label is given; c7 swap: exchanging the members of every pair gives 1 - AUC exactly.

## 3. A3 — imagination skill per variable (IMAGINED)

Anchors t = 10, 12, 14, ... with every needed future frame inside the clip. Rollout of each level from its encoded state at t under the LOGGED actions (raw (a, yaw rate) scaled by `wm.scale_actions`; macro-actions above level 1 from `stack.encode`, which reads actions only), context 1. Horizons: L1 k = 5, 10 (1, 2 s); L2 k = 5, 15 (2, 6 s); L3 k = 5, 15 (4, 12 s). Variables: `speed`, `lead_gap`, `ag1_pos`, `lead_closing`. Probe = P-LIN on the level's encoded state alone (no EGO), fitted out-of-fold on encoded frames. Errors against the true label at t+h: IMAG (probe on the rollout), COPY (probe on z(t)), ORACLE (probe on the encoded true future z(t+h)). Skill = 1 - MSE(IMAG) / MSE(COPY); interval = paired bootstrap of MSE(COPY) - MSE(IMAG). **Labels per (level, horizon, variable):** UNREADABLE if the ORACLE R^2 < 0.10 at the seed mean; else IMAGINES if the lower bound > 0 at all 3 seeds; WORSE-THAN-COPY if the upper bound < 0 at all 3 seeds; COPIES if the interval contains 0 at all 3 seeds; SEEDS-DISAGREE otherwise. "Imagines the world" (plan) needs IMAGINES on an agent variable (`lead_gap`, `ag1_pos`, `lead_closing`), not only on `speed`. Control c8: a rollout of 0 steps equals COPY exactly (MSE difference 0.0).

## 4. A4 — action counterfactuals (IMAGINED under the ego's own action)

On the A3 anchors, rollouts under the logged actions plus an offset held over the horizon: LON +-1.0 m/s^2 on channel a; LAT +-0.1 rad/s on the yaw-rate channel. Read at 2 s: L1 k = 10, L2 k = 5; L3 k = 3 (2.4 s) beside. Responses through the A3 probes (difference of the + and - rollouts): `lead_gap` (lead anchors) expected -(2.0) h^2 / 2 = -h^2; `speed` expected +2.0 h; `ag1_pos` y-coordinate under LAT expected -(v(t) * 0.2 * h^2 / 2 + x1(t) * 0.2 * h) (the agent shifts right when the ego turns left). Slope = sum(measured) / sum(expected) over anchors; bootstrap CI. **Labels:** KINEMATIC if the seed-mean slope is in [0.5, 1.5] and the lower bound > 0 at all 3 seeds; WEAK if the lower bound > 0 at all 3 seeds and the mean < 0.5; OVERSHOOT if lower > 0 at all seeds and mean > 1.5; WRONG-SIGN if the upper bound < 0 at all 3 seeds; INERT otherwise. Control c9: an offset of 0 gives a response of exactly 0.0. (PROOF-68 read the LS arm WRONG-SIGN through a supervised head; here the read is through probes on RH, which has no lead head.)

## 5. A5 — use attribution by masking (USED)

On eval windows with a lead within 40 m in the field of view, the lead's token cells in the 4 x 10 pool grid are found by projecting its 3-D box (agent join, the agent with x > 0 and |y| <= 2.0 m nearest to the ego) through the cylindrical front-camera model (azimuth -> column, 120 deg across 10 columns; elevation -> row, by the tool's camera height and pitch, written in its IMPL note before any plan is read). LEAD mask = those cells; CONTROL mask = the same number of cells in the same rows at the column mirrored about the image centre, shifted outward when it overlaps the LEAD cells or holds any agent within 50 m. Masking = replacing a cell's input token by that cell's mean token over all eval-141 frames (primary; zeroing beside). Read: the plan head's planned speed at 2.0 s (one planning call of the model on the window, logged nav and history, as the T1 evaluator builds the batch). **Metric:** |dv(LEAD)| - |dv(CONTROL)| per window, paired bootstrap. **Labels:** USED if the lower bound > 0 at all 3 seeds; NOT-USED if the interval contains 0 at >= 1 seed; signed read beside (masking a close lead should raise the planned speed). **Controls:** c10 manipulation check: a lead-presence probe (P-LIN on TOK-GRID) must drop more under the LEAD mask than under the CONTROL mask (paired lower bound > 0, pooled seeds), else A5 reads MASK-FAILED; c11 an empty mask gives dv = 0.0 exactly.

## 6. The Card and what each outcome triggers (ranking rule for Phase B, committed now)

The World Understanding Card is a table level (L1, L2, L3, TOK-GRID, TOK-MEAN) x variable x {ENCODED (A1, with dR^2 and CIs), IMAGINED (A2 AUC, A3 skill, A4 slope), USED (A5)}, with the three seed values and the row ids. Phase B levers are ranked by the size of the measured shortfall on the bar variables (`lead_gap`, `ag1_pos`), largest first (Rule Zero point 5), under this map:
* gap / agent NOT-ENCODED at L1 to L3 but ENCODED-BEYOND-EGO in TOK-GRID -> **B1 keep the objects** (grid or object-slot world-model state) first: the information is in the tokens and the mean pool drops it.
* NOT-ENCODED in TOK-GRID too -> **B2 privileged auxiliary losses** and **B3 temporal context** first; B1 alone cannot add what the tokens lack.
* ENCODED-TEMPORAL-ONLY -> **B3** first.
* ENCODED but A3 COPIES or A2 BLIND -> **B4 anti-copy imagination** with B2.
* A4 WRONG-SIGN or INERT on the gap -> the action-consistency term of PROOF-76, anchored by the rollout loss.
* ENCODED in TOK-GRID and A5 NOT-USED -> **B5** (the planner's cost reads the gap) after B1.
Expected (HYPOTHESIS, from LATSEM2B-1 and PROOF-17, on older checkpoints): `speed` passes, the lead and the agents are at most PARTIAL on L1 to L3 and better on TOK-GRID, A3 COPIES, A5 open. A refutation of any expectation is reported as such and the next lever is read off this map in the same run.

## 7. Limits stated before the data

One evaluation corpus (eval-141, 141 clips, about 55 with a lead); the agent join covers 139 clips; traffic light is beside with n of about 23 clips; probes measure linear (and one small non-linear) decodability, so a NOT-ENCODED is a statement about these probes (a negative from a linear probe is not a negative about learnability); intervals carry episode draw, the three seeds carry training variance; RH was fine-tuned from UE (init sha 2fd4b747…), so the three seeds share a lineage per seed but not across seeds.
