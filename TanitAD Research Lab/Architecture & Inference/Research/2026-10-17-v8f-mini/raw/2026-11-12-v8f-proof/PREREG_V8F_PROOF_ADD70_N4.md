# Note N4 to card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD70 (LONGROLL10K): a fault of the read tool found AFTER the read of record, the repair, and the re-read

Written by the coordinator fork after the read under note N3 was opened, after row H-V8F-PROOF-128 was landed (hold 92, commit 99313c08) and after the fault below was found and logged (line "FAULT OF THE READ TOOL" in `TIMES_NOTE.md`). This note is NOT written blind: the names and values of the read under note N3 are known. It is written before the re-read is run (time: the line of this note in `TIMES_NOTE.md`; hash in the sidecar file). Arms, pairs, estimators, name rules and consequence rules of the card are unchanged.

## 1. The fault

The family reader and the headway reader derive the speed, the heading, the yaw rate, the curvature, the manoeuvre class and the time gap from the steps of a track, and they take step 1 from the frame origin (0, 0) to the first track point (`hierrep_read.kinematics` and `four_families._seq_geometry` prepend the origin; `headway_read_r2.step_speeds`, HEADWAY_DEFS: p_0 = (0, 0)). For a window started from the logged state this is right: the track starts at the origin of the logged frame at t0. The wrapper of this card writes the track of a pre-rolled window in that same logged frame (note N1, repair R3), where it starts at the carried offset. Step 1 of every pre-rolled window was therefore a jump of the size of the offset.

Probe on the pod (seed 0, read-only): distance of the first track point from the origin, median 2.10 m in a fresh run and 1.99 m in the state-carry control, against 6.02 m and 6.19 m in two pre-rolled runs whose second step is 2.0 m; the first step exceeds twice the second plus 0.5 m in 0.595 and 0.585 of the pre-rolled windows and in none of the fresh or carry windows.

Affected, in every pair of the card with a pre-rolled run: the unsafe rate U (its time gap divides the gap by the step speed, and one unsafe step makes the window unsafe), the speed error, the heading error, the yaw-rate error, the curvature error and the decision error rates. Not affected, because they use positions only: along-track and cross-track error, the tactical track error, the contact share, the falling-behind share, the gap bias, and the route goal of the strategic instrument. The controls of the card could not see it: the state-carry control has offset 0 by construction, and the pre-rolled runs had no known-value check of a step-derived cell.

Root-cause class: an implicit frame assumption of an instrument (the window starts at the origin of the frame it is scored in) broken by a change of the frame an upstream tool writes in. The frame mapping of the wrapper was checked; the reader's use of the mapped track was not.

## 2. The repair (no free parameter)

Wrapper `integ10k/read_longroll10k_n4.py` around the reader of note N3; no reader file is edited. The own start point p0 of every window is the own position minus the logged position at t0 in the logged frame, read from the tick file of the run (tick 0: `pose`, `pose_w0`). Every quantity derived from steps is computed on the track moved to its own start (track minus p0: steps 2 to 30 are unchanged, step 1 becomes the own first step). Every quantity that is a position in the logged frame is computed on the track as written. For a run without a pre-roll p0 is 0 and the reader is the reader of note N3.

Controls added (all must pass, else nothing is named): n1 every run without a pre-roll has a largest |p0| of at most 1e-6 m; n2 every pre-rolled run has a tick row for each dump row, and the first track point lies within 0.5 m plus one step of travel of p0 in at least 0.99 of its windows; n3 a planted case through the real engines: a straight track at 6 m/s started 5 m behind the logged origin with a lead 12 m ahead reads unsafe in every window and a speed error above 0.1 m/s with the engines as they were, and reads no window unsafe, speed, heading and yaw-rate error 0, along-track error 5 m and the same gaps with this reader; with p0 = 0 this reader equals the engines exactly. The controls of the card and of note N3 stay, among them the reader identity l6 on fresh runs.
