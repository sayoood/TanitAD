# Note N1 to card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD71 (AGERULE10K): the reader takes the own start point of a pre-rolled window (note N4 of card ADD70)

Written by the coordinator fork while the six rollouts of part A run and before any read of this card (no value of the card is opened; time: the line of this note in `TIMES_NOTE.md`; hash in the sidecar file). Arms, pairs, estimators, name rules and consequence rules of the card are unchanged.

1. After the freeze of this card a fault of the read tool of card ADD70 was found: the readers take step 1 of a track from the frame origin, and a pre-rolled window starts at its carried offset (note N4 of card ADD70, with the probe and the list of affected and unaffected quantities). Section 1 of this card quotes the unsafe rate at age (higher by 0.47 to 0.50) from the read under note N3 of card ADD70; that figure is not to be quoted from here, and the re-read under note N4 replaces it in the row of this card.
2. Part A is read with `integ10k/read_agerule10k.py` on top of `integ10k/read_longroll10k_n4.py` (step-derived quantities on the track moved to its own start, positions as written), with the controls n1 to n3 of that note. Control a1 compares with the result of card ADD70 as re-read under note N4 (pairs AGE-K and AGE-CONV). The reader landed in hold 92 imported the reader of note N3; it has not run on data.
3. Part B: the displaced fresh track d and the aged track a both start at the offset. The tool of part B applies the same rule (step-derived cells from the track moved to its own start) before its first run on data. With that rule the POSE part of a step-derived cell is the effect of the carried heading offset only, and the POSE part of a position cell is the carried position offset; the card's definitions of the two parts, of the names and of the controls b1 to b6 are unchanged.
4. The pod read of part A starts only after the re-read of card ADD70 under note N4 has passed its controls.
