# Note N2 to card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD71 (AGERULE10K), part B: control b4 stopped the read; what it showed, the form of record of the parts, and two statements fixed before a part is opened

Written by the coordinator fork after the read of part B ended with control b4 not passed and with no name and no part value written (`integ10k/read_agerule_decomp/pod/READ_AGERULE_DECOMP.json`: names and parts empty), and before any part value of a re-read is opened (time: the line of this note in `TIMES_NOTE.md`; hash in the sidecar file). Known at this time: the values of part A of this card (opened after its controls) and the total of the pair AGE-K-NAVLOGGED from the re-read of card ADD70 (row 129). No POSE part and no STATE part is known.

## 1. What the stopped read reported (controls only)

* b1, b2, b3, b5, b6 passed (zero offset is the identity; one metre ahead changes the along-track error by 1.0 m and no lateral cell; a quarter turn reads a heading error of 90 deg; the first point of every aged track lies at the offset of its tick file, share 1.0 at both seeds; the six dumps hold the same 1,271 windows in the order of the grid).
* b4 did not pass: the total (aged minus fresh) equals the banked total of the re-read of card ADD70 exactly (largest deviation 0.0), and POSE plus STATE is away from it by 0.0173 in one cell against the tolerance of 1e-9.

## 2. Why (probe on the pod, controls only: `podread/probe/probe_b4.py`, folder `podread/probe/decomp_b4/`; it prints per cell the deviation and the counts of windows with a value and keeps no part value)

The deviation is in the heading error alone (0.0173 deg); the nine other cells are at 3.6e-15 or below. The family engine pairs two tracks on the windows where their difference is finite. The heading error, the yaw rate error and the curvature error have no value in a window whose track does not move enough. Seed 0: the heading error has a value in 1,240 of the 1,271 windows of the fresh track, in 1,249 of the aged track and in 1,238 of both (seed 1: 1,244, 1,246, 1,239); yaw rate and curvature 1,237, 1,244, 1,234 (seed 1: 1,239, 1,240, 1,232). The displaced track has a value in exactly the windows of the fresh track (0 windows differ). The POSE pair was therefore read on 1,240 windows and the STATE pair and the total on 1,238. For yaw rate and curvature the POSE difference is 0 in every window, so the different count left their sum exact.

## 3. Form of record of the parts

Each cell is kept on the windows where it has a value in all three tracks (fresh, displaced, aged), per seed; the three pairs and STATE minus POSE are read on that one set of windows. Because the displaced track has a value exactly where the fresh track has, that set is the set of the banked total. Control b4 stays as written (1e-9 on the sum of the parts and on the total); nothing of the control is changed. The counts are written with the result (`common_windows_note_n2`). File `integ10k/read_agerule_decomp.py`: function `common_support` added and called before the parts are read (hash before and after in `TIMES_NOTE.md`); its self-test plants a case in which pairwise windows leave the sum away from the total and the common windows make it exact (44 of 44 checks).

## 4. Two statements fixed before a part is opened

* A limit of the decomposition that follows from its geometry and was not written in the card: a rigid move of a track leaves its step lengths and its turning unchanged. The POSE part of the speed error, the yaw rate error and the curvature error is 0 by identity; it is not a measured zero, and in those cells the whole cost of age is the STATE part by construction. The decomposition measures something on the along-track error, the cross-track error, the heading error, the tactical track error and the decision error rates. A family name is reported with this limit: for LONGITUDINAL (speed, along-track) and LATERAL (heading, yaw rate) the name POSE-LARGER cannot be read, and STATE-LARGER on such a family rests on the cell that can move (along-track; heading) as much as on the cell that cannot.
* The reading of the name BOTH. Section 3 of the card says: BOTH "when both parts exclude 0 on a named cell and neither is larger by that rule". The tool as built read "on every named cell". The card's wording is the record: BOTH is read when neither STATE-LARGER nor POSE-LARGER holds for the family and both parts exclude 0 on at least one named cell. The tool is changed to that wording before a part value is known; with "every" the name BOTH could not be read for LONGITUDINAL and LATERAL at all (item above).
