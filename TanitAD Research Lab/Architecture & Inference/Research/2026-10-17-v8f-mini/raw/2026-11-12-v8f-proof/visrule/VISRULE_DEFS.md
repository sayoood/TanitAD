## 0. FIRST: clauses of the card that could fail by construction, and what the code cannot do as written

1. **v1 can fail on rig noise or on the generation of the wrapper, not on the variant.** V0 (the variant with the new arguments off, form RR) is compared with the BANKED RR rollouts (`I10RQ50KF5P30Hb20`, queue `integ10k/pod_longroll10k_queue.sh:254`), which an earlier wrapper generation made. The pod check of this kit (`check_wrapper_v.py`, V2A) shows only that the variant with the arguments off equals the source wrapper (rzgfhqetj) on the pod on a few clips; it does not show that a fresh RR rollout equals a banked one (other pod state, GPU arithmetic, the generations between). Two windows of 650 per rate is the whole slack. **Reading chosen:** "within 2 windows of the 650 with a lead per rate" is applied per rate (C, U, F) AND per model seed, on the per-seed levels (`ctl_v1`: |V0 - RR| <= 2/650 for each of C, U, F and for each of the two seeds); the pooled differences are printed beside it. The card does not say per seed or pooled. A failed v1 fails the card with no name; I did not widen it.
2. **v2, second clause ("on the steps where the head has no output it equals the reader's own gap as RR feeds it").** The rule log of a rollout has what v2 needs: `ci`, `j`, `present`, `g_src` (the gap the rule received, before `g0 = g_src + a_l - a_m`) and `c_src` (the closing input the rule received), in the tick log `rule_log_<run>.npz` (`distrule/v8f_t1_eval_rule_mix.py:106-107`) and in the later-look log `rule_log_mid_<run>.npz` (`gaprule/...tj.py` `mid_looks`: `j=jq`, `g_src`, `c_src`, same keys); the later-look log exists because `--rule-every 5` is below the replan interval of 10. The reader's own gap as RR feeds it is read from the rule logs of **V0 at the same (clip index, frame)** (the RR form run by this wrapper), not from the banked RR logs (an older generation). A lead row of VD5 or VD5c with no head output that has no V0 lead row at that key, or differs from it by more than 1e-4 m, fails v2 (`n_ref_missing`, `n_bad_ref`). This is a reading of the card's sentence, not a loosening. The banked RR logs are read only for the printed shares, with V0 standing in (and the payload saying so) when they are not in the pull folder.
3. **v0 inherits the point of ADD101 section 0.1: a dump with more token frames than the join has frames fails v0 by construction.** The dump has one row per token frame (`closeread/closeread_dump.py:164-182`); v0 requires the rows of every dump clip to be the frames 0 to n-1 of its join clip once each (`n_frame_count_mismatch_clips == 0`, `n_rows_without_join_row == 0`, `n_dump_rows == n_join_rows`) and the dump's clips to be the join's 141 clips in the same order (`clips_same_order_as_join`, `n_join_clips == expected_clips`, `--expect-clips` default 141). One addition from the kit of ADD101 (not in the card): the dump's label gap equals the join's lead gap within 1e-4 m on every label frame. A second addition: the build check (the saved gap file equals a second routine written apart from the build within 1e-9, the output flag is the finiteness of the dumped gap, the rows cover the join once in order); the reader's v0 is the card's clause AND these (`ctl_v0`). Look at the two counts in `VISRULE_GAPFILES_RECORD.json` `dumps.VGAP_s<k>.e0` before anything else.
4. **"The head has an output" is read as: the dumped gap is finite** (frames 0 to 3 of a clip are NaN: `cropread/fit/crop_dump.py:29-35`). The dump's `valid` field (the present reader's own validity) is not used; `n_finite_but_dump_not_valid` is counted in the record. A frame the reader marks and the head has an output for is the same set as the frames from 4 on in practice (the reader needs five frames too: `_reader_at`, `e - (hist - 1) >= 0`), so a run of the closing slope does not mix the reader's own gap with the head's gap unless the dump's first finite frame is not 4 (`first_output_frame_per_clip_counts` shows it).
5. **The closing input needs a reading of "the run rule" for frames where the reader, not the label, marks the lead.** Chosen: a frame is in the run when the reader marks a lead on it (probability >= 0.5 and a full history: `feed`, `...tjv.py:237`) AND the gap the rule receives there (float32, as fed) is finite and in 0 to 60 m (the test of `LabelSource._ok`, `distrule/v8f_t1_eval_rule.py:88-90`). The current frame not in the run gives 0. The card is silent on the 0-to-60 m test; it is the label source's own, so it is applied, and a head output above 60 m or below 0 m ends a run. If the head's gap leaves 0 to 60 m often, VD5c reads closer to VD5 than the card's sentence suggests; `n_out_below_0`, `n_out_above_60` per file are in the record.
6. **The change sits after the reader's own map and minimum** (where exactly: section 2). The rule therefore receives the head's raw gap, which can be above 60 m (the rule is inactive above 60 m) or below 0.
7. **q-style clauses carried:** v3 and v5 read the engine as ADD101 does (`R.read_grid`, `R.strategic_grid`). I could not run the engine here. **Unverified:** whether an engine control inside `ALL_PASS` assumes that the closing column of an RR-form rule log is zero (`integ10k/read_closrc10k.py:112` has a closing-source test of that kind for another form); if it does, v5 fails by construction on VD5c. The lead sees it at the first `controls` read. v4 reads the text of `lead_pytest.out` ("N passed", no "failed"/"error"); the code cannot know who ran it. The card's logging of the controls in `TIMES_NOTE.md` before the names is the lead's step.
8. **H.** The card prints H = 0.0477; the names use H = half of the point value of C(RR) minus C(RLc) (as ADD101). `h_note` in the names result gives both.
9. With one control failed nothing is opened: the names stage refuses unless all of v0 to v5 are present and PASS and a payload exists; the controls stage writes the values under `withheld` when any control fails (the strategic grid is still computed there, as in ADD101, but nothing is printed from it).

## 2. Definitions with sources
* **The gap file** (`make_vis_gapfile.py:build_arrays`): one row per join frame, arrays `clip_index` (join row of the clip), `frame`, `gap` (float64, the head's output as dumped, NaN where none), `has_out`, `clips`, `seed`. Dump: `<dump-root>/D_H5/reader_s<k>_score.npz` (`cropread/fit/FIT_DEFS.md` section 2); the model seed k reads fit seed k. No map, no clipping, no minimum.
* **Where the change sits** (`...tjv.py`): after `feed()` (`:237`), which gives `present = (p >= 0.5) & ok` and `g_used = min(g_reader, Q(g_reader))` (the reader's own map Q and minimum). At the tick: `head_values_reader` (`:497`), edit V4 replaces `used` by `vis_tick(...)` before the log columns are written, so the side file `_rd.npz` column `g_used` carries the gap the rule received. At the later looks: `mid_looks` (`:814`), edit V6 does the same on `used` after its own `feed()` call. `vis_replace` (`:1460`) sets `used[present & has_out] = file gap`; every other row keeps the reader's own `used` (map and minimum applied). The reader's presence output `p` and the full-history flag `ok` are not changed, so the frames with a lead are the same as RR's.
* **The closing input** (`vis_run_closing`, `:1486`, `vis_look`, `:1511`): the arithmetic of `LabelSource.read` (`distrule/v8f_t1_eval_rule.py:92-112`): trailing run of at most 5 frames (`VIS_RUN_MAX`), a step above 3.0 m ends it (`VIS_STEP_MAX_M`), fewer than 3 frames gives 0 (`VIS_RUN_MIN`), least squares slope of the gap against `arange * 0.2 s`; the earlier four frames of each look are read again from the logged tokens (`_reader_at`, `:779`) and fed the same way (reader's presence, map or file gap). With `--vis-closing` the mix closing source is `head` (edit V9) and the slot carries this slope (edits V5 and V7); without it the slot is `zero` as in RR. It never reads the label join.
* **Arms:** VD5c = `--vis-gap-file VGAP_s<k> --vis-closing`; VD5 = `--vis-gap-file VGAP_s<k>`; V0 = neither (control v1). Runs `I10RQ50KF5P30Hb20v<ARM>_s<k>`, form RR, reader `lr16/TK16_s<k>`, q50 map `qmap_sweep/QMAP_LR16_s<k>_q50.json`, arguments of the banked RR call plus the wrapper. The rollout function of the queue is the ADD101 one plus five inserted lines (the two new arguments) and one longer run-text line; asserted in `make_visrule_queue.py` and in `test_visrule.py`.
* **Run record meta** (rule log keys): `vis_gap_file`, `vis_gap_file_sha256`, `vis_closing`, `vis_counts` (JSON: lead frames at the looks, from the file, no head output, no row in the file, rows with a non-zero closing input).
* **v0:** section 0 item 3. **v1:** item 1. **v2** (`v2_one`): per rollout of VD5c and VD5 (4 rollouts), tick log and later-look log together; every row with `present`: head output at its (ci, j) in the dump (a plain loop, `make_vis_gapfile.second_routine`, read from the dump itself, not from the gap file) -> |g_src - dumped| <= 1e-3; no output -> equals V0's g_src at the same key within 1e-4; VD5: `c_src == 0` on every row of both logs; also the arm is what it says (`vis_gap_file_sha256` equals the record's, `vis_closing` is 1 for VD5c, 0 for VD5). **v3:** `ctl_v3`, four decimals. **v4, v5:** item 7.
* **Names** (`names_of`, card section 3): per arm four: `VISRULE10K-<ARM>-` + `CUTS-CONTACT | CONTACT-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-RR | MORE-CONTACT` (C(X)-C(RR)); `WITHIN-HALF-THE-GAP` (upper end <= H) `| HALF-THE-GAP-NOT-REACHED` (lower end > H) `| HALF-THE-GAP-NOT-DECIDED` (C(X)-C(RLc)); `PRESENCE-COSTS-CONTACT` (lower end > 0) `| LESS-CONTACT-THAN-KD5` (upper end < 0) `| CONTACT-NOT-SEPARATED-FROM-KD5` (C(X)-C(KD5)); `FALLS-BEHIND-MORE | FALLS-BEHIND-LESS | FALLING-BEHIND-NOT-SEPARATED` (F(X)-F(RR)). Card name from VD5c (`card_names`): `VISRULE10K-WITHIN-HALF-THE-GAP | -HALF-THE-GAP-NOT-REACHED | -HALF-THE-GAP-NOT-DECIDED`, derived a second time from the interval of the payload (`card_check`); the names stage refuses on a mismatch. Estimator: the engine's paired differences, two-level bootstrap over model seeds and evaluation episodes, 2,000 draws (`--n-boot`; the engine controls of the controls stage use 300 as in ADD101).
* **Printed with no name** (payload): unsafe rate (levels and pairs U), levels per model seed, differences against KD5 (C, U, F), the share of rule steps with a lead in V0, VD5c, VD5 and RR, the share of frames with a valid label lead that the reader marks and the share of marked frames with no label lead (from the rows of RR), rows with a non-zero closing input per rollout.
* **Families:** LONGITUDINAL (C, U, F, the engine's pair read), LATERAL and TACTICAL (engine pair read against the logged drive, `families`), STRATEGIC read inside the reader under the engine's own controls for every arm against RR and RLc (`strategic`).
* **Reading committed in advance** (card section 3): WITHIN goes to the PI and the other session as a possible input of the rule with its price in the family reads beside it; NOT-REACHED names the presence as the next lever; NOT-DECIDED goes to the PI with the number.
