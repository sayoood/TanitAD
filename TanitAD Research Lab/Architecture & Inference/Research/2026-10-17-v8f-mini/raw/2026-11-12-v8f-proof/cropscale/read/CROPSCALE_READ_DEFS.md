# CROPSCALE_READ_DEFS -- reader kit of card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD106 (CROPSCALE10K), card sections 3 (T0 read and names), 4 (T1-kin names), 5 (controls g0 to g10) and 6 (families)

Tier T0 for the gap error, T1-kin/hb0.2 for the rule, pre-roll of 30 steps (6 s), UE_s0 and UE_s1. The card (frozen), the readers of cards ADD104 (`visseed/read_visseed10k.py`) and ADD105 (`visens/read_visens10k.py`) and the encode and fit kits of this card (`cropscale/enc/`, `cropscale/fit/`) were read, not edited. Everything new is in `F:/TanitAD-scratch/v8proof/cropscale/read/`. No result file, dump, token, rollout or controls file of real data was opened; every test runs on tiny synthetic data. The engine (`R.read_grid`, `R.strategic_grid`, `S.pair_read`) is not on the dev box: `measure()` is adapted from the ADD104 reader and has run only against a stand-in in the tests.

## 0. FIRST: clauses of the card that could fail by construction, or that the code cannot do as written (closest reading in each case)

1. **"The four T0 names" are four values of ONE name.** Section 3 names `CROPSCALE-T0-GAP-ERROR-CUT`, `-CUT-WITHIN-FIT-NOISE`, `CROPSCALE-T0-MORE-GAP-ERROR`, `-NOT-SEPARATED`, chosen by the rules in the order the card gives them. Coded as one name `T0` (`t0_name`); an upper end exactly at 1.0 is not below 1.0, a lower end exactly at 1.0 is not above 1.0. The prefix is `CROPSCALE-` (as the card writes it), the T1 names have `CROPSCALE10K-`.
2. **Which rows, which errors, in the second clause.** The card measures on "the rows ... with an output of every head compared". The ratio and the second clause use ONE row set: the rows with a lead in the label (`lab_present` and a finite `lab_gap`) and a finite output of all eight heads (O0, O1 = fit seeds 0, 1 of card ADD99; M2, M3, M4 = the 500-clip fits of card ADD104; L2, L3, L4). "The smallest error of the five 500-clip fits on record" is read as the smallest of the five errors on THAT row set, so that a large fit and a 500-clip fit are compared on the same rows. The other reading (against the recorded 3.913 m of the five-head row set, 3,841 rows) is printed with no name beside it (`each_large_below_recorded_smallest_3.913`); if the two readings differ, the name follows the first and the log shows both.
3. **Interval level and bootstrap.** The card gives 2,000 draws and "a fixed generator seed" but no level. Coded: two-sided 95 % percentile interval, `numpy.random.RandomState(106)`, clips drawn with replacement from all 141 evaluation clips (a clip with no selected row adds 0), statistic = sum of the clip sums of the mean of the three large errors over the same of the three 500-clip errors of seeds 2, 3, 4 (the row count cancels, the fits are held fixed). `boot_ratio` returns None when fewer than half of the draws have a finite ratio. The T1 intervals are the engine's (not mine).
4. **g9 extra clause.** The measure is defined only when the five dumps carry the same label fields on the join rows; the code counts the rows where they differ and g9 requires 0 (not in the card). g9 compares the five errors with 3.960, 3.913, 4.112, 4.079, 4.056 (fit seeds 0 to 4) with `abs(diff) <= 1e-3` and requires 3,841 rows. Those five numbers are the record of card ADD105 rounded to three decimals; a stored value of 3.9604 passes, one that differs by more than 0.001 m fails. If the pod's dumps of fit seeds 0 and 1 are not at `/workspace/v8proof/cropread/run1/dump/D_H5/` (the path the lead gave), g9 fails on a missing file.
5. **g10 added clauses.** The card says the lower end of the ratio (permuted-label fit to L2, cluster bootstrap over the 141 clips, 2,000 draws, fixed seed) is above 1.0 (strict). The ratio is read on the rows with a lead in the label and an output of both heads. To make "the fit with permuted labels" checkable the code also requires: its `FIT_RECORD.json` (`fits/D_H5_s2_perm`) says `permute_labels` true and `--permute-labels` in its call, seed 2, arm D, head H5, the fit clips and selection clips of L2, a head file on disk equal to its record and different from the eight on record; its dump meta (`dump/D_H5_perm/reader_s2_meta.json`) names its head file, seed 2 and `permute_labels` true; the label fields of its dump equal those of L2. These names come from the fit queue's PERM stage and from `crop_dump.py:103-106`; I could not look at a real meta file. If the tool writes the permute flag under another key, g10 fails by construction (the text says which clause).
6. **g0 and g1: what is read again.** g0 is read again entirely from the files on disk with `ctl_g0g1.g0` (default 50 selection clips): the five clauses of the card and the two the encode kit adds; the flags of `ENC_CONTROLS.json` (`pass_g0`, each of the seven clause entries) must also say PASS. g1: the flags (`pass_g1`, `pass_all`, the four clause entries) must say PASS and the cheap clause, the three pilot clips encoded again against the pilot's tokens within 1e-3, is read again from disk (ids = the first three of the pilot's `train_final_ids.txt`, check tokens at `cropscale/check_tok/tok/D`). The re-hash of about 49 GB of tokens is NOT repeated: the encode kit's own record is the evidence for the other three clauses. If the check tokens were swept, g1 fails.
7. **g2 and g3: re-derived from disk.** Both use the functions of `make_scale_gapfiles.py` (imported from `kit_fit`): the facts are collected again from the fit records, head files, lists and dump metas (`MSG.collect`), `g2_check` is run on them, and for g3 the build facts are recomputed from the gap files and dumps on disk (`measure_file` and `e0_stats` for the three single files, `mean_facts` for both mean files against a second routine), then `v0_check`, `build_check`, `meta_check`, `mean_check` twice and `mid_binding_check`; the hashes of the five gap files, of the three large dumps and of the six dumps behind the two mean files must be those the record names, and the record must be for this evaluation join (sha256). The record's own `g2` and `g3` (with all six parts) must say PASS. This takes a few minutes of CPU on the pod (python loops over the join rows, as in the ADD104 reader).
8. **g4: ten run texts.** Ten expected (five arms by two model seeds); nine found fails.
9. **g5 reads the gap file on disk for every arm.** For G2, G3, G4 the card says "the gap file ... equals"; the reader compares the rule log with `VGAPL_s<S>.npz` as it lies on disk (g3 shows that file equals the dump), for GEc with `VGAPLE.npz` and for E3c with `VGAPSE3.npz`. The rule log meta must name the sha256 of that file as the record has it, and `vis_closing` 1. A step with a lead and no output equals a gap of the control run V0 (any logged value, 1e-3 m); the closing input is non-zero on a positive share of the lead rows. V0 is `I10RQ50KF5P30Hb20vV0`.
10. **g6 values.** Rounded to four decimals as the ADD104 reader does (`round(x, 4)`): RR 0.0954, RLc 0.0000, VD5c 0.0169, F2 0.0138, F3 0.0185, F4 0.0123, E5c 0.0138, each the mean over the two model seeds of the banked rollouts. E5c at 0.0138 is the same number as F2 by the card; an unrelated value would fail.
11. **g7 reads three files at controls time.** `kit_enc/lead_pytest.out`, `kit_fit/lead_pytest.out`, `kit_read/lead_pytest.out` each need `N passed`, N > 0, no `failed`, no `error`. The last of them is made by the lead's run of THIS test file; the tests do not look for it.
12. **g8 inherits the unverified point of cards ADD103 and ADD104:** the engine's `ALL_PASS` is read on twelve rollout folders plus R0 here (the ADD104 read had eleven); whether an engine control assumes a zero closing column for RR-form rule logs could not be checked. The strategic grid reads the five arms, F2, F3, F4, RR, RLc (ten runs and R0).
13. **Counting rules.** The card name `CROPSCALE10K-HOLDS`, `-DOES-NOT-HOLD`, `-NOT-DECIDED` counts G2, G3, G4 only (the card says "over G2, G3, G4"); GEc gets its four names and does not enter the count. H is half of the pooled point value of C(RR) minus C(RLc) (0.0477 on the banked values), as in cards ADD104 and ADD105; `h_note` in the result shows H again as 0.0477 and says if a name changes. The scale name per measure counts over the three pairs G2-F2, G3-F3, G4-F4 (at least two with the upper end below 0 and none with the lower end above 0, and the mirror). The mean pair GEc minus E3c has no count in the card: coded as the plain interval bracket (upper end below 0 LOWERS, lower end above 0 RAISES, else NOT-SEPARATED). An end exactly at 0 is not below or above.
14. **Independent re-derivation.** `all_names` raises (REFUSED) when `derive_all` (written apart, with signs and counts) disagrees on any of the 24 names (16 arm names, the card name, 3 scale, 3 mean, T0) or when the T0 point ratio is not the ratio of the printed errors (`card_check`).
15. **"controls prints only the control lines".** The stage prints `g0 PASS` ... `g10 FAIL` and the last line; the engine's own output is redirected into `<out>.engine.log`. A control carries no number: those it computes (shares of rule steps, the five errors of g9, the g10 ratio) are moved by `assemble_controls` into `payload.control_values`, or, when a control fails, into `withheld`, where every value of the card sits. Nothing is opened by the names stage unless g0 to g10 are all present and PASS.
16. **Added pair.** `E3c-VS-RLc` is read in the grid so that "falling behind against RLc for every arm" has all five arms; it carries no name.
17. **Not done by code (lead):** freezing the hash file, logging the controls in `TIMES_NOTE.md` before the names, running the tests (g7 reads their output), running the stages on the pod.

Printed with no name: error of every fit, correlation of each head's gap with the label gap on those rows, the kept epoch, selection loss and number of fit clips per fit (from the gap-file record's `fit_facts_for_reader`, plus the permuted-label fit's record), the clips left out with their reasons (from `lists/LISTS_LARGE_RECORD.json`).

* **T1-kin read** (`measure`, 649): levels of every arm per model seed and pooled; pairs X against RR, RLc, VD5c and against the 500-clip arm of the same kind (G2-F2, G3-F3, G4-F4, GEc-E3c), each with C, U, F intervals; families LONGITUDINAL, LATERAL, TACTICAL per pair (the engine's pair read, `R.reading`); STRATEGIC (`R.strategic_grid`, `S.pair_read` at tick t0+20 on the arcs of 30, 50 and 80 m) for G2, G3, G4, GEc against RR, RLc and F2, F3, F4, E3c. Unsafe rate = U, falling behind = F (more than 10 m, as in the engine).
