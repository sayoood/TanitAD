# LEADSUP_READ_DEFS: definitions and decisions of the lead read (section 3a of the frozen card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD24)

Written 2026-11-15, before any arm of the card exists. Tier T0 (a world-model diagnostic on logged frames), never driving performance. Code: `leadsup_read.py` (reader), `pod_lead_apply.py` (pod side), `run_leadsup_read.sh` (lead's command), `test_leadsup_read.py`. The card is not edited. Everything below that goes beyond the card is a decision, listed so the lead can review it before the first real number.
STRATEGIC: PAUSED (PI item 25). LONGITUDINAL, LATERAL, TACTICAL are not measured by this read.

## Taken unchanged from earlier cards (imported, not rewritten)

* Windows, label frames, `hist_run`, `ls_slope`, rules R1 to R3, the copy, `label_pipeline`, `scored_mask`, the bootstrap functions: `spatial/objslot/objslot_s0.py` (card ADD20). Window frame f of the window with S0 step g0 is state-file frame t = g0 - 4 + f; S0 = frame 4; closing speed label = least-squares slope of the gap over the trailing run of at most 5 labelled frames (a 0.2 s step above 3.0 m breaks the run, at least 3 frames, positive = the gap opens).
* Fidelity (R2 and RMSE with intervals), the rule path with a head's gap and closing speed (`head_pipeline`, R3 coefficients fitted on the other four eval folds), `history_index`, `build_fit_sets`, `control_h6`: `spatial/leadhead/leadhead_s0.py` (card ADD21).
* Estimator: episode-cluster bootstrap, B 2,000, rng 0, one draw of episode counts shared by every paired difference (`resprobe.boot_counts`); 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Episodes of the 429 scored windows: 48. Episodes over all 1,948 windows: 139. On the train probe set the cluster is the clip (1,200 clips).
* Train probe labels as `leadhead_b.py` builds them: gap = join `lead_gap` where `lead_valid == 1`, finite, 0 to 60 m; closing speed from the 5-frame history by `build_fit_sets`; ego speed = `kin[:, 0]` of the probe-set shards.

## Inputs and layout

* **R1 Windows and labels are frozen once into packs** (`leadsup_read.py pack`, files `eval_pack.npz`, `train_pack.npz`; sources and sha256 in their `meta`). The eval pack holds, per window of the 1,948: episode, g0, the eval fold (`w_fold` of the banked latents file), the 16 logged ego speeds, the 15 label gaps, the bank presence at S0, and the bank rows (`beid`, `bg`, finite-ego mask, gap, presence) for the one-frame probe. The windows are those of the latents file `LATENTS_CTX_G_s0_R1.npz`, identical for every arm (ADD20/21/23). The train pack holds the layout of a train state file (clips, clip_idx, t; 121,166 rows, 1,200 clips), the label gap, the `lead_valid` presence and the ego speed per row.
* **R2 Window states come from the arm's own eval state file**, row (clip, t) for t = g0 - 4 .. g0 + 10. The earlier cards (ADD21, ADD23) read the window states from the per-window latents file; that file does not exist for the new arms. The state file holds every frame of every clip, so frames 0 to 4 of every window are present (control a4 counts them and checks that they are finite). Frames after S0 are only ever used as noise targets in control k4.
* **R3 Float16.** The extraction writes states as float16. The head is applied to those float16 values (cast to float32) both on the pod and here; the head saw float32 states in training. Stated as a limit; control k2 compares the two applications on the same float16 states.
* **R4 One head per arm.** The head tensors come from the checkpoint through `pod_lead_apply.py` (six tensors, float32). The reader applies the head with its own forward (`head_forward`: per-frame linear, concatenation oldest first, linear, GELU, linear), independent of the pod module; control k2 ties the two. The window outputs used for M1 to M4 are the reader's own forward on the pulled states. The head files of the eval and train application must be identical (control a3).
* **R5 Output units.** gap_m = output x 50, closing_ms = output x 10 (positive = the gap opens), presence logit as is; scales `LEAD_SUP_GAP_SCALE_M`, `LEAD_SUP_CLOS_SCALE_MS` of the patched model (asserted equal in the pod script).
* **R6 Input records.** Every pulled file is checked against the sha256 in its record (control a1): the four state files and two lead outputs by `out_sha256`, the head files by `head_sha256`; the state file the head was applied to is the pulled one (`states_sha256`); one checkpoint sha256 across the four records of an arm. An arm whose records fail is not read and nothing is named.

## The four numbers (eval, 429 scored windows)

* **M1** closing speed at S0: R2 and RMSE of the head output against the label closing speed, on `valid` = scored and with the label-history rule (413 windows in ADD21), the set of `leadhead_s0.fidelity`. The card says 429 scored windows; the closing label needs 3 labelled frames, so M1 and M2 use the 413 with a label; M3 uses all 429 (the rule path needs no closing label).
* **M2** gap at S0: R2 and RMSE of the head gap against the label gap on the same 413 windows, beside the one-frame probe of the same arm: the level probe of the r-b read (`s0_read.fit_probe`), fitted per eval fold on the eval-bank rows of the other four folds (finite gap, finite ego row) and applied to the S0 state of the fold's windows. The head is fitted on the train clips and the probe on eval-bank rows: two protocols, reported beside each other with the paired difference of R2 (head minus probe) as information; neither names anything.
* **M3** slot: rule R3 of card ADD20 with the head's gap and closing speed at S0: skill over the copy of the same head (head gap at S0 as the prediction for S0 + 2.0 s), level R2 of R1, R2, R3 and of the copy, on the 429 scored windows. **Banked rollout read (decision R7):** the card says "the banked rollout read". No rollout read is banked for LS, LSH or LSD. The reader uses the arm's own `OOF_RB_<ARM>_R1.npz` when it exists, else the base arm G of the same seed (`OOF_RB_G_s<seed>_R1.npz`, windows asserted equal), the only banked rollout of this encoder and gate; the verdict states which file was used. Where no file exists the difference is "not available" and `SUP-SLOT-BEATS-ROLLOUT` is "not evaluable".
* **M4** presence: AUROC of the presence logit at S0 against the bank `lead_present` at (episode, g0), over every window that has a bank row at S0 (n printed; the card says 1,948, the bank has no row at S0 for some windows, which are left out, not counted as absent). **Decision R8:** the eval presence label is the bank `lead_present` (the label of ADD20/21), the train label of the head is `lead_valid`; the two are different definitions of presence and the difference is a limit of M4. M4 names nothing.
* **Train probe clips** (reported only, names nothing): M1 on rows with a closing label, M2 on rows with a gap label, M3 on rows with a 5-frame history and a gap label on every frame t .. t + 10 without a jump (`dir_ok`), R3 coefficients cross-fitted over 5 clip folds (`RP.make_folds`), M4 on rows with a 5-frame history against `lead_valid`. Presence AUROC bootstrap is done in chunks of 100 draws (memory). All 121,166 rows of the probe set are used; the kinematic-finite mask of `leadhead_b` drops no row (asserted when the pack is built).
* **Paired differences** of M1 (R2) between arm types of the same seed (LS minus LSD, LSH minus LSD, LSH minus LS): R2 of the closing head against the label on the same windows, bootstrap of the difference over episodes with the shared draw. Floor = |M1 R2(LSD_s0) - M1 R2(LSD_s1)| (point R2).

## Controls

* **k1** label path: `control_h6` (the label rule path reproduces the banked ADD20 R2 skill 0.7499068 within 1e-6, n 413) and the value is within 5e-4 of the card's 0.750.
* **k2** head forward: the reader's forward on the pulled states equals the pod outputs, maximum absolute difference of gap_m and closing_ms at most 1e-4 over every row with a history (eval: 13,673 rows on the plumbing layout; the windows' S0 rows are counted apart and must be at least 64), the has-history masks equal. Eval and train applications are both checked. Deliberate regression: one tensor changed fails it (tested).
* **k3** shuffled states (**decision K3**): the states of the **scored windows are permuted among the scored windows** (rng 0); closing-speed R2 (413 windows) and rule R3 skill over the head copy (429 windows) must both be at most +0.02. Reason, measured on the planted case before any real number: a permutation over ALL windows puts the states of windows without a lead (head gap near 0) onto scored windows; the head gap then has an offset of -12.5 m against the labels and the intercept of R3 removes it, giving a skill of +0.43 from a perfect head on shuffled states. That is a property of R3's fitted intercept, not a leak. The all-window permutation, the offset, and R3 skill for three further scored-window permutations are written beside, information only. The deliberate-regression form: the identity permutation fails the control (tested). Risk stated for the lead: a trained head with a gap offset on the scored windows can still show an R3 skill above 0.02 under any permutation; the control reports the offset so that a fail can be read.
* **k4** no future input: every state after S0 replaced by noise (scale 5, rng 3): no head output and no R1, R2, R3 output changes. Deliberate regression: a head read at end frame 5 (it peeks) fails (tested).
* **a1 to a5** (the reader's own checks, all must pass): a1 input records, a2 pack counts (1,948 windows, 429 scored, 48 episodes), a3 same head in the eval and train application, a4 window states present and finite, a5 train state file layout equals the pack. **k5** (train log), **k6** (checkpoint identity with the T1-kin dump; the checkpoint sha256 of each arm is written to the result file) and **k7** (`q3_read.py` controls) belong to the lead and are not run here.
* No name is written if any control of any arm fails, if an arm was not read, or in a plumbing run.

## Names (card section 5, implemented as written; both seeds needed)

* Closing: `SUP-READS-CLOSING` if M1 R2 is above 0 with a lower interval bound above 0 at both seeds, else `SUP-CLOSING-FLAT`.
* Slot: `SUP-SLOT-IMAGINES` if the R3 skill over the head copy is above 0 with a lower bound above 0 at both seeds, else `SUP-SLOT-FLAT`; in addition `SUP-SLOT-BEATS-ROLLOUT` if the level R2 of R3 minus the banked rollout read is above 0 with a lower bound above 0 at both seeds.
* Gradient (LS against LSD, LSH against LSD, same seed, paired over episodes): `SUP-GRADIENT-HELPS` if the M1 R2 difference is positive in both seeds, each paired interval excludes 0, and the mean of the two differences is larger than the floor; `SUP-GRADIENT-HURTS` with the signs reversed (the mean of the two negative differences below minus the floor); else `SUP-GRADIENT-EQUAL`. "Larger than the floor" is read as the mean difference of the two seeds against the floor of the two LSD seeds, as the card says.
* Reading with the earlier cards: the three cases of the last bullet of section 5 from this card's own closing names; the precondition "flat in cards ADD21 and ADD23" is stated in the sentence and left to the lead.
* Cost (`SUP-NO-COST`, `SUP-COSTS`, `SUP-IMPROVES`): not named here; the T1-kin cost read of the lead (section 3b).

## Decision M3-BC, 2026-11-15 (lead decision, made before any arm number)

Reason: the plumbing head (random, gap offset -22.6 m on the scored windows) read slot skill 0.82 from the intercept of R3 alone, with no information from the states.
* The comparator of the GATED M3 is the **bias-corrected copy** copy_bc = (head gap at S0) + b, b = mean of (target gap at S0 + 2.0 s minus head gap at S0) over the scored windows of the OTHER eval folds (the folds R3 is cross-fitted on; never the scored fold itself). "R3 skill" for `SUP-SLOT-IMAGINES` / `SUP-SLOT-FLAT` and for the slot part of k3 is the skill of R3 over copy_bc (same bootstrap, same windows).
* The skill over the raw copy, as the card writes it, is still computed and printed beside it (JSON `skill_over_copy`, verdict "raw copy, names nothing"). `SUP-SLOT-BEATS-ROLLOUT` is unchanged (level R2 of R3 minus the banked rollout read).
* The head's gap offset on the scored windows (mean of head gap minus label gap at S0) is printed per arm (`M3.gap_offset_m`).
* k3 itself is unchanged (scored-window permutation, all-window permutation printed beside, not gated); its slot number is now the bias-corrected skill, the raw one is information only.

## Plumbing run (tag PLUMB)

`tmp/plumb/`: a tiny V8FMini checkpoint of the patched tree with a random head (`make_tiny_ckpt.py`, d 32, 40 x 16 grid, seed 0), applied by `pod_lead_apply.py` (`--plumbing`, the only flag that lets a state file of another checkpoint through) to `G_s0_eval.npz` and `G_s0_train.npz`, read by `leadsup_read.py run --plumbing`. No number of it is a result. It shows the window assembly (1,948 windows, 429 scored, 413 with a label history), labels, estimator and controls execute on the real layout; k1 reproduces 0.7499068 (card 0.750).

## Limits

Two seeds per arm type; an interval answers another draw of episodes, not another training run. One corpus. The pod and the dev box apply the head to float16 states. M2's probe and head are different protocols. The eval presence label differs from the train presence label. R3's ego speeds are the logged ones (the action input of a rollout); no goal input, nothing of the situation classifier.
