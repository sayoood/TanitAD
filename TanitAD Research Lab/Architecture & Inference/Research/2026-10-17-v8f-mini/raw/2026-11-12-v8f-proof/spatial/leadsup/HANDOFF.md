## Known limits
* No real-data training, no pod-size model through the trainer; the real-data evidence is the label rule on the probe join (above). The G-LIVE on the pod is the first run of the head on real batches.
* Labels are in the data path (decision 1 of DEFS): under the DAgger / ego-perturb / recover stages the labels stay those of the logged frames (not used by these arms).
* `lead_sup_detach` in `built_levers()` is the stored wiring flag; the behaviour is pinned by the exact-zero control and the mutation, not by the readback.
* Not built: `--encoder lora` (gate refusal), mean-state arms (refused), per-part loss weights, any use of the head at inference.
* The synthetic world carries no lead track by default; `--synthetic ... --lead-sup on` fills a deterministic one (selfcheck only).
* Huber deltas (5 m, 2 m/s) and presence = `lead_valid` (not "within 40 m" like the earlier `--lead-head`) are the builder's choices: lead to review (DEFS decisions 2 and 4).
