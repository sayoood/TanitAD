# LEADHEAD_B_DEFS (card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD23, stage 0b). Every choice the card leaves open, fixed before any number on a real target.
Tier T0: a world-model diagnostic on logged frames, never driving performance. CPU only, zero training of the model. Code: `leadhead_b.py`; it imports `spatial/leadhead/leadhead_s0.py` (history builder `build_fit_sets`, inner folds, rule path `head_pipeline`, fidelity, banked comparison, control h6, MLP trainer), `spatial/objslot/objslot_s0.py` (windows, labels, speeds, rules, bootstrap) and `spatial/s1/s1_read.py` (state-file loader and the alignment check t1). The card and the existing files are not edited.
Before this file was written only structure and counts were read (row layout, frames per clip, label counts of the join, file sizes); no head was fitted and no rule evaluated on a real target. Everything not named here is as in `leadhead/LEADHEAD_DEFS.md` (L1 to L19) and `objslot/OBJSLOT_DEFS.md`.

M1 Probe set and frame step. `spatial/s0e/data/INDEX.json` (1,200 clips) with the shard fields `clip_idx`, `t`, `kin` (v, a, yaw rate) and the join `C:/Users/Admin/lorapod/ship2/join_train.npz`; rows ordered by (clip, frame) exactly as `s0e_read.load_train` (the camera tokens `X` are not read: the row layout is all this read needs; the shard sha256 check of `load_train` is therefore not repeated here and the state files carry their own record). MEASURED: 121,166 rows, 1,200 clips, 100 to 104 frames per clip, every clip's frames are 0 .. n-1 with stride 1, all 121,166 rows have a finite kinematic vector (none dropped). The cache step is 0.2 s (`spatial/s0c/s0c_read.py`: pair geometry (g, g+10) = 2.0 s "cache steps of 0.2 s"; the eval bank is one row per 0.2 s, L1); so the stride that gives 0.2 s is 1 and the history is built at stride 1 (offsets -4..0). `leadhead_b.frame_step_check` asserts stride 1 and first frame 0 and the result file carries it.
M2 Fit-row labels (the card's definitions through `leadhead_s0.build_fit_sets`, rows t-4..t of the same clip): gap(t) = the join lead gap with `lead_valid` 1 and 0 <= gap <= 60 m (rule E3 of the probe set), else NaN; closing label c(t) = least-squares slope of the gap over the trailing run of labelled frames t-4..t, a step above 3.0 m breaks the run, H_MIN = 3; direct label gap(t+10) - gap(t) with the continuity rule over t..t+10 (finite gap on all 11 frames, no 0.2 s step above 3.0 m). MEASURED counts: rows with five frames 116,366; gap rows 32,025 (548 clips); closing rows 30,865 (525 clips); direct rows 24,533 (484 clips).
M3 Ego speed of the train rows = `kin[:, 0]` (v of the probe set). The probe set holds the ego speed, so the direct read is fitted WITH the ego term X as the 3,201st input (the same formula as the windows: X = T*V(t) - sum of 0.2*mean(V(t+k), V(t+k+1)), K = 10). Difference to note: the train gap is the join gap restricted to 0..60 m; the scored windows and the eval-bank rows use `res/cache/frames.npz` `lead_gap` as in card ADD21 (which the eval-bank read of ADD21 and ADD20 used). Not harmonised; named as a limit in the verdict.
M4 HT. Ridge with intercept on the standardised 3,200 inputs (five frames of 640, time order t-4..t), penalty lambda = g * n_fit / 1000 with g in {1e0 .. 1e7} (the ADD21 grid extended by 1e6 and 1e7). The solution is the same as the dual form of ADD21 (verified by a test); it is computed from block-wise accumulated sufficient statistics (n, sum x, sum x x', sum x y, sum y, sum y^2; 3,000-row blocks; the Gram matrix) so that the memory is under 3 GB. Selection of g: 4 inner folds by clip; the held-out squared error of an inner fold is computed from the statistics of that fold; each inner model is standardised and scaled on its own inner-train rows; g = the grid value with the smallest pooled inner error; the final model is refitted on all fit rows with that g. Nothing outside the fit rows is read.
M5 Inner folds by clip. `leadhead_s0.inner_folds(clip names, 4, seed 100)`: the sorted unique clip ids are permuted with rng 100 and assigned round robin to 4 folds; it is called on all 121,166 rows of the pool so that the partition is one function of the clip set and is the same for the three targets (a target's fit rows take the fold of their clip). For the held-out-tenth fits the pool is the 1,080 remaining clips; for the learning-curve fits it is the clips drawn for that size.
M6 HM. As ADD21 L6: standardised inputs, PCA to 64 components, each score standardised to unit variance on the fit rows, 64-64-1 GELU network, target z-scored, Adam lr 1e-3 weight decay 1e-3, 300 epochs full batch, 3 initialisations (torch seeds 0, 1, 2) averaged, one network per target; the ego column of the direct read bypasses the PCA. DEVIATION (forced by the row count, written before any real-target number): the PCA is taken from the 3,200 x 3,200 covariance of the standardised inputs (primal form) instead of the Gram matrix of the fit rows (30,000 x 30,000); the subspace and the unit-variance scaling are the same. Inner error of HM: the same 4 inner folds, HM refitted on the inner-train rows (PCA included).
M7 Choice of the head for the names (card section 2): the lower mean inner-fold squared error of the closing-speed target wins (HT on a tie); the direct read has its own choice on the direct target. Never the scored windows. The other head is always reported beside.
M8 Windows and application. The heads are fitted ONCE on all labelled train rows (no cross-fitting of the heads over eval folds is needed: the eval episodes are disjoint from the train clips, j1) and applied to the window states `Z1[:, 0..4]` of the 429 scored windows (only frames 0 to 4 are ever sliced). Rule R3 keeps its cross-fitted coefficients over the eval folds (`head_pipeline`, `fit_r3`). Windows, folds, targets, rules R1 to R3, the copy, the banked rollout read, ego speeds and the episode-cluster bootstrap (B 2,000, rng 0, 48 episodes) are those of ADD21.
M9 Held-out tenth of the train clips (j4, j8, j5). The first 120 clip ids of the permutation (rng 0) of the sorted clip ids; HT fitted on the other 1,080 clips (its own inner folds); the R2 on the tenth and in sample are computed from the sufficient statistics of the tenth and of the fit rows (R2 around the mean of the set scored).
M10 Controls. j1: the 1,200 probe clip ids against the scored-window episode ids and the eval-bank episode ids; no common id. j2: `s1_read.check_t1` (clips, row count, clip_idx, t, finite state) for each arm's `<ARM>_train.npz`; the record's width and mode are printed. j3: the closing labels permuted across all fit rows (rng 7), HT and HM refitted, R2 on the 413 label-history windows at most +0.02. j4: HT (closing) on the 1,080 clips, in-sample R2 must exceed the R2 on the tenth. j5: the control h1 of ADD21 on the row layout of the train probe set: all 121,166 rows and 1,200 clips, a synthetic linear gap per clip (g ~ U(15,60), c ~ N(0,1.5)), state = noise + gap * 10.0 * s * u (u a fixed random unit direction in 640 dimensions, per-dimension noise s = 0.8641, the value of the ADD21 control h1; amp 10.0 as in ADD21 L13), HT on the 1,080 clips, held-out R2 on the tenth above 0.9; run once (it does not depend on the arm). j6: `leadhead_s0.control_h6` (label path reproduces 0.750 within 1e-6). j7: all window states after frame 4 replaced by noise (scale 5, rng 3): every head output and every rule output identical (0.0), HT and HM. j8: not a gate: the gap R2 of the 1,080-clip head on the tenth and on the eval windows, and the same for the closing speed.
M11 Learning curve (secondary a, HT, closing and gap). Clips in the order of the permutation (rng 0) added whole until the labelled rows of the target reach the size; sizes 2,000, 8,000, 32,000 (card) plus 16,000 (added; there are only 32,025 gap rows and 30,865 closing rows in all, so 32,000 is nearly or exactly "all labelled rows"); inner folds over the drawn clips; the "all" point is the main fit.
M12 Pooled fit (secondary b, HT). For each eval fold f: the train probe rows (their 4 inner groups as in the main fit) plus the eval-bank rows of the training folds (`build_fit_sets` of ADD21 on the eval bank; their inner groups = `inner_folds` over those episodes, seed 100 + f); one penalty by the pooled inner groups; the windows of fold f are scored; the rules as above. Cross-fitted over eval folds.
M13 Seeds, fixed: inner folds 100 (+ fold for M12), tenth and learning-curve clip order rng 0, shuffle 7, torch 0, 1, 2, bootstrap rng 0, mutation rng 3.
M14 Outcomes (card section 4): both seeds of an arm type needed; "interval excludes 0" = the lower end of the 95 % percentile interval above 0; fewer than 30 windows: no name. `HEAD-SLOT-BEATS-ROLLOUT-B` on the banked rollout read (i) [R1 file] as ADD20/ADD21. If a control fails, no outcome is named.
M15 Staging and files. Stages `ht` (HT, shuffled-label, no-future, tenth controls, direct read with ego term, slot read), `lc`, `hm`, `pooled`; each stage is cached under `C:/Users/Admin/v8proof_c/leadhead_b/cache/` and RESULT/VERDICT are rewritten after every arm. One command reproduces everything: `python leadhead_b.py run`. Tests: `test_leadhead_b.py`.
