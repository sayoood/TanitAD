# S0E_DEFS (PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD12, note N7, stage 0e). Every choice the note leaves open, fixed before any number on true labels.
Tier T0: a world-model diagnostic, never driving performance. CPU only (CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1). Code: `s0e_read.py` (this folder); it imports `spatial/s0/s0_read.py` (pca_basis, cell_block, grid_state, mean_state, mean_token, fit_probe, planted_setup, permute_within_fold, paired, single_r2, check_s2, load_data for e3, the constants) and `spatial/s0c/s0c_read.py` (build_pairs with PAIR_RULE "continuous", ego_block, arm_features, arm_oof_fold, k2_setup, k2_side, k2_fold, skill, single_skill, paired_skill, analyse_main, check_k2), and through them `res/resprobe.py` (make_folds, boot_counts, boot_auc, auc_pooled, r2_pooled, ci, diff_row). Stage-0 rules R1 to R19 and stage-0c rules C1 to C16 apply wherever this file is silent. The note and `spatial/s0/`, `spatial/s0c/` are not edited.

E1 Data. `data/INDEX.json` (1,200 probe clips, positions 600 to 1,799 of the train manifest), `data/proj.npy` (the fixed projection 1,024 to 64), `data/shard_0000.npz` .. `shard_0011.npz` (`X` float16 [N, 40, 64], `kin` float32 [N, 3] = v, a, yaw_rate, `clip_idx`, `t`). Every shard read is checked against its sha256 in INDEX.json; a mismatch stops the run. Rows are ordered by (clip, frame). Rows with a non-finite kinematic value are dropped and counted (the pair builder only sees rows that exist).
E2 Labels. Join `C:/Users/Admin/lorapod/ship2/join_train.npz`; frame t of a clip is join row `off + t`; the join count `n` must equal the INDEX frame count for every clip. Cell c of the tokens is the row-major index of the 4x10 grid (the cache order).
E3 Lead rule. Presence = `lead_valid` (0/1). Lead gap = `lead_gap` where `lead_valid` is 1, the gap is finite and 0 <= gap <= 60 m (both ends kept); otherwise NaN (not a gap row). The 0 to 60 m rule is applied to the join labels before anything else.
E4 Probe subsample. A probe (a fit of `S0.fit_probe`) sees at most 20,000 training rows (r-a: rows with a finite target; presence: all rows) or 20,000 training pairs (change read), a fixed random subsample (numpy default_rng(0), sorted). All test rows / pairs of the fold are predicted. The sizes available and used are written per fold to RESULT_S0E.json and printed in the verdict. The kernel candidate inside `fit_probe` keeps its own cap of 2,400 (candidate search) and 3,000 (final fit rows), as in stage 0.
E5 Folds. `RP.make_folds(clip name of each row, k=5, seed 0)`: the unit is the clip. The fold of a pair is the fold of its clip (row i). Every fitted object (PCA, scaling, probe, penalty) sees training-fold clips only.
E6 States, per fold. Grid state = per cell 16 principal directions of the 64-wide cell, fitted on ALL rows of the training folds (no subsample; 40 eigendecompositions of a 64x64 covariance), cells concatenated in cell order: 640 numbers, unwhitened scores. Mean state = the mean over the 40 projected cells (64 numbers), centred on the training folds, ALL 64 directions kept (the stage-0 128 directions do not exist at width 64; the rotation changes no ridge or kernel distance). Rows outside the training folds are projected with the training basis.
E7 Ego vector. (v, a, yaw_rate) of the row. For the r-a read it is z-scored per column on the training rows of the fold (the probe divides by one global scale, so raw columns of very different size would not be read evenly). The change read uses the stage-0c ego block `ego_block` = the vector at g, g-5 and g+10 (9 numbers), z-scored on the training pairs and, in arms with a state, rescaled by the state's spread (stage-0c C4, by `arm_features`). The ego floor of r-a is the stage-0 probe `fit_probe` on the 3 ego columns (S0 R7 says one probe function serves every probe of the read); it is not the best-of-three of the stage-0 R8 floor.
E8 r-a. For grid state, mean state, ego vector: lead gap R2 (pooled over the five out-of-fold predictions, metres, untransformed, on rows with a finite gap) and presence AUROC (all rows). Differences: grid minus ego, mean minus ego, grid minus mean, for gap R2 and for presence AUROC, paired on the same bootstrap draw. The presence AUROC bootstrap is computed in chunks of 50 draws (same estimator, bounded memory).
E9 Pairs (change read). Stage-0d construction: `build_pairs` with PAIR_RULE "continuous" on the E3 gap: a pair is a row (clip, g) with rows at g-5 and g+10 present, a finite gap on all 11 frames g .. g+10, and no 0.2 s step above 3.0 m. Target d = gap(g+10) - gap(g); persistence predicts 0. Arms H-g, H-m, N-g, E, skill = 1 - SSE / sum d^2; as stage 0c (C5 to C7). The count of pairs with three rows and the count removed by the continuity rule are printed. The 0 to 60 m rule acts first (a frame outside it is NaN and breaks continuity).
E10 Label-free controls run on fixed random subsets: e2 position on 30,000 rows (default_rng(0)), e2 motion on 30,000 pairs (default_rng(1)); their folds are the E5 folds. On the subsets the training part of each fold is cut to E4's 20,000. Both use the planted vector of stage 0 (`planted_setup`: unit direction seed 5, times the median norm of the projected tokens in the planted cell) and the cells of stage 0 (position: the cell of `planted_setup` and its mirror cell of the same grid row, per row side from seed 6) and stage 0c (motion: interior cell from seed 8, side from seed 9).
E11 Bootstrap. `RP.boot_counts(n_clips = 1200, B = 2000, seed 0)`, one draw shared by every number and every difference. A difference qualifies when its point value is above 0 and the lower end of its 95 % percentile interval is above 0 (the stage-0 and stage-0c reading of "excludes 0").
E12 Outcome names, exactly as the note lists them, each printed with the suffix "stage 0e (train corpus)": per state `S0-LEAD-KEPT` if gap R2 (state) minus gap R2 (ego vector) qualifies, else `S0-LEAD-LOST`; `GRID-ADDS` if gap R2 grid minus mean qualifies, else `GRID-EQUAL`; change read: `S0-CHANGE-READ` / `S0-CHANGE-EGO` / `S0-CHANGE-FLAT`, `GRID-ADDS-CHANGE` / `GRID-EQUAL-CHANGE`, `HISTORY-ADDS` / `HISTORY-EQUAL` by `s0c_read.outcome_names` unchanged.
E13 Controls, each must read its known value; they are printed first. e1: labels permuted within fold (`permute_within_fold`; gap seed 1, presence seed 2, d seed 1) and every fit repeated through the same code path: every r-a gap R2 (grid, mean, ego) and every change skill (four arms) at or below 0.02; the presence AUROC is printed beside, not barred (an AUROC is not an R2). e2 position: AUROC from the grid state above 0.95 and from the mean state at or below 0.60 (`S0.check_s2`). e2 motion: grid with the two frames above 0.95, mean with the two frames at or below 0.60, grid with one frame at or below 0.60 (`S0C.check_k2`). e3: the stage-0 r-a read of the eval bank (`S0.load_data`, tokens projected with the same `proj.npy`, rounded to float16 as the train tokens are, grid state 16 per cell, mean state 64, `fit_probe` with seed 0 + fold, pooled gap R2): grid within 0.03 of 0.3981 and mean within 0.05 of 0.2577. e4: see E14.
E14 e4. (i) 30 random (clip, frame) rows: the join labels are re-derived from the join by clip NAME and frame number (not by the row index used to load them) and equal the loaded labels exactly (a shifted offset fails; tested); (ii) join `n` equals the INDEX frame count for every clip; (iii) rows per clip equal the INDEX frame count and the frames of a clip are exactly 0 .. n-1; (iv) clip count 1,200; (v) no probe clip is an eval clip (names against the file names of `sfresh_inputs/cache/eval/clips`, one directory listing; count must be 0); (vi) the fold-leak guard: the states of training rows are bitwise unchanged when the tokens of held-out rows change (red under `LEAK_PCA_FIT`; tested); (vii) shard sha256. 
E15 e4, kinematics against the cache. The note asks that the extracted kinematics equal the pod cache values for 30 rows. The pod cache is not on this box, so this part runs only if `data/KIN_REF.npz` is supplied (arrays `clip` (str), `t`, `kin` [n, 3], exact comparison on every listed row present); otherwise the verdict prints "NOT CHECKED" for it and e4 can still pass. This is a named gap (G1); `--require-kin-ref` is not provided, the lead decides whether to supply the file before the run.
E16 Reporting. Every number carries n (frames or pairs, and clips), tier T0, the estimator (paired episode-cluster bootstrap, clips as clusters, B 2,000, seed 0) and "a world-model diagnostic, never driving performance". Four-family line: LONGITUDINAL, LATERAL, TACTICAL not measured by this read; STRATEGIC: PAUSED (PI item 25). Also printed: std(d), mean(d), share |d| above 2 m, pairs removed by the continuity rule, the subsample sizes.
E17 Exit. Controls first. If any of e1 to e4 fails the numbers are written to RESULT_S0E.json, no outcome name is written anywhere, exit code 2. Exit 0 only when all pass. Resumable per fold (`stages/foldK.npz`, with a signature of rows, pairs, subsample sizes); fold caches hold out-of-fold predictions only.
E18 Smoke (`s0e_read.py smoke [--clips N] [--folds K]`): the first N clips of INDEX (default 60); the (gap, presence) track of every clip is replaced by the track of another clip (a fixed permutation, seed 0, cycled to length) BEFORE anything is computed; B 200; prints shapes, counts, timings, e2 and e4 only, quotes no r-a or change value, stores nothing but RESULT_S0E_SMOKE.json. e3 runs on the 12 eval clips with permuted eval labels for timing only.

## Departures from the note's wording (each a reading choice, not a change of the question)
D1 (E6) The mean state keeps 64 directions (all), as the note says; the stage-0 128 directions cannot exist at width 64.
D2 (E7) The ego floor of r-a is the single stage-0 probe on the 3 ego columns, not the best of three candidates picked on the pooled test metric.
D3 (E10) The label-free controls e2 run on fixed 30,000-row / 30,000-pair subsets, not on all 121,166 frames, to bound the cost.
D4 (E15) The comparison of kinematics with the pod cache needs a reference file that is not on this box.
D5 (E18) The smoke swaps whole label tracks between clips (row-level permutation would destroy the continuity rule and the pair set the timing depends on).
