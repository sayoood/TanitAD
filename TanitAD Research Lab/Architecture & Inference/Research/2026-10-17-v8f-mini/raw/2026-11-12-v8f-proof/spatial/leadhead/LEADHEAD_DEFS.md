# LEADHEAD_DEFS (card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD21, stage 0). Every choice the card leaves open, fixed before any number on a real target.
Tier T0: a world-model diagnostic on logged frames, never driving performance. CPU only, zero training of the model. Code: `leadhead_s0.py`; it imports `spatial/objslot/objslot_s0.py` (loaders, rules, scoring, bootstrap) and through it `rb_trained.load`, `s0_read`, `resprobe`. The card and the existing files are not edited.
Only structure, counts and key layout were inspected before this file was written (file keys, shapes, row spacing, that window speeds equal bank speeds); no head was fitted and no rule evaluated on a real target. Everything not named here is as in `objslot/OBJSLOT_DEFS.md` (D1 to D20).

L1 Bank row layout. `rb_trained.load` gives the bank rows with a finite ego vector: episode id `beid`, step `bg` (one row per 0.2 s), state `Sb` (640), lead gap `gap` (nan where no lead), ego vector `E`. The five-frame history of a bank row t is the rows (episode, bg-4) .. (episode, bg) (offsets -4..0, 0.2 s apart; window frame f is bank step g0 - 4 + f, as in D4). A row whose five rows are not all in the loaded bank is dropped from the fit rows and counted ("no five frames").
L2 Ego speed of a bank row = `E[:, 0]`. MEASURED before this file: for all windows, frames 0 to 14, the window speed file value `V[:, f]` equals `E[row(g0 - 4 + f), 0]` with maximum absolute difference 0.0. So the ego term of the direct read for a fit row is built from `E[:, 0]` of the rows t .. t+10 with the same formula as D7/D8 (X = T*V(t) - sum over k of 0.2*mean(V(t+k), V(t+k+1)), K = 10, T = 2.0 s); the scored windows use the window file `V` as in ADD20.
L3 Fit rows of a fold: the bank rows whose episode is not in the fold (fold map `RP.make_folds` of the bank rows, as ADD20) and which have the five frames (L1). Per target further: gap head, finite gap(t); closing-speed head, the trailing-run history rule of D5 on the labelled gaps of the five frames with H_MIN = 3 (label of card section 2); direct head, finite gap on t .. t+10 with every 0.2 s step within 3.0 m (the continuity rule D2) and the rows t+1 .. t+10 in the bank, target gap(t+10) - gap(t). Counts and drops are printed per fold.
L4 Head input = the five grid states in time order t-4 .. t, flattened (3,200 values), float64. Standardised per column with the mean and standard deviation of the rows of the fit being done (a column with zero deviation is divided by 1). The direct read appends the ego term X as the 3,201st column, standardised like the others.
L5 HT. Ridge with intercept (target centred on the fit rows), solved in the dual form (identical solution to the closed form in the 3,200 inputs). Penalty lambda = g * n_fit / 1000 with g in {1e0 .. 1e5} (card grid), n_fit the number of rows of the fit being done. Selection: 4 inner folds by episode among the fit rows (episodes permuted with rng seed 100 + outer fold, assigned round robin), the inner models standardised and scaled on their own inner-train rows, g = the grid value with the smallest pooled inner-validation squared error; the final model is refitted on all fit rows with that g. Nothing outside the fit rows is read. One penalty per fold per target.
L6 HM. Standardised inputs (L4), PCA by the Gram matrix of the fit rows to 64 components (each component standardised to unit variance on the fit rows (decided after the synthetic test of the pure machinery showed that one global scale hides the slope direction, whose variance is far below that of the gap level, from a 300-epoch network; no real target was read before this change)), the 3,201st column of the direct read bypasses the PCA (65 inputs). Network: Linear(64, 64), GELU, Linear(64, 1) ("64 - 64 - 1" read as one hidden layer of 64). Target z-scored on the fit rows. Adam, learning rate 1e-3, weight decay 1e-3 (Adam's L2 term), 300 epochs, full batch, 3 initialisations (torch seeds 0, 1, 2), predictions averaged. One network per target. Float32 in torch, CPU, 4 threads.
L7 Choice of HT or HM (card: "chosen by the inner folds of the fit rows, never by the scored windows"). Per arm, for the closing-speed target: the mean over the five outer folds of the inner-validation mean squared error (HT at its chosen g; HM by the same 4 inner folds, HM fitted on inner-train). The lower mean wins; ties go to HT. HEAD-READS-CLOSING and HEAD-SLOT-IMAGINES are named on that head (the pair gap head + closing-speed head of that type). The direct read has its own choice by the same rule on the target gap(t+10) - gap(t). The other head is always reported beside it. A name that would differ with the other head is flagged in the verdict.
L8 Windows. The scored windows of ADD20 (D2, D10; 429 scored, folds of the episodes as in `arm_windows`), the held-out head of a window is the head of the fold of its episode, applied to the window states `Z1[:, 0..4]` (read through `speed_and_states`, array shape windows x 15 x 640; only frames 0 to 4 are ever sliced). The states of the bank rows and of the window frames are the same numbers; the maximum absolute difference is printed.
L9 Slot read. The head gives gap_h(S0) and c_h(S0) (closing speed as a number, not a slope). `rule_inputs(gap_h, c_h, V, K)` then R1, R2 as in D8; R3 = gap_h + a*(T*c_h) + b*X + d with (a, b, d) by least squares on the scored windows of the training folds using the cross-fitted head values (each window's head never saw its episode), applied to the held-out fold. The copy is gap_h(S0). Skill over the copy of the same head on the 429 scored windows, bootstrap D10 (B 2,000, rng 0, one draw per window set).
L10 Fidelity (card metric a): R2 around the mean (`RP.r2_pooled`) and RMSE of the head gap against the logged gap at S0, and of the head closing speed against the label closing speed (D6) on the scored windows with the label history rule (413). Intervals by the episode-cluster bootstrap on the same draw (R2 as `level_row`; RMSE as the square root of the ratio of the resampled SSE and n).
L11 Level R2 (metric c): level R2 of R3 against the logged gap at frame 14, and the paired difference to the banked rollout read (i) `OOF_RB_<ARM>_R1.npz` `p_i` on the common scored windows, as D11. The name `HEAD-SLOT-BEATS-ROLLOUT` is on R1 (as in ADD20).
L12 Direct read. Heads fitted to gap(t+10) - gap(t) (L3) with the 3,201 inputs; applied at S0: prediction = gap_h(S0) + delta_h; copy = gap_h(S0) (the gap head of the same type). Skill over the copy as L9, interval as D10.
L13 Controls (each prints its numbers; none may be skipped).
  h1 planted state: 60 synthetic episodes of 30 rows. Per episode a gap series gap(t) = g_e + c_e * 0.2 * t with g_e ~ U(15, 60) and the closing speed c_e ~ N(0, 1.5) m/s (constant within an episode); state of a row = noise + gap * amp * s * u, u a fixed random unit direction in 640 dimensions, per frame, noise per-dimension standard deviation s = the mean per-dimension standard deviation of the real bank states of the arm P_s0 (the measured size), amp = 10.0 per metre (a design number the card does not give; the projection on u then reads a metre with noise 0.1 m per frame). DEVIATION RECORDED BEFORE ANY REAL-TARGET NUMBER: amp 5.0 was the first design number and read held-out R2 0.8174 (60 episodes, n 1,560) and 0.8560 (90 episodes, n 2,340), below the card's bar 0.9 for what is a planted signal; the plant is too weak for 3,200 inputs and about 1,250 fit rows per fold, so the amplitude was raised to 10.0 (reads 0.9440 on the same 60 episodes). Both first readings are kept here; the control is run with 10.0; standard folds by episode; HT through the same code as the real run (fit rows, inner penalty choice, cross-fit); the held-out R2 of the closing speed must be above 0.9.
  h2 shuffled labels: the closing-speed labels of the fit rows permuted across the fit rows of each fold (rng seed 7 + fold); held-out R2 against the true label closing speed on the 413 scored windows at most +0.02, for HT and HM, every arm.
  h3 fidelity of the reference: HL = the level probe of ADD20 (`fit_vision_probes`, `vision_gaps`, frames 0 to 4) recomputed here (cached after the first computation) and compared with `objslot/VG_<arm>.npz` `VG`; max absolute difference at most 1e-6.
  h4 leak detector: the same HT fitted on all folds (all fit rows of all five folds, penalty by the same inner selection) and applied in sample to the scored windows; its closing-speed R2 on the 413 windows must be higher than the cross-fitted HT R2 of the same windows.
  h5 no future input: the window states at frames 5 to 14 replaced by random numbers (scale 5, rng seed 3); every head output (gap, closing speed, direct delta) and every rule output (R1, R2, R3) at S0 must be identical (maximum absolute difference 0.0), for HT and HM.
  h6 label path: the label gap(S0) and the label closing speed (D5, D6) pushed through this script's rule path (`head_pipeline`, R2) reproduce the R2 skill over the copy of ADD20 (`objslot/RESULT_OBJSLOT_S0.json`, label, 2.0 s) within 1e-6, and also the value computed now by `objslot_s0.label_pipeline`.
L14 Outcomes. As card section 4 on the head chosen by L7, both seeds of an arm type needed for a name; "interval excludes 0" = the lower end of the 95 % percentile interval above 0 (D10). Where a quantity has fewer than 30 windows no name is given on it (D20). If a control fails, no outcome is named.
L15 Horizon profile (decides nothing): target frames 6, 8, 10, 12 (0.4 to 1.6 s) with the same heads, T = 0.2 K, the continuity rule over frames 4 to target, R3 refitted per horizon; for the chosen head and the other head.
L16 Staging. Stage 1 runs HT and its controls (h1 to h6 for HT) for all four arms and writes RESULT and VERDICT; stage 2 adds HM; stage 3 adds the direct read; stage 4 the horizon profile. One command reproduces all: `python leadhead_s0.py run`. Each stage rewrites RESULT_LEADHEAD_S0.json and VERDICT_LEADHEAD_S0.md with what has been computed so far and says which parts are missing.
L17 Seeds. Fixed: outer seed 0 (folds), inner folds 100 + fold, shuffle 7 + fold, torch 0, 1, 2, bootstrap rng 0, mutation rng 3.
L18 Measured after stage 1 (about the data, no target-quality number): the window state `Z1[:, f]` and the bank state of the same (episode, step) are not bit-identical; per element the median absolute difference is 0.015 and the 99th percentile 0.07 against a per-dimension standard deviation of 0.864 (P_s0, frames 4 and 2; maximum 0.22 to 0.34 over the arms), and 139 of the 9,740 window frames 0 to 4 have no bank row (first row of an episode, ego vector missing). The heads are fitted on bank states and applied to window states, which is the form of the card (the HL reference of ADD20 does the same). It is a limit and is stated in the verdict.
L19 Stage 1 outputs are kept as `RESULT_LEADHEAD_S0_STAGE1_HT.json` and `VERDICT_LEADHEAD_S0_STAGE1_HT.md` before the full run overwrites the main files.
