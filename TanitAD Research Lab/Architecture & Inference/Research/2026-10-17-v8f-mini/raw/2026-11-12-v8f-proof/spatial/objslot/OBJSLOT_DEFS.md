# OBJSLOT_DEFS (card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD20, stage 0). Every choice the card leaves open, fixed before any skill number on real targets.
Tier T0: a world-model diagnostic on logged frames, never driving performance. CPU only. Code: `objslot_s0.py`; it imports `spatial/q2/rb_trained.py` (`load`, so that windows, bank rows, states, folds and the probe are the objects of the r-b read) and, through it, `spatial/s0/s0_read.py` and `res/resprobe.py`. The card and the existing files are not edited.
Only structure and counts were inspected before this file was written (file keys, shapes, row spacing, which window list each file holds); no rule was evaluated on a real target.

D1 Label source and spacing. The lead gap labels are the bank column `lead_gap` of `res/cache/frames.npz` (rows (clip, grid step g), one row per 0.2 s step, finite on about 26 % of the rows). Window frame f of the window with last cache index t is bank step g = t - 15 + f. So the labels exist on EVERY frame of the window (spacing 0.2 s) wherever the bank row exists and the gap is finite; the history of frames 0 to 4 is therefore up to five labelled frames. The bank has no lead identifier (columns: sel, beid, bg, E, lead_present, lead_gap, agents30, nearest).
D2 Identifying the lead (the continuity rule of note N6, stage 0d code `s0c_read.build_pairs`). The same lead = the gap is finite on every frame S0 .. target and no 0.2 s step of the gap exceeds STEP_MAX_M = 3.0 m in absolute value. A window failing this is dropped from the scored set and counted ("lead_changed_or_gap_missing", with the sub-counts missing / jump).
D3 Window set. The windows are those of the S0 = 4 files of the ctx read: `ctxroll/LATENTS_CTX_<ARM>_R1.npz` (window g0 = start + 4 = the bank step of frame 4; target frame 14 = bank step g0 + 10), read through `rb_trained.load` (windows with a state and a bank row at S0 and at frame 14, finite actions and latents). This is 1,948 windows (not the 1,809 of the frame-0 read, which starts at frame 0 and reads frame 10). The card's "1,809 used" is the count of the frame-0 file; the S0 = 4 file is the one the card's own definitions (S0 = 4, K = 10, target frame 14) and its banked reads (control o4, the rollout read (i)) refer to. Both counts are printed. Every arm's file must list the same windows (asserted); the LABEL result uses that one list.
D4 Window to per-frame arrays. `SPEED_WINDOWS.npz` and `latents/<ARM>.pt` hold the windows in the order of `open_windows()` and are matched to the S0 file by (eid, t) with t = g0 + 11 (asserted: the matched `Z1[:, 4]` equals the file's `z0` within float tolerance, and the speed file's eid/t equal).
D5 Label history and H_MIN. For the LABEL source the history is the trailing run of labelled frames ending at frame 4 inside frames 0 to 4, extended backward only while the 0.2 s step stays within 3.0 m (the same lead, D2). A window enters a label rule only if that run has at least 3 frames (frame 4 plus at least two more; H_MIN = 3). Windows without it are dropped from the label rules and counted. For the VISION source the history is all five frames 0 to 4 (the probe gives a value on every frame; presence is not a vision output); this is stated as a limit, the card says "the gap source has a value".
D6 Closing speed c. Least-squares slope of gap against time (0.2 s per frame) over the history frames of D5, in m/s.
D7 Ego speed. `V[:, 4 .. 14]` of `SPEED_WINDOWS.npz` (logged, teacher-forced). Ego speed at step k (k = 0 .. K-1) = mean of V at frames 4 + k and 5 + k.
D8 Rules. R0 = gap(S0). R1 = gap(S0) + T * c with T = 0.2 * K. R2: vL = V[4] + c; pred = gap(S0) + sum over k of 0.2 * (vL - ego_k). R3: pred = gap(S0) + a * (T * c) + b * (X) + d with X the ego term of R2, X = T * V[4] - sum_k 0.2 * ego_k (so R2 = gap(S0) + T*c + X exactly, and R2 is R3 with a = b = 1, d = 0). The three coefficients are fitted by ordinary least squares of (gap(target) - gap(S0)) on [T*c, X, 1] over the scored windows of the training folds; applied to the held-out fold. The fold of a window = the fold of its episode in `RP.make_folds` of the bank rows (the fold map of the r-b read, asserted equal to `w_fold` of the latents file). For the VISION source the gap(S0) and c of a training-fold window are its own out-of-fold probe values (each window's probe never saw its episode), so no fitted object sees the held-out fold. R3 is not defined for the LABEL source in the outcomes (R2 is its primary); it is also printed for the label source.
D9 Vision probe. For each fold f the probe `fit_probe(Sb[vtr], y[vtr], epi[vtr], SEED + f)` with vtr = the bank rows of the training folds with a finite gap (as `rb_trained.run_folds`), applied to the grid state at frames 0 to 4 of the held-out windows (`Z1` of the arm's `.pt`). Nothing of a frame after 4 enters (o6).
D10 Scored set. Windows with a finite gap at frame 4 and at the target frame and the continuity rule of D2 over frames 4 to target; further per rule the history rule of D5. Rules and R0 of the same source are always compared on the same windows (the windows of the rule). Skill = 1 - SSE(rule) / SSE(R0 of the same source) on the target gap; the interval is the episode-cluster bootstrap, B = 2,000, `RP.boot_counts(n_episodes of the scored set, 2000, 0)`, resampling the per-episode sums of SSE; one draw shared by every rule of a source. Differences between two rules are paired on the same draw. The level R2 uses `S.single_r2` / `S.paired`. "Interval excludes 0" = the lower end of the 95 % percentile interval above 0 (rule R12 of stage 0).
D11 Banked rollout read (i). The banked rollout of the card = `ctxroll/rb/OOF_RB_<ARM>_R1.npz` (`p_i`: context 1, the rule of the r-b read, started at frame 4, target frame 14, same yk). `OOF_RB_<ARM>_R3h.npz` and `_R3g.npz` (the predictor with its own three-frame context) are printed beside it as secondary comparisons; the outcome SLOT-BEATS-ROLLOUT is named against R1 (the card says "the banked rollout read (i)", the one-state rule of the r-b read), and is stated with a flag if it differs against R3h / R3g. Level difference = level R2 of R3 minus level R2 of the banked p_i on the common scored windows, paired bootstrap.
D12 Control o1 construction. With the least-squares slope as the estimate of c, an exactly constant lead speed and a varying logged ego speed in the history give a slope that is not lead speed minus ego speed at S0, so R2 cannot reach 1 to 1e-9 on such a series. The planted series therefore uses a history that is exactly linear in time with slope vL - V[4] (so c is exact), followed by the future gap given by the identity with constant vL and the logged ego speeds, vL drawn per window (seed 0) in [-3, 3] m/s: R2 skill must read 1 within 1e-9 and R1 must read below 1 (strictly: R1 skill < 1 - 1e-6 whenever the ego speed varies). Printed beside it, report only: the series with a strictly constant lead speed over all frames (its R2 skill is below 1 by construction when the ego speed varies in the history, which is why it is not the pass criterion).
D13 Control o2. The future gap is permuted across the scored windows (seed 0) BEFORE any fit of R3; every rule of every source reads skill at most +0.02.
D14 Control o3. Copy skill is exactly 0.0; a rule that returns the logged target gap reads exactly 1.0.
D15 Control o4. The level R2 (around the mean, `r2_pooled`) of VISION R0 equals the banked level R2 of (ii) in `OOF_RB_<ARM>_R1.npz` (`p_ii`) on the windows where that file has a finite yk, within 0.005. Both are computed on the same windows and the same yk.
D16 Control o5. R1 with the history frames 1 to 5 (base gap = gap(frame 5), c from frames 1 to 5 under D5, same T) on the LABEL source on the windows of the R1 scored set that also have frames 1 to 5; its skill over the S0 copy must read above the skill of R1 (same windows, same denominator).
D17 Control o6. Two mutation checks in the script, both required to leave the outputs unchanged (max abs difference 0.0): the grid states of the frames after 4 are replaced by random numbers and the VISION rules recomputed; the target gaps of the held-out fold are replaced by random numbers and the R3 predictions of that fold recomputed. Plus an assert that the rule functions take as inputs only the history gaps, the ego speeds and gap(S0).
D18 Horizon profile. Target frames 6, 8, 10, 12 (K = 2, 4, 6, 8; 0.4 .. 1.6 s) with T = 0.2 K and the continuity rule over frames 4 to target; every rule scaled, R3 refitted per horizon. Printed for the LABEL R2 and the VISION R3 (also R1, R2); decides nothing.
D19 Seeds. The vision outcome needs both seeds of an arm type (P_s0 and P_s1; G_s0 and G_s1); one seed alone does not name an outcome.
D20 Fallback for "not defined". Where a quantity has fewer than 30 scored windows it is printed with its n and no outcome is named on it.
