# S0_DEFS (PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD12, Stage 0). Every choice the card leaves open, fixed before any outcome number was computed.
Tier T0: a world-model diagnostic, never driving performance. CPU only (CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1). Code: `s0_read.py` (this folder). Reused unchanged by import: `res/resprobe.py` (make_folds, inner_split, ridge_solve, ALPHAS, fit_p1, fit_p2, run_probe_cv, boot_counts, ep_stats_r2, boot_r2, diff_row, auc_pooled, r2_pooled, ci).

R1 Token source. `E:/Projects/TanitAD-artifacts/sfresh_inputs/cache/eval/clips/<clip>.pt`, key `pool4x10`, fp8 [T,40,1024] (141 clips; MEASURED on this box 2026-11-13: all present). Converted fp8 -> float32 for every computation; held in RAM as float16 (an exact copy of an fp8 value), never written to disk. Cell c = row-major index r*10 + col of the 4x10 grid (the cache order). Nothing is rebuilt on the GPU; `full/feat/LO_r128.i8` is not used.
R2 Rows (the unit of r-a, of the PCA fit and of s1/s2). The bank frames of `res/cache/frames.npz` (139 episodes) with finite ego vector E (this drops grid step 0, whose ego row is NaN): (clip, g) with tokens at cache row g (RES_DEFS a). Labels per row: `lead_gap` (metres, finite only with a lead within 40 m) and `lead_present` (finite 0/1), exactly the banked arrays.
R3 Windows (the unit of the predictor and of r-b). The WMACT eval-141 windows with 16 real frames (`latents.pt`, `ok.all`, 1,976) restricted to the clips of the bank (LATVIS R1: 1,948 expected; the count is printed). Start frame g0 = t - 15, horizon +10 cache steps = 2.0 s (LATVIS R1). A window is USED when the bank rows (clip, g0) and (clip, g0+10) both exist (E finite at g0 and g0+10). Dropped windows are counted in RESULT_S0.json.
R4 Folds. `RP.make_folds(episode id of each bank row, k=5, seed 0)`: the LATVIS construction (LATVIS R7 applies the same function to the pool clips, which are the same 139 episodes; the run asserts equality of the two episode-to-fold maps on the full set and records it). Window folds = the fold of the window's episode. Every fitted object (PCA, predictor, probes, penalties) sees training-fold episodes only.
R5 States. Per fold, on the bank rows of the TRAINING folds only (all of them, no label used): S0g = per cell, centred on the training mean, the first 16 principal directions of the 1024-d token (covariance eigenvectors, numpy eigh), cells concatenated in cell order: 640 numbers, unwhitened scores. S0m = the token mean over the 40 cells (1024-d), centred on the training mean, first 128 principal directions: 128 numbers. Test and window rows are projected with the fold's training basis. (The `LEAK_PCA_FIT` switch of the module exists only so that the mutation test can remove the fold restriction.)
R6 Predictor. Per fold and per state, a multi-output ridge fitted on TRAINING-fold windows: inputs = [state at g0 (centred), the logged actions `A_raw` rows 0..9 of the window (20 numbers = 10 steps x 2), standardised on the training windows and scaled to the mean variance of the state columns]; target = state(g0+10) - state(g0); prediction = state(g0) + ridge. Penalty = RP.ALPHAS (times trace/dim), chosen by the validation squared error of the change on an inner episode split (RP.inner_split, 20 %, seed 0) of the training windows, then refitted on all training windows. Intercept included. Horizon 0 (control s4) runs the same function with target = state(g0) - state(g0) = 0.
R7 Probes (the card's "ridge and kernel ridge"), one function `fit_probe` used for every probe of this read (states, planted flag, ego-only floor of r-b): candidates = ridge (RP.ALPHAS, trace-scaled) and RBF kernel ridge (gamma = {0.5, 1, 2} / median squared distance of a training subsample, lambda in {1e-3, 1e-2, 1e-1, 1}, targets centred); the better candidate by the validation squared error on the inner episode split of the training rows is refitted on all training rows (kernel ridge on at most 3,000 training rows, drawn with a fixed seed, to bound cost). Features are centred on the training rows and divided by one global scale (square root of the mean column variance). Binary targets (presence, planted flag) are fitted as 0/1 regression and read as scores.
R8 r-a (retention). Rows of R2; for each state the probe of R7 on the 640 (or 128) state numbers ALONE (no ego block), target `lead_gap` on the rows where it is finite (R2 pooled over the five out-of-fold predictions; metres, untransformed) and `lead_present` on the finite rows (AUROC, pooled). Ego-only floor of r-a = the FULLRES rule R7: per target the better (pooled out-of-fold metric) of RP ridge P1, RP MLP P2 and a plain linear fit on the 8 standardised E features, same rows and folds (this is what control s3 must reproduce: gap R2 0.133 within 0.03).
R9 "Lead at both times". A used window with finite `lead_gap` at (clip, g0) and at (clip, g0+10). n is printed. Both gaps are the banked labels.
R10 r-b (imagined lead). Rows of R9, target = lead_gap at g0+10. The probe of R7 is fitted on the TRUE state(g0+10) of the training-fold windows of R9 (the card: fitted on true future states) and applied, unchanged, to (i) the predicted state of R6, (ii) the present state(g0), (iii) the true state(g0+10) (ceiling). (iv) ego-only floor = the probe of R7 fitted and applied on [E at g0 (8 numbers), the 20 logged-action numbers of R6] on the same rows. Pooled out-of-fold R2 of metres.
R11 Bootstrap. RP.boot_counts(n_ep = 139, B = 2,000, seed 0): paired, episode cluster, ONE draw shared by every pair of this read (B = 200 in smoke). A difference is "separated above 0" when its 95 % percentile interval has a lower end above 0. Episodes without a valid row add nothing. For r-a the clusters are the episodes of the bank rows, for r-b the episodes of the R9 windows.
R12 Outcome names, exactly as the card writes them, with "beats" read as "difference above 0, interval excluding 0" (separated above 0):
 - per state: `S0-LEAD-KEPT` if R2gap(state) - R2gap(ego floor of R8) separated above 0, else `S0-LEAD-LOST`;
 - per state: `S0-IMAGINE-LEAD` if R2(i) - R2(ii) AND R2(i) - R2(iv) are both separated above 0; `S0-IMAGINE-EGO` if only the first is; else `S0-IMAGINE-FLAT`;
 - headline pair: `GRID-ADDS` if R2gap(S0g) - R2gap(S0m) is separated above 0 (r-a), else `GRID-EQUAL`.
R13 Controls (each must read its known value, or no outcome name is printed; every control is printed first):
 - s1: all labels permuted within fold among their valid rows (seed 1; r-a gap and presence, r-b gap): every R2 of r-a gap (S0g, S0m, ego floor) and of r-b ((i), (ii), (iii) of both states, (iv)) at or below 0.02; the presence AUROC under permutation is printed beside (not part of the bar).
 - s2: planted object (the FULLRES f5 construction, with the amplitude fixed in natural units): a fixed random unit vector (seed 5) times 12 sigma_v is added to ONE random cell (of 40, seed 5) in a random half of the bank rows (seed 5), where sigma_v = the standard deviation, over all bank rows, of that cell's token along the vector (label free). The planted flag is the target; the PCA of that cell and of the mean token are refitted on the training folds with the planted tokens, all other cells unchanged; AUROC from S0g above 0.95 and from S0m at or below 0.60. DEPARTURE FROM THE f5 WORDING (found by the smoke, label-free, before any outcome): a vector of the median token L2 norm (8.3 here) is about 100 sigma_v (sigma_v 0.083), and then even the mean state reads it (AUROC 0.9996 on the 12-episode smoke), so the bar "S0m at or below 0.60" cannot hold at that amplitude. A label-free calibration on the same 12 episodes (planted cell only, amplitudes 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 sigma_v) read grid AUROC 0.50, 0.51, 0.52, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00 and mean AUROC 0.46, 0.46, 0.46, 0.46, 0.48, 0.70; 12 sigma_v lies inside the window where the grid sees the object and the mean does not. The amplitude is fixed at 12 sigma_v before the full run.
 - s3: ceiling: the point R2 of (iii) is at or above (i) and (ii) for each state; and the ego-only floor of r-a gap reads 0.133 within 0.03.
 - s4: horizon 0: the predicted state equals the present state, maximum absolute difference exactly 0.0 over all test windows of all folds, both states.

R18 Control s2p (LEAD rule, added 2026-11-13 before the full read and before any outcome number; note `PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD12_N3.md`). The card's s2 speaks of an object "with a known position". The solver lemma of group P (`fv/FV_SPEC_V5_POOL.md`, VC-P3, VC-P4, written before this control was measured) says the POSITION of an object cannot be read from a mean over cells at any amplitude, while its PRESENCE can (VC-P7). So the position is the amplitude-free form of the control: every bank row carries the planted vector at the f5 amplitude (the median token L2 norm, no tuning) either in the planted cell or in its mirror cell of the same grid row (column 9 - col), chosen per row (seed 6); the target is which of the two. The PCA of both cells is refitted on the training folds with the planted tokens; the mean token gets the same shift vector / 40 in every row. Bars as s2: AUROC from S0g above 0.95 and from S0m at or below 0.60 (the lemma predicts 0.50). s2 (presence at 12 sigma_v, R13) is kept as written by the build; BOTH controls must pass for any outcome name to be printed. The smoke prints s2p beside s2 (label free).
