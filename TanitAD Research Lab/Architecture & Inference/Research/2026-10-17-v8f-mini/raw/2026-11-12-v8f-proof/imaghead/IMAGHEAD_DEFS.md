# IMAGHEAD_DEFS: the read tool `imaghead` of card PREREG_V8F_PROOF_ADD36 (built 2026-11-15, dev box, CPU, no training, no git, no pod)

Tier T0 (a world-model diagnostic on logged frames, never a driving number). STRATEGIC: PAUSED (PI item 25). LONGITUDINAL, LATERAL, TACTICAL are not measured by this read.
Files: `imag_dump.py` (pod side), `imag_read.py` (dev side), `test_imaghead.py` (31 tests, 15 s), this file, `HANDOFF.md`. Tree: `moved_from_c/leadsup/tree_patch/stack`; the 11 pinned sha256 of `spatial/leadsup/PINS.txt` were verified on it (all match; also a test).

## What it reads
* Windows: the 1,948 windows of `LATENTS_CTX_<ARM>_R1.npz` (`eid`, `g0` = bank step of S0, `act_scaled` [n,10,2] = scaled actions of transitions 4..13); frame f of a window = state-file frame `g0 - 4 + f`; frame 4 = t, frame 9 = t + 1.0 s, frame 14 = t + 2.0 s. Labels from `leadsup/read/eval_pack.npz` (`G15` label gap frames 0..14, `V` ego speed per frame, `pres0`), same order.
* Dump windows = those with a finite label gap at t (MEASURED on the pack: 534). Of them 441 have a lead at t and at t + 2.0 s (MEASURED: equals the finite `yk` of `OOF_RB_G_s0.npz` exactly, same windows, label difference 0.0, 48 episodes with a label, 139 in the floor file). H2 windows (gap 5 to 50 m, ego speed >= 5 m/s at t): 356, of them 146 with a gap below 20 m (MEASURED on the pack).
* Head reads (physical units: presence logit, gap [m] = out x 50, closing [m/s] = out x 10): present (frames 0..4), true future 1.0 s (5..9) and 2.0 s (10..14), rolled out at 1.0 s (steps 1..5) and 2.0 s (steps 6..10) under the logged actions and under offsets d = 0 (offset code path), -2, -1, +1 m/s2.
* The rollout is the stack's own `HJEPAStack.rollout(1, z0, actions, context=ctx)` (`hjepa.py:919`), start state = frame 4, 10 steps, context = the predictor's own (3 by default; read as in `ctx_roll.py`), the context grows by its own predictions. No predictor is re-implemented.
* Offset units (file:line, each re-asserted against the source by `facts_check`, test `test_facts_of_the_tree_are_on_the_stated_lines`): the level-1 acceleration channel enters the predictor as `act[...,0] / WM_ACC_SCALE` (`v8f_mini.py:947`, `WM_ACC_SCALE = 2.0` at `v8f_mini.py:201`), then the action embedding (`hjepa.py:650`). The offset d [m/s2] is added to the SCALED channel as d / 2.0 at every step, yaw channel unchanged. MEASURED: the banked `act_scaled[:, :, 0]` equals `A_raw[:, 4:14, 0] / 2.0` of `SPEED_WINDOWS.npz` with max abs difference 0.0 (1,948 windows).
* Floor read: `p_ivb`/`p_iv` of `OOF_RB_G_s<k>.npz`, chosen per seed by the pool_rb rule (larger R2). MEASURED: these arrays are bit-identical in G_s0, G_s1, W_s0, W_s1 (the floor is ego-only and arm-independent), so the G file of the same seed stands for any arm.

## Statistics
Calibrated reads as `pool_rb.py` (five folds by episode, fold = episode index of the floor file's `uep` modulo 5, linear fit out of fold); H1 = R2(ii,i) - R2(ii); H1e = R2(ii,fl,i) - R2(ii,fl); H1c, H1ec with the true future (iii). Two-level bootstrap = `pool_rb.pooled` draw (seeds with replacement, then episodes inside each drawn seed, B 2,000, rng 0). Equality with `pool_rb.calibrate`, `ep_stats_r2`, `r2_pooled` is a test (to 1e-12). H2: b = sum(r e) / sum(e^2) over windows and the three offsets, e = -0.5 d T^2; per seed with an episode-bootstrap interval, pooled with the two-level interval; T = 2.0 s named, 1.0 s and the gap < 20 m subsets reported; mean response per offset, presence response (logit and probability) beside.

## Controls and where each is measured
* c1: dump time and reader: `roll_off[d=0] == roll_logged` exactly (0.0). Mutation: an offset path that adds a constant at d = 0 reads > 0 (RED); a 1e-3 change in a dump makes the reader fail c1.
* c2: two code paths on the SAME model: the tool's chunked `rollout` against the trainer's own rollout core `roll_pairs` (start s = 4 of the 16-frame window), and the head on the tool's 5-frame slices against the head on the whole 16-frame window sequence (the call the trainer's `WMAux.forward` makes), both <= 1e-5; the head comparison is in the trainer's output units (gap / 50); the same difference in metres is 50 times larger (float32 noise of matmul over different batch shapes, 2.05e-5 m on the tiny model) and is reported beside, not the bar. Reader side: the label of the reader equals the banked `yk` (0.0 difference, MEASURED on real files). **Banked files: NOT checked.** No banked rollout or head output exists for LS or RL (they are untrained); the banked rollouts of the G arms (`LATENTS_CTX_G_s*_R3g.npz`) could be compared with `--banked-roll` but need a G checkpoint, none was at hand on this box and G has no lead head (the tool refuses it). The existing LSD head outputs in `leadsup/read/in/` belong to a checkpoint not at hand. The check against banked files is therefore open until a head checkpoint exists.
* c3: model level (test): planted predictor (exact constant-acceleration kinematics per step on a displacement coordinate) and planted head through `compute_reads` and the reader's slope code: slope 1.0 within 1e-6 at T = 2.0 and 1.0; reader level: planted exact response through `slope_stats`/`pool_slope`. Mutations: wrong sign, wrong time scaling, offset in wrong units (slope 2.0) go RED.
* c4: dump time: a deep copy whose level-1 action embedding (`st.act_embed`, all weights and biases) is zero; reader needs exactly 0.0. The copy rolls with its own stack. Mutations: an un-zeroed copy reads > 1e-6 (RED); a dump with 1e-3 in the zero-copy response fails c4 and blocks every name.
* c5: reader, on the arm's own data: labels permuted inside each seed (interval lower bound of H1 and H1e <= 0); present read given as rollout (|H1|, |H1e| <= 1e-9); true future given as rollout (H1 > 1e-9 at every seed). Mutations: identity permutation, no substitution, and an uninformative true future each go RED.
* c6 and c7 are the lead's (pod gate, family read); not part of this tool.

## Decisions and departures from the card (for the lead's note before data)
1. **Input path.** Card 3: "the eval cache, the eval grid, the eval join". The dump reads the eval STATE FILE of `extract_states.py` (made from the cache), the window list and the pack (carry the grid and join information: window ids, labels), as the existing LEADSUP read does; it does not rebuild states from the cache. States are float16 files cast to float32, as in `lead_dump.py` (the head saw float32 in training); an `--window-states` npz (`Z1` [n,16,D]) is also accepted.
2. **Actions** come only from `act_scaled` of the window file (transitions 4..13); that is all a 2.0 s rollout from frame 4 needs.
3. **Names**: "the two-level interval lies above 0" = the lower bound of the pooled two-level interval > 0, and each seed's point value in the stated range. The H1 name is printed as `IMAG-HEAD-ADDS + IMAG-HEAD-ADDS-LEAD` or `... + IMAG-HEAD-ADDS-EGO-ONLY` (the card's two lines read together). H2 overshoot (b > 1.5) is listed per seed. Pair names: `RL-IMAGINES-BETTER` needs H1(RL) - H1(LS) > 0 at both seeds and the paired two-level interval lower bound > 0 (same draw for both arms); `RL-RESPONDS-BETTER` needs |b - 1| smaller for RL at both seeds and the upper bound of the paired difference < 0.
4. **c5 third part** "H1 lies above 0": every seed's point value > 1e-9 (a numerical zero is not above 0; found by a mutation run).
5. **c3 in the reader** is the planted-slope variant (no model); the model-level c3 is the test file.
6. **Floor** from the G file of the same seed (see above); `--floor-oof` overrides.
7. Dump windows: only those with a lead at t; the reader uses the floor file's 1,809-window list and the dump's rows by (eid, g0).
8. The dump needs the checkpoint to have `lead_sup on`, a grid state and level-1 action width 2 (acc_yaw); an EGOV (speed) arm is refused.
9. float32 nondeterminism on GPU could break the exact 0.0 of c1 (same shapes, same chunking are used for the logged and the d = 0 run; exact on CPU, MEASURED); if a GPU run fails c1 or c4 at 1e-7 level, say so, do not loosen.

## Not verified

The real-data checks were window and label identity, the acceleration scaling and the floor identity (above).
