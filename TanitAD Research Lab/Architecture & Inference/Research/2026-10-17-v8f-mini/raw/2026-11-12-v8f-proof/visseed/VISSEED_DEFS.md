## 0. FIRST: clauses of the card that could fail by construction, and what the code cannot do as written

1. **x0 "the 500 train clips".** Implemented as: fit clips plus sel clips read by the fit (`clips_read` in `FIT_RECORD.json`, `crop_fit.py:300`) are 500 in all, all distinct, and equal (order too) to the clips read by the two fits on record. Card ADD99 section 2 takes the first 500 of the ordered list as the train list (fit and sel together). If the pod's train list has another size, x0 fails by construction. I could not look: the fit records are result files.
2. **x0 "the settings of card ADD99".** Checked as `hparams_equal_card` true, `hparams` equal to `CARD_HPARAMS` (copied from `crop_fit.py:32`) and equal to the `hparams` of the records of seeds 0 and 1. The tool hash `tool_sha256` is kept in the record but is NOT part of the pass (the card does not name it).
3. **x0 additions that only make the card's words checkable** (each is a way for x0 to fail that the card's sentence implies): the record's `head_pt_sha256` equals the file on disk; the arguments of the call (`argv`) name arm D, head H5 and the seed, no `--permute-labels` (the queue calls the tool with exactly those words); `eval_list_sha256` of every record equals the sha256 of the list on disk and that of the records of seeds 0 and 1; the two head files of seeds 0 and 1 on disk, where they exist, equal their records.
4. **x1 inherits the v0 point of card ADD101 (section 0.1 of VISREP_DEFS).** A dump with more token frames than the join has frames fails by construction. The three dumps are made on the rows of the present reader's dump of seed 0 (the row template, card section 2), the same rows the dump of fit seed 0 of card ADD102 passed with; not expected to fail. v0 also needs `dump_seed == S`: `crop_dump.py:70` sets `seed` to the call's `--seed`, which is S.
5. **x1 third clause (the dump's meta names the head file of its fit).** `reader_s<S>_meta.json` (`crop_dump.py:103-106`) must carry `head_pt_sha256` equal to the sha256 of `fits/D_H5_s<S>/head.pt` read from disk at build time, `seed` S, arm D, head H5, `permute_labels` false and `dump_sha256` equal to the dump file read. The reader repeats all of it from disk when it opens the controls.
6. **x3, a lead step with no head output equals V0.** Inherited from the readers of cards ADD102 and ADD103 (any V0 value at the same clip and frame within 1e-3 m; `n_ref_missing` fails). The head H5 has no output at frames 0 to 3, so such rows exist; V0 has a row at every lead step it logged. Expected to pass; I could not look.
7. **x4 needs C(VD5cx) = 0.0200 to four decimals** from the banked rollouts, next to RR 0.0954, RLc 0.0000, VD5c 0.0169. A banked value of 0.02004 or 0.01996 passes; 0.0200 is a rounded value of card ADD103.
8. **x6 inherits the unverified point of card ADD103 (its DEFS 0.6):** whether an engine control inside `ALL_PASS` assumes a zero closing column for RR-form rule logs. The engine now reads 11 runs (six new, RR, RLc, VD5c, VD5cx, R0).
9. **The name `VISSEED10K-HOLDS` is hard to reach, not impossible by construction.** It needs all three arms CUTS-CONTACT and two of three WITHIN-HALF-THE-GAP (upper end of C(X) minus C(RLc) at or below H = 0.0477); card ADD103 had VD5cx's upper end at +0.0498. With few windows a name can turn on two windows (card section 6). Not changed.
10. **Card name for the case "all three cut, none NOT-REACHED, fewer than two WITHIN" is `VISSEED10K-NOT-DECIDED`;** `HOLDS` and `DOES-NOT-HOLD` cannot both be true (HOLDS needs all CUTS and none NOT-REACHED). Implemented as written (`holds_name`); `card_check` re-derives it from the intervals and the names stage refuses on a mismatch.
11. **H.** The names use H = half of the point value of C(RR) minus C(RLc); `h_note` in the names result gives it next to 0.0477 and flags a difference.
12. **The per-fit contact table assumes the engine's `levels_per_seed` is ordered model seed 0, 1** (as in the reader of card ADD103). It is printed with no name; it is not used by any control or name. VD5c at model seed k is fit seed k and VD5cx at model seed k is fit seed 1-k (card section 3).
13. **Not done by code (lead):** freezing the hash file, logging the controls in `TIMES_NOTE.md` before the names, running the pytest on the dev box (x5 reads the text of `lead_pytest.out`: "N passed" and no "failed"/"error"), running the fits and the rollouts.
14. With one control failed nothing is opened: the names stage refuses unless x0 to x6 are all present and PASS; the controls stage writes the values under `withheld` when any control fails.

## 2. Definitions with sources
* **Fit queue** (`pod_visseed_fit_queue.sh`). The tools read and write under `--root` as follows (checked in the source): `crop_fit.py` reads `<root>/lists/train_final.txt` (`:232`), `<root>/lists/eval141.txt`, `<root>/tok/D/<clip>.npy` (`TokenStore`, `:104-121`) and the train join; writes `<root>/fits/D_H5_s<S>/head.pt` and `FIT_RECORD.json` (`:236`, `:298-306`) and refuses when that record exists. `crop_dump.py` reads `<root>/fits/D_H5_s<S>/{FIT_RECORD.json,head.pt}` (`:78-93`), the tokens, and `<present-dump>/reader_s<S>_score.npz` (`:85`); writes `<root>/dump/D_H5/reader_s<S>_score.npz` and `reader_s<S>_meta.json` (`:79-106`) and refuses when the score file exists. The queue links: `fitroot/tok` -> `run1/tok` (one link), `fitroot/lists/{train_final.txt,eval141.txt,LISTS_RECORD.json}` -> the same files of `run1/lists` (the record is not read by the tool, linked for provenance), and makes real folders `fits/`, `dump/`; `present_tpl/reader_s{2,3,4}_score.npz` -> `/workspace/v8proof/closeread/dump/both/reader_s0_score.npz`. Nothing is created or linked inside `run1`. A link that already exists must point to the same target, else STOP. Three fits start together (`VS_MAX_JOBS` 3), then three dumps; a fit with its `FIT_RECORD.json`, a dump with score and meta file are skipped; a score file without its meta file is a STOP.
* **Gap files** (`make_seed_gapfile.py:build_arrays`, unchanged from card ADD102): one row per join frame, `clip_index`, `frame`, `gap` (the head's output as dumped, NaN where none), `has_out`, `clips`, `seed` (the fit seed); dump `<fit-root>/dump/D_H5/reader_s<S>_score.npz`. No map, no clip, no minimum. The same file serves both model seeds.
* **x0 record** (`x0_check`): per new fit and for the two fits on record the facts of `collect()`; see section 0 items 1 to 3. **x1 record**: `v0`, `build` (saved gap equals a second routine within 1e-9, output flag is finiteness) and `meta` (section 0 item 5); `x1` = all three. The record carries `eval_join_sha256`, the facts of the fit records and the dump metas.
* **Arms** (`pod_visseed10k_queue.sh`, last rollout lines): per model seed k, form RR, reader `lr16/TK16_s<k>`, q50 map, the arguments of the VD5c call of card ADD102 plus the closing switch and the gap file of fit seed S: `VGAP=$GF/VGAPF_s<S>.npz VCLOSE=1`, run folders `integ10k/t1/I10RQ50KF5P30Hb20fF<S>_s<k>`.
* **Admission** (queue): wrapper 28bc31f21ecb4ccb with the pod check record of card ADD102 (`visrule10k/check_v`); `$GP/gapfiles/VISSEED_GAPFILES_RECORD.json` with `x0` and `x1` PASS, the sha256 of the three files and of the evaluation join; banked runs RR, RLc (dump folders), `I10RQ50KF5P30Hb20vV0`, `vVD5c` (ADD102) and `wVD5cx` (ADD103) with T1_DONE, both seeds; the pinned tool, overlay tree, checkpoints, grid, join, cache as in the ADD103 queue.
