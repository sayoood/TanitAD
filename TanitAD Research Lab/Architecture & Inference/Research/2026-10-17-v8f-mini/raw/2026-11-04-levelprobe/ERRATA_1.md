# ERRATA_1 to PREREG_LEVELPROBE.md (written 2026-11-04 before any fit, any feature load or any rollout; hashed into FROZEN_SHA.txt as an appended line)

**E1. Section B2 contains an internal inconsistency in its computation clause, found when writing the extraction code.** B2 defines the pooled feature as "the mean of each 4x5-token block of the 8x20 grid (2 rows x 4 columns of blocks) x 1024 channels = 8,192 features" and then says it is "computed in float32 as the mean of probe 3's exact G4 over 2x2 blocks of G4 cells". The second clause is impossible: G4 is 4x10 cells, and 2x2 blocks of it give a 2x5 grid (10,240 features), not 2x4. **The definition stands (4x5-token blocks, 2x4 cells, 8,192 features, the dimension B2 and B3 state); the computation clause is replaced by the direct computation described next.** The features are computed directly from the G8 fp16 tokens: float32 cast, then the mean over each 4-row x 5-column token block, i.e. `x.reshape(n, 2, 4, 4, 5, 1024).mean(axis=(2, 4))` flattened in the order (block row, block column, channel).
No rule, threshold, estimator, control or population of the card changes; no number from any fit existed when this was written.
