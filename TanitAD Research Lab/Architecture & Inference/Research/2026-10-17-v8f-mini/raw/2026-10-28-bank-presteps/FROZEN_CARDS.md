### H-DD-FITGAP (HYPOTHESES.md lines 27-36)

## H-DD-FITGAP (rank 0a): is the launch crawl a fit gap or a generalisation gap?

**Hypothesis.** On the models' own TRAIN launch windows, the T1-kin launch-speed deficit is at least half its size on held-out windows: the crawl is fit- / loss-limited, not data-limited, so no data-amount or data-selection lever can clear a crawl bar.
**Instrument.** The M-LAUNCH statistic, mean(v_own - v_GT) at ticks 1-2 over GT-launch windows [MI REG H-V8F-LAUNCH-INTENT-1: 327 windows / 49 episodes on eval-141], computed on three window sets for 6 checkpoints (M2b s0/s1, DAG-OACC s0/s1, REP s0/s1): (i) TRAIN launch windows (M2b's own bank already holds 613,132 TRAIN own states with the matched route point: a file read [MI RAW/2026-10-27-dagger-run/bank_s0/bank.json]; other checkpoints need rollouts on a ~10% TRAIN clip subset); (ii) the 60-clip holdout `540:600`; (iii) eval-141 as the reference. r = C_train / C_eval, episode-cluster bootstrap.
**Proposed decision rule (commit before any number).** FIT-LIMITED if r >= 0.5 at >= 5 of 6 checkpoints; GEN-GAP if r <= 0.25 at >= 5 of 6; MIXED otherwise (holdout decides the tie: r_holdout within 0.1 of r_eval).
**Predicted [EST].** FIT-LIMITED, P = 0.6. Basis: the register already records "at full data T is fit-limited, eval ~ train" [MI REG H-V8F-DSCALE-1, R70 correction]; a 1.58926x weight on exactly the TRAIN launch windows left the eval crawl unmoved [MI LAUNCH-INTENT, M-LAUNCH +0.03 m/s]; point-estimate regressors saturate early [PUB 2603.15185 p6] and interpolate between trajectory modes [PUB 2603.15185 p1 Fig 1c] (stay vs go as the mode pair is my HYP).
**Falsifier.** r <= 0.25 at >= 5 of 6: the crawl is a generalisation gap; promote H-DD-LAUNCHMIX and H-DD-SEMBAL.
**Cheapest experiment.** Zero-training, dev-box inference only; ~1 agent-day. No box 2, no PI decision.
**Admissibility.** Read-only; eval used as a reference, never to select; no new input. Parity: none.


### H-ST-RECOVERYLABEL (HYPOTHESES.md lines 50-59)

## H-ST-RECOVERYLABEL (rank 1): give the discarded off-route own states a valid recovery label

**Hypothesis.** The DAgger bank throws away the model's largest deviations, because the human label at the matched route point is invalid beyond 3 m lateral / 30 deg heading (tick 2: 12.3% + 12.0% of 306,566 records; tick 1: 0.2% + 6.9%; coverage 0.756) [MI bank.json]. Labelling those states with a kinematically feasible recovery plan onto the human path (geometric blend at a lookahead, human speed profile from r', bounded by the P10 accel / curvature limits) and adding them to the own-state draw pool lifts usable tick-2 states from 53.9% to ~78% and lowers T1-kin ADE6 versus DAG-OACC at p = 0.1.
**Why now.** (i) CAT-K's principle: closed-loop SFT works because visited states stay near GT so the GT label stays a valid supervision signal; a 7M model beat a 102M one [PUB 2412.05334 p1, p6]. (ii) OPTED (LTFv6): logged-state BC 12.8, closed-loop baseline with recovery supervision 32.9, privileged-teacher supervision 41.8; the authors credit "unbiased rollouts and better supervision signal" [PUB 2609.20756 p4 Table I, p6]. (iii) Our ORC arm moved states but **left targets unchanged** and harmed (R-ADE +0.1434 m, R-T0 fails both seeds) [MI REG H-V8F-DAG-ORC-1]: the label half is the untested half. (iv) Iteration 2 added nothing (DAG2 - DAG ~0) [MI REG H-V8F-DAGGER2-1], so more of the same states is not the lever; different states with valid labels is.
**Predicted [EST].** T1-kin ADE6 vs DAG-OACC -0.12 m (80% PI -0.35..+0.15), concentrated in turn windows; P(<= -2F at both seeds) = 0.15; P(HARMS: R-ADE or R-T0 breach) = 0.30 (label noise; ORC precedent).
**Cheapest discriminating experiment.** (1) Zero-training, agent 1-2 days: build the labeller on the existing bank; identity test (an on-route zero-offset state reproduces the human label bit-for-bit) and a deliberate-regression arm (a labeller that ignores the offset must fail the gate); report the kept fraction (predict 53.9% -> ~78%) and the feasibility violation rate (must be 0). (2) Box 2: A_0, A_1 (p = 0.1, recovery pool on) against banked DAG-OACC s0/s1, plus A_rep,0/1: 4 runs x 38 min = ~2.6 h [MI summary.json 2,273 s per fine-tune]; T1-kin reads on the dev-box CPU.
**Falsifier.** ADE6 point estimate >= DAG-OACC's at either seed, or R-T0 above +0.0604 m, or the identity test fails. Then label validity beyond 3 m is not the lever and the DAgger axis is closed with DAGDOSE.
**Admissibility.** Labels use the future route (allowed); inference unchanged; no classifier output. Parity: derived from canonical TRAIN windows, eval-141 excluded by the bank's K-B3 control; kind = within-corpus derived states. Caveat: the bank rollout equals the eval rollout, so the gain is T1-kin-matched; T2 untested [HYP].
**Cost.** New code + 4 box-2 runs; box-2 PI go.


### H-ST-REGRET (HYPOTHESES.md lines 74-83)

## H-ST-REGRET (rank 3): outcome-weighted DAgger draws (RL-lite)

**Hypothesis.** Drawing bank states with probability proportional to the model's own deficit at that state (progress shortfall versus the human at r', plus curvature-chord noise), mixed 50 : 50 with uniform draws, concentrates the p = 0.1 own-state budget on crawl states, and moves the crawl rows (M1, L2) by >= their bars at both seeds with guards held.
**Why.** 88-96% of the L2 excess sits on states "that exist only in the model's own rollouts" [MI LCDIAG]; the uniform draw dilutes them. This is the cheapest RL-flavoured stage: an advantage-style weight on already-labelled on-policy data, no simulator, no reward model. Published forms: closed-loop-selected upsampling cuts collisions up to ~35% [PUB 2210.02174 p4]; difficulty-prioritised training -15% collisions at 10% of data [PUB 2212.01375 p1]; critical-state sampling +16% [PUB Prakash2020_DARB_cvlibs abstract]; Alpamayo-R1's RL data = disagreement samples plus an equal random share [PUB 2511.00088 p22]. Against: LAUNCH-INTENT's window-level weight read null / harmful [MI]; uncertainty-as-hardness is negative [PUB 2604.08366 via DSR; Prakash2020_DARB_cvlibs p8], so the weight must be an outcome (deficit), not a confidence.
**Zero-training pre-step.** Measure the crawl-state share of the 613,132-state bank (own speed < 0.5 x GT speed at r', GT >= 3 m/s) and the effective sample size of the uniform draw. If the share is >= 15% the uniform draw already covers it: card dies. If < 3%: the uniform draw yields ~450-600 crawl samples in the ~15-19k own-state draws; weighting gives >= 5x.
**Predicted [EST].** M1 / L2 move ~0.3 of their bars; P(M1 or L2 clears at both seeds) = 0.20; P(all four crawl rows) = 0.05; ADE6 +-0.1.
**Cheapest experiment.** Pre-step (CPU); then a sampler flag (`--dagger-weight deficit`, G-DVB entry required) and 4 box-2 runs (~2.6 h).
**Falsifier.** Crawl rows within RF0 at both seeds, or launch speed moves less than the 0.556 m/s training-draw floor in the M-LAUNCH instrument.
**Admissibility.** Weights computed from GT quantities at r' (labels); inference unchanged. Parity: reweighting within the bank.

## READING FIXES (one reading per ambiguity, fixed before data; added by the Research Lab agent, not by the cards)

Tier stamp for every number in this package: **T1-kin rollout states on TRAIN** (the DAgger bank, banked 2026-10-27, schema `v8f-dagger-bank-v1`, m = 10 periodic replan, logged camera). They are reads of banked states, not driving performance. Where eval-141 appears (FITGAP only) it is a reference, never a selector.

### Common
* **C1 Banks and checkpoints.** `bank_s0` = rollouts of M2b seed 0 (generating ckpt sha256 601cb435..., equal to the init ckpt sha256 in `v8f-dagger/M2b_s0_dag/summary.json` levers.init_ckpt); `bank_s1` = M2b seed 1 (6104fb15...). The two banks are different files (SHA256SUMS differ). The brief says "1 of the card's 6 checkpoints"; the two banks are **2 of the 6** (M2b s0 and M2b s1). This discrepancy is reported, not smoothed.
* **C2 Cluster unit.** Bootstrap clusters = clips (`ci`), 4,512 TRAIN clips; one clip is treated as one episode (whether several clips share a drive/session is UNVERIFIED; a session-level cluster would widen every CI). Estimator = episode-cluster bootstrap in the construction of `taniteval/ci.py` `_draws` (resample clusters with replacement, B = 2000, seed 0, 95 % percentile CI), computed on per-clip sufficient statistics (equivalent to resampling the clips' records and recomputing the statistic; a test pins the equivalence to `ci.episode_cluster_bootstrap`). It answers "would another draw of CLIPS say this?" only; it is blind to training-seed and inference-seed variance (`H-ESTIM-SEED-1`).
* **C3 Speeds.** `v_own` of a record = `kin3[0]` at row j (own state speed). It must equal the last row of `p10f` feature 0 (the quantity the eval-side M-LAUNCH reads as `batch["p10f"][:, -1, 0]`); if the two differ anywhere, FITGAP uses `p10f[:, -1, 0]` and the discrepancy is reported. The logged ("GT") speed of the human at row t of clip c = `D.yaw_kin_from_track(tracks[c])[0][t, 0]` (the same rule that produced the own state's speed, applied to the logged native track; `native_tracks.pt` sha256 1db431f1... equals the bank header `inputs_sha256.tracks`).

### H-ST-REGRET
* **R1 usable** = bank records with `reason == ok` (`v8f_dagger.py` module docstring: "only reason == OK is eligible"). Cut records are reported beside, never in the decision.
* **R2 crawl state** = `v_own < 0.5 * v_GT(r')` AND `v_GT(r') >= 3.0 m/s`, strict `<` and non-strict `>=` exactly as written, with `v_GT(r')` the logged speed at the MATCHED row r' (not the time-matched row j).
* **R3 decision statistic** = pooled (tick 1 and tick 2 together) crawl share among usable records, per seed; tick 1 and tick 2 are reported separately. **Card dies iff the pooled share is >= 15 % at BOTH seeds.** **">= 5x" branch iff the pooled share is < 3 % at BOTH seeds AND the achievable multiplier M_def (R5) is >= 5 at both.** Any other outcome (including seeds on different sides) = "middle band: report the multiplier, card neither dies nor is confirmed". The verdict is read on the point estimate; if the 95 % CI contains the threshold it is labelled "CI straddles".
* **R4 sampler-effective share (reported beside, not the decision).** The swap draws a training window (ci, t) uniformly, and if the bank has eligible records with r' == t picks one uniformly (`DaggerBank.pick`). Effective share = mean over eligible windows of (crawl count / candidate count). If it falls on a different side of a threshold than the record share, that is stated.
* **R5 multiplier.** Weighted draw = 50 : 50 mixture of the uniform draw and a draw proportional to `d_i = max(0, v_GT(r') - v_own)` over usable records (speed shortfall is the progress-shortfall proxy; the card's "curvature-chord noise" term has no definition in the card and is omitted, stated). `M_def = P_mix(crawl) / P_unif(crawl)`; ceiling `M_max = 0.5 + 0.5 / share` (all weight on crawl states).
* **R6 expected crawl count in a uniform p = 0.1 own-state draw** = `dagger_n x share`, with `dagger_n` read from the DAG-OACC run summaries (`v8f-dagoacc/M2b_s{0,1}_dagoacc/summary.json`: s0 14,688, s1 14,839; MEASURED, identical to the DAG run's, same sampler and seed). Both the record share and the sampler-effective share are applied.

### H-ST-RECOVERYLABEL
* **L1 scope.** The labeller acts on records with `reason in {off_lat, off_head}`. `ok` records keep the human label (the existing DAG-OACC behaviour). `end_edge` / `nonfinite` stay cut. An off_lat / off_head record whose matched row has no complete logged 6 s future (r' > T - 31, which the matcher's first-failing-cut order hides) cannot be labelled and is counted as `no_logged_future`.
* **L2 envelope (the stack's one spelling, `stack/tanitad/refs/feasible_decode.py`):** `A_MAX = 4.0 m/s^2`, `KAPPA_MAX = 0.2 1/m`, `MU_KAMM = 0.7` (friction disc `sqrt(a^2 + a_lat^2) <= mu g`, g = 9.80665), `MIN_SPEED = 0.5 m/s` (curvature denominator floor), `MARGIN = 1e-2` (limits scaled by 0.99). The brief's "P10 floor planner's accel / curvature limits": **no accel / curvature limit exists inside `P10Prior` itself** (it is an MLP on 8 kinematic features, `v8f_mini.py:239`); the only accel / curvature envelope in `stack/` is this one, so it is used and the gap is flagged UNVERIFIED-AS-"P10's".
* **L3 labeller.** World frame, 0.2 s grid, 30 waypoints. Speed profile `v_l[0] = v_own`; while `v_l[k-1] != v_h[k-1]`, `v_l[k] = v_h[k]` if `|v_h[k] - v_l[k-1]| <= 0.99 * 4.0 * 0.2` else `v_l[k-1] +- 0.99*4.0*0.2`; once equal it follows the human unconditionally (so a zero speed offset reproduces the human profile exactly). Along-track progress `a[k] = s_h[k] + delta_s[k]`, `delta_s[k] = sum_i 0.5((v_l[i-1]+v_l[i]) - (v_h[i-1]+v_h[i])) * 0.2` (human polyline arc plus the label's lag). Path = a cubic Hermite blend from the own pose (position q, tangent = own heading) to the human-polyline point at arc `a[k_rej]` (tangent = the polyline tangent there), tangent magnitudes = 1.0 x chord, traversed with arc fraction `a[k]/a[k_rej]` for `k <= k_rej`, then the human polyline at arc `a[k]` for `k > k_rej`. **Rejoin ladder k_rej in {5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30}; the first rung that passes the independent feasibility checker is emitted; if none passes the record is `refused_infeasible`.** No constant is tuned after seeing data.
* **L4 feasibility checker (independent of the labeller).** On [virtual predecessor (-v_own*0.2, 0); own origin; label waypoints 1..k_rej+1] in the own frame, `feasible_decode.recover_controls` convention at dt = 0.2 s (speed = chord / dt, heading = atan2, accel, yaw rate, `v_mid = max(speed, 0.5)`, `kappa = yaw_rate / v_mid`, `lat_acc = v_mid * yaw_rate`). Violation = `|accel| > 0.99*4.0` or `|kappa| > 0.99*0.2` or `sqrt(a^2 + lat^2) > 0.99*0.7*g`. Only the recovery phase (up to the rejoin) is asserted: beyond it the label IS the human path and its own controls are the human's (their violation rate at the 0.2 s resolution is reported beside as a baseline). **Reading of "(d) must be exactly 0": zero violations among EMITTED labels, with refusals counted and reported separately (a refusal is not a violation).**
* **L5 identity (a).** `recovery_label` on a state with own pose == logged pose at r' (bit-equal float64 x, y, yaw) and `v_own == v_h[0]` (bit-equal) must equal `human_label` (the `taniteval.floors_kin.gt_waypoints` expression, rotated by -yaw) with `np.array_equal` (zero tolerance), on >= 1,000 real windows. A near-zero offset (1e-6 m) must stay within 1e-4 m of the human label (continuity, so the identity branch is not a trapdoor).
* **L6 deliberate-regression arm (b).** `label_ignore_offset` returns the human path regardless of the own state. The gate G-REC = (identity L5 passes) AND (zero feasibility violations by L4 on every emitted label of a panel of >= 200 real off_lat / off_head records). The regression arm must FAIL G-REC (violations > 0 on the panel); the real labeller must PASS; a no-clip labeller (speed rate limit and ladder checker removed) must also be caught (violations > 0).
* **L7 kept fraction (c).** `kept(tick) = (ok + emitted recoveries) / 306,566`. The card's "~78 %" = (165,093 + 37,652 + 36,716) / 306,566 = 78.1 % is the no-refusal ceiling. **Frozen reading: prediction CONFIRMED iff tick-2 kept in [75 %, 81 %]** (+-3 points around 78); otherwise reported as below / above with the refusal breakdown. This band is my reading of "~78 %", fixed here.
* **L8 deviation (e).** Per emitted record: mean and max Euclid distance between the recovery label and the human label waypoints (world frame, k = 1..30) and the 6 s endpoint distance; quantiles p5/p25/p50/p75/p95/p99/max per reason and overall.

### H-DD-FITGAP (M2b s0 and s1 only)
* **F1 statistic.** `C = mean over GT-launch windows of w(window)`, `w = mean over ticks 1 and 2 of (v_own(tick) - v_GT(tick))` (the M-LAUNCH reading R1 of `launch_intent_mlaunch/NOTES.md`: 0-based ticks 1 and 2; per-window value = mean of the two ticks). `v_own(tick)` = `p10f[-1, 0]` of that tick's record. `v_GT(tick n)` (j = t0 + 10 n) = `|grid[j+1] - grid[j-1]| / 0.4` on the logged 0.2 s pose grid (the closed form that control d of the M-LAUNCH instrument proves equal to its `state_from_knots` rule, max |d| 0.0).
* **F2 TRAIN launch windows.** Window (ci, t0) of the bank with the LOGGED `v0 = kin3_log[t0, 0] < 6.0` and `|grid[t0+30] - grid[t0+29]| / 0.2 >= v0 + 1.0` and a complete 30-step future (`t0 + 30 <= T - 1`, finite) (`v8f_mini.py:396-406`, the train-side twin of `hmc_read.py:386-388`). The statistic uses ALL records of those windows regardless of their cut reason (the cut concerns label validity, not the state's speed); ok-only is reported beside.
* **F3 deficit and ratio.** `D = -C` (m/s, positive = slower than the human). `r = D_train / D_eval`. If `D_eval <= 0` r is undefined and is reported as such (no eval crawl to explain). CI: TRAIN side clip-cluster bootstrap and eval side episode-cluster bootstrap, independent resampling (disjoint clip sets), B = 2000 each, paired by draw index; 95 % percentile of r.
* **F4 eval reference.** C_eval for M2b only if a banked T1-kin dump of M2b on eval-141 exists (search `v8f-dagger-t1/`, the register, `dag*` dirs); otherwise FITGAP is reported with C_eval = NOT AVAILABLE and r undefined (no rollout is re-run).
* **F5 verdict is PARTIAL.** Per-checkpoint class: FIT-LIMITED if r >= 0.5; GEN-GAP if r <= 0.25; MIXED if 0.25 < r < 0.5 (the card's thresholds). The card's aggregate (>= 5 of 6) is NOT evaluable with 2 of 6; the report states what the 2 observed classes leave reachable (FIT-LIMITED needs >= 3 of the other 4 to qualify at the same class; GEN-GAP is unreachable if either observed r > 0.25) and what the other 4 checkpoints need.
