## 4. Things a reviewer should know
1. K-SHARE vs K-MASK. The brief names K-SHARE as the run-record share check (launch weight share within the registered band). The same `kshare` command of the checks module also asserts K-MASK (`launch_rows == launch_eligible`, `launch_n / launch_eligible` within f +- 0.02); a failure of either is a `ZZLAUNCHINTENT-CONTROL-FAIL` stop for that run. K-MASK is part of the build, not something the brief asked for: say so if you want it removed.
2. REP's `levers.launch_intent_w` is ABSENT (its trainer pre-dates the lever), not 0.0. The REP licence accepts absent or 0.0 (`rep_licence`, condition 1); the A runs REFUSE an absent key. The control comparison for A_rep,s is therefore on ARGV (REP_s's `argv.json` equals DAG-OACC_s's apart from `--seed`/`--out`, asserted when the chain is rendered), not on a summary lever block.
