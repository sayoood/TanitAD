## Limits, stated plainly

* **One interval, one question.** The intervals answer another draw of episodes. The two seeds differ by about as much as the intervals are wide (DAG-OACC L2 T-share +0.771 against +0.660; yaw-rate -0.424 against +0.205), so a verdict that flips on one seed is a statement about this rig's run-to-run spread, not about a lever.
* **Class O is 8 windows / 3 episodes**; its Delta_c alone is not interpretable and it only enters D2' (inside O + M). Classes S and O hold **no** valid window for L2 and M1 (no GT-fast pair), so for those two rows the trigger set is L alone.
* **Yaw-rate shares are unstable** (heavy-tailed MAE; DAG-OACC s0 Delta has lower bound +0.66 against a point +3.47). The verdict follows the card as written; the point share there is negative because the GT-launch windows are better than P10 on that row (Delta_L = -10.6) while the moving windows are worse (+6.05).
* **Not an intervention.** The split says where the measured gap sits; it does not say what a launch fix would score.
