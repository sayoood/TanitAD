## 2. TD3, FLAT, goals, references
* **Time grid.** Levels step 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 s (strides 2, 2). NOW = frame 0 of the 16-frame window (IMAG1B / WMACT / LATSEM2 convention); the goal frame is frame 12 (2.4 s). Plan = the 12 transitions 0 to 11; logged actions = `A_raw[:, 0:12]`.
* **TD3**: `plan_topdown(goal_level 3, top_horizon 3)`, `subgoal_K = {2: 3, 1: 6}`: level 3 plans 3 steps (2.4 s); level 2 plans H_2 = 3 x 2 = 6 steps against the 3 level-3 states; level 1 plans H_1 = 6 x 2 = 12 steps against the 6 level-2 states (so level 1 plans all 12 primitive actions).
* **FLAT**: the same function with `goal_level 1, top_horizon 12`, `lam` irrelevant, same S, iterations, lr as TD3's level 1. **Beside, no bar:** FLAT1 = FLAT with `last_k = 1` (cost on the last state only), because the planted control (section 6) shows the production last-2 cost is not identifying on a stiff linear system. FLAT1 decides nothing.
* **Goals**: G-TRUE = the pointwise encoder map (`initial_latents`, w = 1) of the level-1 latent of frame 12, in the planner's goal level (level 3 for TD3, level 1 for FLAT). G-RAND = the same quantity of window `donor[i]`, `donor_indices(n, episode_ids, seed 0)` (a derangement over episodes: every donor is from a different episode). G-STAY = the pointwise map of frame 0's latent.
* **HOLD** = `A_raw[:, 0]` held 12 steps (a0 and the measured yaw rate0 = v0 kappa0; the loader's channel 1 in `kin_mode yaw`). Disclosed: a0 is the first logged action, so HOLD scores step 1 exactly; the card names this reference. **PRIOR** = the level-1 prior mean (scaled), converted to raw, held.
* **Metrics** (RAW units): acceleration MAE [m/s^2] and yaw-rate MAE [rad/s] against the 12 logged actions, mean over steps then over windows. Beside: unicycle integration (dt 0.2, v' = max(v + a dt, 0), psi' += w dt, v0 = `native_tracks` column 3 at raw row 2g, g = t - 15, position by the speed at the step start) of the plan and of the logged actions, then speed error at 2.4 s, heading error at 2.4 s (wrapped), ADE over the 12 steps. Cost monotonicity (hjepa `monotonicity`, Tab. 10 statistic = negative Spearman of the goal distance against time) of each level's chosen rollout lifted into the goal level (`HJEPAStack.lift`, index 0 = the lifted start); fraction of windows where the best cost is below the best initial candidate's cost, per level.
* **TRAIN action std** (read before this file, TRAIN windows with all 16 frames real, 1,833 windows): acceleration 0.860 m/s^2, yaw rate 0.0953 rad/s.

## 4. Statistics
Paired episode-cluster bootstrap over the 141 episodes, B 2,000, seed 0, the rng calls of `wmact_lib.boot_weights`; statistic = ratio of per-episode sums (mean over windows) of the per-window difference. Estimator named on every row. **Separated-better** (MAE): the 95 % percentile interval of (A minus B) lies wholly below 0. This is interval-only (the card names no reference draw). It answers "would another draw of episodes say this?" and is blind to training-seed and planner-seed variance (`H-ESTIM-SEED-1`): both training seeds are required, and the planner is evaluated at ONE inference seed (0). A planner-seed replicate (seed 1, TD3 and FLAT under G-TRUE) is run beside if the timing allows.
Family labels per (planner, family, seed): PLANNABLE, GOAL-BLIND, NO-GAIN as the card; **WORSE-THAN-RAND** is added here for (G-TRUE minus G-RAND) separated WORSE (a case outside the card's list; it satisfies no committed outcome and triggers nothing). A planner-family label needs both seeds to agree, else `SEEDS-DISAGREE`. HIER-ADDS: TD3 minus FLAT under G-TRUE separated-better at both seeds, per family.
Family statement: LONGITUDINAL (acceleration), LATERAL (yaw rate) as above; TACTICAL not computed (n = 0, no decision output); STRATEGIC PAUSED (PI item 25).

## 6. Controls (a failure makes the read INVALID)
1. **Planted dynamics** (`test_plantest_controls.py`): a 3-level `HJEPAStack` (D 32) with identity encoders and a known linear system z' = A z + B a (A = 0.95 exp(3.0 S), S a random skew matrix of spectral norm 1; level 2 acts on two concatenated level-1 actions, level 3 on two of those, so all levels carry the same physics and the 24 level-1 numbers are identifiable from the 2.4 s state). Planted actions N(0, 0.5^2) clipped at +-1.5 sigma; the priors are fitted on 600 other planted sequences through `apply_prior`; the plan is made by the same `run_planner`. **Tolerance (fixed now): TD3 MAE <= 0.05 and FLAT1 MAE <= 0.05 (10 % of the planted std), and each <= 0.2 x the prior-mean baseline MAE, over 64 planted windows.** FLAT with the production last-2 cost is run and printed beside (it does not recover on this stiff system: both last states are asked to equal the goal, and consecutive states differ as much as the state; disclosed, a property of the cost). **Deliberate-regression arm**: the predictors ignore the action (output x 0.0 to keep the graph); TD3 and FLAT1 must then read MAE > 0.05. Dev reading (synthetic only, before this file): TD3 0.0035, FLAT1 0.0077, baseline 0.37 at lr1 0.1.
2. **Goal plumbing**: `pipeline(goal='true')` and `pipeline(goal='rand')` equal the direct `run_planner` calls on G-TRUE / G-RAND exactly (same seed), so swapping the goal arrays swaps the outputs; mutation: a pipeline that ignores the goal name goes red.
3. **Bit-repeat**: the same seed gives bit-identical plans (and costs) on a 16-window subset, twice; mutation: a different seed must differ.
4. **No-leak**: the logged actions of the eval windows are replaced by random values and the planner outputs must be bit-identical (`A_raw` never enters `run_planner` / `plan_all`); mutation: a planner that seeds `init_actions` from the logged actions must differ. File:line statement in the result.
Beside: two untrained stacks (seeds 5000 + r, default init) of the same architecture on M2b_s0's level-1 latents, 256 windows (the adapter and projection are not re-run; the levels, predictors, encoders and macro encoders are untrained), through the same pipeline.
