## 2. Rows, stages, controls
* **Rows read:** per tag (`M2b_s0`, `M2b_s1`), level 1..3, k = 1..3: Oracle, Predicted and Copy-Last scores of `lead_gap` (deciding); `lead_closing` and `ttc` beside, no bar (label printed marked beside). IMAG1B's offsets (decorrelation), IA-3, IA-6 and the kinematic and inverse-probe beside rows are not part of this read.
* **IA-0 health** (the `read` flag of IMAG1B's labels): retrieval of the encoded true future k = 1 among the windows of the same clip, query the predicted latent, derangement reference (199), IMAG1B's `mrr_mom` unchanged, on the new windows. Printed per level; a label on an unhealthy level is marked.
* **Not-future** (IMAG1B control 2): window i takes the encoded true-future latent of a window j of a different episode (`default_rng(0)`, windows in order, `j = rng.choice(flatnonzero(qe != qe[i]))`). A cell is (tag, level, k, target) whose label is not UNREADABLE; **control 2 passes iff there is at least one cell and every cell is separated** (Oracle minus not-future, bootstrap bound > 0 AND point > 97.5th of the reference of that difference), at both seeds. Because `lead_gap` may have no cell, **`speed` is scored as a control-only target** (not reported as a result): the machinery is then tested on a target known to be encoded. If `lead_gap` has cells they are listed too.
* **Calibration (control 3):** the 20 draws are scored as if real against the references; rows counted per kind: Oracle rows, `pred - copy` rows (both sides, split printed), for the three read targets (and `speed` printed). **At most 5 % separated per kind**, for `lead_gap` alone and for the three targets pooled.
* **Control 4 (tests, run before the read, `test_imag1c_controls.py`, synthetic arrays through the same functions):** (a) a planted channel in the bank latents with Oracle = latent of the true future gives READABLE; with Predicted = future + noise gives IMAGINES; (b) Predicted = Copy gives COPIES; (c) the same world with the bank labels deranged across episodes (fit labels do not belong to the latents) gives no READABLE Oracle; (d) pure Gaussian latents give no READABLE; (e) primal ridge = `capladder_lib.Split` dual ridge at fixed and at selected lambda; (f) deliberate-regression arm: not-future := the Oracle's own latent makes control 2 FAIL; (g) calibration share on the planted world with deranged labels <= 5 % (R = 39, 6 draws for the smoke). Also a reproduction check on the real arrays (section 3).
* **Banner:** VALID iff control 3, control 2 and the tests pass and the reproduction checks hold. INVALID writes only the control rows and names the cheapest next lever.
