**7.46.8 Theory Amendment 4, T6: render-domain information loss, and what P6 and the GAP48 log did to it [H, then M].** `raw/2026-11-07-t1-blind/cards/THEORY_CRAWL_AMEND_4.md` (sha256 `016c2950…`, written 04:53 dev-box clock before any T1-kin BLIND rollout), its log `raw/2026-11-07-t2-ralign-gap48/cards/THEORY_CRAWL_AMEND_4_LOG.md`.
* **Definitions.** Let $x = E(I)$ be the scene tokens ($40 \times 1024$) of a real frame $I$ at scene $s$ and pose $p$, and $x_r = E(R(s, p))$ those of a NuRec render of the same scene and pose. The planner is $\pi(x, h)$, with $h$ the measured ego history, and $\bar x$ is the BLIND mean (the eval-141 mean tokens, sha256 `cd6a7ab5…`). For a population $\mathcal{D}$ and a driving row $d$ (ADE6, or the M1 crawl indicator) define the **token dependence**
$$
\Delta_d(\mathcal{D}) \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{(x,\,h) \sim \mathcal{D}}\Big[\, d\big(\pi(x, h),\ \pi(\bar x, h)\big) \Big].
$$
**T6** [H]: $\Delta(\mathrm{real}) > 0$ and $\Delta(\mathrm{render}) \approx 0$: on real frames the plan depends on what the camera sees, and renders land where $\pi$'s response to $x$ is flat. **The rival T6′** [H]: $\Delta(\mathrm{real}) \approx 0$ as well, so the T2 crawl is a function of $h$ and the prior and BLIND ≈ C0 at T2 is not a render effect.
* **The data-processing consequence.** At T2 the chain is $S \to R(S, P) \to x_r \to \pi \to \hat Y$, so $I(S; \hat Y) \le I(S; x_r) \le I(S; R(S, P))$: a lever downstream of $x_r$ cannot raise what the render carries. In the limit $\Delta(\mathrm{render}) = 0$ for every row, $\pi(x_r, h) = \pi(\bar x, h)$, $\hat Y$ is a function of $h$ alone and $I(\hat Y; S \mid h) = 0$: **no scene information reaches the plan at T2, whatever the encoder or head**. Whether a perception lever trained on real frames (the LoRA arm L) moves $\Delta(\mathrm{render})$ is not determined by its effect on real frames; so under T6 a T2 dominance read of L can fail for a reason that says nothing about L's driving on real frames **[H]**.
* **P6 = the T1-kin BLIND read (frozen `9619414a…`), VISION-USED [M, T1-kin].** Empirical $\Delta(\mathrm{real})$ is $B = \mathrm{BLIND1} - \mathrm{RIC0}$ on real eval-141 frames (§7.45.6). The committed outcome, quoted: *At both seeds, ADE6 B is separated-worse with a point above F, **or** M1 B is separated in either direction.* ADE6 B is **+0.8313 [+0.5576, +1.1530]** and **+0.8158 [+0.5309, +1.1454]** m against F = 0.12074 m; M1 B is −0.0246 [−0.0428, −0.0085] and −0.0415 [−0.0654, −0.0208] (without its tokens the planner crawls LESS); gates G-ID, G-MEAN and G-EGO pass (`raw/2026-11-07-t1-blind/RESULT_T1BLIND.md`). Per the amendment: **VISION-USED, so T6 survives P6 and T6′ falls. That is a survival and not a proof**, because T2's $\Delta(\mathrm{render}) \approx 0$ rests on the stage-2 BLIND arm on 35 scenes: BLIND − C0 on M1 is +0.011 [−0.070, +0.092] and −0.076 [−0.160, +0.014] **[M, quoted from the amendment's §2; the stage-2 raw files are the sources of §7.42 and were not re-read for this section]**. Its width matters: those T2 intervals contain M1 effects of the size P6 finds on real frames (−0.0246 and −0.0415), and the unpaired T2 ADE6 means point the other way (BLIND 7.729 against C0_s0 7.980 on launch starts, 6.794 against 7.336 on moving starts), so the T2 null is a non-detection at that width and not an equality **[E]**.
* **The log, section 5(a): does not hold [M, descriptive].** The amendment predicted that on the 48 read scenes render tokens sit at about the same cosine from the BLIND mean as from their own real frame. GAP48 reads render-to-real 0.8401 [0.8307, 0.8503] and render-to-mean 0.7098, 0.130 apart **[E: 0.8401 − 0.7098]**, and the render lies FARTHER from $\bar x$ than its real frame does (0.7098 against 0.7427; paired −0.0331 [−0.0388, −0.0267]; closer in 4.4 % of pairs, 0 of 48 scenes; §7.45.7). **T6's mechanism sentence ("renders land where π's response is flat, like x̄") loses its descriptive support.** What remains of T6 is the behavioural claim, $\Delta(\mathrm{render}) \approx 0$ on M1: renders are off the real distribution in a direction of their own, not toward the mean.
* **The log, section 5(b): ALIGN-FAILS-MECH [M, T2-RA].** An inference-time map from render tokens toward real-token statistics should raise $\Delta(\mathrm{render})$. The per-channel affine map lifts the held-out cosine from 0.8401 to 0.8937 (bar 0.90; the same-fold arm reaches 0.9015). The failure is of the map's mechanism gate: the behavioural prediction of 5(b) was therefore not tested, because no T2 stage ran. Read with 5(a): the render gap is not a per-channel shift or scale of the tokens.

**7.46.9 The synthesis, as an argument, and the committed next lever.**
1. **Every admissible downstream lever lies on one chain [M, P3; post hoc, n = 5].** The five admissible C0 levers (LF050, LF075, A6 LFB05, A6 LFB10, A5 LF100) form one monotone chain, Spearman 1.0 between closure and ADE6 cost at both seeds; T2's theorem says no function of the features can leave the Bayes frontier, and the only arms that left the chain used the logged speed.

The edits are functions of the same features, which is what T2 predicts, and the data-matched edit closing more of M1 than the dose-1.0 edit while adding about the same number of new rows (nine at each seed against ten and nine) is the frontier's shape, not a lever.
3. **The corner is unreachable by this lever operator [M, T1-kin, CEILING-TYPE].** AUCMAP outcome (iii): no gate on the floor and forced-level levers reaches CORNER-FULL, even with a perfect go/stay posterior; the guard rows come from the operator. The argument ranks the levers, it does not prove the top one works.
* **The committed next lever: `PREREG_T2_RENC.md` (frozen prefix 5,544 bytes, sha256 `425d3496…`; first freeze `15be11b5…`, re-taken once, wording only, before any data). PRE-REGISTERED, NOT RUN: no training image has been rendered and no adapted encoder exists.** `raw/2026-11-07-t2-renc-prereg/`.

$$
\mathcal{L} \;=\; \big\lVert P(E'(\mathrm{render})) - P(E(\mathrm{real})) \big\rVert^{2} \;+\; \lambda_{\mathrm{keep}}\, \big\lVert P(E'(\mathrm{real})) - P(E(\mathrm{real})) \big\rVert^{2}, \qquad \lambda_{\mathrm{keep}} = 1,
$$
with $P$ the encoder's fixed 4 × 10 pool. AdamW, learning rate 1e-4, weight decay 0, batch 8, bf16 with fp32 master weights, exactly 10 epochs, seed 0, no early stopping and no selection on any held-out number. Real frames enter only as targets, from the training fold.

Read by the pinned dominance read (pin 4, `--arm C0C-RE --arm-stage 5`) and by the pair read C0C-RE − C0C, paired scene-cluster bootstrap B = 2,000, seed 0, four families, STRATEGIC PAUSED.
