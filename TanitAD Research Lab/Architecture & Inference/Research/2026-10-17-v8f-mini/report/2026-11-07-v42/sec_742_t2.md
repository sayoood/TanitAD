### 7.42 T2 re-perception on NuRec scenes (2026-11-06): the crawl persists under a re-rendered camera, and the composite C0C is NOT-DOMINANT at both seeds

This section reports the first T2 numbers of the programme. **T2 here means re-perception**: the camera is rendered at the pose and time the car actually reaches, so the model sees its own consequences, but the other agents replay their logged tracks and do not react to the ego. A T2 result is therefore never a reactive-traffic result, never pooled with T1-kin, and never a claim about PhysicalAI-AV at large (§7.42.1, §7.42.3). Every number is **[M]**, tier **T2** unless a row says T1-kin, from the raw folders listed in §7.42.7. The estimator is the paired scene-cluster bootstrap (B = 2,000, seed 0), which answers *another draw of SCENES only*; the training-seed question is answered by requiring both seeds, and no replicate arm exists on this rig (`H-ESTIM-SEED-1`). The four metric families are reported wherever a read carries them; STRATEGIC is **PAUSED** by PI ruling ITEM 25 (2026-09-19) and its rows are shown, never counted as a pass.

**7.42.1 The tier and the instrument (`PREREG_T2_CRAWL`, frozen `2c4c6557…`, 2026-11-06 13:05, before the first real render of any v8F planner) [M].**
* **T2 (deciding):** the camera is rendered from a NuRec reconstruction (gsplat) at the executed pose and time; the reconstruction's agents are replayed at log time (condition `objects`); the executor is PLAYBACK, so the car executes its plan's knots exactly, as T1-kin assumes. Each start takes the logged 16-row history at 10 Hz, then three replans of 10 executed knots (2 s each, 6 s in all), each plan scored over its 30 knots at 0.2 s: the T1-kin geometry.
* **RR, render-replay (deciding, for attribution):** the camera rendered at the LOGGED pose and re-indexed by the car's own along-track progress, that is, `reindexed` inside the same renderer. It removes the render domain gap from the contrast T2 − RR. `empty` (no agents) is beside and never deciding.
* **Arms.** C0_s0 and C0_s1 (the floor reads' planner, `v8f-recover/M2b_s{s}_recft_C0`); DAG-OACC_s0 and _s1 (beside); P10 (the frozen prior from the same forward); ZERO (a wrapper returning the prior, which must equal P10 exactly); BLIND (C0 with the scene tokens replaced by their banked training mean, an instrument, never deciding). Inputs are as in T1-kin: measured ego history including yaw rate from the executed track, nav from the log route and the causal speed bucket.
* **Gates before any deciding rollout, each with a deliberate-regression arm that must go RED:** T0 (policy reproduces the T1-kin tick-0 plan), T1 (encoder fidelity), T2g (playback identity to 1e-6 m), T3 (ZERO equals P10 exactly), T4 (the scorer reproduces the T1 reader's M1 share and four family rows bit for bit), T5 (determinism), and the actor falsifier (the replayed lead must be drawn in the rendered frame, and a planted no-lead scene must read absent).
* **The render gap, beside and never deciding.** Pooled DINOv3 tokens of the frame rendered at the logged pose against the real camera frame (stage 1, 2 scenes, 9 ticks each): cosine 0.833 and 0.755, relative L2 0.567 and 0.683, against the encoder's own floor on a logged frame of cosine 0.99965 and relative L2 0.0264. The gap is about 20 times the floor in relative L2, which is why the attribution contrast X2 = C0(T2) − C0(RR) is measured inside one renderer.
* **The M1 crawl share** is computed by the T1 reader's own function per rollout window: logged route speed of at least 5.2 m/s at the car's route position, own v0 below 5.2 m/s (gate T4 pins it).

**7.42.3 Rule R-SCN: the renderer refuses 14 of the 62 scenes before any planner runs (`AMENDMENT_T2_SCENE_REFUSAL.md`, frozen `2167a7eb…` on 2026-11-06 19:27, before any stage-2 row of run 2 was computed) [M].**
* **What was seen first.** Stage 2's second run finished with 98 failed cells, which were every cell of 7 launch scenes and no cell of any other scene. The cell log shows each failure at `attach_actors_verified`, before the first planner forward: 60 lines of *actor placement REFUSED by its own falsifier* and 10 tracebacks ending in a missing `dynamic_rigids` layer. Neither card said what happens to such a scene, and a relaunch reproduces the refusal, so neither read could ever be satisfied as written.
* **The rule.** A scene leaves a stage's deciding set if and only if all of its cells in that stage failed with 0 records, all arms, both conditions, and each ended on one of two named refusals raised before any planner forward: ACTOR_REFUSED (fewer than 2 of 3 falsifier frames pass) or NO_DYNAMIC_LAYER (the reconstruction carries no `dynamic_rigids` time ranges). Attribution is mechanical code (`scene_exclusions.py`, sha256 `83a5f647…`): failed cells are matched one to one with the log's terminal events; every excluded scene carries exactly one reason; an ACTOR_REFUSED scene's track count equals its own `sequence_tracks.json`. Any failure that is not one of the two reasons leaves the read NOT-READ. A scene excluded in any stage is out of every pairing, and the excluded scenes' starts are never re-drawn and never replaced.
* **Why it cannot select on the outcome.** Both refusals are computed from the reconstruction and the logged frames before the first planner forward, and P10, ZERO, BLIND and C0 are refused identically. The rule names its two reasons in advance and is fixed before any row is computed.
* **The count.** 14 of 62 scenes are excluded in the union of stages 2, 3 and 4 (7 launch and 7 guard; 12 ACTOR_REFUSED and 2 NO_DYNAMIC_LAYER). The read covers **48 scenes and 93 of 121 starts** (launch 67 of 81, guard 26 of 40); 28 starts are lost (launch 14, guard 14). Stage 4's `EXCLUSIONS4.json` names exactly the 14 scenes that stages 2 and 3 refused, so stage 4 adds no refusal. The result file states the cost: *a result on the refusal-surviving scenes, never on the 62*; and the amendment adds that a scene whose replayed agents the renderer cannot place is, on that evidence, outside what T2 `objects` can test.

**7.42.6 What T2 establishes, what it cannot, and the committed next (Rule Zero) [M, with the next stated as the card commits it].**
1. **The crawl is not a camera artefact.** X2 is null at both seeds, so re-rendering the camera at the executed pose does not move it; BLIND is null, so the frozen tokens do not carry the cue. This closes the question T1-kin could not ask.
2. **The composite does not dominate P10 at T2.** D1 fails by a wide margin (+0.79 / +0.64 m against a bar of −0.2415 m) and D2 fails on ADE6, the crawl, along-track and TAC-LON, so the failure is longitudinal, not lateral. The lateral rows hold up, which excludes the committed alternative for "D2 on LATERAL rows only" (the `hold` executor card).
3. **Committed next (the card's section 5, outcome 2).** D2 fails on M1 and on LONGITUDINAL rows, so the next card is the crawl card's lever for CRAWL-PERSISTS (a): the perception lever (`PREREG_V8F_LORA`) with T2 as its evaluation and this read as its case. It was drafted in the same turn: `DRAFT_PREREG_T2_DOM_LORA.md` (a draft, not a frozen card). Its read script has since been re-pinned to take the arm name (`--arm`): the fourth pin, sha256 `7fd65ffd…`, reproduces the read of record on the real pulls (decision, gates, both seeds' 19 rows, the scene refusals and the markdown equal; outcome NOT-DOMINANT, 48 scenes, 93 starts), and 80 of 80 mutations of it read RED; the full-suite run and the entry in the card's log were still in flight (E: only, `E:/Projects/TanitAD-artifacts/dagland/motion_c/t2v8f/HANDOFF_Q.md`, **not yet landed**). 4. **What this read cannot say.** It does not say the car is safe or unsafe (agents replay and do not react); it does not generalise past the 48 surviving scenes; it has two training seeds and no replicate arm; and the T2 rollouts carry 3 ticks per start, so any iterated quantity is read at depth at most 2 (§7.44.2).
