**7.43.4 The token identity: the device alone misses the bar by 21 to 23 times, so the base is B′ [M] (`identity_pod/IDENTITY_POD.json`, `IDENTITY_POD.md`).**
* **Decision rule, fixed before the measurement:** the banked base B is kept iff the relative L2 between the pod's pooled fp8 tokens and box 2's stored `cache_full` is at most τ_device = 1e-3 on all three clips; otherwise the card's committed fallback (Amendment 1 item 3, kept by Amendment 2) runs.
* **Three comparisons, relative L2 per clip (`8f387977`, `bf5ec76e`, `dca157a0`):**

| comparison | what varies | clip 1 | clip 2 | clip 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a vs c | the device alone (byte-identical banked frames) | 2.11e-2 | 2.25e-2 | 2.14e-2 |
| b vs c | the pod frames on this GPU (the route that will be used) | 2.42e-2 | 2.63e-2 | 2.25e-2 |
| a vs b | the frame route alone, same GPU | 2.42e-2 | 2.62e-2 | 2.27e-2 |

* **Reading.** The device alone (a vs c) already reads 2.11 to 2.25e-2, so byte-identical frames would not rescue B on this GPU; the frame route adds a little (b vs c 2.25 to 2.63e-2) and the whole gap is about 24 times the bar of 1e-3. The maximum absolute difference is 0.25 (one fp8 e4m3 step) in every case, and 74 to 80 % of fp8 values are bitwise equal but 0 % of whole tokens. The mechanism is the fp8 token grid: any last-ULP bf16 change turns into about 2e-2 (same-device batch 16 against 8 also reads 2.2e-2). Controls: a repeated encode is bit-equal; the arm's module at B = 0 equals the builder's `encode_episode` bitwise; the driver sha256 equals the banked AR-2 run 3 driver.
* **⇒ The B′ route (committed).** The train and eval caches are re-encoded on this GPU from the pod frames (bf16 weights, batch 8, `--amp` off, `pool4x10`, fp8 e4m3fn); the base B′ is M2b retrained at seeds 0 and 1 on that `cache_pod` with the banked argv and only the cache roots changed (19,164 steps, 4,512 trained clips with the RA-1 holdout withheld, the same heads' initialisation seed and eval-141 grid); F′ is computed from B′_s0 and B′_s1 by F's definition, and the PRIMARY bar becomes |δ_s| > 2F′ with δ_s = ADE6(L_s) − ADE6(B′_s). The banked B and F stay reported beside, deciding nothing. Base and arm are on one device by construction, so the arm stays one lever.
