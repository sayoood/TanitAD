**7.47.1 The model, the statistics and the controls [M, base report §4.7; card `PREREG_V8F_WMACT.md`, frozen prefix 5,478 bytes `d21b14cc…`].** `WMAux` projects the mean-pooled adapter token of each cached frame to a 128-d level-1 latent $z^1$ and builds levels 2 and 3 at strides (2, 2), so the three levels step at 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 s. Level 1 is conditioned on (acceleration, yaw rate) through adaptive LayerNorm, the loss carries an inverse-dynamics term ($\gamma = 100$) and SIGReg ($\beta = 0.08$), and there is no inference path into the planner. With $z_0$ the start latent, $z_k$ the true latent $k$ steps ahead, $\hat z_k(a)$ the rollout under the logged actions $a$ and $a'$ the actions of another episode (or the mean action):

$$
\mathrm{SKILL}_\ell(k)=1-\frac{\sum\lVert \hat z_k(a)-z_k\rVert^2}{\sum\lVert z_0-z_k\rVert^2},\qquad
\mathrm{USE}_\ell(k)=\frac{\sum\lVert \hat z_k(a')-z_k\rVert^2-\sum\lVert \hat z_k(a)-z_k\rVert^2}{\sum\lVert z_0-z_k\rVert^2}.
$$

SKILL above 0 means the rollout beats "nothing changes". USE above 0 means the true action predicts the real future better than another episode's action (ACTION-USE) or than the constant mean action (ACTION-USE-MEAN). Committed outcomes: PREDICTS(l) is SKILL(l, 1) > 0 separated at both seeds; ACTION-REACTIVE(l) is both USE rows > 0 separated at both seeds. Data: eval-141, the 1,976 of 2,399 windows whose 16 frames are all real, 141 episodes (`raw/2026-11-07-v8f-wmact-result/RESULT_WMACT.json`). **Four binding controls all read their known values [M]:** the identity donor reads exactly 0.0 (ACTION-USE and sensitivity); the zeroed level-1 action embedding reads exactly 0.0 for ACTION-USE at k = 1, 5, 10; a rollout started from another window's start loses at every row (SKILL −39.2162 [−42.8020, −36.1577] at level 1, k = 1, s0, and −40.5909 at s1; below 0 and separated at all 7 rows at both seeds); the model's own WM loss recomputed through `HJEPAStack.loss` equals `WMAux.forward` with a maximum absolute difference of 0.0.

Not established: any driving effect of the world model, since no planner reads it and the reliance arm is not run; any claim that the yaw channel is understood by any level; any W1 number (MRATE is not run); that any level imagines the scene or answers the action through the scene (S3-PARTIAL has the wrong sign everywhere); any IMAG1 or VOE1 result (pre-registered, none on the tip); the cause of level 1's overshoot (UNVERIFIED). The reads share one estimator that answers another draw of episodes; the two seeds agree in sign and significance on every deciding row except where stated, and there is no replicate arm, so a separated interval is necessary and not sufficient. The mean action of ACTION-USE-MEAN is the mean over eval-141's real frames (the TRAIN cache was on a busy box), one constant for every window. Rollouts use context 1 (the planning convention) while training is teacher-forced with a longer context, so the numbers describe this read-out. The TRAIN read covers 141 of 4,512 clips (3.1 %).
