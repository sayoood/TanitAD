### 7.38 Option (c), motion from vision (2026-11-05): the base is clock-insensitive, the cached tokens carry no usable motion, half the crawl sits behind a lead, and the pixel form stops on two named blockers

* **Status of this section (v39) [M].** Three zero-training measurements and the design rule that scopes them (`R/raw/2026-11-05-motion-c-ri/`, `-motion-c-probe/`, `-motion-c-crawllead/`; landing commits `{{LAND_RI}}`, `{{LAND_MP}}`, `{{LAND_CLD}}`). No (c) arm was trained. Tiers: RI-C0 is T1-kin; MOTIONPROBE is a T0-class diagnostic of cached features (no driving model, so the four-family table is not engaged); CRAWL-LEAD reads T1-kin bank states of the v8F model on 4,512 TRAIN clips (a statistic about where crawl states sit, no capability claim, eval-141 not involved). The estimators differ by instrument and are named in each bullet.

**7.38.1 The design rule, and what `reindexed` is (MM design note before any (c) data).**

* **The leak the decision item did not state [M].** T1-kin integrates the model's own actions but feeds the LOGGED camera frames on the human's clock (the frame-freeze HANDOFF discloses that the current frame still depicts the human's position at tick j). The frozen DINOv3-L features carry no motion the trunk uses (§7.24, §7.31, §7.33), so no arm so far could exploit this. An arm that can read motion from vision would read the human's speed off the logged frames; in the M1 crawl that is the logged-speed oracle HLVL (§7.35, closing 0.599 to 0.633) arriving through the camera, a leak of the evaluation target and not a capability. On a real car the camera shows the car's own motion, which the measured ego history already carries (and the history at the executed speed, HCV, made M1 worse, §7.35). The decision item's sentence that (c) supplies the speed level from vision describes this leak under T1-kin on logged frames; what (c) can legitimately add is scene dynamics the ego history does not carry: other agents' motion (a lead vehicle pulling away or closing, crossing traffic moving or stopped) and the static scene's rate of approach consistent with the own progress.
* **`reindexed` (RI), defined [M].** `--scene reindexed` re-indexes the frames to the model's OWN along-track progress on the logged path. For a row at time i after t0, the frame shown is the logged frame at which the human was at the model's own along-track position, never a frame from after time i (tau(i) = min(i, tau*), where tau* is that logged row), non-decreasing in i; rows of the measured past stay logged. It is a longitudinal-only approximate re-perception: static geometry is consistent with the own position, dynamic agents are shown at the human's time, and the lateral offset is ignored. It is not T2. The tool is `motion_c/ri/code/v8f_t1_eval_ri.py` (sha256 `0417c838…`, built after the frame-freeze tool's pattern; 12 tests passed, three mutations RED).
* **The rule for reading any (c) arm [M].** Each (c) arm and its base are read three ways: `logged`, `hold_now` (frame freeze) and `reindexed`. *"The deciding read is `reindexed`."* A closure or a DOMINANT on `logged` that `reindexed` does not reproduce is reported as the camera-clock leak, never as a result, and an arm whose `logged` closure exceeds its `reindexed` closure by more than half of the `logged` closure is labelled LEAK-BORNE whatever else it reads. The rule was committed before any (c) number (`DESIGN_C.md`, sha256 `{{RES_DC}}…`) and enters the (a) card verbatim (§7.39).

**7.38.3 MOTIONPROBE: v WEAK, dv ABSENT (`R/raw/2026-11-05-motion-c-probe/RESULT.md`).**

* **Setup [M].** The question: do cached frozen DINOv3-L token grids (4 x 10 per frame), stacked over frames, carry ego motion? 600 TRAIN clips kept (0 dropped; clip-list sha256 `cbbe333d…`), 5 folds by clip, 29,406 windows in all and 7,772 in the 3 to 8 m/s band; eval-141 disjointness asserted (0 shared ids). Targets: the current speed v and dv = v(t) − v(t−3). Readouts: ridge and a 2 x 256 MLP on per-cell PCA to 32 dimensions. Feature sets: F1 (frame t), F4 (four frames), D3 (differences of consecutive frames), with controls K1 (permuted labels), K2 (frames from another clip) and K3 (frame order shuffled). Estimator: the fold mean ± SD (ddof 1) over five folds BY CLIP, which answers *another draw of CLIPS* only; it is not the episode-cluster bootstrap and answers neither a training-run nor an inference-run question. Card `PREREG_MOTIONPROBE.md` (sha256 in `FROZEN_SHA.txt`), two amendments before any real-data fit.
* **The committed rule (card section 9) [M].** g = band R² of the better of F4 and D3 minus F1's. ABSENT if g < 0.05; PRESENT if g >= 0.15 and gK3 <= 0.5 g and gK2 <= max(0.02, 0.10 g); WEAK otherwise (a gain with a failed control is not shown to be motion-borne).

**7.38.5 Status of (c): two named blockers, and what carries forward (`C_STATUS.md`, MM, 2026-11-05).**

* **Why (c) stops here on the allocated resources [M/E].** (1) *The pixel form cannot be read faithfully on any provisioned tier.* Its one admissible cue in the crawl is a visible lead moving away from a model that lags. On `logged` frames the lead sits at the human's gap at time t; on `reindexed` frames it sits at the gap the human had at the model's own position, i.e. closer than the lead really is. Neither renders the lead pulling away, and the model's own speed read from pixels duplicates the measured history, so a pixel-motion arm read on T1-kin would give a number that cannot count. **Unblocks with:** a re-perception tier (T2: AlpaSim, CARLA or a neural re-renderer), not provisioned. (2) **Encoder adaptation (LoRA on DINOv3-L)**, which would also need T2 to be read for this cue, is projected at 470 h per seed on box 2 (§7.9, ESTIMATED). **Unblocks with:** the A40 or other compute the PI allocates. Building the pixel frame cache (a frame store exists for 6 clips) and the network is days of box-2 and dev-box work whose read is blocked by item 1, so it was not started.
* **What carries forward [M, then H].** The lead association is a pointer for (a) and for the next longitudinal design: a single frame shows the lead's presence and distance, a static and admissible cue. Next, in the PI's order: (a), the level-from-image head, read on `logged`, `hold_now` and `reindexed`. **[H]:** a lead-aware head (§7.39, arm LLR) tests the part of (c)'s admissible content that single-frame features can carry, on the allocated resources.

**7.38.6 What §7.38 establishes, and what it cannot.**

* **Establishes [M].** (1) The C0 base does not use the camera clock (RI-C0, ratio 1.002 / 1.003), so logged-frame reads of frozen-feature arms stand. (2) The cached frozen tokens carry no usable motion at these readouts (v WEAK, dv ABSENT). (3) A lead-agent target covers part of the crawl: L40(CRAWL) 0.4639 / 0.4956 against 0.2409 / 0.2687. (4) `reindexed` is defined and is the deciding scene for (c) and (a).
* **Cannot [M].** No (c) arm was trained, so nothing here shows that a motion-reading arm closes the crawl; `reindexed` is a longitudinal-only approximation and not T2; the MOTIONPROBE verdict is about one reduction and two readouts; the lead's speed is ESTIMATED; what sits in the no-lead half of the crawl is not in the lead file. No T2 number exists.
