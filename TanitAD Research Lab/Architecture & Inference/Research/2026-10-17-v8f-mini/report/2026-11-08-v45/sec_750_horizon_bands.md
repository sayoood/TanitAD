**7.50.4 The redesign: designed, not trained [H, design; `SPEC_HWM.md`; no training run, no gate token and no result exist for any item below].**

| | Operative (L1) | Tactical (L2) | Strategic (L3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Band | 0 to 2 s | up to 6 s | 6 to 30 s |
| World-model step | 0.2 s | 0.6 s (stride 3) | 3.0 s (stride 5) |
| Trained reach | k = 10, 2.0 s | k = 10, 6.0 s | latent targets to k = 6 (18 s), masked to frames that exist; 18 to 30 s by rollout supervised through decoded geometry |
| Outputs | the whole 6 s plan, 30 knots (one trajectory, or one per tactical hypothesis); band split in the loss and the metrics (knots 1 to 10, knots 11 to 30) | vocabulary (goals, lateral and longitudinal classes) and constraints, up to 6 s, which condition O; no trajectory head, no knots | time-indexed checkpoints at 6 to 30 s, detected context, derived goals |

The design adds a free-run k-step loss per level (with k = 1 bit-equal to today's loss), a world-model-only window sampler with per-level masks and a frozen-adapter pooled path that fits an 8 GB GPU, a multi-rate history as an arm, inverse dynamics kept on at every level, a 6 s plan emitted whole by the operative level (30 knots, scored per band on knots 1 to 10 and knots 11 to 30, with no continuity term because one decoder emits every knot) and conditioned on the tactical level's vocabulary and constraints, which the tactical level emits without a trajectory head, every layer reading the current measured ego state, a strategic head with route checkpoints at 6, 9, ..., 30 s and vision-only detected context (country, period of day, illumination, weather, surface, visibility), and a per-frame label release `tanitad-context-labels-v1` (private on the PI's HF account, parquet labels only, no raw frames). The baseline planner HWM-PLAN plans top-down through the existing `plan_topdown` (goal latent, then L3 macro-actions, L2, L1) and is compared on the same windows, per band, in the four families, with STRATEGIC PAUSED, against HWM-ORACLE (T0 ceiling), FLAT-WM, REACTIVE (DAG-OACC and its band-split retrain), HYBRID, REL and P10. Order of work: PLANTEST and the per-band reactive baseline (stage 0), per-frame labels and the HF release (1), ladder code and its card (2), HWM-PLAN harness and goal head (3), band split of O and re-labelled T (4), strategic heads with context-swap controls (5), encoder LoRA if positive (6), all arms (7). The frozen cards MRATE, LFS, DIVRS and REL do not carry over to the new ladder unchanged: MRATE's question stays valid but its arms are defined on the old strides; LFS is subsumed by a ladder card; DIVRS needs re-binning on each level's reach window; REL's token list needs an amendment [M, `AUDIT_MODEL.md` section 6.1]. The plan states its own limits: no real image exists past 20.17 s in this corpus; all 25 countries use right-hand traffic, so left-hand adaptation cannot be validated; and two earlier world-model planners failed on this programme, so the reactive planner stays the fallback arm and PLANTEST on the new ladder is a required gate [I for the last statement].

The design was reviewed against H-JEPA (arXiv 2610.06805) in `horizon_review/REVIEW_HJEPA_CONSISTENCY.md` [M, code and paper read; no planner result]: per-level encoders and predictors, SIGReg and inverse dynamics at every level and the planner's Eqs. 4 to 6 match; the frozen DINOv3 encoder and the added k-step loss are declared deviations; and nine gaps (G1 to G9) were found, among them the planning context the k-step term trains, the goal horizon, the missing goal head and the direction of the tactical to operative edge. The PI's rule to condition the lower levels from the goals of the higher ones fixes the direction of every edge (the planner's sub-goal cost and the reader cascade, each with a control that goes red when the edge is cut), and the ruling of 2026-11-08 on the tactical to operative edge is the one recorded in the table above.

**7.50.7 PLANTEST: is the latent space of today's world models plannable? [M, T0 with an oracle (true-future) goal, never a driving result; `raw/2026-11-08-v8f-plantest/RESULT_PLANTEST.md`, `RESULT_PLANTEST.json`, `RESULT_PLANTEST_controls.md`, row `H-V8F-PLANTEST`; landed `8f2a62cc`].** This is the gate that `SPEC_HWM.md` section 8 requires before a world-model planner is trusted. It is a read with no training: the planner plans actions in the latent space of a frozen world model, and the plan is scored against the 12 actions the human logged in the same window. Two world models (M2b s0 and M2b s1, one step at 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 s) and two planners: TD3, the three-level top-down planner (`plan_topdown`), and FLAT1, the flat planner that plans at level 1 only with a last-state cost. Estimator: paired episode-cluster bootstrap over 141 episodes, B = 2,000, seed 0, on 1,976 windows (0 dropped); it answers another draw of EPISODES, not another training run or another planner seed, so both training seeds are required and the planner-seed floor is printed beside.
