### 7.49 What the world model imagines (2026-11-08): every level notices a swapped action, the encoded future carries the lead gap at levels 2 and 3, and the imagined future does not carry it

The PI asked, 2026-11-07, for the imagination of each world-model level to be validated (plan and cards in §7.47.9). This section reads the four imagination experiments that have run since: VOE1B (does a level notice an impossible future, against a calibrated untrained reference), IMAG1B (does a probe fitted on the encoded latent still work on the imagined latent, and is the imagined latent better than copying the last one), CAPLADDER (at which rung between the camera tokens and the world-model levels the lead gap is lost) and IMAG1C (IMAG1B's lead-gap row re-read on frames with CAPLADDER's probe). Evidence classes as in 7.44: **[M]** a measurement of ours with its artifact path, **[E]** arithmetic on measured numbers, **[I]** inherited, **[H]** a hypothesis. **Tier T0 throughout (teacher-forced one-step prediction on cached latents of true frames): a world-model diagnostic, never a driving result, never pooled with T1-kin or T2.** The planner (S, T, O) reads none of these predictions (§4.7, §7.2). Model: M2b, two training seeds s0 / s1 of one recipe, no training in any read here. Estimator: the episode-cluster bootstrap of `taniteval/ci.py` (B = 2,000, seed 0) over the 141 eval episodes, which answers "would another draw of EPISODES say this?"; a row is called **separated** only when its interval excludes the null AND its point lies beyond the 97.5th (or below the 2.5th) percentile of a permutation reference (R = 199 draws, labels deranged across episodes, probe refitted); neither answers "would another training run say this?" (`H-ESTIM-SEED-1`), so both seeds are required and no replicate arm exists on this rig. STRATEGIC is **PAUSED** by PI ruling item 25: not read, not a pass. **The result of the section in one line: a swap of the logged action is noticed at levels 1 and 2 against an untrained network of the same architecture and, when the swapped action is matched in size and opposite in sign, at all three levels (ACTION-SWAP-M, both seeds); the lead gap is readable from the encoded true future at levels 2 and 3 (6 of 9 cells, both seeds) and from the camera tokens through the readers S, T and O; and the imagined lead gap is a copy of the present one in every readable cell, with 0 IMAGINES of 9 (level, k) cells in IMAG1C, while a frozen pause, a reversed segment and a teleport are not distinguished from the Copy-Last detector.**

**7.49.2 VOE1B: a swapped action is noticed at every level; a pause, a reversal and a teleport are not told apart from a frame-change detector [M, T0, eval-141].** Each manipulation is applied to a window from onset on. The score of a window is the largest normalised one-step surprise (prediction error divided by its level's scale) over the 1.6 s after onset. The accuracy is the share of pairs in which the manipulated copy scores higher than its unmodified copy (0.5 is chance). NOTICES needs all of: (a) accuracy two-part separated above 0.5; (b) separated above the Copy-Last null where the card puts the null in; (c) accuracy above the **maximum** of the 16 untrained networks' accuracies of the same chassis, manipulation and level, at both seeds. INIT-LEVEL means (a) and (b) hold and (c) fails (an untrained network of the same architecture does this too); NULL-ONLY means (a) holds and (b) fails; BLIND means (a) fails. ACTION-SWAP-M replaces the logged action by that of a donor window of the same magnitude (within 10 % on mean longitudinal and mean yaw action) and the opposite sign, 530 windows of 104 episodes.

| manipulation (n windows, episodes) | level 1 (0.2 s) | level 2 (0.4 s) | level 3 (0.8 s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACTION-SWAP-M (530, 104) | NOTICES | NOTICES | NOTICES |
| ACTION-SWAP (1,976, 141) | NOTICES | NOTICES | INIT-LEVEL |
| TELEPORT (1,976, 141) | NULL-ONLY | NULL-ONLY | NULL-ONLY |
| SPLICE (1,976, 141) | NULL-ONLY | NULL-ONLY | NULL-ONLY |
| REVERSE (1,976, 141) | NULL-ONLY | NULL-ONLY | BLIND |
| FREEZE (1,725, 137) | BLIND | BLIND | BLIND |

(a) A level notices a wrong action: the error at the one-step prediction rises when the action fed in is not the logged one, by more than any of 16 untrained networks shows, at levels 1 and 2 for both swap forms and at level 3 for the size-matched, sign-reversed form. (b) At level 3 the plain ACTION-SWAP accuracy (0.738 / 0.733) is below the largest untrained accuracy (0.759 / 0.743), so that cell is INIT-LEVEL: the architecture alone produces a swap response of that size; the size-matched form removes it (0.668 / 0.677 against 0.515 / 0.532). (c) Against the Copy-Last null the ACTION-SWAP rows read 0.500 by construction (a copied latent ignores the action), so the model-minus-null difference equals the accuracy minus 0.5 (ACTION-SWAP-M level 3 +0.168 [+0.122, +0.213] / +0.183 [+0.132, +0.236]). (d) A frozen pause (FREEZE) is BLIND at every level and both seeds: the accuracy is below 0.5, separated (0.315 [0.299, 0.331] at level 1 s0), and sits below the minimum of the 16 untrained networks at every level (for example level 2 s0 0.322 against a minimum of 0.408). (e) TELEPORT and SPLICE are detected at 0.77 to 1.00 accuracy, but so by the Copy-Last distance, and the model minus null difference is not separated above 0 in any cell (SPLICE level 1: 1.000 for both, difference +0.000 [+0.000, +0.000]); the surprise at these levels is therefore a frame-change detector for those two manipulations. (f) Event alignment, reported beside with no bar: the surprise peaks do not align with braking, turning or lead-change onsets at any level (47, 100 and 27 onsets in the 141 clips; ALIGNS: no level); turn at levels 2 and 3 has AP minus chance separated above 0 (+0.0188 [+0.0107, +0.0314] and +0.0253 [+0.0118, +0.0512] at s0) while AP minus the Copy-Last AP is not separated at level 3 and at level 2 only at s0.

**7.49.3 IMAG1B: a probe fitted on the encoded latent works on the imagined latent only where it also works on a copy [M, T0, eval-141].** For each target, a ridge and a two-layer MLP probe are fitted on the encoded latent at t, then applied to the model's predicted latent at k steps, to Copy-Last (the encoded latent at t) and to the Oracle (the encoded true latent at t + k). Labels, in order: UNREADABLE (the Oracle is not readable from the encoded future either), IMAGINES (the predicted latent beats Copy-Last, two-part separated), WORSE-THAN-COPY, COPIES. **7.49.6 The four families, and the agreement with §7.47 [M, T0].** LONGITUDINAL: the speed of the present frame is encoded and the action response is WEAK (gain 0.35 to 0.46); acceleration, lead gap, closing speed and time to collision are not readable from the imagined latent (IMAG1B) and the lead gap is readable only in the encoded future at levels 2 and 3 (IMAG1C). LATERAL: yaw rate and curvature are UNREADABLE in the imagined latent in all nine cells; the lateral gain is INERT or WRONG-SIGN. TACTICAL: lead presence and the 7-class longitudinal manoeuvre are COPIES (no IMAGINES by decision); the manoeuvre decision of the planner itself is read in §7.48.3. STRATEGIC: PAUSED (item 25). This agrees with §7.47.2 on action use and with §7.47.4 on the lack of acceleration and yaw content. **Two points where the sources differ and the report does not choose:** (1) §7.47.8 (LATSEM2B, TRAIN windows, 139 episodes, one permutation-calibrated instrument) finds the lead gap encoded at no level, while CAPLADDER and IMAG1C (eval-141 frames with a lead, a ridge probe, a different target definition) find it readable at levels 2 and 3 and in the readers; the two differ in probe, population and target construction, and a bridging read is not run. (2) §7.47.5 reports level 3 decoding to a future speed better than persistence, while IMAG1B's pooled decision for speed is COPIES at all nine cells; the two use different references (the decode of the rolled latent against "speed stays the same" against the probe transfer to Copy-Last under a two-part rule).

**7.49.7 What the triggers commit (the cards' mechanical rules, evaluated in the result files).** IMAG1B: E3 CAPLADDER fired (lead gap UNREADABLE at every level) and has since run (O-NONE, then IMAG1C); NO-SPLIT fired, so MRATE (multi-rate history, §7.47.7) is the carded lever for the task-structure index; E7 DIVRS (lateral gain WEAK, INERT or WRONG-SIGN at every level) did not fire; the L1 residual-parameterisation arm did not fire; E9 REL has no (level, k, target) row that came out IMAGINES, so no REL token list is carded. VOE1B: the FREEZE rule fired (BLIND at every level), which points at MRATE; the TELEPORT / SPLICE rule fired (NULL-ONLY at every level), so the surprise at those two manipulations is not evidence of an internal model; the ACTION-SWAP-M rule fired for NOTICES at all three levels. No arm has been trained on any of these triggers.

**7.49.8 What §7.49 establishes and what it does not.** *Establishes [M, T0]:* each world-model level responds to a wrong action beyond what an untrained network of the same architecture does (ACTION-SWAP-M, both seeds, all three levels); the lead gap is present in the encoded true future at levels 2 and 3 and in the camera tokens at every rung tested; the imagined latent does not carry acceleration, yaw rate, curvature, closing speed, time to collision or the agent count beyond the copy, and its lead gap and speed are the copy under the two-part rule. *Does not establish:* that any planner can use these predictions (none reads them); that the action response has the physical size (longitudinal gain 0.35 to 0.46, never inside the card's 0.5 to 1.5); anything about the strategic band (PAUSED); a result that holds across training runs (two seeds of one recipe, no replicate arm); a driving effect (T0 throughout). The untrained-network reference adds initialisation variance, it is not a replicate of the training run. A NOTICES label is necessary, not sufficient, for "training taught the model to use the action".
