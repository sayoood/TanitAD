# CITATION TABLE — every cited work, mapped to the pathology it attacks

**Companion to `R3_DESIGN_RESEARCH.md`.** Date 2026-07-27. All entries `PUBLISHED`.

## How to read the columns

* **Pathology** — ⚡ **OSCILLATION** (high-frequency / saturating command when the demand is small) ·
  ➡️ **DRIFT** (covariate shift: the learner reaches states the expert never demonstrated) ·
  **BOTH** · ⛔ **NEITHER** (aimed at something we do not have).
* **Depth** — how much of it I actually read. **FULL** = full text or HTML body read ·
  **PARTIAL** = abstract + a rendered section or a results table · **SUMMARY** = search-result
  abstract/summary only, **not** independently verified. ⚠️ **Nothing in the design rests on a
  SUMMARY-depth row.**
* **Demonstrated vs asserted** — kept separate on purpose, per the brief.

## D. ⭐ THE ONE-SCREEN ANSWER — which pathology does each family attack?

| family | attacks | our pathology is an **oscillation** — does it fit? |
|---|---|---|
| **DAgger · DART · ChauffeurNet · scheduled sampling · professor forcing** *(= R3's family)* | ➡️ **DRIFT** — covariate shift on the **state** distribution | ⛔ **NO. Not one of them attacks an oscillation.** Their premise is *"the learner reaches states the expert never demonstrated"*. Ours is *"the learner's command is a differentiated noise signal"*. |
| **CAPS · Grad-CAPS · LipsNet · LCP · L2C2 · action-rate rewards · low-pass filters** | ⚡ **OSCILLATION** | ⚠️ **Right pathology, wrong object.** Every one regularises a **state→action network**. Our blind loop has none — the map is `a = ((v(ẑ_j) − v(ẑ_{j−1}))/DT).clamp(±3)`, hand-written. ✅ **They DO apply to the tactical planner — R1's arm, 30 GPU-min, unrun.** |
| **Bang-bang (Seyde et al.)** | ⛔ **NEITHER** | ⛔ **A named cause we do not have.** No reward, no policy, no optimisation in this loop. |
| **ACID · action–state consistency · cycle-consistency** | **BOTH**, via *realizability* | ⭐ **YES — the only family that fits.** It asks whether an imagined transition is producible by the action that claims to have produced it. **It cannot be satisfied by ignoring the action channel**, which is what makes it the safe choice under refusal condition S5. |
| **Koopman / gradient-penalised latent dynamics** | ⚡ oscillation **at the dynamics operator** | ⚠️ Right level, but an **architecture change** — out of scope for R3, and the right reading for v5. |
| **Ensemble gating · λ-return calibration · low-pass · our α-blend** | ➡️ mitigates **at inference** | ✅ **Already done, and already at 101.1 % of the registered ceiling.** This is exactly why R3 has no headroom left on its own metric. |
