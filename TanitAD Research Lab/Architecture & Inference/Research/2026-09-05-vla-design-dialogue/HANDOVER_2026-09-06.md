## 1. Your question, answered as far as measurement can take it without training

> *A CoT that INFLUENCES behaviour while staying CONSISTENT with the selected trajectory.*

The frontier literature's verdict is *"neither design satisfies both desiderata"* — comparing
**two discrete models**. The Energy Bridge has a **continuous dial**, so the trade-off was
**traced on your own trained model**, at zero GPU:

| β | authority | CON rank (chance 64.5) | ADE |
|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 1.00 | 8.1 % | 64.40 — overlaps chance | 0.6521 |
| **1.50** | **12.2 %** | **59.06 [53.79, 64.27] SEPARATED** | **0.6479** ← best in sweep |
| 2.00 | 16.3 % | 53.66 SEPARATED | 0.6583 |
| 3.00 | 24.4 % | 38.70 SEPARATED | **0.8168 — separated WORSE** |
| 160 | 1302 % | **1.15** (near-perfect) | **7.62 — 11.5× base** |

⭐ **Stage A: β = 1.5.** Smallest authority at which consistency is measurable at all, and it
carries the best ADE in the sweep.
⛔ **Near-perfect consistency is purchasable, and it costs the car.** Raising β alone cannot
meet your requirement — **the energy must get better, not louder.**
⭐ Scene-specificity **grows monotonically and is separated at seven consecutive β**
(+0.0115 → +0.0845), so in the safe band more authority buys understanding, not obedience.

## 2. The mechanism is validated on a real model

* The zero-init port is **exactly inert at init** (0/0/0) and moves selection in **23.2 %** of
  windows once trained.
* Its effect is **scene-specific**: swapping in a prior from a *different episode* costs
  **−0.0254 m** [−0.0425, −0.0093], **replicated** under a second window draw
  (−0.0375 [−0.0521, −0.0236]) and **broad-based** (23/34 and 26/34 episodes agree; medians
  −0.0251 / −0.0253).
* Spearman(Δ, S0) = **0.096** — the port adds **new ordering, not an echo**.
* Headroom confirmed: the fan holds **0.2315 m** while the model ships **0.65 m**.

## 3. Your semantics question — the route you proposed is the one that works

**DINOv3 ViT-L/16 is the only arm of four clearing its own within-episode null on agent
presence** (`vru<20m` p 0.000, `vru<60m` p 0.020), against a matched pixel floor, a
random-weight architecture floor, and REF-C's trunk. And the trunk's deficit is **not a
pooling artifact** — the full 8×8 map is the *weakest* arm. On the **far** band a nonlinear
read rescues the trunk (p 0.040 / 0.020); on the **near** band nothing does.

⇒ **Distillation targets NEAR-agent semantics only.** Far agents are already reachable by the
reasoner's own MLP; a teacher there would be paying twice.
