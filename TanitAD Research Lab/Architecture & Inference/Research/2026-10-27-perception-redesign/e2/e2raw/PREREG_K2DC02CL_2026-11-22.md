## 0. Why this arm exists, and what it may decide

* K2DC02 (seed 0) and K2DC02s1 (seed 1) were both meant to test a 0.02 map-share dose. The logged controller did not hold it. Offline replay of both runs' logged probe rows (`E:/Projects/TanitAD-artifacts/refcv9_e2/dcreplay/`, `dc_replay.py` + `replay_bounds.txt`; **MEASURED** by the MM, 2026-11-22, on the logged `dc_*` / `dcp_*` rows of both runs, `r9e2-K2DC02.tsv` 24 rows and `r9e2-K2DC02s1.tsv` 22 rows, `SHA256SUMS.txt` beside them):
  * the dead-beat controller turns one-batch reading noise into weights up to 20.5;
  * mean share at steps ≥ 3,000: **0.0801** (K2DC02, 13 probes, 3 in the band) and **0.0625** (K2DC02s1, 11 probes, 1 in the band), against a 0.02 target;
  * a weight clamped to [2.5, 3.5] gave a replayed mean of **0.0261** (5 of 13 probes in [0.01, 0.03]) and **0.0252** (2 of 11 probes in the band).
  * Replay controls (pre-committed in the `dc_replay.py` header; the replay is void if either fails): (1) the share recomputed at the logged weight equals the logged `dc_share_read`, max |Δ| 2.8e-17 (K2DC02) and 1.4e-17 (K2DC02s1); (2) the replayed dead-beat rule reproduces the logged `dc_w_eff` at every probe, max |Δw| 3.6e-15 and 7.1e-15. Both hold.
  * The replay ran the dead-beat rule with bounds [1.0, 25.0] where the runs used [0.1, 25]. The lower bound never bound on these rows: the smallest logged weight is 1.1039 (K2DC02) and 1.0114 (K2DC02s1), and control (2) reproduces every logged weight.
  * The one-batch reading noise at a fixed weight is sd ≈ 0.02–0.03, the width of the [0.01, 0.03] band itself. That is why a controller that re-solves the weight from one reading chases noise.
* The replay's **pre-committed** test refuted the smoothed controller (EMA + damping: 0–1 of 13 probes in the band on the FIT run, against 3 of 13 for dead-beat). Its pre-committed selection picked a constant weight, FIX 2.0 (4 of 13 FIT, 5 of 11 held-out). The PI chose the clamp over a constant weight (chat, 2026-11-22).

## 6. Estimator, limits

* Paired episode-cluster bootstrap, B 2000, 70 DEV episodes, both inference seeds. It answers "another draw of EPISODES", not "another training run" (`H-ESTIM-SEED-1`) and not "another inference draw".
* One training run of this arm. A VALID dose and a separated interval are necessary, not sufficient, for a lever claim.
* **Replay limitation (named):** the clamp bounds were chosen from an offline replay on logged readings in which a different weight sequence would have changed the model (`dc_replay.py` header). The clamp row was post-hoc; this run is its test, and a read that misses §2 item 2 or 3 is the clamp failing, not the replay confirming.
* Clamping removes the controller's authority: a weight pinned at 2.5 or 3.5 is a fixed dose, so the realised share depends on the trunk, not on the target. The dose-verdict output reports how often the weight sat on a bound.
