## 3. The dose rule on the replicate (PREREG_K2DC constants, unchanged)

The rule must hold at inference seeds 0 and 1, read twice: EMA (K2DC02s1 vs K0 and K2DC) and tail (K2DC02s1t vs K0t and K2DCt).

| outcome | rule, at both inference seeds |
|---|---|
| **SUPPORTED** | K2DC02s1 − K0 `LAT_cross_mae_m` not separated worse, AND K2DC02s1 − K2DC `LAT_cross_mae_m` ≤ −2.5 mm, AND the jitter delta K2DC02s1 − K2DC < 0 |
| **REFUTED** | K2DC02s1 − K2DC `LAT_cross_mae_m` and `LAT_cross_abs_final_m` both within ±1 mm |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | anything else |

* Tool: `r9_e2_dose_verdict.py --arm K2DC02s1`, with K2DC (EMA) or K2DCt (tail) in K2's slot.
* The TAIL outcome decides the dose question, because tail weights are what E3's 73,280 steps would produce
  (PREREG_K2DC02TAIL §0). The EMA outcome is reported beside it.

## 4. The training-run floor: K2DC02s1 − K2DC02 (identical flags)

* Every row of all four families at both inference seeds, on EMA and on tail, by the same paired bootstrap.
* Produced by the same tool with K2DC02 (EMA) or K2DC02t (tail) in K2's slot. Its "within ±1 mm" row is reported as a
  floor reading, never as a verdict about a lever.
* **Admissibility:** a dose effect is claimable at an inference seed only if |K2DC02s1t − K2DCt `LAT_cross_mae_m`| exceeds
  |K2DC02s1t − K2DC02t `LAT_cross_mae_m`| at that seed.
* The floor's rows are also the first measured training-run noise on this rig for every family. They are banked as such for
  `H-ESTIM-SEED-1`, and register rows that rest on a single E2 training run are read against them.
