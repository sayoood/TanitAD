## PI amendment, 2026-11-08: MAP-2's 5-10 m and 10-20 m value is 0.90

**PI, verbatim:** "Use 0.9 bar". The ruling answers the Master Mind's report of the same day that the 0.99 value would very likely be missed and could be changed before E2 is read.

* **The binding value is ≥ 0.90 at 5-10 m and at 10-20 m**, on the sidewalk-only `nf_prec_*`. The instrument, 0-5 m ≥ 0.60 on lift-valid cells and 20-40 m separated above refcv8 do not change.
* **What was visible when it was set.** No E2 read exists, no refcv9 full-model checkpoint exists, and MAP-2 has not been read on HOLD for any model. The E1 perception-only reads on DEV were visible: M1N20 0.646 [0.530, 0.752] at 5-10 m and 0.662 [0.542, 0.762] at 10-20 m; C0 0.622 [0.520, 0.712] and 0.633 [0.506, 0.732] (MEASURED, `dagland/e1/verdicts/M1N20/read_v3/arm_M1N20/thr_44f7c569_7e89334f/EVAL_{M1N20,C0}.json`, T0 label level, 68 DEV episodes, episode-cluster bootstrap B 2000, one training seed). So 0.90 is still above every interval read so far.
* **The ruling changes the bar, not E0h's measurement.** With GT-recall-1 maps carrying false sidewalk flags, E0h reads a sidewalk rate of 0.1332 at p 0.9 (one placement seed; BASE 0.1792) against MAP-1's ≤ 0.098, which it meets on 3 of 3 placement seeds only at p 0.99 (MEASURED, `e0/E0h_CARD.md`, T1 self-action open loop). Passing MAP-2 at 0.90 therefore does not imply passing MAP-1. MAP-1 stays binding and is judged on its own.
* **The value is fixed from here.** It is not lowered after E2 or E3 is read. A miss is reported as FAILED, as written, and section 5.4's "MAP-2 FAIL at 5-40 m" lever runs.
* The table above keeps the earlier decision as the record of what was decided before this ruling. The insert-only seal note in `REFCV9_DESIGN.md` carries 0.90 and cites this amendment.

**What sealing strictly needs from HOLD.** Only MAP-1 names a refcv8 re-read on HOLD before sealing. The other perception bars are absolute values plus a paired refcv9-against-refcv8 comparison made at the E3 final read, on inputs that exist. MAP-1's ≤ 9.8 % stays as written whatever the HOLD read shows; if refcv8's HOLD rate makes that bar inconsistent with its "≥ 50 % of the gap" wording, the discrepancy is reported at sealing and resolved toward the stricter value.
