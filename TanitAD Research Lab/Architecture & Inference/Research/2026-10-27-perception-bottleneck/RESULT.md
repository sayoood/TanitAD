## The plan — perception levers ranked by MEASURED evidence

### Box head (measured now, final)
1. **Head/loss: one-to-one detection.**
   - The duplicate-suppression gap is worth +0.12 AP@2 m at zero training (INHERITED, PERC8).
   - That is the largest measured box effect in the programme.
   - Redesign: train it out, with a set loss and one-to-one matching or a learned NMS.
2. **Range and resolution of the box representation.**
   - AP falls about 6× from 0-20 m to 40-60 m (0.539 → 0.092), with separated CIs.
   - The class head is not collapsed, so the range term is localisation and recall at distance, not classification.
   - In a full-training redesign, the box head should read features that keep far-range resolution, not a pooled 0.5 m
     grid. That is HYPOTHESIS for the mechanism; the drop itself is MEASURED.
3. **Class head.** Not the bottleneck. One residual: person↔rider confusion (18 % of person TPs).

### Map head (MEASURED, Q1/Q2 final)
1. **0-5 m: supervise and fuse the cells the camera cannot see.** Measured: 93.6 % of the 0-5 m sidewalk flags sit on
   lift-invalid cells, and a 3x3-conv probe on the SAME frozen features, merely trained on those cells, gains +0.118 AP
   there. Cheapest lever: stop masking lift-invalid cells in the map loss (refcv8 trained with `--map-lift-valid-mask`).
   Full lever: temporal BEV fusion (the cell was visible 0.3-1 s earlier) — HYPOTHESIS for its size.
2. **Beyond 5 m: perception training, not head training.** O2 + trunk ceiling: the frozen features hold no readable
   headroom (head > every probe, not data-limited), so the decided full training (backbone + lift with the map/box
   losses) is the lever; a stronger pretrained encoder is second (HYPOTHESIS, not measured here).
3. **Unfreezing only the lift + BEV encoder:** not supported — the lift does not lose the trunk's information.
4. **Map GT:** no obstacle share found (Q2); a curb-height audit remains open.
5. **Head/loss change beyond 5 m:** last — the head already beats every probe class tested.
