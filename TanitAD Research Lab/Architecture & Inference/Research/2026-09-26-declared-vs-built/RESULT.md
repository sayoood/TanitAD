## 4. G-HYG — config classes refuse undeclared attributes (`stack/tanitad/train/config_hygiene.py`)

`@strict_fields` (outside `@dataclass`) makes `setattr` of an undeclared name raise `UndeclaredConfigAttribute`
(an `AttributeError`) naming the class, the attribute and the declared fields; declared fields, `replace`, `__init__`
and `__post_init__` are unchanged; frozen classes are left alone. Applied to all **11** config dataclasses of
`refc.py` and to `RefCV3Config`. `undeclared_attributes()` / `assert_config_hygiene()` walk a config tree for the one
route the decorator cannot see (`__dict__` writes: pickle, `copy`, `vars().update`, and undecorated classes of other
modules); `_pin_trainer_cfg` ends with that walk. **The FIX-3 mechanism is now impossible at the moment of assignment.**

## 10. Batch 2 — D3 (gradient-reach rows) and the τ script's evidence class

(`:8496`), and the row was written on `step % log_every == 0 or step == steps` (`:8497`, `:8544`). Pre-steps 0, 50,

`step + 1`. The existing tests could not see it: they log every step, or read the final row.

* one fill rule, `_logged_after(step, log_every, steps)` (= `(step + 1) % log_every == 0 or step + 1 >= steps`), used by
  BOTH pre-increment instruments (`_gp_row` keeps its semantics exactly; the `ga_*` fill is the fix);
