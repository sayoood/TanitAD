### F3. L3: the *lazy equilibrium*, and the one published clearance (Q-R3, H-SOTA-R3)

Their quick variant on freshly generated scenes **under-counted a render-sensitive supervised encoder at 0.35 against its in-distribution 0.84.** ⇒ an ablation run on a differently-generated split can manufacture a false collapse.
2. **`2607.06925` §5.4 dose–response:** the natural alternative hypothesis — *"it leaks only because the other inputs are weak"* — is **refuted**. Scaling the action input α: 1.0 → 0.5 → 0.0 with everything else fixed gives counterfactual cosine **0.975 → 0.984 → 0.986** (transcription available) and **−0.00 → −0.01 → −0.08** (referent-only, no transcription). **Degrading the other inputs never increases leakage.** ⇒ we cannot argue our decoder "leans on nav because vision is weak"; on this evidence, fixing vision would not remove the leak.

**Answer to (c) with the explicit comparison to ours.** The field **does** report the ablation we now require — twice at readout level (`2607.06925`, `2607.27017`) and once at system level in our own domain (`2312.03031`) — and in all three the shape matches `D-REFAV1-TAC-DECODER-PANEL` closely enough that our number is **not** an outlier: 0.90 → 0.27 (chance 0.25) there, **0.873 → 0.520 (no-information 0.500)** here. The published diagnosis transfers with two riders. (i) `2607.06925`'s split of exposed-vs-immune designs is **structural**: *"designs that place the goal in the planner's COST … are immune (the predictor never sees the instruction), whereas designs that condition the PREDICTOR on language (FiLM-into-the-predictor) are exposed."* refav1 conditions its predictor on nav — **exposed by construction** (UNVERIFIED whether the conditioning is FiLM-form; the tactical package's own read is that nav reaches the logits). (ii) There, the goal *is* available at deployment and the objection is scientific (it is not perception). **Ours is worse in kind: `nav` is an ego-future derivation that will not exist at deployment at all**, so the leak is not only a measurement error, it is a capability that cannot ship — which is exactly the `2312.03031` Ego-MLP situation with the sign flipped (their ego status ships; our nav does not).

### F5. Readout geometry: token-level attention vs our grid pooling (Q-R5, H-SOTA-R4)

**This is the second finding the first attempt's KB entry points at; it stands and gains one number.**
