## 0. Verdict in five lines

1. **Q1/Q2 (positional transfer) — the specific experiment does not exist in the literature, in either direction.** No banked or searched primary transfers a pretrained ViT's blocks into an architecture with a **different positional-encoding family** and measures what survives. What exists bounds it loosely from two sides: training a ViT with **no PE at all** costs **−4.0 top-1** (`lib:2102.10882` Table 1: DeiT-tiny 72.2 learnable → **68.2** none), and interpolating a PE across a geometry change is the field's standard remedy which **still degrades without fine-tuning** (`lib:2102.10882` §3, `lib:2212.08013` §3). Neither is our experiment. **Our measurement will be the only evidence.**
2. **The PE family is measured to matter in the ENCODER and measured NOT to matter much in the PREDICTOR.** `lib:2506.09985` §3.1: 3D-RoPE instead of absolute sincos *"helps stabilize training for the largest models"* (encoder). `lib:2512.24497` §"Predictor architecture" + Fig. 5a: *"We do not see a substantial improvement when using RoPE instead of sincos positional embedding"* (predictor; on Metaworld sincos+ftcond is the best row). ⇒ the risk sits exactly where our transfer happens — the encoder — and the reassuring result is from the wrong module.
3. **Q3 (partial transfer) — the one primary that measures component re-initialisation says the transferable benefit is concentrated NEAR THE INPUT**, which is where `pos` lives. `lib:2008.11687` §3.4: module criticality rises with depth; *"feature-reuse is happening in layers that are closer to the input"* while *"higher layers … have tighter valleys"* (specialised). It is a **ResNet** study and it **never re-initialises a positional embedding** — so it is a *directional* prior, not a number for us, and it points the **unfavourable** way.
4. **Q5 (ego-motion degeneracy) — this now has a 2026 primary and it is close to a bullseye, with one honest gap.** `lib:2607.21576` (SDM) runs **self-supervised FUTURE-FEATURE PREDICTION on FROZEN DINOv2-B/14 patch features** — structurally our O5 on a frozen trunk — and finds the model's dominant learned component **is camera motion** on dynamic-camera video: Table 5, Kubric camera motion **p 0.16 vs r 0.28 MSE**; roles reverse on static-camera video. ⚠️ **It does not run the arm that would prove our claim** (plain future-feature prediction with no structured decomposition and no weak supervision); the closest is Table 13(a), where removing static-camera supervision makes the primary token **more** predictive of object motion than the residual (0.14 vs 0.38). ⇒ **corroboration of the mechanism, not a measurement of our failure.**
5. **E-DEC-7 gains a published THEORY it did not have.** `lib:2407.03475` (Apple, deep linear analysis of JEPA/self-distillation) — *"JEPAs are biased to learn **high-influence features**, i.e. features characterized by having high regression coefficients"*. Our register argues E-DEC-7 from **construction** (a self-generated target *permits* the ego+noise optimum). This paper argues the stronger form: latent-space prediction **actively selects** the highest-influence, most-predictable direction. On a forward driving camera that direction is ego motion. ⚠️ **Deep LINEAR models** — a scale/nonlinearity mismatch that must travel with the citation.

### Q1 — Positional-encoding transfer / swap in pretrained ViTs

**Our premise, restated so the literature can be aimed at it** (MEASURED, from `stack/scripts/dinov3_seed_checkpoint.py`'s own docstring, which is the tool that performs the transfer):
* DINOv3 is **RoPE-only** — *"its state dict contains **no** learned absolute-position table (verified on the ViT-L/16 snapshot: 415 tensors, none positional)"*.
* `ViTEncoder` is the mirror image — `encoder.py:125` `self.pos = nn.Parameter(torch.zeros(1, self.n_tokens, cfg.d_model))`, **no RoPE**. At our 256×640 / patch-16 geometry `n_tokens = 16 × 40 = 640`, so `pos` is **640 × d_model** parameters: **491,520** at the converter's documented `--enc-dim 768`, **655,360** at width 1024 (the orchestrator's figure — INHERITED, consistent with the 640-token grid).

## 3. ⛔ What the literature does NOT support — stated bluntly

1. **"DINOv3 as init, therefore the Observer-Effect ρ 0.91 reference applies."** ⛔ **Unsupported.** No primary measures a cross-PE-family weight transfer, and `lib:2403.13298` Eq. 16 gives a concrete reason to expect the transplanted `Wq`/`Wk` to be off-distribution without rotation. The prereg's own §10 D1 already ranked this: option **B** (porting into `ViT5Encoder`) was rejected as *"lossy and unauditable"* precisely because *"the step-0 control would no longer be DINOv3"* — **the shipped converter targets `ViTEncoder` instead, but the positional half of that objection survives the change of target.** The bar 0.70 must be re-derived from a step-0 measurement on the seeded trunk, or dropped.
2. **"`resize_pos_embed` in our encoder mitigates the positional loss."** ⛔ **False by construction** — it interpolates a *source* table and DINOv3 has none (`encoder.py:38,68`; converter docstring). Do not let its presence in the codebase read as a mitigation.
3. **"The PE family doesn't matter — What-Drives-Success showed RoPE ≈ sincos."** ⛔ **Scope error.** That result is about the **predictor** on a frozen encoder (`lib:2512.24497` Fig. 5a); every encoder in that paper is used out-of-the-box. The one encoder-side statement in the banked corpus points the other way (`lib:2506.09985` §3.1, stability at scale).
4. **"A −4 % ImageNet drop bounds our risk."** ⛔ **Not our experiment.** `lib:2102.10882`'s 72.2 → 68.2 is *trained from scratch with no PE*. Ours is *pretrained blocks + random PE + subsequent training*, which could be better (the PE is learned, not absent) or worse (the blocks were co-adapted to a different positional operator). **No published number brackets it.**
5. **"Sweeping the O7 weight will settle whether the external target dominates."** ⛔ **The literature offers a better instrument and the programme is not using it.** GradNorm's premise is exactly that loss weights are the wrong control variable; and the banked corpus's **complete silence** on gradient balancing (0/83 texts) means this is a gap in our reading, not in the field.
6. **"SDM proves that future-feature prediction collapses onto ego motion."** ⛔ **It does not.** It has no unstructured-baseline arm (Table 13(a) is an ablation of *their* supervision, not of the decomposition against a plain predictor). Cite it for the mechanism and for the **frozen-trunk + future-feature-prediction + moving-camera** setting; do not cite it as a measurement of our failure.
