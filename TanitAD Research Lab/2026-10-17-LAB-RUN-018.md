| `2007.03964` Stooke 2020 | The textbook multiplier update is integral control on the constraint. ### 2.2 Does any result say when a gradient-ascent dual lags or oscillates? — yes, and the numbers

* **Oscillates:** Stooke §6: "oscillations or overshoot with slow settling time in a majority of Safety Gym environments" for the plain Lagrangian at moderate `K_I`; the mechanism is an ill-tuned integral controller (90-degree phase). Elenter Fig. 1: 30-45 % of iterates infeasible per constraint. ReLOAD Fig. 1: single-run oscillation invisible in the seed mean.
* **Lags / releases late:** CRPO's release-delay passage; CPO §8.1: "after the dual variable decreases, the agent could learn a new behavior that increases the correct dual variable more quickly than PDO can attain it".
* **Saturates:** **none of the five dual-method primaries reports a `λ_max` default or a saturation event.** Only RCPO mentions the projection, to say it may be infinite. Single-pass absence (five primaries read in full for this; not hardened).

### 2.3 What the published defaults are, and why they do not port as numbers (PUBLISHED, `2007.03964` Tab. 1, Figs. 3-9)

`K_P ∈ {0.1, 0.25, 1}`, `K_I ∈ [1e-4, 1]` (moderate **1e-3**), `K_D ∈ {2, 4, 5}`; measurement smoothing **EMA 0.95 on the P input, 0.9 on the D input, D as a 15-iteration delayed difference**; control rescale `u = λ/(1+λ)` so the step size stays consistent ("our experiments use this re-scaling, including for traditional Lagrangian baselines"); `λ0 = 0`; policy lr **1e-4**; one dual update per iteration of **128 × 104 = 13,312** samples, so their 4e7-1e8-step runs are **~3,000-7,500 dual updates** (DERIVED from Tab. 1 and the figure axes). The ratio `K_P/K_I` is **100** in the two runs where both are stated (Fig. 3: 1 / 1e-2; Fig. 9: 0.1 / 1e-3); Fig. 4 sweeps `K_I` over 1e-4…1 at `K_P ∈ {0.25, 1}`.

* ⚠️ **The absolute numbers do not port**: their cost is an episode count in tens (limits 25-200), ours a probability near 0.9 with a violation at 0.005. Per-update λ movement, however, is the same order: moderate `K_I = 1e-3` on a violation of tens gives ~0.01-0.1 per iteration, our `eta · g = 4 × 0.0174 = 0.070` (DERIVED). **So `eta = 4` is not obviously extreme; what differs is the horizon: 1,000 updates is 1/3 to 1/8 of Stooke's runs, and a cycle of a few hundred updates would put one to two periods inside the whole run.**
* ⚠️ **Our SPEC §1.2 has no counterpart to the `1/(1+λ)` rescale.** Under AdamW a λ ramp changes the gradient scale and the second-moment estimate lags. HYPOTHESIS (not read in a trace): check `con_lam_*` against update size in R0d's first 250 updates.

### 3.1 Carve-size arithmetic (DERIVED, ESTIMATED inputs)
