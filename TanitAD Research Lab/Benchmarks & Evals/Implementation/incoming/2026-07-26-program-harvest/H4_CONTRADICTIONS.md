## Method, and its limits — read before quoting anything below

**What was swept.** All **137** agent deliverable directories under `TanitAD Research Hub/*/Implementation/incoming/` (Architecture & Inference 43, Benchmarks & Eval 49, Data Engineering 26, Opponent Analyzer 3, Production & Optimization 7, Tools&DevEnv 4), plus `Project Steering/` (`MODEL_REGISTRY.md`, `RETRACTION_LOG.md`, `LOOP_STATE.md`, `GATE_PROTOCOL.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, and the independent chief-scientist review under `Reviews/`), `Paper/TANITAD_PAPER.md`, `Benchmarks & Eval/LEADERBOARD.md`, `TanitAD Research Hub/HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER.md`, and `taniteval/results/` (raw JSON, opened directly for the highest-stakes rows).

**How.** Six parallel read-only extraction passes (one per hub-category/date-slice) each built a claim index — quantity, value, exact path, date, evidence class — from primary sources (their own `.md` reports plus the raw JSON each one cites), and flagged internal candidate contradictions. I independently re-verified the highest-stakes candidates myself by opening the cited raw files directly (upgrading them from INHERITED to MEASURED), cross-checked every candidate against `RETRACTION_LOG.md`, `MODEL_REGISTRY.md`'s own self-corrections, and `HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER.md` to exclude anything already resolved, and ranked what remained by blast radius (whose live decision or widely-read document rests on the weaker side).

**What I could not verify / limits, stated rather than hidden:**
- The `Architecture & Inference` 2026-07-25/26 slice (21 dirs) was extracted by a single dedicated pass; I independently re-verified only its highest-stakes rows (the ones below), not every cell of its 220-row index.
- `Data Engineering`'s 2026-07-26 dirs were extracted same-day as several of the corrections they report; a few of their "same-day, self-corrected" findings (e.g. wheelbase, "N of 36 features") were cross-checked against `RETRACTION_LOG.md`/`CLAUDE.md` and found **already resolved there** — they are named in §"Excluded as already-resolved" below rather than re-listed as live.
- I did not re-run any script or reproduce any number that required a pod or GPU; every MEASURED tag below means I personally opened the cited file (not that I recomputed the statistic from scratch).
- Dozens of lower-confidence, lower-blast-radius candidates surfaced during the sweep (rounding-scale prose/JSON mismatches, definition-mismatches explained by different sample sizes, same-day self-corrections that never left their own directory) and are **not** reproduced here to keep the ranking usable; they remain visible in the six scratchpad claim-index files this sweep produced (paths in the deliverable manifest) for a follow-up pass.
- 🔒 No PhysicalAI-AV clip UUIDs or raw content appear below.

**Evidence discipline.** Every row states an evidence class per side (MEASURED/PUBLISHED/INHERITED/ESTIMATED/HYPOTHESIS) and a tier: **CONFIRMED** = I opened both sides' primary artifacts myself; **PROVISIONAL** = one path, from a single sibling sweep, not independently re-opened by me. Nothing below is DECISION-GRADE (that requires a pre-registered falsifier, which a *retrospective* contradiction audit does not carry).
