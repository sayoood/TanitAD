The proposal

Four layers, defined by ordinal position

STRATEGIC GOALg_str
The OVERNEXT manoeuvre — the one after the one being planned — with args. FOLLOW_ROUTE when there is none. This is the change that makes the layer meaningful: it answers "and then what", which is exactly what a 6 s plan cannot see.
STRATEGIC ACTIONa_str
What the ego does about the overnext manoeuvre: PREPARE_TURN_L/R PREPARE_STOP PREPARE_LANE_CHANGE HOLD_CORRIDOR REDUCE_TO RESUME_CRUISE PREPARE_EXIT — with within_m / by_time_s.
TACTICAL GOALg_tac — ONE token
The NEXT manoeuvre, as a single semantic intent with continuous args. No longer split into LAT and LON. A goal is one decision; splitting it forced two half-answers that could disagree with each other.
TACTICAL ACTIONSa_tac — lat × lon
Stays split, because steering and speed are genuinely two independent control axes. This is where the factoring earns its keep.

§4 — the one objection I owe you

Unifying the goal reverses an earlier decision

The LAT×LON goal split exists to retire the 5-way mixed softmax

The current split was introduced deliberately: E-AG1 measured that a K-way anchor_id classifier is near-adequate laterally (1.331 vs a 0.680 floor) and hopeless longitudinally (13.350 vs 0.895, 14.9×), because the 2 s goal point's variance is 98.8 % longitudinal. One categorical carrying both axes is the programme's single largest known defect, one level up.

Why unifying is nonetheless safe here — and I want you to check this reasoning. That failure is about quantising a continuous position into a categorical index. The proposal keeps the token semantic (TURN_L, YIELD, STOP_POINT) and puts all geometry in continuous args (goal_x_m, within_m, v_target_ms). Nothing forces one softmax to encode both axes — so the defect the split guards against cannot occur.

⇒ The split was the right fix for the wrong-shaped token. With a semantic token and metric args, the split costs consistency (two halves that can disagree) and buys nothing.

Sizing

How much of the corpus this actually touches

Manoeuvre sequence depth (801 clips)

16.4%

131 clips have an overnext manoeuvre — only these can carry a meaningful strategic goal token. 214 (26.7 %) have exactly one manoeuvre ⇒ strategic becomes FOLLOW_ROUTE. 456 (56.9 %) have none ⇒ both layers are follow-route/lane.

⚠️ Consequence worth deciding on: under this design the strategic goal is a turn token on ~16 % of clips and FOLLOW_ROUTE on ~84 %. That is a heavily skewed target — honest, but it will need class weighting or it teaches the head to always say follow-route.

Yield coverage

5.8%

15 of 257 Alpamayo-covered clips mention a yield. So YIELD_FOR_TURN_* is emittable but rare, and per the existing rule (n < 200) it will be representable, not scoreable.

Geometry can supply the hold; the VLM term corroborates. I would emit it from geometry (brief hold immediately before an arc) and let the VLM term raise confidence — never the reverse, given the measured hallucination rate.
