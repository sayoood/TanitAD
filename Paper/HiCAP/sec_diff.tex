\paragraph{What is new, and what is not.}
Frozen-backbone contrastive selection (CLM, CoVer-VLA), large fixed vocabularies with per-candidate cost heads (VADv2, Hydra-MDP, GTRS) and coarse-to-fine scoring (DriveSuprim) are prior art; so are latent world models and conformal prediction.
Tree retrieval over node embeddings, cascades of conformal sets with a summed miss budget \citep{fisch2021cascade}, predict-then-plan with an open-loop forecast and an analytic time-to-collision cost, and outcome critics of the Contrastive RL kind are likewise known. The claim of this paper is the \emph{composition} and its application to driving under this programme's constraints, together with four points whose \emph{combination} we did not find in the literature we read (absence at the depth of our search, not a proof):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A frozen, versioned, hierarchical token vocabulary as the action side of a contrastive selector}, with text-defined nodes, a factored path-by-profile trajectory vocabulary beneath them (the structure of \citet{sun2026sparsedrivev2}, the densest we found, here partitioned by the programme's lateral and longitudinal tokens), level-wide marginal critics, cost and bandwidth that scale with the number of rows touched rather than entries (Proposition~\ref{prop:cost}), and factored lateral/longitudinal scoring so that the two decisions remain separately measurable.
\item \textbf{A pruning cascade with a stated recall bound whose survivors return to the backbone.} Physics, state-conditioned admissibility, hysteresis and conformal keep-sets reduce the set before any expensive step (Proposition~\ref{prop:conformal}, measured mask in \S\ref{sec:prelim}), and the survivors become candidate tokens for late-interaction re-scoring and an optional language verifier. Coarse-to-fine scorers prune with learned scores and no coverage statement.
\item \textbf{Identifiable, tiered imagination.} Restricting imagination to outcome-aware soft targets, an analytically integrated forecast of non-reactive exogenous descriptors and a gated critic with a disagreement veto makes the counterfactual evaluation of a candidate a statement about observational data (Proposition~\ref{prop:ident}); our own toy and earlier measurements showed why an unrestricted learned counterfactual is unsafe (anti-conservative without counterfactual labels, and it inverts the sign of confidence against regret).
\item \textbf{Elastic sensing and audio under one embedding interface with a distillable state side.} Camera and token budgets are chosen per tick from cheap cues, audio (optional at inference) enters the backbone's token space with explicit null and shuffle controls, and because action embeddings never depend on images, a small student can occupy the same interface and reuse the teacher's action table, with a decision-preservation margin (Proposition~\ref{prop:distill}) should a latency tier fail.
\end{enumerate}
\paragraph{Relation to the programme's own designs.}
Two internal designs overlap with HiCAP, and it is stated here so that neither is re-invented. The flat contrastive selector (REF-F, pre-registration H-F1--H-F8) is the one-level special case of HiCAP, and its hypotheses are contained in \S\ref{sec:protocol}.
TanitLang's ``Energy Bridge'' design (the programme's latent-reasoning proposal, dated 2026-09-06) re-ranks the planner's fan by a learned compatibility energy $S_1(k)=S_0(k)-\beta\,E_\phi(z,\tau_k)$ and audits consistency with the same function; language is kept off the control path, and its released-vocabulary layer enters as zero-initialised log-priors on the anchors\evI.
HiCAP can be read as the missing \emph{objective} for that energy: with $E=-e^{\tau}\langle\hat{\mathbf s},\hat{\mathbf a}\rangle$ the authority $\beta$ and the logit scale are one degree of freedom, and InfoNCE is the contrastive answer to the design's open question of how the energy should be trained.
The differences are that HiCAP's vocabulary nodes are \emph{actions} carrying trajectory codes rather than priors on a fixed fan of $117$--$128$ anchors, that the selector may be standalone (full authority) rather than a re-ranker, and that the model-selected positive of the consistency loss and the ground-truth-nearest positive of the selector loss are kept as separate terms. HiCAP inherits TanitLang's constraint that language is not on the control path, and its controls (constant, shuffled and norm-matched state) reappear as NC1.
The symbol $\beta$ means authority there and the log-prior weight here; we use $\beta_\ell$ only for the latter.

\paragraph{Where HiCAP is deliberately conservative.} Language never generates: it embeds token definitions and, only in uncertain ticks, verifies. Nothing in the goal path carries a situation-classifier output. Every oracle input is stamped and paired with its ablation. And hypotheses are registered with their refutation consequences, so that a negative outcome (for instance, that a larger vocabulary does not help, H-HC5) redirects the programme instead of being explained away.
