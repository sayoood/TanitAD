\section{Problem setting and data interface}\label{sec:prelim-setting}\label{sec:problem}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Decision problem.}
At each tick $t$ the system receives a state $s_t=(I_t^{\mathcal S_t},\,e_t,\,n_t,\,u_t)$: frames $I_t^{\mathcal S_t}$ from a chosen
camera subset $\mathcal S_t$ (three frames, 100\,ms apart, per camera); the ego kinematic state $e_t=(v,a_{\text{lon}},\dot\psi)$ at the
cycle time; a navigation command $n_t\in\{\text{follow},\text{left},\text{right}\}$ with optional range and time arguments; and, in the audio arm only,
a window of microphone samples $u_t$. It outputs a 6\,s ego trajectory $\hat y\in\Real^{8\times2}$ at the slots
$t_k\in\{0.5,1,1.5,2,3,4,5,6\}$\,s in the ego frame, which is the trajectory contract of the programme's planner line.
The decision is a \emph{leaf} $a=(v_1,v_2,v_3)$ of the action tree of \S\ref{sec:vocab} plus a small continuous refinement $\delta\in\Real^{8\times 2}$:
$\hat y=\pi_{\text{prior}}(e_t)+\rho(v_3)+\delta$, where $\pi_{\text{prior}}$ is a constant-turn-rate-and-acceleration (CTRA) roll-out of the ego state and
$\rho(v_3)$ is the residual profile of the leaf's code.

\paragraph{Data.}
We build on the newest programme corpus, not on the older parity corpus that the repository's default branch still describes.
The corpus is the private Hugging Face dataset \texttt{tanitad-v7-training-corpus}: $4{,}719$ clips, $26.2$ hours of front-camera driving,
$4{,}572$ train and $147$ evaluation clips, corpus id \texttt{a48251e89c7a8603}, label schema \texttt{s2-geom-v7}, released as v7, v7.1, v7.2 and v8.1
(the vocabulary is identical across releases; releases add fields)\evI. It is deliberately a \emph{new comparability domain}: it does not re-select
the canonical parity corpus of $2{,}376$ episodes, and arms trained on it are compared only with arms trained on it. Frames are a cylindrical
projection at $256\times640$ pixels covering a true $120^\circ$ field of view (linear in azimuth, $f_{\text{ref}}=305.577$), with a mandatory
per-clip vertical crop because two camera rigs differ by about $215$ pixels. Only the front-wide camera is cached; the upstream dataset has seven
cameras, which matters for \S\ref{sec:elastic}. None of the 36 upstream features is audio and no label or cache carries it; the audio stream inside the shipped video container is unprobed (UNVERIFIED).

\paragraph{What the labels are, and what they are not.}
Labels may use privileged quantities (ego future, other agents); inference may not: it sees frames, the ego state admitted by the PI on 2026-09-02, the stamped navigation command and, optionally, the audio channel admitted on 2026-09-29. Table~\ref{tab:inputs} states, for every quantity,
whether it is a training label, an admissible inference input, or an evaluation stratifier only. The strategic and tactical tokens are derived offline from the
\emph{ego's own future path} (geometry), so they are legitimate targets and inadmissible inputs. The perception-style tactical goals
(traffic-light colours, gap targets, oncoming reactions) are teacher signals parsed from Alpamayo's chain-of-causation text; $49.6\%$ of records carry at least one
goal marked disputed and traffic-light colour was never independently verified\evI. They may supervise auxiliary heads but never enter as inputs, and every head on them
carries an ego-only control.

\paragraph{Information-disjointness of the goal path.}
Two programme rules bind the design. First, a route or goal input may not carry the output of a situation classifier, otherwise attribution between
goal and classifier is lost and the classifier is scored through the planner. HiCAP contains no situation-classifier \emph{head}, and its only route-side input is the
stamped navigation command; evaluation-time situation strata (\texttt{tac\_SIT}) never re-enter. But the tactical cells and the 22-goal head share the readout $\mathbf s_2$ of one trunk, and fourteen of those goals are teacher-text situation-like labels (traffic-light colours, yield, gap, merge). We therefore do not claim disjointness: only geometry-derived goals may act on the decision (rule R4), the teacher-text goals are auxiliary with no path into any decision, and a goal-shuffled control and a strategic-token-null ablation bound what the goal path carries (NC6).
Second, the parent's selection restricts the child candidates
(a hard beam) but is not fed to the child's state readout, so the child level cannot echo the parent's decision as a feature; the control ``flat tactical'' (no parent restriction) tests whether the restriction itself matters (\S\ref{sec:protocol}).

\subsection{Training on the programme corpus}\label{sec:training-data}
HiCAP is trained on the programme's own corpus with the newest tactical-label release, which the PI identifies as v9, hosted on the Hugging Face hub and the release that the reference arms refcv8 and v8f use. \textbf{We have not read that release:} the dataset identifier, schema, clip and hour counts, vocabulary strings, skip-hash and evaluation split are not in the repository, and our search of the PI's Drive did not locate its manifest (UNVERIFIED). Every v7 figure in this paper (4{,}719 clips, 26.2 hours, 4{,}572 train and 147 evaluation clips, the 35 tactical cells, the 22 goals, the 4{,}823-window panel, the refcv5-v2 reference) is therefore inherited and is re-based when the v9 manifest is read; the first action of the plan is a diff of the v9 vocabulary and counts against Appendix~\ref{app:vocab}, and every table that depends on them is regenerated by the committed scripts.
The training plan has four steps, each with a stated cost. (1)~Build the backbone cache once per backbone and camera set (\S\ref{sec:eff}); this is the only step that needs the large backbone. (2)~Gate H-HC0 on the cache. (3)~Train the $\approx27$\,M trainable part on cached tokens through the three-stage curriculum with the level-wide losses of \S\ref{sec:train}: stage~1 on the strategic and tactical levels with soft targets, stage~2 adding the factored trajectory levels and cost heads, stage~3 hard negatives and the refinement head with $40\%$ replay; one run is GPU-minutes to GPU-hours on cached tensors, not the $\approx45$ GPU-hours of the flat reference. (4)~Evaluate against refcv8 and v8f on the identical windows of the v9 evaluation split, all four metric families, paired episode-cluster bootstrap on the full-set mean, each arm with a seed-1 replicate; the v8f arms are the hierarchy-attention reference. Evaluation clips are never used for any fit, and the contamination screen of \S\ref{sec:backbone-choice} runs on them first.
