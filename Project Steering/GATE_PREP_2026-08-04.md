### Two guarantees, kept separate

`be2da04` distinguishes a **structurally exact VARIABLE** policy (decode every survivor) from a
**FIXED `N_suff` budget**, which is an **EMPIRICAL CALIBRATION** — XL's worst window had 102
survivors against a budget of 92, and held only because the winner's rank never exceeded 92 *on that
corpus*. `anchor_prefilter_report` therefore asserts `winner_survives_frac == 1.0` **per run**.
Below 1.0 the prefilter would change the emitted trajectory: a **correctness** failure, not a speed
regression.

⚠️ **The ego-dropout leak is WORSE here than at S2.** `v0` is the pre-dropout speed; at S2 it could
bias a ranking, but pre-decode it decides which candidates **exist**. Callers must OR in `~ego_keep`
exactly as `refc.py` does. Pinned by `test_the_band_is_defeatable_per_sample_for_ego_dropout`.
