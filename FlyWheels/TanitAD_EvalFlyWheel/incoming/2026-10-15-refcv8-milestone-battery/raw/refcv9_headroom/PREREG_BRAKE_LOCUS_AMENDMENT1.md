## A3. The random-candidate control for the sibling's "SELECTION" verdict (coordinator, 2026-10-19): row ALONG-RAND

Sibling: `refcv9/L1_DESIGN_ANNEX.md` section 1.2, code `refcv9/l1_probe/along_track_localise.py`: on S2 compliant EMA-30k the emitted plan's along-track alignment is r 0.38-0.40, the GT-nearest refined candidate (`oracle_sel`, a_star) reaches r 0.76.  That compares a pick-by-GT-proximity with the model's pick, which looks well-timed by construction.

**Fan banked?  NO.**  Only `oracle_sel` (the a_star candidate, refined, S2 grid) and the selected anchor's unrefined path exist; a random or median candidate of the refined fan cannot be formed from the dumps.  The control therefore needs a re-roll (design: `BRAKE_LOCUS_REROLL_DESIGN.md`), and is NOT MEASURABLE here.  Pre-registered now, for when the fan exists:

* Statistics, EXACTLY as `along_track_localise.py`: arc length `s_X(t_k)` = cumulative chord length of the polyline through (0,0) and X's points at the S2 instants (slots 0..3 = 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 s); `P` = the causal prior path (`ha0_ext_pose`, the model's own `residual_prior_path`); `d_X = s_X - s_P`, `h = s_H - s_P`; pooled over windows x instants with per-episode sums `<d,h>`, `<d,d>`, `<h,h>`; **g\* = <d,h>/<d,d>**, **r = <d,h>/sqrt(<d,d><h,h>)** (the sibling's r is this UNCENTRED cosine, which is what is committed; the corr(d,h) wording of the request is the centred variant and is carried as a sensitivity).
* Arms, on the SAME windows (S2 compliant, EMA-30k eval139, 4,159 windows / 138 episodes, or the re-rolled subset of them) and BOTH inference seeds separately: **os** (emitted), **RAND** = one uniformly random candidate of the fan per window (fixed rng seed 20261019, drawn per window), **MED** = the median-progress candidate (the candidate whose arc length at 2.0 s is the median over the fan; the lower median for an even count, ties to the lowest index), **ORC** = the a_star candidate (reproduces the sibling).  Fan = the reach-surviving candidates (`reach_keep == 1`); sensitivities: all 149; base 117 only.  Additional descriptive: 20 further random draws (spread of r), and the **best-of-k curve**: the GT-nearest candidate (a_star rule, squared error over the 8 slots) of a random k-subset, k in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, all}, which shows how much of ORC's r is the by-construction effect of picking by GT.
* Statistic of the rule: `dR = r_os - r_RAND`, paired episode-cluster bootstrap (shared resample matrix, B 2000, seed 0), per seed.  **SELECTOR-ADDS-VALUE** iff the CI lower bound of `dR` > 0 at BOTH seeds (os separated above the random candidate); **SELECTION-SUPPORTED** iff `lo <= 0` at BOTH seeds (os not separated above random); otherwise **SEED-SPLIT**.  MED is reported beside it (same statistic) and is descriptive; a disagreement between the RAND and MED readings is stamped.  Same reading rule is written for g\*.
* Controls to read before any number: K1 `d := h` -> g\* = r = 1; K2 `d := 2h` -> g\* 0.5, r 1; K3 `d := h + e`, e orthogonal with |e| = |h| -> g\* 0.5, r = 1/sqrt(2) (all implemented and tested in `brake_locus_r8.along_stats`, control C7); a synthetic iid-residual fan -> RAND r ~ 0; a synthetic fan whose selection picks the human-exact candidate -> r 1.  When the re-roll exists: (R6) the re-rolled N arm's os must equal the banked `os` on the same windows (max abs reported; r and g\* from BANKED os on the same subset must equal the N-arm values), and ORC from the re-rolled fan must reproduce the banked `oracle_sel` plan.
* Interpretation limit, stated in advance: a random fan candidate has a residual that is unrelated to the human's unless the fan itself is concentrated near plausible behaviour, so `os` separated above RAND is the EXPECTED outcome and says only that the selector is better than chance; it does not say the selector is near the best the fan offers.  The best-of-k curve is the control that bounds that.
