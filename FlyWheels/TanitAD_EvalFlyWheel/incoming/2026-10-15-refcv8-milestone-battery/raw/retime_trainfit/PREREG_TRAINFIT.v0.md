## 0. Why this test exists (what the parent result can and cannot say)
`RESULTS_BRIEF.md` of a0k0_retime: RETIME_XF clears the E1 form and ACC-LAT, BUT its gains were fitted on the EVAL episodes (5-fold episode cross-fit) and the arm form was chosen after two earlier arms (RS_XF, RS_LON_XF) were read on the same eval set. That is a **lever estimate**, not a bar pass. This test removes the eval-fit: the gains are fitted on TRAIN clips only, frozen, and applied unchanged to the existing eval139 EMA-30k trees (zero GPU for the scoring half). It does NOT remove the disclosure that the arm FORM was chosen after seeing eval reads of its predecessors; it only tests whether the calibration transfers from clips the model trained on to clips it did not.

## 5. Outcome labels, committed in advance (RETIME is the labelled arm; GATED_RETIME is reported beside and may not rescue or sink it)
Let on the VERDICT tree, compliant set, both inference seeds: **E1** = the `bar_E` verdict of FZR-E1; **E1P** = arm - `ha0_ext_pose` S2 ADE is < 0 and separated at both seeds; **LAT** = the ACC-LAT label; **NR** = every family of the FZR-NR rollup is PASS; **MOVES** = S2 ADE arm - os is < 0 and separated at both seeds.
* **FAIL** = LAT label is FAILS, OR any family of NR is FAIL, OR NOT MOVES.
* **DEPLOYABLE-PASS** = not FAIL AND E1 verdict starts with PASS AND E1P AND LAT label is HOLDS (not "HOLDS VACUOUSLY") AND NR.
* **PARTIAL** = not FAIL and not DEPLOYABLE-PASS (the lever transfers and does no lateral/NR harm, but does not clear the echo bar: E1 FAILED or NOT PROVEN, or E1P not separated).
What each implies (named now, so a result is never left as a diagnosis):
* DEPLOYABLE-PASS -> RETIME with train-frozen gains is the first honest, zero-training lever on refcv8's plan. Next (no new compute decision needed except the PI's adoption call): apply `FROZEN_GAINS_RETIME_train.json` UNCHANGED to the step-40k / 48,852 trees when they exist (`retime_trainfit_r8.py forward --tree <tree>`, zero GPU) as the genuine different-checkpoint test, and put the adoption of an inference-time re-timing stage (a post-hoc planner stage, not a retrain) to the PI.
* PARTIAL -> it transfers, attenuated. Next, executed in the same run: report the attenuation against the eval-fit gains and against the measured train-vs-eval `os` gap (section 6); the PI decision is whether to ship the smaller honest gains or to fit on a clip set the model did not train on; the 40k forward test still runs.
* FAIL -> the eval-fit estimate in `a0k0_retime` is not a transferable lever at this calibration. Per PREREG_RETIME's FAIL branch the next step is the PI decision on a LEARNED calibration trained on TRAIN with the lateral rows in its loss (refcv8.x / refcv9), NOT a fourth hand-crafted rescaling. Reading caveat committed now: train clips are in-sample for the model, so a FAIL by MOVES with fitted gains all near 1 is the expected failure signature of the shrinkage caveat below, and is reported as "uninformative about the lever, informative about the fit set" - it is still labelled FAIL, because the bar is the bar.

## 6. The named caveat, and its own measured number
Train clips are IN-SAMPLE for the model. **Prediction (HYPOTHESIS, written before data): `os` is better on the train windows than on eval139, and the train-fitted gains sit closer to 1 than the eval-fitted ones (a conservative test).** Measured and reported as its own table: `os` ADE on train vs eval (S2 and S6; all windows and compliant; per inference seed) each with an episode-cluster bootstrap CI, the unpaired difference (independent episode-cluster resampling within each set - the two sets are different clips, so a paired estimator does not exist), and the same for the echo `ha0_ext` so the **difficulty-normalised ratio `os / ha0_ext` on each set** is the headline gap (the raw gap confounds in-sample-ness with clip difficulty). Also: train-fit vs eval-fit gains per instant, and `(1 - g)` shrinkage.
