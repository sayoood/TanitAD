## 6. Caveats - read before quoting

1. **One training seed per model.** The bar compares ONE refcv8 training run to ONE refcv7 training run. A separated interval is necessary, not sufficient (H-ESTIM-SEED-1); a replicate (same flags, other training seed) is what would make 'refcv8 lowers LEAD-VIOL' a lever claim.
2. **Small event counts.** 33-37 violating windows (refcv8) vs 49-50 (refcv7) among 830 at-risk, from 63 episodes. The signal is a difference of ~12-17 windows.
3. **Feed asymmetry in the baseline.** refcv8 is scored on its per-frame nav feed (manifest `nav_feed.mode = perframe_full`); the refcv7 panel's manifest carries no `nav_feed` block - refcv7 is the banked battery roll (`D:/refcv7_eval_kit/battery/step50400_a6text`), not re-rolled on the per-frame feed. Both navs are ego-future-derived oracle tokens (optimistic by construction), so neither row is a deployable-nav number.
4. **Lost-lead windows are not violations under the registered text** (a plan that leaves the lead's corridor has an undefined time gap). They are counted separately: refcv8-48,852 loses the lead on 27 / 23 windows (refcv7: 28) and `share_incl_lost_lead` ranks the same way (0.0539 / 0.0460 vs 0.0652 / 0.0660). Sensitivity, not the bar.
5. **Lead windows are the B1 block's `LEAD` state, not the v9 `lead_valid`** (same definition as the refcv7 baseline; both sides must use it). S-ROUTE LEAD-VIOL (SPEC names S2 and S-ROUTE) is NOT computed - blocker below.
6. **`separated` for S2 `+0.0011 [0.0000, 0.0036]` is a display rounding of a lower bound a hair above/below zero**; the instrument decides on unrounded bounds and says not separated.
